## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**From: Atomic Habits** — *James Clear* — "You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems." Identity-based change means you don't just set a target — you decide who you are becoming, then cast small daily votes to prove it to yourself. The person who never misses twice isn't more motivated; they've built a system that makes consistency the path of least resistance.

**From: The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People** — *Stephen R. Covey* — The key is not to prioritize what's on your schedule, but to schedule your priorities. High-effectiveness people protect Quadrant 2 (Not Urgent but Important — studying, planning, relationships, learning) because that's where crises are *prevented* before they ever become urgent. Most people live in Q1 and Q3 reacting to whatever feels pressing; guarding Q2 time is the single discipline that changes everything downstream.

**From: Steve Jobs** — *Walter Isaacson* — Jobs's most underrated lesson is the humanities × sciences intersection: a calligraphy class he audited after dropping out of Reed College directly gave the Macintosh its beautiful proportional typography — the first personal computer that cared about type. The innovations that reshape industries rarely come from technical depth alone; they emerge at the crossroads where craft, taste, and engineering collide. "Creativity is just connecting things."
