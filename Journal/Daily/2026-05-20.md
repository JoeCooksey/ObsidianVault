# Daily Note: 2026-05-20

## 🙏 Daily Prayer

*Category: Supplication*

Heavenly Father, I come to You today not because I have it all figured out, but precisely because I don't. Give me wisdom for the decisions in front of me — the ones that feel heavy and the ones I've been avoiding. Where I've been shortsighted, open my eyes. Where I've been driven by fear or pride, replace those with genuine faith and humility. Provide for the people I love — their health, their peace, their sense of purpose. Protect those who are struggling in ways I can't see. Give me courage to be useful to the people around me today, not for recognition, but because serving others is what You've called me to. And when the day gets noisy and I lose the thread, bring me back to what actually matters. Amen.

## 📚 Lesson of the Day

**From: The Psychology of Money** — *Morgan Housel*
Money's highest dividend isn't wealth itself — it's the freedom to control your own time. The goal of building financial security isn't a target number; it's reaching the point where you can do what you want, with whom you want, when you want. Most people chase more money to impress others, but no one looking at your car is thinking about you — they're imagining themselves behind the wheel.

**From: The Laws of Human Nature** — *Robert Greene*
Without a clear Life's Task rooted in your childhood Primal Inclinations, you drift — pulled by whatever trend or opinion is loudest at the moment. The greatest sense of purpose doesn't come from money, status, or attention-chasing; it comes from aligning what you do daily with what naturally energized you before the world told you what to want. Break that long-term vision into descending Goal Ladders so anxiety doesn't swallow your direction.

**From: Never Eat Alone** — *Keith Ferrazzi & Tahl Raz*
"The currency of real networking is not greed but generosity." Build relationships before you ever need them — a network built out of desperation is weak by design. The shift from *what can I gain?* to *what can I give?* is what separates people who make meaningful connections from people who just collect contacts. Follow up within 24 hours of every meaningful interaction; the follow-up matters more than the first meeting.
