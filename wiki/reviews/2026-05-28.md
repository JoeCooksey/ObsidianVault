## Card 1 · The 7 Habits

**Q:** What is the Maturity Continuum, and why does most self-help stop too early?

> [!tip]- Answer
> Dependence → Independence → Interdependence. Most self-help targets Independence ('I can do it'). But the highest leverage is Interdependence ('we can do it together') — results achievable in collaboration exceed anything an individual can produce alone.

## Card 2 · Leonardo da Vinci

**Q:** What is Sfumato, and what is its philosophical meaning beyond painting?

> [!tip]- Answer
> Sfumato (Italian: 'gone up in smoke') — Leonardo's painting technique of blurring edges, avoiding hard outlines, letting forms emerge from shadow. The Mona Lisa's smile is sfumato — it shifts as you look. Philosophically: the willingness to live in uncertainty without forcing premature resolution. Hold contradictions simultaneously.
