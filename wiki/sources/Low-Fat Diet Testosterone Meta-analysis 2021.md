# Whittaker & Wu — Low-Fat Diets and Testosterone in Men (2021)

Systematic review + meta-analysis (J Steroid Biochem Mol Biol), 9 databases, 6 eligible intervention studies, 206 participants.

## What it contributes
Quantifies that **cutting dietary fat lowers androgens** — consistent with the cholesterol→testosterone pathway:
- Total T: SMD **−0.38** (95% CI −0.75 to −0.01)
- Free T: **−0.37** (−0.63 to −0.11)
- Urinary T: **−0.38**; DHT: **−0.3**
- European/American subgroup: stronger, **−0.52**

Supports the "eat adequate fat (≥~20–35% kcal)" recommendation and flags very-low-fat dieting as a suppressor.

## Caveats
- Few, mostly small/older studies; observational-leaning designs
- Authors explicitly call for confirmatory RCTs → confidence **medium**.
