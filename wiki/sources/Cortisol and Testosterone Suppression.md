# Chronic Stress, Cortisol and Testosterone Suppression

Mechanistic + animal evidence on how chronic stress lowers testosterone.

## What it contributes
Four converging mechanisms:
1. **Central suppression** — cortisol inhibits hypothalamic GnRH and pituitary LH release
2. **Direct gonadal** — cortisol inhibits Leydig cells; chronic stress damages Leydig-cell mitochondria via Atp5a1
3. **Precursor competition** — cortisol and T are both made from cholesterol; stress prioritizes cortisol
4. **Resource prioritization** — perceived ongoing threat down-weights reproduction/muscle

A 2023 analysis found **cortisol spikes precede T drops by 0.5–3 days**, a clear temporal link.

## Practical takeaway
Stress management (sleep, recovery, breathwork/mindfulness) removes a real suppressor — though sleep and training do most of the heavy lifting. Evidence **medium** (much is animal/mechanistic).
