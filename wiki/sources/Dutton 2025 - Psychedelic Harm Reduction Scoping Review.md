# Dutton 2025 — Harm Reduction Practises for Users of Psychedelic Drugs: A Scoping Review

*Harm Reduction Journal*, 2025. Peer-reviewed scoping review — the best single evidence map of what psychedelic users actually do to stay safe, and the backbone source for [[Psilocybin Trip Activities Tier List]].

## Structure of findings

**Before use:** knowledge seeking (16 studies — peers/forums, not doctors), mindset cultivation (13 — surrender, trust, calm), setting selection (16), safety strategies (12 — source verification, dosing, no poly-drug), body prep (5 — diet, abstinence).

**During use:** emotional support / [[Trip Sitter]] (16 studies), **music** — playlist preparation and changing music in real time to regulate emotion (4 studies), environmental modification — moving to nature or changing rooms to manage distress (4 studies).

**After use:** journaling, peer discussion, reflection (7 studies); users with integration strategies showed greater psychological growth.

## Other findings

- [[Set and Setting]] confirmed as the central controllable variable, consistent with clinical practice
- Higher doses → more sitter use; ~21% accept remote sitting
- Poly-drug psilocybin use in naturalistic settings linked to negative consequences and emergency admissions
- Intervention gap: only 4 evaluated programs (KomsiCare ~50% distress reduction; Fireside Project 65.9% de-escalation; DIPP 21-day prep program; drug checking — low uptake, rare adulterants like methamphetamine found)

## Contribution

Grounds the S/A tiers of the activity list in peer-reviewed evidence: music, support, nature/environment change, journaling, and surrender are not folklore — they are the documented practice set.
