# 101 Habits — Dan Zakaria

YouTube listicle (~30 min) of 101 habits for discipline, mindset, identity, focus, time, health, and success. Heavily derivative of **[[Atomic Habits - James Clear]]** — opens and closes on James Clear's systems-over-goals and identity-based-habits framing.

## Core Thesis
- **Systems > goals.** "You don't rise to the level of your goals, you fall to the level of your systems." Habits *are* the systems.
- **Identity is upstream of habits.** "You don't have a discipline problem, you have an identity problem." Every action is a *vote* for the person you're becoming → see [[Identity-Based vs Goal-Based Habits]].
- **Make good habits easy, bad habits hard** (reduce/add friction) → the [[Atomic Habits - James Clear|Four Laws]] in compressed form.
- **Consistency > intensity.** "The best habit is the one you can stick to for 10 years." 1% better daily ≈ 37× per year.
