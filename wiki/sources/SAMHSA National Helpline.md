# SAMHSA National Helpline

The U.S. government's free national helpline for mental health and substance use.

## Key facts
- **1-800-662-HELP (4357)** — free, confidential, **24/7/365**, English and Spanish.
- For individuals **and family members** facing mental health and/or substance use disorders.
- Provides **referrals** to local treatment facilities, support groups, and community-based organizations — not counseling itself, but the routing layer to find it.
- Complements **988** (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) for acute crisis. See [[Crisis and Help Resources (Mental Health and Substance Use)]].
