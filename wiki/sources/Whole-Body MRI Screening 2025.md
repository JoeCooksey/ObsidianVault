# Whole-Body MRI Screening (Prenuvo et al.) — 2025 Assessment

Whole-body MRI markets itself as a longevity/cancer screen but lacks outcome evidence for average-risk asymptomatic adults. ACR advises against routine use; ~1.6% confirmed-cancer yield with a high false-positive cascade. Basis for the **D-tier** rating: expensive, low yield, anxiety-inducing for most healthy people.
