# The AI Revolution in Math (Quanta)

Quanta Magazine feature on AI's arrival in research-level mathematics, and the corresponding debate among mathematicians about obsolescence vs. augmentation.

## What It Contributes

- Evidence that AI is becoming a genuine *collaborator* in math (hypotheses, proof assistance), not just a calculator.
- The countervailing point relevant to learners: to advance, build efficient models, troubleshoot algorithms, and think creatively, a **deeper understanding of the underlying mathematics** is still required — AI raises the floor but the ceiling still needs human math.
- Supports the case that foundational math (esp. [[Linear Algebra for AI and Quant|linear algebra]]) is a rising, not falling, asset.
