# Cyclic Sighing Breathwork RCT (Cell Reports 2023)

Stanford randomized controlled trial (Balban et al., *Cell Reports Medicine*, Jan 2023) comparing three 5-min/day breathwork protocols against mindfulness meditation over 28 days.

## What it contributes

First peer-reviewed head-to-head of breathwork vs meditation. **Cyclic sighing** — repeated **double inhale + long slow exhale** (exhale-emphasized, ~1:2 ratio) — produced the **largest improvement in mood and the biggest drop in physiological arousal** (lower resting respiratory rate), beating box breathing, cyclic hyperventilation, and mindfulness.

- Daily positive-affect gain: **+1.91** (cyclic sighing) vs **+1.22** (mindfulness).
- Just **5 minutes/day** for 4 weeks.

**Mechanism:** extended exhales raise parasympathetic (vagal) tone. The acute version — the **physiological sigh** (two inhales + long exhale) — calms within a few breaths.
