# Patch 26.x Top Lane Tier Lists (Aggregate)

Aggregate snapshot of top-lane tier lists for patch 26.11–26.12 (June 2026), drawn from stat aggregators (METAsrc, u.gg, Mobalytics) and editorial lists (AltChar, playhub). Filed as **low confidence by design** — tier data expires every patch.

Stat aggregators rank by win rate, pick rate, ban rate, KDA, and game volume across the live patch.

## The Contradiction This Page Documents

For the same 26.12 patch, stat-aggregator sites place **Darius S+** while one editorial list places him **D-tier** after the 26.x bruiser nerfs. Both can't be right; the stat sites have the data, the editorial list has a thesis. The lesson is structural, not about Darius: **same-patch tier lists routinely disagree**, so a tier list is a weak basis for champion-pool decisions compared to archetype fit and mastery. (See [[Top Lane Champion Pool]].)

## Method Note

The simple kits (Garen, Malphite, Mordekaiser) sit at the top of every list aimed at sub-Diamond play — consistent with the skill-floor argument in [[EloAscend — Top Lane Guide 2026]]: stats win over mechanics at the ranks where most games are decided by mistakes.

> [!gap] Re-check u.gg or lolalytics on the current patch before acting on any placement here. This snapshot is from patch 26.12 (June 2026).
