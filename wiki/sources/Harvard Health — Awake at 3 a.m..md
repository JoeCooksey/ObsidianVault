# Harvard Health — Awake at 3 a.m.

Consumer-health article from Harvard Health Publishing (author: Julie Corliss) on why people wake in the early morning and how to return to sleep. A reputable secondary source synthesizing sleep-medicine consensus.

## What it contributes

- **The 20-minute rule** (stimulus control): leave the bed if you can't return to sleep within ~20 minutes → [[20-Minute Rule for Nighttime Awakenings]].
- **Environment**: cool, dark, quiet room; white noise if needed; comfortable mattress → [[Sleep Environment Optimization]].
- **Consistency**: same bed/wake times to regulate the sleep-wake cycle → [[Circadian Rhythm Alignment]].
- **Stimulants**: avoid caffeine after 1–2 p.m. (coffee, tea, chocolate, soft drinks).
- **Exercise**: aerobic activity improves sleep quality and reduces awakenings.
- **CBT-I**: reframe negative sleep thoughts over 6–8 weekly sessions; online program SHUTi cut mid-sleep awakenings ~50%.
