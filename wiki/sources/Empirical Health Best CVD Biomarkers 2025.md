# Empirical Health — Best Biomarkers for Heart Health (2025)

Direct-to-consumer cardiology resource summarizing the strongest cardiovascular biomarkers. Aligns with 2025 ACC guidance and ESC/Canadian Cardiovascular Society recommendations to use **ApoB over LDL-C** as the primary lipid metric.

Contributes the **price + predictive-power ranking** for blood-based heart metrics in the master tier list: ApoB and hs-CRP as A-tier, Lp(a) as a one-time A-tier test.
