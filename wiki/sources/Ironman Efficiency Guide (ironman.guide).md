# Ironman Efficiency Guide (ironman.guide)

The most granular route available: a 569-step ordered checklist descended from the classic Oziris efficiency guide, updated for 2026 content. Where the OSRS Wiki describes *what* the stages are, this source prescribes the exact *order*.

## Route structure

| Stage | Focus | Steps |
|-------|-------|-------|
| 1.1 | Early quests, Wintertodt, Ardy cloak 1 | 286 |
| 1.2 | Thieving (blackjack 50+), fishing, mining | 33 |
| 1.3 | Fairy rings, 43 Prayer, Kingdom, 99 Thieving | 112 |
| 1.4 | Agility for graceful + misc skilling | 52 |
| 1.5 | Diaries + finishing RFD (Barrows gloves) | 20 |
| 2.0 | Goals after Barrows gloves | 66 |

## Post-gloves (Stage 2.0) contributions

- Skill targets: 61 Crafting, 70 Mining, 65 Atk/Str, 58 Slayer, 70 Agility, 77 Herblore, 70 Construction, 70 WC, 70 Smithing, 60 → 87 Ranged
- Quests: Lunar Diplomacy, Dream Mentor, Making Friends with My Arm, Song of the Elves, Dragon Slayer II
- Gear: Neitiznot helm, fighter torso, dragon defender, black mask, slayer ring, range void → elite void (Western hard diary at 70 Ranged)
- Ranged is deliberately staggered: 60 at Pest Control → 87 via chinning → Corrupted Gauntlet farming
- Endpoint: camp Corrupted Gauntlet until **2 enhanced weapon seeds**

## Pre-route rules

- All quest lamps/books → Herblore until 77, then Prayer or Agility/Runecrafting
