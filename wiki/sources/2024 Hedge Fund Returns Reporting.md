# 2024 Hedge Fund Returns Reporting

## What this source contributes

Recent, verifiable performance data for the major quant / multi-strategy funds — the evidence base for "are they actually successful *now*."

## Key Claims (2024 calendar year)

- **D.E. Shaw** topped the rankings with **$11.1B in net investor gains**; its **Oculus fund returned 36.1%**, its diversified **Composite returned 18%**. (confidence: medium — press-reported, funds don't publish audited retail figures)
- **Citadel's** flagship **Wellington** multi-strategy fund returned **15.1%**. (confidence: medium)
- **Millennium Management** returned **~15%**. (confidence: medium)
- Hedge funds **overall averaged ~10.7%** through the first 11 months of 2024. (confidence: medium)
- In 2023, D.E. Shaw returned profits to investors after only single-digit gains — even elite quants have modest years. (confidence: medium)

## Notes

These are the "platform" or "pod-shop" multi-strategy quants (Citadel, Millennium, D.E. Shaw, Point72) — distinct from Medallion's closed pure-quant model. Their 15–18% net returns are excellent but **an order of magnitude below Medallion's 39% net** — and they charge "pass-through" expenses that can exceed traditional 2-and-20. They are successful, but they are *not* Medallion.

> [!gap] Hedge-fund returns are self-reported and survivorship-biased (failed funds drop out of the averages). Treat single-year figures as directional, not audited.
