# Durable Human Skills AI Cannot Replace (search synthesis)

Aggregated 2026 coverage (HBS Online, Workday, Deloitte Human Capital Trends, Metaintro) on which human skills resist AI displacement.

## What It Contributes

- **Learning agility** named the "skill of skills" — learn/adapt/unlearn fastest.
- The AI-resistant clusters: judgment & discernment, taste & creative agency, problem-framing ("teaming with machines"), communication/coaching.
- The recurring thesis: **AI makes human skills more important, not less.**

## Reliability Note

Synthesis across multiple reputable career/business outlets plus lower-tier blogs; the *direction* is well-corroborated but specific framings vary by source — **medium** confidence. Primary anchor: HBS Online.
