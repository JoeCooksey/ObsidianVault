# Norwegian 4x4 — PureGym Practical Guide

Practical execution guide consolidating warm-up/cool-down, no-monitor intensity gauging, running application, and common mistakes.

## What It Contributes
The "how to actually do it" layer — especially gauging intensity by feel and applying 4x4 to running.

## Key Points
- **Warm-up:** 5–10 min cardio + 2–3 min mobility/activation. **Cool-down:** 5 min light cardio + stretching.
- **Intensity without a HR monitor:** hard-but-sustainable; talking should feel challenging; if you can't hold pace across all four reps you're too hard, if you could keep going it's too easy. Target band typically reached ~1–2 min into each rep.
- **Running (outdoor):** 4 min hard (harder than 5K pace) / 3 min easy jog or fast walk.
- **Running (treadmill):** control via high speed, incline, or both; reduce speed/incline to recover.
- **Mistakes:** starting too fast in rep 1; skipping warm-up; resting fully or stretching between reps; exceeding 2 sessions/week.
