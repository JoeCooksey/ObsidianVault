# Halling 2020 — PGC-1α and Mitochondrial Function

According to PubMed. Review of how the transcriptional co-activator **PGC-1α** controls mitochondrial number and quality in skeletal muscle, where most human energy metabolism happens. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1139/apnm-2020-0005)

## What it contributes

The molecular answer to **how exercise optimizes [[Mitochondria]].**

- **PGC-1α = master regulator of mitochondrial biogenesis** (building new mitochondria). (Source: Halling 2020, confidence high)
- It also runs **mitochondrial quality control beyond biogenesis**: network dynamics (fission/fusion) and **mitophagy** (autophagic removal of damaged mitochondria). (confidence high)
- PGC-1α is central to **antioxidant defense** against reactive oxygen species.
- Its regulation of mitochondrial quality affects **age-related mitochondrial dysfunction and insulin sensitivity** — the link between training and metabolic health. (confidence high)

Exercise is the strongest physiological activator of PGC-1α — endurance training and high-intensity intervals both upregulate it, driving the biogenesis that raises mitochondrial density.
