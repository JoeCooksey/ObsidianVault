# North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method

Vendor guide from North Spore (a major U.S. mycology supplier) describing their "ShroomTek" all-in-one bag system. Aimed at gourmet cultivation but the method is species-agnostic.

## What it contributes

- **Bag anatomy**: a proprietary blend of sterilized nutrient-rich manure-based compost + organic grain + coco coir, a **filter patch** for gas exchange, and an **injection port** for cultures.
- **Break-and-shake rationale**: after 4–8 weeks, once white mycelium extends 3–4 inches from the injection point, breaking up the colonized substrate and mixing it distributes active mycelium and **dramatically speeds up colonization**.
- **Fruiting**: the four variables — light (indirect or ~12 h artificial/day), temperature (60–80°F for most species), FAE (sensed via lower CO₂), humidity (essential). Users cut a slit above the substrate and expand it into a hood for misting.
- **Multi-flush**: remove all fruit bodies after harvest down to the myceliated substrate to prevent rot/contamination; between flushes spray the hood interior over hours to rebuild humidity, then close to re-establish CO₂.
