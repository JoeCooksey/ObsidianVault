# PHGG Microbiome Trials

Set of RCTs on **partially hydrolyzed guar gum (PHGG / Sunfiber)**, a soluble, fully fermentable prebiotic fiber that is unusually well tolerated.

## Key findings

- PHGG modulates the gut microbiome at relatively **low doses** and improves gut health.
- At the genus level: significant ↑ in **Bifidobacterium** and **Parabacteroides** (the latter inversely associated with IBS and ulcerative colitis); shifts in Clostridium subcluster XI.
- 3 months of intake **improved stool form** (no change in frequency); correlated with reduced diarrhea and improved fecal-excretory feeling.
- Positive results in **constipation-dominant IBS**: better symptoms, stool consistency, reduced laxative use.
- **Low gas** relative to other fully fermentable fibers — its main practical advantage.

> [!gap] No head-to-head RCTs of PHGG vs psyllium were found; ranking is on tolerability + IBS evidence, not direct comparison.

## Contribution to the topic

Justifies **B-tier** placement for PHGG in [[Fiber Supplement Tier List]]: a gentle, gut-friendly prebiotic and a strong fallback for IBS patients who don't tolerate psyllium — but non-viscous, so it lacks the cholesterol/glucose benefits.
