# Public Books — 16 Books That Make You Want to Be an Engineer

Essay surveying fiction (and film) that captures the engineering impulse and has drawn people into the profession. Primary source for the ethics and "build-from-what-you-have" entries in [[Fiction Roadmap for EE Skills (Sci-Fi to Skill Map)]].

## What It Contributes
- **Rocket Boys / October Sky (Homer Hickam)** — the design → build → test → refine loop, made emotional.
- **Flight of the Phoenix (Elleston Trevor)** — heroic problem-solving + the friction of collaborative work.
- **Cat's Cradle (Kurt Vonnegut)** — the cautionary tale of abstraction without responsibility → engineering ethics.
- **The Right Stuff**, **The Invention of Hugo Cabret**, **The Intuitionist** — invention, risk, and broadening engineering method.
- Core thesis: fictional engineers inspire real ones; people enter the field wanting to emulate a character.
