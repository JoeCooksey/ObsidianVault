# The Cook's Treat — Vanilla Honey Custard Ice Cream

A complete honey-sweetened custard recipe — effectively the home version of the [[Ice Cream for Bears]] base.

## Recipe
- 3 egg yolks · ½ cup (6 oz) honey · 1 cup whole milk · ¼ tsp salt · 2 cups heavy cream · 1 Tbsp vanilla

## Method
1. Whisk yolks + honey + milk + salt in a saucepan; heat over medium until bubbles form, whisking constantly.
2. Off heat, stir in cream + vanilla. Cool, press plastic wrap to surface, chill 6–24 h.
3. Churn per machine; freeze ≥2 h.

## Key tips
- **Honey ≈ keeps custard thinner** than sugar; it won't thicken as much.
- **Hygroscopic honey → stays scoopable** from the freezer without thawing.
- Use **raw honey** and real vanilla; higher-fat cream (40%) helps.
