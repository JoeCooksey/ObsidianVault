# Etsy — Milk Kefir Grains Marketplace

The budget floor for buying live kefir grains online. Independent sellers list live grains from **$8 to ~$17**, with the cheapest around **$8–$10.95** and many offering free shipping. Quality is seller-dependent — verify the grains are **live and never dried** and read reviews before buying.

Basis for the budget-sourcing section of [[Kefir Grain Sourcing and Care]]. For truly free grains, local fermenters (Facebook groups, r/Kefir, farmers' markets) beat any marketplace.
