# 6 Inspiring EE Projects — NJIT

NJIT Online list of EE project ideas. Used to anchor the S-tier hardware build in [[Freshman Summer Project Plan (Tier List)]].

## The six

1. **Smart home automation** — microcontroller + smartphone control; digital/analog I/O, wireless, circuit design.
2. **Portable power supply / battery management** — regulated multi-device power; voltage regulation, power electronics, thermal management. *(Best fit for Joe's WBG/power track.)*
3. **Small robotics kit** — sensors + motor control + feedback loops.
4. **Renewable energy dashboard** — data acquisition, signal conditioning, visualization.
5. **Wearable biosensor** — low-power electronics + wireless comms.
6. **Control-systems simulation** — MATLAB/Simulink feedback modeling (more advanced).

NJIT's own note: projects 1–4 are realistic for a motivated first-year with an Arduino/Raspberry Pi kit.
