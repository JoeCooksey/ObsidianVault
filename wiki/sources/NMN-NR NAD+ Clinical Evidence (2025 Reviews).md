# NMN / NR NAD+ Clinical Evidence (2025 Reviews)

Synthesis of 2025 systematic reviews and meta-analyses on the NAD+ precursors nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN) and nicotinamide riboside (NR), the marquee "longevity supplements."

## What it contributes

Clarifies the gap between the marketing and the human data. The biomarker moves; the outcomes mostly don't.

## Findings

- **NAD+ rises** — Both NMN and NR reliably raise blood NAD+ within days. This part is real. (Source: Yang 2025; nad.com)
- **Outcomes underwhelm** — A 2025 meta-analysis of **10 RCTs found no benefit for muscle mass, strength, or physical function** in older adults. "NAD+ rises, and then in most well-designed trials, not much else clearly happens."
- **One genuine signal** — 250 mg NMN/day for 10 weeks improved **muscle insulin sensitivity** in prediabetic postmenopausal women (Science, 2021).
- **Animal→human gap** — Robust rodent lifespan/metabolic effects have not translated to humans, likely due to model biology, dosing/timing, and the complexity of human aging.

## Caveats

> [!gap] No human trial shows extended lifespan or healthspan. Raising a biomarker (NAD+) is not the same as improving an outcome. Treat as **low-to-moderate value, biomarker-only** until longevity endpoints replicate in humans.
