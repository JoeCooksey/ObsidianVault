# Triangulation — Family Systems Theory (GoodTherapy)

Explainer applying **Bowen Family Systems Theory** and Haley's "perverse triangle" to family conflict dynamics.

## What it contributes
- **Triangles are the unit** — families operate as emotional units, and the smallest *stable* relationship system is a **triangle, not a pair**; when two people have tension, a third is pulled in to relieve it.
- **Cross-generational coalition** — Jay Haley's "perverse triangle" (1977): two people on different hierarchical/generational levels form a **covert coalition against a third**, undermining that person's standing.
- **Effect on the excluded member** — the third person is isolated and entrenched; the alliance regulates the others' anxiety but **blocks direct, one-on-one engagement.**
