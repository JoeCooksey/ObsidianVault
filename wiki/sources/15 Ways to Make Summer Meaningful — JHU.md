# 15 Ways to Make Your Summer Meaningful — JHU

Johns Hopkins Life Design Lab (2025) list for engineering students with *any* summer plan, including no internship. Reframes summer value away from job titles toward **projects, skills, network, and reflection**.

## What it contributes

Anchors the claim that a freshman without an internship is not "behind" — the highest-value moves are self-directed. Directly supports [[Freshman Summer Project Plan (Tier List)]].

## The 15 (condensed)

1. **Informational interviews** with professionals/alumni. 2. **Volunteer** for leadership reps. 3. **Personal projects** (apps, robots, etc.) that demonstrate capability. 4. **Go deep on ONE technical skill** → portfolio piece. 5. **Any summer job** teaches transferable skills. 6. Values journal. 7. Diverse reading. 8. Resume enhancement. 9. Join professional Slack/Discord communities. 10. **Shadow** an engineer. 11. Micro-goals to test interests. 12. First-year reflection. 13. **Network mapping**. 14. Life-design "5 years if money/status didn't matter" prompt. 15. **Intentional rest** — sleep + unstructured time.
