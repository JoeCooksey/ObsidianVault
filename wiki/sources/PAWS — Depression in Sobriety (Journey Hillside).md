# PAWS — Depression in Sobriety (Journey Hillside)

Treatment-center explainer on why people get depressed *after* quitting alcohol and how the timeline runs.

## What it contributes
- Acute withdrawal (days 1–7) is mostly physical; **depression often arrives as those symptoms fade.**
- **PAWS (weeks 2–12)** is the most common period for significant depression; many notice gradual easing within weeks and clearer improvement by 1–3 months.
- Symptoms can persist weeks to months — sleep disturbance, anxiety, and mood instability up to ~2 years after heavy long-term drinking.
- Frames the low mood as **a phase of brain recovery**, not failure of sobriety.
