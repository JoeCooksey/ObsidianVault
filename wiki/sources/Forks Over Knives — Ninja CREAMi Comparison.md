# Forks Over Knives — Ninja CREAMi Comparison

Explains the appliance mechanism and benchmarks it against high-speed blenders, food processors, and traditional churns.

## Mechanism
Blend → freeze in canister 24h → "creamerizer blade shaves off fine layers and adds air as it goes" → done in under 4 minutes. **Re-spin** reprocesses hardened/refrozen pints.

## Advantages
- Restaurant-quality creamy texture vs blender/processor results
- Handles silken tofu, oats, nuts, sweet potato, frozen fruit — not just fruit
- Two compact 24 oz canisters; fast final processing

## Drawbacks
- $250 + 24h advance freeze required

## Health angle
Plant-forward, whole-food bases without processed sugar or high-fat dairy are easy to build. Supports [[Ninja CREAMi]] and [[Protein Ice Cream (Ninja CREAMi) Health Profile]].
