# VO2 Max & Functional Fitness as Mortality Predictors

VO2 max is the strongest single predictor of all-cause mortality among trackable metrics (confidence: **high**). Cleveland Clinic's ~120,000-patient cohort found low fitness more dangerous than smoking or diabetes.

Functional companions — grip strength and gait speed — add cheap, equipment-light mortality signals, especially after midlife. Basis for the S-tier (VO2 max estimate) and B-tier (grip strength, gait speed, lab VO2 test) ratings.
