# Boosteria — Jungle Pathing Basics

Jungle pathing guide read from the laner's perspective: how to infer the enemy jungler's position from observable clues instead of guessing.

## Contributes

- **Leash read**: late top arrival → topside start; predicts the whole first clear (strong-side path or full clear to opposite buff)
- **CS counting**: ~12 CS at 2:45 = 3-camp clear, level 3, gank window open; ~24 CS at 3:30 = full clear heading to scuttle
- **Absence signals**: what *didn't* happen matters — no-show + your wave pushing + no vision = gank or trap incoming
- **Cooldown tracking**: enemy summoners/ults down → gank pressure drops; reduces gank anxiety
- **Proactive defense**: ward to answer a specific question about the jungler, not out of habit
