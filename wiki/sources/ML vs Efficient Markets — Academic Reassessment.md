# Machine Learning vs. Efficient Markets — Academic Reassessment

Cluster of 2024–2025 peer-reviewed papers reassessing whether ML/AI can predict markets in the big-data era.

## What it contributes

The credibility-weighted answer to "can an algorithm beat the market" for the synthesis. The honest academic verdict is **mixed-leaning-no for replicable retail edges**:

- Some studies report ML pattern-recognition generating strong returns on specific indices (NASDAQ, TAIEX) → evidence against *strict* EMH.
- But empirical analysis is broadly **consistent with EMH**; the headline wins are frequently **overfitting** artifacts that fail out-of-sample.
- Mechanism: discovering a tradable signal and trading on it **prices the signal away** — markets are adaptive, edges decay. This matches the Renaissance pattern (information profitable only for minutes; trading on it makes the market more efficient).

> [!gap] In-sample/backtest outperformance is not evidence of a durable edge. Demand walk-forward, out-of-sample, after-cost results before believing any "AI beats the market" claim.
