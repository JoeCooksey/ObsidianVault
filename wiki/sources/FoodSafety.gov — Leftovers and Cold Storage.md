# FoodSafety.gov — Leftovers and Cold Storage

Official US government (USDA FSIS + HHS) food-safety guidance. The authoritative source for leftover and meal-prep storage limits.

## What It Contributes

- **Cooked meat / poultry / fish: 3–4 days** refrigerated at ≤40°F.
- **2-hour rule:** refrigerate cooked food within 2 hours (1 hour if >90°F).
- **Reheat to 165°F**, steaming throughout.
- **Freezer (0°F):** cooked ground beef 3–4 months; cooked fish 2–3 months; soups/stews/casseroles 2–3 months — quality limits, food stays *safe* indefinitely at 0°F.
- Cooled rapidly in shallow containers; fridge kept ≤40°F.
