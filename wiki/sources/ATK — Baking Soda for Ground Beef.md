# ATK — Baking Soda for Ground Beef

America's Test Kitchen technique (via The Kitchn) for using baking soda to improve browned ground beef. Corroborated by an independent [Food52 test](https://food52.com/story/25923-internet-s-favorite-ground-beef-trick).

## What it contributes
The single highest-leverage browning trick (high confidence — multiple independent tests agree):
- **Mechanism:** baking soda raises the meat's pH. Higher pH (1) stops proteins bonding tightly so they retain water → less pooling, more tender; (2) **alkalinity accelerates the [[Maillard Reaction]]** → deep crust in seconds.
- **Dose:** ¼ tsp per 12 oz (≈450 g) ground beef (1 tsp per 12 oz for *sliced* meat). Mix into raw meat, rest 10–15 min, then brown.
- **Limit:** overdosing → metallic/soapy taste.
