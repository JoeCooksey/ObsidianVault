# Saladino Animal-Based Diet Official Guide

Primary source: [[Paul Saladino]]'s official definition of the animal-based diet on paulsaladinomd.com. This is what HE says the diet is — confidence is high that these are his claims, not that the claims are correct.

## The Definition

> "An animal-based diet is centered around the most optimal foods for humans; meat, organs, fruit, honey, and raw dairy."

## Prescribed Amounts

| Food | Amount |
|---|---|
| Meat | ≥1 lb per 100 lbs bodyweight/day; protein = goal bodyweight × 1–1.2 g |
| Liver | 0.5 oz/day (2–3 oz/week) |
| Other organs | A few oz/day — heart, kidney, brain, bone marrow, pancreas, spleen, testicle, tripe |
| Carbs (fruit + honey) | Goal bodyweight × 0.7 g (sedentary) to × 1.7 g (12+ hrs activity/week) |
| Raw dairy | Saladino personally: 1 L raw milk/day split across meals + butter + colostrum |

## Saladino's Own Day (as listed)

1.25 lbs meat across 3 meals, 2 oz beef heart + organ powder, ~10 tbsp honey, 550 g blueberries, watermelon, papaya, cucumber and squash (skin and seeds removed), 1 L raw milk, butter.

## What This Page Notably Does NOT Contain

- No detailed scientific rationale or citations on the page itself — the "most optimal foods" framing is asserted, not argued
- No vegetable prohibition stated explicitly here; cucumber and squash appear (peeled, deseeded) — the anti-vegetable case lives in his other content ([[Heart and Soil Plant Toxins and Food List Guide]])
