# TechCrunch — Robinhood Now Lets Your AI Agents Trade Stocks

News coverage of the 2026-05-27 launch of Robinhood Agentic Trading and an accompanying "agentic" credit card with 3% cash back.

## What it contributes

Confirms launch date, the bring-your-own-agent framing, and the security model from press reporting (corroborates the [[Robinhood — Agentic Trading Overview]] first-party doc).

- Agents connect via Robinhood's **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** service.
- Agents can analyze **concentration risk and sector exposure** and review **analyst notes** to surface ideas, then execute trades — but only against a pre-loaded wallet balance, not the main account.
- Controls: trade notifications, preview-approval for certain trades, built-in fraud detection with staff review.
- Exec quote (Abhishek Fatehpuria, VP of Product): "We've heard a lot of demand from our customers to bring their own tools, LLMs, and agents, and connect them to Robinhood."
- Beta = stocks only; options, crypto, futures, prediction markets later.

> [!note] Reporting corroborates the first-party doc. No independent risk analysis offered in the article itself.
