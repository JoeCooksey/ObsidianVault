# Boosteria — LoL CSing Fundamentals

Laning-phase mechanics guide (Nov 2025). Single commercial source — medium confidence; drills are standard community practice.

## What It Contributes
- **Last-hit mechanics:** wind-up timing, damage calibration at levels 1/3/5, Attack Move on Cursor + Target Champions Only hotkeys, orbwalking/animation canceling.
- **Metrics:** CS@10 and CS@15 as the laning scoreboard. Habit rule: "stack certainty — favor guaranteed last hits over 'maybe I poke them' when you don't have prio."
- **Four trading templates:** ranged bully (auto on their last-hit, step back), melee punish (posture in minions, engage when they step up for CS), all-in threat (stack wave, ult on cannon timing), support engage (trade when ADC can follow and wave favors you).
- **Minion advantage rule:** trading with 6 minions vs their 3 "borrows" DPS from your wave.
- **Practice drills:** 10-min no-pressure CS test; full wave under tower 10× in a row; 30 consecutive cannons; hold a freeze for 2 full waves. Daily 20-min block: 5 min last-hit + CS@5 tracking, 5 min under-tower, 5 min freeze, 5 min VOD review of one CS loss.
- 2025 context: turret plating expires at 14:00 — fast pushes must convert before plates fall.
