# Why Do My Farts Smell So Bad? (Cary Gastroenterology)

Gastroenterology-clinic patient-education article. Practical, lay-level. ([source](https://www.carygastro.com/blog/why-do-my-farts-smell-so-bad))

## Key Points
- **High-sulfur foods** named: cruciferous vegetables (Brussels sprouts, broccoli, cabbage, kale), lentils, beans, legumes. H₂S = rotten-egg smell.
- **Eating behaviors** that worsen gas: swallowing air while eating, eating fast / mouth open, carbonated drinks.
- **Prevention**: smaller meals, chew slowly, more water + less carbonation, probiotic foods (yogurt), more physical activity, limit sugar alcohols (sorbitol).
- **See a doctor** if foul gas comes with severe abdominal pain, nausea/vomiting, bleeding, fever, or unexplained weight loss. Possible causes: food intolerances, bacterial infection/**SIBO**, IBS, IBD, rarely colorectal cancer.
