# TripSafe.org — Psilocybin Safety Guide

Harm-reduction-focused educational site covering psilocybin and LSD safety. Community/educational source (medium confidence) but aligned with the clinical literature.

## Key guidance

- **Set and setting** framing: prior emotional state + environment dominate outcomes ([[Set and Setting]])
- **Trip sitter**: prepared, trained companion conveying "a solid sense of security and calm"; respond to anxiety with personal support and reassurance ([[Trip Sitter]])
- **Environment**: safe and comfortable; explicitly avoid clubs and high-stimulation settings
- **Dose management**: start low; tolerance-aware dosing
- **Hard avoids**: misidentified mushroom species (organ-damage risk), unsafe activities under impaired judgment, use with family history of schizophrenia
- **Support**: Fireside Project — free, confidential peer support by phone/text during or after an experience
