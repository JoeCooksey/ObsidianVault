# Diet, Sleep and Exercise — Keystones of a Healthy Lifestyle

A descriptive cross-sectional review (PMC, 2022) framing **sleep, diet, and exercise as three interdependent pillars** of health, using a Nepalese medical/health-science student cohort (n≈217) as the data source.

## What It Contributes
Establishes the **interdependence** at the core of the health-and-fitness domain in [[Research - Daily Habits Every Man Should Adopt]]: good sleep makes diet and exercise easier; exercise improves sleep; poor diet undermines both.

## Key Findings
- **Sleep:** 44% of the cohort had poor sleep quality (worse during exams). Recommendation: sleep hygiene — reduce evening screens, avoid evening caffeine/alcohol, consistent schedule. (Observed typical duration ~6.5h; general guidance elsewhere is 7–9h.)
- **Exercise:** ≥30 min/day minimum. Two-thirds of the cohort had low activity; only 7% met WHO's >600 MET-min/week. Walking, yoga, swimming, or home workouts all acceptable.
- **Diet:** balanced nutrition affects cognition; healthy breakfast (fruit, eggs), regular water, minimal junk food. 15% overweight, 12% underweight in cohort.

> [!gap] This is a cross-sectional descriptive study in students — associative, not causal, and not a male-specific or RCT design. The *pillars* concept is well-supported by broader literature; the specific cohort numbers are context-bound. **medium confidence.**
