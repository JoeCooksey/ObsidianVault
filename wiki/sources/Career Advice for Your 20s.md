# Career Advice for Your 20s (aggregated)

Career-guidance outlets on the twenties. Practical, professionally edited but not academic — **medium confidence**.

- **Be a sponge.** Early career = gathering rocket fuel; ~1/3 of employers can't fill roles for lack of technical skills and few train you, so self-equip with in-demand skills.
- **Network early.** Social networks compound exponentially "like interest"; face-to-face connection has an irreplaceable dimension.
- **You don't need it figured out.** Treat a career as an evolving entity to investigate/nurture; your 20s afford mistakes.
- **Relationships drive success** — build authentic, honest ones and value them.
