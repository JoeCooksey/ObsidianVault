# Heat-Formed Food Toxins (HCAs, PAHs, AGEs, Acrylamide)

The four classes of harmful compounds that **cooking method** creates — the core reason gentle/moist/lower-heat cooking is healthier. All four rise with **higher temperature, more browning/charring, and drier heat**.

## The Four Hazards

| Toxin | Formed by | Worst foods | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HCAs** (heterocyclic amines) | Amino acids + creatine at high heat | Well-done/charred muscle meat (beef, poultry, fish) | DNA-damaging; probable carcinogens |
| **PAHs** (polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons) | Fat/juices dripping onto flame → smoke coats food | Grilled/smoked/barbecued meat | DNA-damaging carcinogens |
| **AGEs** (advanced glycation end-products) | Maillard browning of protein+sugar, esp. dry high heat | Grilled/roasted/fried animal foods | Inflammation, insulin resistance, atherosclerosis |
| **Acrylamide** | Asparagine + sugars browning in **starchy** foods | Fried/roasted potatoes, chips, over-toasted bread | Probable carcinogen, neurotoxin |

## Mitigation (evidence-graded)

### Meat (HCAs/PAHs)
- **Marinate ≥30 min** → up to **90–99% reduction** in some HCAs (e.g. PhIP in chicken). *Marinating beats lowering the temperature.* Acidic/herb/spice marinades work best.
- **Don't char**; remove any blackened parts.
- **Trim fat + avoid flare-ups** (less dripping → less PAH smoke).
- **Pre-cook in microwave** then finish — shortens high-heat time and pours off HCA precursors.
- **Flip frequently**, keep flame off direct contact, lower-temp longer over hot-and-fast.

### Animal foods generally (AGEs)
- **Moist + low heat wins:** poaching/steaming generates ~**¼** the AGEs of roasting/broiling.
- Eggs over **low/medium** heat = ~half the AGEs of high heat.
- **Acid marinade** (vinegar/lemon) before cooking lowers AGEs.
- Microwaving produces fewer AGEs than other dry methods.

### Starchy foods (acrylamide)
- **"Golden, not brown"** — brown areas hold the most acrylamide.
- **Soak** raw cut potatoes 15–30 min, or **par-boil** before frying/roasting → strips surface asparagine/sugars.
- **Air-frying** potato chips ≈ **78 ppm** acrylamide vs **290 ppm** deep-fried.
- Store potatoes in a **pantry, not the fridge** (cold raises reducing sugars → more acrylamide).

## Bottom Line (confidence: high)
The same three dials cut all four toxins: **lower temperature, less browning/char, more moisture** — plus **marinate meat** and **don't burn starches**. None of this means "never grill"; it means grill smarter and make moist/gentle methods the default.
