# Resume Without a Summer Internship — Ohio State ECS

Ohio State Engineering Career Services (2024) on building an engineering resume when no internship lands — the common freshman case.

## What it contributes

Provides the concrete alternative-experience menu behind A-tier of [[Freshman Summer Project Plan (Tier List)]], and the permission to list a non-engineering job.

## The six alternatives

1. **Undergraduate research** — on-campus or summer; demonstrates problem-solving (→ [[ASU FURI — Fulton Undergraduate Research Initiative]]).
2. **Micro-internships** — project-based, flexible, via Parker Dewey.
3. **Personal projects** — software/hardware that show initiative.
4. **Online courses & certs** — Coursera, edX, SolidProfessor, Tinkercad, LinkedIn Learning.
5. **Non-engineering work** — server/cashier/camp counselor; responsibility + soft skills.
6. **Volunteering** — community involvement and leadership.

The thesis: complete **at least one** and the summer counts.
