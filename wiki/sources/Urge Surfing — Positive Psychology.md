# Urge Surfing — Positive Psychology

Practitioner reference describing the urge-surfing technique, its origin, and an 8-step protocol.

## Key claims

- **Urge surfing** was developed by **Marlatt and Gordon (1985)** as a relapse-prevention skill for addictive urges. (Source: PositivePsychology.com) `confidence: high`
- Core principle: urges behave like **waves** — they rise, peak, and subside on their own if you neither suppress nor act on them. Suppression tends to *increase* an urge's power.
- Urges "generally last only seconds or minutes." (Note: other clinical sources, e.g. Therapist Aid, cite ~20–30 minutes.) `confidence: medium`
- **8-step protocol:** recognize the urge → locate the body sensation + its intensity → observe without judgment → focus on breath → ride the wave → notice the sensation change → continue until it dissolves → self-appreciation.
