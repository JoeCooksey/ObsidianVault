# Dignitas — LoL Ranked Climbing and Tilt Guides

Two articles from esports org Dignitas. Older (2021–2022) — mark game-specific numbers as potentially stale, but the methodology is system-level and ages well.

## Climbing Guide (lolmjlauer, Jan 2022)
- **Champion pool:** shrink it. Top NA players average ~33 games per champion. Keep champions with 30+ games at ≥50% win rate; drop ones with 50+ games but sub-55% win rate or that feel uncomfortable. Cover off-roles with low-ban-rate (<10%) picks to avoid dodges when filled.
- **Warm-up:** 5–10 minutes — last-hitting, wave management, champion combos slow-to-fast, kiting/ganking drills.
- **Goals:** specific quantifiable stats (CS/min, wards, kill participation) + general decision-making goals; set season rank goals once and lock them away.
- **Game plan:** use loading screen to set win conditions for early/mid/late before the game starts — antidote to autopiloting.
- **VOD review:** check each game phase against the plan; identify controllable decisions that diverged.

## Tilt Guide (Teatones, Jul 2021)
- Tilt = performance deterioration from emotional frustration; triggers: toxic chat, expectation violations, dehydration/poor sleep, LP-watching, marathon sessions.
- Between games: step back to other modes/games; hydrate and sleep on schedule; play 3- or 5-game sets.
- In game: mute chat and pings early; ignore LP, play to improve; "embrace the chaos" — accept teammates' play and keep critical distance.
