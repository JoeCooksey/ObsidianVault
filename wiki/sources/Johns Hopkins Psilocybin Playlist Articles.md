# Johns Hopkins Medicine — Psilocybin Playlist Articles (2020, 2021)

Two Johns Hopkins Medicine articles: "Inside the Johns Hopkins Psilocybin Playlist" (Oct 2020) and "Playlist for a Psychedelic Journey" (Feb 2021), documenting the session-design protocol behind the center's trials.

## Key content

- Session structure: participant on a couch, **eyeshades and headphones**, ~5 hours, two monitors present who do not talk to or guide the participant
- The playlist: **7 hours 40 minutes**, designed to match the arc of a medium/high-dose session — soft background music on entry, then opening up, onset, launch, peak, return
- Composition: Mozart, Bach, Vivaldi and other classical works; lineage runs from **Helen Bonny's** 1960s music-therapy tapes through **Bill Richards**, who formalized the playlist when Hopkins psilocybin research began in 1999
- The playlist supported the JAMA Psychiatry 2020 trial (psilocybin for major depression)
