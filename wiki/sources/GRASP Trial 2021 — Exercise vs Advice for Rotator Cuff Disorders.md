# GRASP Trial 2021 — Exercise vs Advice for Rotator Cuff Disorders

*The Lancet* (2021). Large pragmatic RCT, **708 patients**, 20 UK NHS trusts, **2×2 factorial** (progressive physiotherapy exercise vs one best-practice advice session; ± corticosteroid injection), 12-month primary endpoint (SPADI). According to PubMed. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(21)00846-1)

## Key Findings
- **Progressive supervised exercise was NOT superior** to a single physiotherapist advice session at 12 months (adjusted mean difference −0.66, 99% CI −4.52 to 3.20).
- **Subacromial corticosteroid injection gave no long-term benefit** (−1.11, 99% CI −4.47 to 2.26).
- No serious adverse events.

## Contribution
For ordinary (non-traumatic, non-instability) rotator cuff pain, an expensive supervised exercise course is no better than good one-time advice plus home exercises — and steroid shots don't help long term. Caveat for Joe: GRASP **excluded** dislocation, instability, and full-thickness tears requiring surgery, so it speaks to cuff *tendinopathy/impingement*, not to his "pops out" instability or to a surgically relevant tear.

## Reliability
**High** — large, multicentre, pragmatic RCT. Generalizes to degenerative cuff *pain*, not to traumatic instability.
