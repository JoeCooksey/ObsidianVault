# Function Health Prep Guidance

Function Health's official pre-draw instructions (FAQ). Function membership covers **100+ biomarkers** across heart, liver, blood, metabolic, hormones, nutrients, thyroid, autoimmunity, kidney, and more, with a 60+ marker mid-year recheck for trending.

## Official prep instructions

- **Fast 8 hours** before the test "to ensure accurate results for key biomarkers."
- **Hydrate well** — makes the draw smoother and improves urinalysis accuracy.
- **Pause biotin (B7) 72 h** — interferes with hormone and thyroid immunoassays.
- **Avoid seafood 48 h** before tests that check heavy metals (mercury).
- **Morning draw** — most accurate for testosterone and cortisol.
- **Keep taking prescription meds** — never stop without talking to your doctor.

## What it contributes
Function's own guidance is lighter than best-practice — it does not mention alcohol or exercise timing. For a clean read those matter a lot (especially for a young athlete), so the [[Blood Test Preparation Protocol]] page adds the evidence-based **no alcohol 48–72 h** and **no intense exercise 48–72 h** rules from the broader literature ([[Exercise Effects on Blood Test Results]]).
