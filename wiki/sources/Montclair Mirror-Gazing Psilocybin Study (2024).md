# Montclair State — Mirror-Gazing on Psilocybin (Reddit Corpus Study)

Researchers analyzed **89 Reddit posts (775 excerpts)** describing mirror-gazing during psilocybin experiences (covered by PsyPost, 2024).

## Findings

- **Predominantly positive**: positive emotional responses significantly outnumbered negative ones
- No significant difference between "real self" and "unreal self" perceptions — psilocybin did not consistently break self-recognition
- Authors: the widespread "never look in a mirror while tripping" advice is probably **overstated**

## Limitations

Self-selected Reddit posts — people who had notable (often positive) experiences and chose to write about them. No controlled comparison. Confidence: medium, direction-of-evidence only.
