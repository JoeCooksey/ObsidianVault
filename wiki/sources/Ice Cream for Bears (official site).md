# Ice Cream for Bears (official site)

Brand's own marketing page. Note: a company's own site is **medium confidence** for factual claims (self-reported), but the ingredient base is corroborated by [[Feast Magazine — Ice Cream for Bears]].

## Base
Grass-fed milk + raw unfiltered honey + nutrient-packed egg yolks. Explicitly avoids gums, stabilizers, and refined sugar — relies on egg yolk + honey emulsification for a clean label.

## Flavors (17+)
Honey Honey, Queen & Bean (vanilla), Bearista (coffee/cacao nib), Mint to Bee, Chocturnal (triple chocolate), Basic Bear (pumpkin spice), Churro & Burrow (cinnamon), Strawbeary, Beary Cherry Chip, Caramel Dunes, Waves of Fudge, Hot Fudge sauce.

Supports [[Ice Cream for Bears]] and [[Honey-Sweetened Custard Ice Cream]].
