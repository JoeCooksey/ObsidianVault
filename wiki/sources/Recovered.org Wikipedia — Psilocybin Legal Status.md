# Recovered.org / Wikipedia — Psilocybin Legal Status

Aggregated current-law sources on U.S. psilocybin status (Recovered.org state-by-state guide, Wikipedia decriminalization article, Cato Institute 2025 analysis, ASTHO).

## What it contributes

- **Federal**: Schedule I; illegal to possess/cultivate/distribute except authorized clinical research; applies regardless of state law.
- **Oregon (Measure 109)**: supervised consumption at licensed service centers with a facilitator, adults 21+; **no home cultivation**.
- **Colorado (Prop 122)**: regulated access **plus** decriminalization; adults 21+ may **grow on a 12×12 ft private plot** for personal use, no possession cap on home-grown; first **Medical Assisted Use licenses issued March 24, 2025**.
- **Decriminalization elsewhere**: 20+ cities (Denver, Oakland, Santa Cruz, Ann Arbor, Seattle…) deprioritize enforcement — **not** legalization, and does not override federal/state criminal law.
- **Trend (Cato, 2025)**: reform is accelerating and highly local; the map changes year to year.

## Confidence

**High** for the federal/Oregon/Colorado framework. Law changes quickly — re-verify current jurisdiction before relying on it.
