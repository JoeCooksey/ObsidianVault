# OutdoorGearLab 2026 — Best Barefoot Shoes (Tested & Ranked)

Hands-on testing roundup. Editors' Choice: **Merrell Trail Glove 7** (men's, $130) and **Vivobarefoot Gobi II Leather** (women's, $210). Best Buy budget pick: **Whitin Minimalist** (~$50, comfortable immediately, flexible, but heavier and durability questioned).

Category winners:
- **Gym:** Vivobarefoot Primus Lite IV
- **Running:** Xero Shoes HFS II
- **Minimalists (max ground feel):** Merrell Vapor Glove 7
- **Casual:** Lems Primal Zen
- **Boots:** Vivobarefoot Tracker II FG (men's), Lems Chelsea Waterproof (women's)
- **Ground feel:** Wildling Arnow
