# Pieter Levels — How He Builds (Case Study)

Synthesized from several profiles of @levelsio — the canonical proof that a single person can run a multi-million-dollar software portfolio.

## What it contributes

**The numbers**: ~$3M/yr ARR, **zero employees**, ~$138K/month (Nov 2025). PhotoAI is ~70% of income; other products are Nomad List and RemoteOK.

**The tech doctrine** — radically simple:
- Vanilla **PHP + jQuery + SQLite**. No React, no microservices, no DevOps team.
- Now uses **vibe coding** to iterate "at light speed"; built a working flight sim by vibing with the model.

**The business doctrine**:
- **Ship before ready.** PhotoAI v1 output was bad; people paid anyway; he improved from real usage.
- **Charge immediately** — paying customers, not surveys, validate the product.
- **Volume of attempts**: famous "12 startups in 12 months" (2014).
- **Audience = distribution moat.** Builds in public on X, posts live revenue; one retweet from a big account reaches millions.

## Confidence

Medium — revenue figures are self-reported (Levels publishes them openly, which raises credibility) and relayed through secondary blogs. The *method* is well-corroborated across many independent profiles. Treat exact dollar figures as approximate.

> [!gap] Self-reported revenue; the qualitative method is high-confidence, the precise monthly numbers are medium.
