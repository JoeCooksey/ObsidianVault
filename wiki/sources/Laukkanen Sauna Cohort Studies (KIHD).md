# Laukkanen Sauna Cohort Studies (KIHD)

Prospective cohort from the Finnish Kuopio Ischemic Heart Disease Risk Factor Study (KIHD): 2,315 middle-aged men (42–60), with a later mixed-sex 2018 follow-up.

## What it contributes

The strongest observational evidence for heat therapy as a longevity lever. Frequent Finnish-sauna use shows a steep, dose-dependent inverse association with cardiovascular and all-cause death.

## Findings

- **4–7 sessions/week** (≈174°F, ~20 min) vs **1/week**: **40% lower all-cause mortality**. (Source: KIHD)
- **63% lower** sudden cardiac death; **~48%** lower coronary heart disease; **~50%** lower cardiovascular disease mortality.
- **2–3 sessions/week** still showed moderate risk reduction.
- Both frequency *and* per-session duration independently predicted lower risk.

## Caveats

> [!gap] Observational cohort — association, not proven causation. Healthy-user confounding is plausible (sicker people sauna less). Effect sizes are large and dose-responsive, which strengthens the causal case, but no large RCT exists. Mechanism overlaps with exercise (heat-shock proteins, improved endothelial function, lower BP).
