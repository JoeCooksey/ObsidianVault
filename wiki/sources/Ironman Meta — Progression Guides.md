# Ironman Meta — Progression Guides

Stage-based progression site with explicit milestone checklists and time estimates. Strongest contribution: the **mid-game slayer ladder** with per-level unlocks, and the four universal mid-game objectives.

## What it contributes

**Time estimates:** early game 50–100 hours; slayer ladder 100–200 hours.

**Four universal mid-game objectives:**
1. Quest cape ("unlocks everything")
2. Fire cape (first major PvM test)
3. Barrows gloves (RFD)
4. 70 Prayer (Piety)

**Slayer ladder** (filed as [[OSRS Ironman Slayer Ladder]]):
58 black mask → 75 gargoyles → 85 whip → 87 trident → 91 primordials → 93 occult.

**Raid prep sequence:** Phase 1 foundation (gloves/fire cape/70 prayer) → Phase 2 main grind (87 Slayer, first zenyte, Gauntlet decision) → Phase 3 final push (91 Slayer, all 4 zenytes, entry-mode raids).

**Late-game raid guidance:** ToA is the most ironman-friendly raid (scalable invocations; fang + Lightbearer priority drops); final upgrades from CoX, ToB, Nex, Yama, Colosseum.

## Caveats

Community site, not primary documentation — claims cross-checked against [[OSRS Wiki — Ironman Guide]] where possible; the two agree on stage structure and all major unlocks.
