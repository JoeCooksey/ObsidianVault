# Longevity Clinic Costs — Fountain Life

Pricing overview for the longevity-clinic tier (Tiers VIII–X of the [[Cost-Barrier Health Hack Ladder]]).

## What it contributes

Hard numbers for the high-barrier end of the ladder.

- **Outpatient diagnostic packages:** ~€1,500–5,000.
- **Human Longevity executive screening:** ~$8,000.
- **Healthy Longevity Clinic:** $15,000–50,000/yr depending on treatment complexity.
- **Overall clinic range:** $10,000–150,000/yr.
- **Residential European programs** (SHA Wellness, Buchinger Wilhelmi, Chenot Palace): €5,000–25,000 for 1–2 weeks.

A modern program bundles deep diagnostics — full-body MRI, hormone panels, epigenetic age, telomere analysis, DEXA, VO2max, microbiome, CGM — then layers protocols (peptides, NAD+, HBOT, hormone optimization, stem/exosome therapy).

> [!gap] Note: most services are **not insurance-covered**, and the marginal healthspan benefit over the DIY ~$2k/yr sweet spot is unproven by RCT. Treat as medium confidence (vendor source).
