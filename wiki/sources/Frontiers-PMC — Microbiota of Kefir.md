# Frontiers/PMC — Microbiota of Kefir

Peer-reviewed review (NCBI PMC, open access) on kefir's microbial composition and health-promoting properties. The anchor scientific source for this topic.

## What it contributes

- **Microbiology:** Defines the bacterial and yeast genera/species that make up kefir grains, and quantifies LAB dominance (60–83%). Notes *Acetobacter* is abundant by DNA methods but rarely cultured.
- **Kefiran:** Identifies the unique exopolysaccharide and its role in the grain matrix and texture.
- **Health:** Catalogs documented effects — cholesterol/ACE inhibition, antimicrobial, antitumor, wound healing, immune modulation — and is careful to flag that **human clinical evidence is limited** relative to animal models.

Used as the high-confidence basis for [[Kefir Microbiology and Health Benefits]]. Corroborated by Springer Nature reviews (2022, 2025) and a 2025 Frontiers functional-probiotic review returning the same species and the same "limited human evidence" caveat.
