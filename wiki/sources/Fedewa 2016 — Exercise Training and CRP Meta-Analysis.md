# Fedewa 2016 — Effect of Exercise Training on C-Reactive Protein

*Br J Sports Med* systematic review and meta-analysis — the landmark quantitative estimate of how much exercise moves CRP (according to PubMed — Fedewa 2016, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2016-095999)).

Pooling **83 controlled trials (n=3,769)**, structured exercise training lowered CRP with a mean effect size of **0.26** (95% CI 0.18–0.34). The reduction was **partly mediated by fat loss**: a drop in BMI and body-fat % independently explained ~11% and ~7% of the response, and the effect was largest when training reduced BMI (ES 0.38). Crucially, **CRP still fell significantly in the absence of any weight loss** (ES 0.19), establishing an independent anti-inflammatory effect of exercise. Benefit held across age and sex.

Contributes the anchor claim that **regular exercise is a robust, dose-sensitive way to lower hs-CRP**, both directly and via fat loss.
