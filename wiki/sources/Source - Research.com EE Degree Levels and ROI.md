# Source - Research.com EE Degree Levels and ROI

A cluster of Research.com career-advice articles (2026) on EE degree levels, ROI, salary, and program duration/funding.

## What It Contributes

- **Duration:** master's 1–2 yrs full-time (2–3 part-time, 1 yr accelerated); PhD 4–6 yrs post-bachelor's, engineering avg ~5.5 yrs.
- **Salary:** master's earn ~15–25% (avg ~18%) more than bachelor's; $300K+ career value. Entry MS ~$85K, median ~$100K.
- **ROI:** total MS cost ~$20K–$70K; payback 3–7 yrs.
- **Funding:** full funding (tuition remission + stipend) is the norm for PhDs (3–6 yr packages), rare for master's; ~40% of MS students get tuition waivers/stipends via TA/RA roles.
- **Field entry:** power systems, comms/signal processing, controls/robotics enter at the bachelor's; master's specializes; PhD for research/academia.
- **Outcomes:** ~72% of MS grads → industry (design, semiconductors, HW/SW systems, CAD for digital/aerospace/defense); ~28% → PhD.

## Reliability Note

Career-information aggregator, not a primary source. Frames advanced degrees positively ("paycheck bonus"). Numbers are consistent with other sources but treat the optimistic ROI framing as `medium` confidence. Useful for duration/funding structure, which is well-established.
