# Index Fund Comparison 2026 (FinanceWonk)

## What this source contributes

Current (2026) expense ratios, minimums, and 10-year returns for every major S&P 500 / total-market / international index fund across Fidelity, Vanguard, and Schwab. The data backbone of [[Index Fund Tier List]].

## Key Claims

**S&P 500:** FXAIX 0.015% ($0 min) · SWPPX 0.02% ($0) · VOO 0.03% (1 share) · VFIAX 0.04% ($3,000). 10-yr ~15.5–15.6%/yr for all. (confidence: medium — single source, but figures match fund-company filings)

**Total US market:** FSKAX 0.015% ($0, ~3,800 holdings) · VTI 0.03% (~3,600) · SWTSX 0.03% · VTSAX 0.04% ($3,000). FZROX is 0.00% at Fidelity.

**International:** VXUS 0.08% · VTIAX 0.12%.

**Guidance:**
- ETFs are more tax-efficient in taxable accounts (in-kind redemptions); mutual funds simpler for automatic contributions in retirement accounts; Vanguard's dual-class structure makes its mutual funds nearly as tax-efficient as ETFs. (confidence: medium)
- "Performance differences are negligible — pick the fund that's most convenient at your brokerage." (confidence: high — consistent with all comparison sources)

## Notes

Commercial finance site, not a primary source — but all numbers are verifiable against prospectuses, and its conclusion (convenience beats micro-optimization at ≤0.04% ER) is the consensus view.
