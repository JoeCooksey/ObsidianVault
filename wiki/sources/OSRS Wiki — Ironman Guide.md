# OSRS Wiki — Ironman Guide

The canonical, community-maintained progression guide for Old School RuneScape Ironman accounts. Most authoritative single source: official wiki, continuously updated, cited by every community guide.

## What it contributes

**Stage model.** Early game = questing, skills to 60–70, transportation unlocks. Mid game = gear upgrades for bossing/raids, centered on Slayer. Late game = raids, endgame bosses, maxing.

**Early-game specifics:**
- Key quests: Waterfall Quest, The Feud, Lost City → Fairytale I (fairy rings), Bone Voyage (birdhouses), Temple of Ikov (Boots of Lightness), Underground Pass (Iban's staff), Tai Bwo Wannai Trio (karambwans)
- Utility unlocks: Graceful outfit, Ardougne cloak 1, games necklace, ring of dueling (Ferox Enclave)
- Multi-skill methods: Wintertodt (cash + supplies), barbarian fishing 58+ (passive Agility/Strength → ~74 both at 99 Fishing), Tempoross 35+
- Kingdom of Miscellania after Throne of Miscellania for passive resources (needs 500–750k coffer)

**Mid-game specifics:** Barrows gloves (RFD), fire cape, fighter torso, dragon defender, black mask → slayer helm (i), elite void, Barrows sets, Moons of Peril, midgame bosses (Scurrius, Sarachnis, Giant Mole, DKs, low-invocation ToA).

**Late-game specifics:** zenytes (demonic gorillas), blowpipe/trident of the swamp (Zulrah), GWD gear, Bow of Faerdhinen (Corrupted Gauntlet), Dragon hunter lance (95 Slayer Hydra), primordial boots (91 Slayer Cerberus), infernal cape, Desert Treasure II as the endgame-bossing foundation.
