# Chen 2016 — Effects of Training in Minimalist Shoes on Intrinsic and Extrinsic Foot Muscle Volume

According to PubMed. Controlled trial in *Clinical Biomechanics* 36:8–13. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clinbiomech.2016.05.010)

20 habitual shod runners completed a 6-month self-monitored minimalist-shoe transition; 18 matched runners continued in standard shoes. Foot/leg muscle volume measured by MRI before and after. The minimalist group showed **significantly larger leg (d=0.62) and foot (d=0.54) muscle volume**, with growth concentrated in the **forefoot** (d=0.64), not the rearfoot. Leg-muscle gain correlated with minimalist-shoe compliance (r=0.51).

Complements [[Ridge 2019 - Minimalist Walking Strengthens Foot Muscles]] with a longer (6-month) intervention and MRI volumetrics, confirming the foot/leg-strengthening effect.
