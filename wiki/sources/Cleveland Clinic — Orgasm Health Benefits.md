# Cleveland Clinic — Orgasm Health Benefits

Clinical patient-education reference on the orgasmic response and its documented benefits. Authoritative (academic medical center).

## Key claims

- During orgasm the body releases **dopamine** and **oxytocin**, which raise positive emotion and **counteract cortisol**, the stress hormone (Source: Cleveland Clinic). `confidence: high`
- Documented benefits include **improved sleep**, **headache and pain relief** (including menstrual cramps), **enhanced cardiac function**, **stress reduction**, and **increased self-confidence**. `confidence: high`
- The benefits arise from hormonal release plus rhythmic pelvic muscle contractions during the response.
