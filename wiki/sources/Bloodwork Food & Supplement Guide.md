# Bloodwork Food & Supplement Guide

Personalized to the out-of-range markers in [[Function Health Labs - 2025-12-29]] (drawn 2025-12-29). Three targets drive everything below:

1. **Atherogenic lipids** — high LDL particle number, small/dense LDL, total cholesterol creeping up, low large-HDL, high **Lp(a)**.
2. **Liver enzymes** — ALT 69 (↑ from 23) and AST 39 (↑ from 19) since July.
3. **Borderline-high Free T4** (1.5, range 0.8–1.4) — monitor only.

> [!warning] Not medical advice
> This is general, evidence-informed guidance built from your lab values — not a prescription. Because your **liver enzymes are elevated**, clear any new supplement (especially red yeast rice, niacin, high-dose vitamin E) with your physician *before* starting. Your doctor may also want to confirm the cause of the ALT/AST rise first.

---

## ✅ Foods to favor

> [!success] Eat more of these
> They lower LDL particle count, raise protective HDL, reduce liver fat, and calm inflammation.

| Food group | Examples | Why — for your markers |
|---|---|---|
| **Soluble fiber** | Oats, barley, beans, lentils, chickpeas, apples, pears, psyllium | Binds bile → pulls down **LDL** and LDL particle number |
| **Omega-3 fish** | Salmon, sardines, mackerel, herring, anchovies | Lowers triglycerides, anti-inflammatory, reduces **liver fat** |
| **Plant omega-3 / nuts** | Walnuts, flaxseed, chia, almonds | LDL-lowering, improves particle profile |
| **Cruciferous + leafy greens** | Broccoli, Brussels sprouts, kale, spinach, cauliflower | Fiber + compounds that support **liver** detox pathways |
| **MUFA fats** | Extra-virgin olive oil, avocado | Shifts LDL→HDL ratio, raises **large HDL** |
| **Soy protein** | Tofu, tempeh, edamame | Replaces saturated-fat protein; modest LDL drop |
| **Berries & polyphenols** | Blueberries, raspberries, beets, artichoke | Antioxidants that protect the **liver** |
| **Coffee (black) & green tea** | 2–3 cups/day | Coffee is repeatedly associated with **lower ALT/AST** |
| **Garlic & alliums** | Garlic, onion, leek | Small lipid + cardiovascular benefit |
| **Plant sterols/stanols** | Fortified foods, nuts, seeds, legumes | Block cholesterol absorption (~10% LDL) |

## ❌ Foods to limit or avoid

> [!failure] Cut these back
> They raise LDL/Lp(a), drive small dense particles, and add fat to the liver.

| Avoid | Found in | Why — for your markers |
|---|---|---|
| **Saturated fat** | Fatty red meat, processed meat, butter, full-fat dairy, coconut/palm oil | Raises **LDL** and total cholesterol — your two upward-trending markers |
| **Trans fats** | Margarine, packaged baked goods, anything "partially hydrogenated" | Worst offender — raises **LDL and Lp(a)**, lowers HDL |
| **Added sugar & fructose** | Soda, juice, sweets, syrups | Fructose → **hepatic fat** (ALT/AST) + small dense LDL + triglycerides |
| **Refined carbs** | White bread, pastries, white rice, most cereals | Drives small/dense LDL and liver fat |
| **Alcohol** | All forms | Direct driver of **elevated liver enzymes**; also raises triglycerides. Strongly limit while ALT/AST are high |
| **Fried & ultra-processed** | Fast food, chips, processed snacks | Combine sat-fat, refined carbs, and processed oils |
| **Sugary drinks** | Soda, sweetened coffee, energy drinks | Concentrated fructose → liver |

## 💊 Best-evidence supplements

> [!tip] Prioritized for your profile
> Ordered roughly by strength of evidence + relevance. Start one at a time so you can attribute any effect (or side effect).

### Tier 1 — strongest fit
| Supplement | Typical dose | Targets | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)** | 1–2 g/day combined | Triglycerides, liver fat, inflammation | Best all-rounder for your panel |
| **Psyllium / soluble fiber** | 5–10 g/day | LDL, LDL particle number | Cheap, safe, take with water |
| **Plant sterols/stanols** | ~2 g/day | LDL absorption | ~8–10% LDL reduction |

### Tier 2 — good evidence, mild cautions
| Supplement | Typical dose | Targets | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bergamot extract** | 500–1000 mg/day | LDL, particle profile | Growing evidence for lipid lowering |
| **Berberine** | 500 mg 2–3×/day | LDL + metabolic | Can interact with meds; check first |
| **Milk thistle (silymarin)** | 200–400 mg/day | Liver support | Commonly used for elevated enzymes; evidence modest |

### Tier 3 — discuss with doctor FIRST (liver-relevant cautions)
> [!danger] Clear these with your physician given elevated ALT/AST
> | Supplement | For | Why caution |
> |---|---|---|
> | **Red yeast rice** | LDL (natural monacolin/statin) | Effective but **can itself raise liver enzymes** — risky while ALT/AST are already high |
> | **Niacin (B3)** | The one option with modest **Lp(a)** lowering | Flushing + can stress the liver at high dose |
> | **Vitamin E** | Fatty liver (NAFLD) | Only if NAFLD confirmed; not a self-start |

## 🧬 On Lp(a) specifically

Your **Lp(a) of 152 nmol/L** is ~90% genetically determined and barely responds to diet. Strategy is not to chase the number but to **keep all *other* risk factors tight** (LDL, blood pressure, no smoking, fitness). Niacin is the only common supplement with modest effect; emerging prescription therapies exist — a conversation for your doctor.
