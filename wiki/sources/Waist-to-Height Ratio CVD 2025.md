# Waist-to-Height Ratio and Cardiovascular Risk (2025)

2025 research (Lancet Regional Health – Americas; corroborated by University of Pittsburgh and ESC press releases) establishing waist-to-height ratio as a **superior, near-free** anthropometric predictor of cardiovascular and all-cause mortality, beating BMI.

Basis for the S-tier rating of waist-to-height ratio: zero cost, monthly cadence, high predictive power, directly actionable via fat loss.
