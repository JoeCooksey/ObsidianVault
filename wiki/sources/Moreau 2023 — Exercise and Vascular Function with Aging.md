# Moreau 2023 — Exercise and Vascular Function with Aging

According to PubMed. Review of how regular exercise protects the vascular endothelium against age-related decline. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1152/ajpheart.00392.2023)

## What it contributes

The mechanism for **how to optimize [[Endothelial Cells]].**

- **Vascular aging** = endothelial dysfunction + large-artery stiffening; both are independent predictors of cardiovascular disease. (Source: Moreau 2023, confidence high)
- Cause: excess **reactive oxygen species + inflammation → reduced nitric oxide (NO) bioavailability** and arterial-wall remodeling. (confidence high)
- **Aerobic exercise** improves arterial stiffness in both sexes and enhances endothelial function in men by **lowering oxidative stress/inflammation and preserving NO**. (confidence high)
- **Resistance + aerobic** training are first-line for reducing cardiovascular risk with aging.

> [!gap] Sex matters: aerobic-training improvements in endothelial function are **not consistently seen in estrogen-deficient postmenopausal women**, likely due to gonadal aging disrupting exercise signaling.

A companion review (Murray et al. 2023, *Exp. Gerontol.*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.exger.2023.112105)) frames aerobic exercise as the most effective intervention for vascular health but notes most older adults don't meet guidelines, motivating time-efficient alternatives.
