# ASU FURI — Fulton Undergraduate Research Initiative

ASU's funded undergraduate research program — directly relevant to Joe (ASU Fulton EE). The realistic high-value alternative to a freshman internship.

## What it contributes

The S-tier research lever in [[Freshman Summer Project Plan (Tier List)]]. Joe qualifies as a rising sophomore; the summer move is to **secure a faculty mentor and submit for fall**.

## Key facts

- **Eligibility:** undergraduate in Fulton Schools, in good standing, **2nd semester or higher**, full-time. Freshmen qualify from semester 2.
- **Funding:** $1,500 stipend/funded semester + up to $400 supplies.
- **Application:** updated resume, 2-page research proposal, bibliography, timeline, 1-page personal statement, transcript, **faculty mentor approval**.
- **Deadlines:** roughly mid-October (spring cycle) and late March (fall cycle) — verify current dates at furi@asu.edu.
- **Bottleneck:** the faculty mentor. Cold-email 3–5 power-electronics / WBG professors before the deadline.
