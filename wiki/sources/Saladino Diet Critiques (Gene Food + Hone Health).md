# Saladino Diet Critiques (Gene Food + Hone Health)

Two informed critiques documenting why [[Paul Saladino]] abandoned strict carnivore and what remains wrong with the replacement.

## The Self-Correction (confirmed across sources)

After ~5 years strict carnivore (2018–2022): testosterone "tanked," sleep deteriorated, heart palpitations, joint/muscle pain. His own conclusion: "long-term ketosis is probably not a great thing for most humans." Fix: ~300 g/day carbs from fruit, honey, raw dairy → the animal-based pivot. Dietitian interpretation (Sydney Greene, RDN): the strict version was likely deficient in nutrients needed to produce testosterone and melatonin — the diet caused the problem it was supposed to prevent.

## The Standing Criticisms of the Current Diet

1. **LDL**: Saladino's own LDL remains at levels "proven dangerous over and over"; he argues high LDL is benign in metabolically healthy people. The **KETO-CTA study** (100 metabolically healthy keto adults, LDL >190) found substantial coronary plaque progression within one year — exceeding rates in type-2-diabetic cohorts. Direct evidence against the "lean mass hyper-responder is safe" thesis. (Joe's own Lp(a) of 152 nmol/L makes this critique personally relevant — see [[Most Important Biomarkers (Priority Shortlist)]].)
2. **Evidence style**: anecdote + ancestral correlation treated as proof; sweeping conclusions without controlled evidence.
3. **Professional consensus**: no fiber (microbiome), deficiency risk from low variety, GI issues; "I would never recommend this diet to anyone... incredibly unethical" (Greene, on an MD promoting it).

## Worth Noting

Both pieces credit the pivot as a genuine self-correction — rare among diet influencers — while pointing out he corrected the *symptom* (no carbs) and kept the *frame* (plants toxic, LDL benign).
