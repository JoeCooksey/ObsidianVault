# Motivational Interviewing — Alcohol.org

Overview of **Motivational Interviewing (MI)** as applied to alcohol use, from American Addiction Centers' Alcohol.org.

## What it contributes
- **Definition** — a short-term, person-centered counseling approach that helps people address and resolve **ambivalence** about changing harmful behaviors.
- **Core stance** — does *not* aim to convince or push; **"rolls with resistance,"** responding with understanding instead of frustration; creates a safe space for the person to consider change *for themselves*.
- **Technique** — open-ended, non-judgmental questions meant to **evoke self-change talk** (the person voices their own reasons to change).
- **Effectiveness** — research suggests MI is especially effective for alcohol use disorder, improving engagement in treatment and reducing problem drinking.
