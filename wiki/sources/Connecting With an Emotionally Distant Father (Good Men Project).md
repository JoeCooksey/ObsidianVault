# Connecting With an Emotionally Distant Father (Good Men Project)

Essay on building a real connection with a father who doesn't open up emotionally — common among men raised under emotional-restraint norms.

## What it contributes
- **Root cause** — distance often reflects inherited masculinity and *avoided hurt*, not absence of love; many such men received little emotional attention from their own fathers.
- **Start small and consistent** — regular shared mealtimes and low-stakes presence in the daily routine; *even a near-silent dinner can be meaningful*. Connection is built through repeated low-pressure contact, not one big conversation.
- **Enter his world first** — talk about "uninflammatory topics" (for Joe's dad: sports, a specific company, a stock thesis) before expecting depth.
- **Listen on his terms** — when he does reciprocate, listen *even if it turns into a lecture you disagree with*; the goal is the channel staying open, not winning the point.
- **Reframe expectations** — actions (providing, showing up) may already *be* his love language; the work is adding emotional clarity to it, not replacing it.
