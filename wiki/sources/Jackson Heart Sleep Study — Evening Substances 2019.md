# Jackson Heart Sleep Study — Evening Substances 2019

Observational night-to-night study among African American adults linking evening alcohol, caffeine, and nicotine to sleep duration and continuity. Supports the mechanism in [[Alcohol and Sleep Fragmentation]].

## What it contributes

- Evening **alcohol** consistently reduces sleep continuity and increases awakenings, day to day.
- Mechanism: early REM suppression → later **REM rebound** → fragmented second half of night.
- Observational design (no randomization); treat causal claims as medium confidence.
