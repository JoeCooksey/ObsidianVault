# NCBI Review — Sexual Health and Well-Being 2025

Systematic review and meta-analysis: *Conceptual Frameworks Linking Sexual Health to Physical, Mental, and Interpersonal Well-Being.*

## Key claims

- Sexual satisfaction and orgasm show consistent links to **greater physical health, higher psychological well-being, and relationship satisfaction**, plus **lower psychological distress**. (Source: PMC12730087) `confidence: high`
- Frames sexual health as a contributor to overall well-being across **physical, mental, and interpersonal** domains — not a standalone concern.
