# McGill OSS — Is 25 Really the Magic Number?

A critical-thinking piece debunking the pop-science claim that the brain "fully develops" at 25. high

- The PFC does keep maturing into the 20s (confirmed by MRI), **but** there is no switch at 25 — development varies, some into the 30s.
- The "25" marker is a general trend, not a definitive study; even Steinberg treats it as a ballpark, not a deadline.
- fMRI findings are messy and prone to misinterpretation; structure ≠ behavior.
- Bottom line: maturity is a *process*, not a number; youthful malleability is potential, not deficiency.
