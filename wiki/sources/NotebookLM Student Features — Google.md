# NotebookLM Student Features — Google

Official Google Labs description of [[NotebookLM]]'s study features. (confidence: medium — vendor source; capabilities are real but framing is promotional.)

## Features That Matter for Book Study

| Feature | What it does | Learning value |
|---------|--------------|----------------|
| **Chat with citations** | Answers grounded in your uploaded sources, with inline links back to the page | Won't hallucinate beyond the book; you can verify |
| **Flashcards & quizzes** | Auto-generated from your docs, with difficulty levels | Active recall / retrieval practice |
| **Learning Guide** | "Like a personal tutor" — asks probing, open-ended questions, breaks problems down step by step | Socratic, not spoon-feeding |
| **Audio Overviews** | Brief / Critique / Debate formats (1–2 AI hosts) | Perspective-taking; review on the go |
| **Reports** | Custom formats — glossary, character analysis, critique | Structured output for review |

## The Key Property

Because NotebookLM is **grounded only in the sources you give it**, it answers about *your book* rather than the open web. This makes it far safer than a general chatbot for studying a specific text — and the citations let you confirm every claim.
