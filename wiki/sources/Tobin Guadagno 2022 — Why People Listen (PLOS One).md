# Tobin & Guadagno 2022 — Why People Listen: Motivations and Outcomes of Podcast Listening

Preregistered study, *PLOS ONE*, April 2022. The best single peer-reviewed answer to "what is the point of podcasts" from the listener's side.

## Method

- 306 adults from 20+ countries (mean age ~28), recruited via Prolific; 240 (78%) had ever listened to podcasts
- Measured Big Five personality, curiosity types, need for cognition, need to belong, and outcomes (autonomy, competence, relatedness, meaning in life, mindfulness, smartphone addiction)

## Findings

**Who listens** — podcast listening is predicted by:
- **Openness to experience** (+)
- **Interest-based curiosity** (+) — learning for the joy of it
- **Need for cognition** (+) — enjoying effortful thinking
- **Need to belong** (−) — contrary to prediction; podcasts are not primarily a loneliness patch
- Neuroticism (−); males more likely than females

**What listeners get:**
- Informational AND social gratification — listening during chores/commutes makes dead time feel productive
- [[Parasocial Relationships (Podcast Hosts)]] with hosts → greater sense of **relatedness**; agreeableness predicts stronger parasocial bonds
- More podcasts per month + social engagement around them → greater **presence of meaning in life**
- Longer listening history correlated with **lower** smartphone addiction
- Raw hours per week: no association with basic needs or mindfulness — volume alone delivers nothing

## Limitations

Cross-sectional; no causal inference; self-reported usage.

## Contributes

The empirical core of [[Research - Podcasts (Purpose, Learning Value, Best Ones)]]: podcasts select for curious, cognition-hungry people and pay off in meaning and connection — not in raw hours logged.
