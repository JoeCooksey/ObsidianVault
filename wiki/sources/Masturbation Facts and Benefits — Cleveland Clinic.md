# Masturbation: Facts & Benefits — Cleveland Clinic

Patient-education reference from a major academic medical center. Authoritative clinical baseline on masturbation.

## What it contributes

**Benefits (general):** reduces stress and tension, improves sleep, increases focus, boosts mood, alleviates aches/pain, may enhance partnered sex, may help prevent anxiety and depression. Mechanism: orgasm releases **dopamine** and **oxytocin**, which counteract the stress hormone **cortisol**. (Source: Cleveland Clinic)

**Sex-specific benefits:**
- *Male*: frequent ejaculation may lower prostate cancer risk (see [[Ejaculation Frequency and Prostate Cancer]]).
- *Female*: increases likelihood of orgasm with partners, relieves menstrual cramps, reduces vaginal dryness in older adults; associated with higher self-esteem and marital satisfaction.

**Debunked myths** — masturbation does **not** cause: vision loss, hairy palms, mental illness, penis shrinkage/curvature, decreased sperm count, erectile dysfunction, lowered libido, or infertility.

**Actual downsides (limited):**
- *Psychological*: guilt/shame stemming from religious or cultural beliefs.
- *Behavioral*: compulsive/excessive masturbation can interfere with work, relationships, responsibilities (overlaps [[Compulsive Sexual Behavior Disorder (CSBD)]]).
- *Physical*: rough technique can cause chafing or temporary swelling; very frequent rough activity can reduce sensitivity.
