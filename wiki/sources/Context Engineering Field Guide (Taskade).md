# Context Engineering Field Guide (Taskade)

Field guide distinguishing **context engineering** (the full information environment — tools, memory, retrieval, state) from **prompt engineering** (the text you send).

## What It Contributes

- The crisp framing: *prompt = what/how you ask; context = what the model knows when it answers.*
- The "2026 defining skill" thesis, backed by a Gartner "breakout capability" label and the 2026 State of Context Management Report (82% of leaders: prompting alone insufficient at scale).
- The **complementarity** point: the best results use both, not one replacing the other.

## Reliability Note

Vendor blog (Taskade builds productivity/AI tooling) — strong as a conceptual map; the survey/Gartner figures are reported secondhand, so **medium** confidence on the exact numbers. The conceptual distinction is corroborated across Firecrawl, Elastic, and arXiv coverage.
