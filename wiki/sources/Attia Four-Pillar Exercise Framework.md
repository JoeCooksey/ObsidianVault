# Attia Four-Pillar Exercise Framework

From Peter Attia's *Outlive* and The Drive #261 ("Training for the Centenarian Decathlon").

## What it contributes

Reframes "exercise" as **four separate trainable qualities**, each needing its own slot:

1. **Stability** — balance + controlled movement; Attia's most-skipped pillar and the foundation that prevents injury (10–20 min/day).
2. **Strength** — resistance training, progressive overload (3–4h/wk across sessions).
3. **Zone 2** — highest output while keeping lactate <2 mmol/L; the aerobic base (3–4h/wk).
4. **VO2 max** — high-intensity intervals; "most uncomfortable pillar, greatest return per minute" (~30 min/wk).

**Centenarian Decathlon:** train now for the specific physical tasks you want to do in your "marginal decade." VO2 max is the strongest fitness→mortality marker.
