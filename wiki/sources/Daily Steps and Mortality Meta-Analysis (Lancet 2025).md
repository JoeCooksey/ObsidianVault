# Daily Steps and Mortality Meta-Analysis (Lancet 2025)

Lancet Public Health systematic review + dose-response meta-analysis (2025), building on the 15-cohort meta-analysis (Paluch et al., 2022).

## What it contributes

The clearest answer to "how many steps?" — an **inverse non-linear** dose-response with inflection around **5,000–7,000 steps/day**:

- **7,000 steps/day → ~47% lower all-cause mortality** vs 2,000 steps/day.
- Risk **plateaus at ~6,000–8,000 steps for adults 60+** and **~8,000–10,000 for under-60s**; lowest hazard ratio overall at ~7,000–9,000.
- The 2022 precursor: each **+1,000 steps ≈ 15% lower mortality**; quartile HRs 0.60 / 0.55 / 0.47.

**Takeaway:** the big gains are getting *off the couch* (2k→7k); chasing 10k+ adds little. Speed/intensity helps but volume is the primary driver. Used as the step-count anchor in [[Research - Biohacking Masterclass (Every Habit by Frequency)]].
