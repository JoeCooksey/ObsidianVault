# Minimum Lifestyle for Lifespan — Lancet eClinicalMedicine 2025

Population cohort study quantifying how **small** combined changes to sleep, physical activity, and nutrition affect both lifespan (life expectancy) and healthspan (disease-free years).

## What it contributes

Evidence that the free, Tier-I end of the [[Cost-Barrier Health Hack Ladder]] is not trivial — minimal upgrades compound into measurable years of life. Grounds the claim that the cheapest interventions carry most of the benefit.

## Key points

- The three free levers — sleep, movement, nutrition — are the dominant joint determinants of healthy aging.
- Effect sizes are meaningful even for *small* changes (e.g. the ~5 min sleep ≈ 1 year association).
- Reinforces that lifestyle is the most powerful and lowest-cost tool in the healthspan kit.

> [!confidence] high — large population cohort, peer-reviewed, 2025. Observational design means associations, not proven causation.
