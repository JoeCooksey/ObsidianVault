# Feynman Technique 2.0 — Using AI Chat for Self-Teaching

How to turn [[The Feynman Technique]] (explain it simply → find the gaps) into a back-and-forth with an AI that *probes* the gaps for you. (confidence: medium — practitioner blog; underlying learning-science citations are solid.)

## The Method

You explain a concept to the AI in plain words; the AI plays curious student / skeptic / professor and asks follow-ups wherever your logic skips a step. Where you stall is the gap — go back to the book only there.

## Concrete Prompts (worth keeping)

- **Setup:** *"I'm going to explain [concept]. Ask me questions to help me find gaps in my understanding."*
- **Curious novice:** *"Act like a curious 10-year-old. Ask 'why?' or 'how?' whenever I use jargon or skip a step."*
- **Skeptic:** *"Argue against my explanation and find the flaws."*
- **Socratic:** *"Don't give me answers — only ask questions that help me figure it out myself."*
- **Escalating:** *"Test my understanding with increasingly specific questions."*

## Evidence Cited

- **Chi et al. (1994):** students who explained their reasoning had **89% better** problem-solving than silent studiers.
- **Roscoe & Chi (2007):** self-explanation → **2.5× conceptual understanding**, 40% better transfer, higher 2-week retention.
- A 2025 study of an AI Feynman bot: **>80% preferred it** to re-reading notes.

> [!important] The catch: benefits appear *only when you genuinely identify and close the gaps.* If the AI just confirms you, nothing happens — you must be explaining first, from memory.
