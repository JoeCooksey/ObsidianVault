# Witkowski 2023 — Erythritol and Cardiovascular Risk (Nature Medicine)

The study behind the 2023 "sweetener causes blood clots" headlines. Important to file because it is **often mis-applied to diet soda** — erythritol is a sugar *alcohol* used mainly in "keto"/stevia-blend and baking products, **not** in most mainstream diet sodas.

## Key Findings (According to PubMed)
- Across discovery (n=1,157) + US (n=2,149) + European (n=833) cohorts of patients undergoing cardiac evaluation, **top-quartile erythritol → ~1.8–2.2× risk of major adverse cardiovascular events** (MACE: death, MI, stroke) over 3 years.
- **Mechanism**: at physiological levels, erythritol **enhanced platelet reactivity and thrombosis** in vitro and in vivo.
- Pilot (n=8): ingesting erythritol produced sustained (>2 day) plasma spikes above the pro-thrombotic threshold.

## What It Means / Caveats
- Cohorts were **high-risk cardiac patients**, and erythritol is also produced *endogenously* — high blood levels may partly be a marker of existing metabolic dysfunction, not just intake.
- Applies to **erythritol specifically**, not sweeteners as a class, and not to standard aspartame/sucralose diet sodas.
- Follow-up work implicated other polyols (e.g. xylitol) similarly.
