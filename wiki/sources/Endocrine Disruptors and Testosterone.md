# Endocrine-Disrupting Chemicals and Male Testosterone — Phthalates & BPA

Composite of human association studies (NHANES; J Clin Endocrinol Metab) and mechanistic/animal reviews on phthalates and bisphenol A.

## What it contributes
- Urinary **phthalate** and **BPA** metabolites are **negatively associated** with total, free, and bioavailable testosterone, and with FSH/LH
- **Mechanism**: phthalate esters act directly on Leydig/Sertoli cells; DEHP inhibits testosterone synthesis
- **Age-specific**: high-molecular-weight phthalates hit men ≥60; low-molecular-weight phthalates hit men 20–39
- **Combined** DBP + BPA suppress T more than either alone (rat data)
- Population estimate: up to **~10,700 US male deaths/year** attributable to phthalate-induced T decreases
- Part of the proposed explanation for the **secular (generational) decline** in male T

## Practical takeaway
Reduce plastic food contact (especially heating food in plastic), choose fragrance-free / phthalate-free personal-care products.

## Caveats
Largely **associational + animal/mechanistic** — controlled human dosing is unethical, so causal magnitude in humans is uncertain (confidence **medium**).
