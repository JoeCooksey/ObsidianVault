# Anhedonia After Addiction (Ria Health)

Explains the dopamine mechanism behind the flat, joyless mood of early sobriety.

## What it contributes
- Alcohol changes brain structure; even though the brain **produces more dopamine after quitting, fewer receptors are available**, so dopamine "can't get where it needs to go" → anhedonia.
- As the brain re-adjusts to normal dopamine levels, it **reopens receptors, and mood improves.**
- Duration of anhedonia scales with the **intensity and duration** of prior alcohol use — weeks to months.
- Directly corroborates the vault's existing [[Dopamine Downregulation and Tolerance]] model applied to alcohol cessation.
