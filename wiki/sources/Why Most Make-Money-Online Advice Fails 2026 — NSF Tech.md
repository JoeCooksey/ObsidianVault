# Why Most "Make Money Online" Advice Fails (2026)

## Key claims
- In 2026 most classic "make money online" paths are "overcrowded, replaced by AI tools, or structured so it's easier to lose money than make it." (confidence: high)
- **"Passive income" is usually active income with the failures edited out** — the polished result hides 14-hour days and deleted wrong turns. (confidence: high)
- **Dropshipping** is not passive: ongoing product research, marketing, customer service, supplier management; faces rising costs + competition in 2026.
- **Crypto "AI trading bots" / HYIP** schemes are Ponzi/MLM structures promising unachievable passive returns; AI-generated scam messaging is rising (DFPI, ASIC warnings).
- What works: "go deep on one thing and become genuinely good at it before monetizing." Realistic: ~$500–2,000 month one, $3k–8k/month within a year — **active, not passive**.
