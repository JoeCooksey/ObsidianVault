# Robert J. Meyers — CRAFT Evidence

The developer's own summary of **CRAFT / CRA / A-CRA** and the empirical evidence behind them.

## What it contributes
- **Mechanism** — CRAFT does not teach confrontation; it teaches **invitation**: how to spot windows of opportunity and discuss change in a way that's interesting to the loved one.
- **Components** — staying safe, mapping the context of the using behavior, using positive reinforcers, and letting natural consequences land.
- **Evidence** — in **4 randomized clinical trials** vs Al-Anon/Nar-Anon or confrontation, CRAFT showed consistent superiority at getting the person into treatment; the smallest margin was **64% (CRAFT) vs 30% (control)**.
- **Family benefit is unconditional** — CRAFT family members become more independent and report reduced depression, anxiety, and anger *even if* the loved one never enters treatment.
