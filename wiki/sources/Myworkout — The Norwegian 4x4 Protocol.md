# Myworkout — The Norwegian 4x4 Protocol

Primary-source description of the protocol from Myworkout, the company founded by its developers [[Jan Helgerud]] and Jan Hoff (NTNU exercise physiologists, 200+ PubMed articles).

## What It Contributes
The canonical protocol parameters and intensity cues, straight from the originators.

## Protocol (as stated)
- **Warm-up:** 6 min, build to intensity
- **Work:** 4 × 4 min at **85–95% HRmax** — heavy breathing, can't speak in full sentences after ~1 min
- **Recovery:** 3 min active; low enough to breathe normally, not so low that ramping up is hard
- **Cool-down:** gradual slowdown
- **Frequency:** 1/week maintains endurance; **2/week recommended**

## Claims
- 4x4 intervals "at least twice as effective as other endurance interventions" (the page asserts this; specific % and citations are not given in the main text — corroborated elsewhere by the 7–13% vs 4–6% VO2 max figures).

## Confidence
**Medium** — authoritative on the *protocol definition* (from the developers) but it is a commercial site; effectiveness multipliers are unreferenced on the page.
