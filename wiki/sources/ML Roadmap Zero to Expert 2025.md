# ML Roadmap: Zero to Expert (2025 aggregated guides)

Synthesis of several converging 2025 roadmap guides.

## Key Claims
- The standard curriculum has four+ levels: **Core Foundation** (Python, math, basic DSA) → **Data Skills** (NumPy/Pandas, data handling) → **Machine Learning** (algorithms + mini projects) → **Advanced AI** (deep learning, NLP, CV, RL), with **MLOps/deployment** as professional polish. (confidence: high — multiple sources agree)
- Math prerequisites: **linear algebra** (vectors, matrices, eigenvalues), **calculus** (derivatives, partial derivatives, gradients), **probability & statistics** (distributions, Bayes), **optimization** (gradient descent, cost functions). (confidence: high)
- **Python** is the default language; NumPy and Pandas are the foundational libraries. (confidence: high)
- Timeline: ~**12 months** of structured study from zero to project-ready, ~**12–18 months** to interview-ready; **6–9 months** if you already know Python/SQL/stats/SWE. (confidence: medium — varies by hours and background)
- Portfolio guidance: **4–6 end-to-end projects** (problem → dataset → EDA → baseline → improvement → writeup), escalating into NLP, CV, and RL. (confidence: medium)
