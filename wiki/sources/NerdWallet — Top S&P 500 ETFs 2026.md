# NerdWallet — Top S&P 500 ETFs 2026

Ranking of low-cost S&P 500 ETFs — the relevant index-fund vehicles on a brokerage like Robinhood that offers ETFs but not mutual funds.

## Key Claims (confidence: high)

| ETF | Ticker | ER |
|-----|--------|-----|
| Vanguard S&P 500 | VOO | 0.03% |
| iShares Core S&P 500 | IVV | 0.03% |
| SPDR S&P 500 | SPY | 0.095% |

- VOO and IVV are tied cheapest at 0.03% — about one-third the cost of SPY.
- SPY's higher fee buys nothing extra for a long-term holder; its edge is trading liquidity (relevant to active traders, not buy-and-hold).
- "You only need one S&P 500 ETF" — all three track identical holdings; stacking them adds no diversification.

## What It Contributes

Establishes that on an ETF-only platform (Robinhood), **VOO** (or IVV) is the default S&P 500 core. Combined with **VTI** (total market) and **VXUS** (international), these are the ETF equivalents of Fidelity's mutual-fund lineup — and they are fully portable between brokers, unlike Fidelity ZERO funds.
