# Bennett 2024 — Practice Behaviours of Expert LoL Players

**Published:** SportRxiv preprint (2024) — exploratory study; **not yet peer-reviewed**, hence medium confidence despite strong behavioral data.

## Design & Findings
- Sample: 913 randomly selected players across four expert tiers (Challenger, Grandmaster, Master, Emerald I) and eight servers (NA, KR, EUW, Vietnam + four smaller).
- Method: behavioral data from the Riot API — timestamps of each player's last 100 matches — not self-report. This sidesteps the measurement problem in practice questionnaires.
- **Challenger players showed the most disciplined play patterns: more matches per day, less variability in total hours, and the fewest days without a match.** (confidence: medium)
- Top players were more consistent across 3-day and 7-day blocks — steady daily volume rather than binge-and-break.
- Larger server player pools correlated with more daily engagement; authors propose pool competitiveness shapes expertise development.

## Caveats
- Correlational: Challenger players may play consistently because they're good (or full-time), not good because they play consistently.
- Match timestamps capture volume and rhythm, not what players do inside or between games (review, drills, study).

## Contribution to Topic
Converges with [[Martončik 2024 — Psychological Predictors of Esports Success]] from a different method: what separates the top tier is **consistent daily volume**, not exotic training. Behavioral signature of expertise = play every day, similar hours, few zero days.
