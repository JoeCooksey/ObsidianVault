# Quantum Mechanics in Semiconductor Devices (overview)

Cluster of consistent secondary sources (Canonica.ai overview, AZoQuantum, Chemistry LibreTexts band theory, Wikipedia electronic band structure) on why quantum mechanics is required for semiconductor device understanding. Individually medium confidence; the core claims agree across all four → effectively high confidence on the physics.

## What It Contributes

- **Band theory's origin**: energy bands and band gaps come from solving for allowed quantum wave functions of electrons in a large periodic lattice — discrete atomic levels broaden into continuous bands.
- **Why classical physics fails**: it cannot account for band formation, conduction only under specific conditions, tunneling-based devices (flash memory), or photon-emission devices (LEDs).
- **Band structure → device properties**: bandgap size sets conductivity, optical absorption, emission wavelength; band theory underlies all solid-state devices (transistors, solar cells).
- **Nanoscale phenomena**: tunneling and quantum confinement grow in importance as devices shrink.
