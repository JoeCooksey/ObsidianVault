# Foods 2025 — Smoke Point Predictive Models for Virgin Olive Oils

Peer-reviewed analytical study (MDPI *Foods*, Nov 2025) on 48 virgin olive oils (19 extra-virgin, 29 virgin) that identified what chemically determines smoke point and built models to predict it.

## What it contributes
Explains *why* smoke points vary and **why refining raises them** — grounding the practical advice in [[Smoke Point (Cooking Oils)]].

## Key findings
- **Free fatty acid (FFA) content** is the primary determinant — strong inverse relationship (higher acidity → lower smoke point).
- **Saturated fatty acids raise** smoke point (greater oxidative stability); **secondary oxidation products (K268) lower** it.
- Antioxidants (secoiridoids) help delay volatile formation.
- Predictive models: PLS using FFA + SFA reached R²=0.77; Gaussian Process Regression using FFA reached R²=0.88.

## Caveats
> [!gap] Authors note the models need external/inter-laboratory validation before broad use. Scope is virgin olive oils specifically.
