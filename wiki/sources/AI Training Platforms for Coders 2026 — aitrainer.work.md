# Best AI Training Platforms for Developers (2026)

## Key claims
- The highest-paying AI-training tasks "consistently require coding skills, STEM knowledge, advanced language abilities, or domain expertise in law, medicine, or finance." (confidence: high — repeated across sources)
- **STEM + coding experts command $40–$300+/hr**; national average AI-trainer freelance pay ≈ **$31/hr** as of early 2026. (confidence: medium)
- Major platforms: **Outlier** (Scale AI; coding/STEM SMEs, easy onboarding), **Mercor** (vetted marketplace, highest rates outside direct employment, longer projects), **DataAnnotation** (accepts beginners), Alignerr, Mindrift, RemoExperts (mostly global).

## Contribution to the topic
Establishes AI training/evaluation work as a top-paying, skill-gated, fully-remote income source — the highest-$/hr online option that matches Joe's Python + STEM profile without requiring a client portfolio.
