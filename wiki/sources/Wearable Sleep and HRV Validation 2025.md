# Wearable Sleep & HRV Validation Studies (2025)

Cluster of 2025 peer-reviewed validations (Oxford *SLEEP Advances* 6-device PSG comparison; MDPI *Sensors* five-study HRV analysis; *Physiological Reports* nocturnal RHR/HRV validation) comparing consumer wearables against gold standards.

**Bottom line:** trust wearable RHR, HRV trends, and total sleep time; distrust precise sleep-stage breakdowns. Oura leads on accuracy. Note manufacturer funding in many studies.
