# Component Meal Prep Bowls — Make-Ahead Method

Aggregate of well-established meal-prep recipe guides describing the component/bowl method.

## What It Contributes

- **Building blocks:** carbohydrate base (rice/quinoa/potato) + protein + 1–2 cups vegetables + sauce/dressing + toppings (toasted seeds, crushed nuts, herbs, pickled onion, avocado, citrus zest).
- **Batch separately** on prep day; **assemble in <5 minutes** through the week.
- **Sauce is the variety lever** — same rice/chicken/broccoli tastes completely different with teriyaki vs. tahini vs. chimichurri; keep 2–3 ready.
- **Store hot and cold apart:** grains + proteins together; fresh veg/greens/wet toppings separate → quality holds **4–5 days** instead of 2–3.

## Confidence

**Medium** — practical technique, broad consensus across many reputable recipe sites; not a primary scientific source but the method is uncontroversial.
