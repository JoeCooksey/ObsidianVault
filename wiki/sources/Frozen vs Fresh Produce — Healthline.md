# Frozen vs Fresh Produce — Healthline

Medically-reviewed nutrition summary aggregating peer-reviewed comparisons (incl. ScienceDirect nutrient-analysis studies and University of Reading research).

## What It Contributes

- Frozen fruit retains **90–100% of vitamin content** vs. fruit at harvest.
- Fresh produce loses nutrients across the supply chain; at consumption it can be **30–50% lower than at harvest**.
- **Vitamin C in frozen blueberries/corn/green beans measured higher** than fresh stored a few days.
- Minerals, fiber, total phenolics well-conserved by freezing; blanching causes a small vitamin C/folate drop.

## Confidence

**High** — synthesizes peer-reviewed primary studies; the frozen≈fresh finding is well-established in food science.
