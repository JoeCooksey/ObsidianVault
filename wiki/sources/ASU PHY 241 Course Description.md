# ASU PHY 241 Course Description

Official ASU catalog/syllabus description of University Physics III — the actual "Physics 3" Joe will take.

## What It Contributes

- **Exact topic list**: introduction to thermodynamics, kinetic theory, physical and wave optics, relativity, photons, matter waves, atomic physics. 3 credits.
- **The prerequisite chain in writing**: PHY 121 (mechanics) and PHY 131 (E&M) with C or better, MAT 266/271 (Calc 2) required, MAT 267/272 (Calc 3) pre/co-requisite — confirming that Physics 3 sits at the *top* of the math-physics dependency graph.
- Syllabus notes a strong calculus background plus vectors and trigonometry are necessary.
