# Boosteria — Top Lane Fundamentals Guide

Comprehensive top-lane fundamentals guide covering wave management, trading, teleport, and macro patterns. The densest single source from this research session — most of the "small details" come from here.

## Contributes

- **Wave rules**: freeze vs short-range melees you can zone; slow push for controlled recalls/dives; fast push to reset or roam
- **Trading**: last-hit punishes (Auto→Ability→Auto), never trade into 6+ enemy casters, level 3 spike windows, cooldown reads
- **TP patterns**: crash→recall→TP-back freeze hold; bot-timer sync; never blind-TP to losing fights
- **Split-push checklist**: summoners tracked, two wards, mid wave safe, team can kite 4v4
- **Champion archetypes**: bully bruisers (Darius/Renekton), scaling tanks (Ornn/Sion), split carries (Jax/Fiora)
- **Small details**: under-tower CS formula; post-crash deep ward (~90 s of jungle entrance vision); Steelcaps vs Mercs by threat type
- **VOD review focus**: first three waves, first recall timing, two lost fights, side-lane decisions
