# White Coat Investor — FSKAX vs FXAIX

Head-to-head of Fidelity's two flagship index mutual funds, aimed at long-term retirement investors.

## Key Claims

- **Identical cost.** Both charge 0.015% expense ratio (Source date: 2026, confidence: high).
- **Holdings differ.** FSKAX (Total Market) tracks ~4,000 stocks via the Dow Jones US Total Stock Market Index; FXAIX (500 Index) tracks the 500 large-caps of the S&P 500. FXAIX holds ~$364B in assets vs FSKAX's ~$66.9B.
- **Performance is near-identical.** Recent trailing returns slightly favor FXAIX (10-yr 12.24% vs FSKAX 11.79%), because large-caps led the last decade — not a structural edge.
- **Verdict:** "Pick FSKAX. It's slightly (but only very slightly) better" for the broader diversification, but "there's not really a wrong choice here."

## What It Contributes

Confirms that for a Fidelity-based core, FSKAX (total market) and FXAIX (S&P 500) are interchangeable S-tier holdings — the decision is preference, not consequence. Reinforces the [[Index Fund Tier List]] ranking. Total-market adds the mid/small-cap slice (~15–20% of US market cap) the S&P 500 omits.
