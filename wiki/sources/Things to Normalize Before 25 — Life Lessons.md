# Things to Normalize Before 25 — Life Lessons (aggregated)

Aggregated popular advice essays on the twenties. Opinion/experience, not peer-reviewed — **low confidence**; used for directional themes only.

- **Time is the greatest asset** at this age — more than talent/ideas; lowest responsibilities = best window for big risks and mistakes.
- **Travel / leave your comfort zone** builds compassion and perspective.
- **Friendships drift** as people change — normal, not failure.
- **Reassess family obligations** and set boundaries to protect well-being.
- **Prioritize health early** rather than learning its value the hard way.
- **Self-exploration and tough stretches** are expected parts of the decade.
