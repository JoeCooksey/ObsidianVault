# UF Physiology — Organs You Can Live Without

University of Florida medical physiology educational article cataloging which organs are survivable to lose and which are vital. Provides the empirical basis for ranking organs by importance.

## What it contributes
- A clean expendable/vital split: **expendable** = spleen, gallbladder, appendix, tonsils, one of each paired organ; **vital** = brain, heart, lungs, liver, kidneys.
- The paired-organ insight: redundancy in lungs and kidneys means partial loss is survivable, which lowers their individual "single point of failure" weighting versus brain/heart/liver.
- Notes the body's adaptive redistribution of function (liver and lymph nodes take over spleen duties).

## Confidence
**High** — university medical physiology source; corroborated by Harvard Health, BBC Science Focus, and The Conversation on the same expendable-organ list. Foundational anatomy.
