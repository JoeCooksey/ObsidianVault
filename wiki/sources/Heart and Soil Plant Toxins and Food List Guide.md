# Heart and Soil Plant Toxins and Food List Guide

Two companion pages from Heart & Soil — [[Paul Saladino]]'s organ-supplement company — that carry the practical food list and the anti-plant rationale his official diet page omits. ⚠️ Conflict of interest: the company sells animal-organ supplements as the alternative to plant foods.

## The Evolutionary Argument ("plants don't want to be eaten")

- Plants can't run, so they defend chemically: "over 50,000 defensive strategies"
- Highest defense-chemical concentrations: **leaves, stems, roots, and seeds** (nuts, grains, legumes) → avoid
- **Fruit is the exception**: plants "want" fruit eaten for seed dispersal, so fruit carries the fewest antinutrients → acceptable

## The Six Named Antinutrients

Lectins (gut damage, autoimmunity), oxalates (kidney stones, mineral binding), tannins (iron), phytates (mineral absorption), goitrogens (thyroid), phytoestrogens (hormones/fertility). Claim-by-claim evidence verdicts live in [[Paul-Saladino-Oats-Vegetables-Claims]] and [[Antinutrients-Plant-Defense-Compounds]] — short version: most are overstated for cooked food at normal doses.

## The Three-Tier Food List

**Low toxicity (primary):** ruminant meat (beef, lamb, bison, venison), organs, pasture-raised eggs, dairy (incl. cheese/yogurt/kefir), nearly all fruit, avocado/olives/squash/cucumber, butter/tallow/ghee, raw honey/maple syrup, bone broth, coconut water.

**Medium toxicity (conditional):** pork and chicken (feed concerns — limit), wild-caught seafood, white rice, sweet potatoes, yams, carrots, sourdough, fermented vegetables, coffee/tea, avocado/olive/coconut oils.

**High toxicity (avoid):** seed oils (canola, soybean, corn, etc.), cruciferous vegetables (kale, broccoli, cauliflower, cabbage), nightshades (tomato, potato, pepper), grains (wheat, corn, oats), nuts, seeds, legumes, processed food, artificial sweeteners.

Notable flexibility clause: "There is no exact formula to follow... Individual tolerance varies" — the diet is softer in practice than the rhetoric suggests.

## What It Contributes

The operational food list for [[Animal-Based Diet (Saladino)]] and the clearest statement of his vegetable position: vegetables aren't "neutral but unnecessary," they're framed as actively toxic — except fruit-type vegetables and (since the animal-based pivot) fermented veg and some roots "if tolerated."
