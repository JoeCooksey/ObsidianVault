# BarBend - Best CrossFit Jump Ropes (2026)

BarBend's tested roundup of CrossFit/performance jump ropes. Ranked by category with prices and pros/cons.

## Picks
- **Best Overall — Rogue SR-2 3.0 ($46.50):** light knurled aluminum handles, fast rotation, two handle sizes. Cons: thin cables can warp, fiddly adjustment screws, indoor-only.
- **Best High-End — RX Smart Gear EVO G2 ($165):** aerospace aluminum, pinch-point grip, dual-bearing swivel, smooth. Cons: well above average price, indoor-only.
- **Best Speed — RPM Comp 4 ($70):** dual-axis rotation eliminates torque, full-handle knurling, travel case. Cons: small 5.5" handles, tiny screws, not beginner-friendly.
- **Best for Double-Unders — Crossrope Get Lean Set ($129–149):** ceramic ball bearings, app tracking, technique feedback. Cons: app subscription extra (~$99/yr), heavy handles.
- **Best for Grip — WOD Nation Atlas Weighted ($27.99):** contoured rubber handles, removable ½-lb weights, no-cut adjustment. Cons: heavy/less portable, indoor.
- **Best Beaded — Elite SRS Beaded ($22.97):** audible rhythm feedback, fits up to 6'4", outdoor-capable. Cons: some fraying, slower than bearings.
- **Best Budget — WOD Nation Speed Rope ($32.67):** ~$17 below average, includes backup cable, fast smooth spin, durable. Cons: plastic handles, occasional loose cable nuts, poor outdoor durability.
