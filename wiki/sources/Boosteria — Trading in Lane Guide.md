# Boosteria — Trading in Lane Guide

Dedicated lane-trading framework (2026). The source for the three-stance model and the trading-windows hierarchy.

## Contributes

- **Three stances**: aggressive (ahead of casters, angled at their last-hit line), defensive (behind casters), cheater (trade only to protect the slow-push plan)
- **Window hierarchy**: cooldowns > last-hit punishes > level spikes (2/3/6) > item timings
- **Spacing**: tethering execution; minion aggro rules (autos on champions draw minions; brush resets aggro)
- **Conversion**: a won trade must immediately become a crash, freeze, or slow push
- **Mistake table**: trading inside the enemy wave, wasting key cooldowns on poke, winning trades without converting, trading blind to jungle info, wrong fight length for your kit
