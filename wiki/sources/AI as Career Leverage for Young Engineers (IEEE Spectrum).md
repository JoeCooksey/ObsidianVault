# AI as Career Leverage for Young Engineers (IEEE Spectrum)

IEEE Spectrum's seven-point playbook for young engineers to use AI as career leverage rather than be displaced by it.

## What It Contributes

The seven moves:
1. **Master fundamentals first** — data structures, algorithms, OS, networking; enables debugging/optimization AI can't.
2. **Collaborate with AI strategically** — clear prompts, validate output, treat AI as a teammate.
3. **Build end-to-end projects** — define → design → ship.
4. **Develop system-design expertise** — responsible AI integration, fallbacks for AI failure, scalability.
5. **Strengthen communication** — explain design choices; cross-functional work stays human.
6. **Continuous learning** — open source, communities, new tools.
7. **Think beyond coding** — problem-framing, architectural judgment, ethics.

> Key quote: *"The most successful engineers treat artificial intelligence as leverage, not competition."*
