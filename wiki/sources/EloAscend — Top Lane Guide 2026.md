# EloAscend — Top Lane Guide 2026

2026 top-lane guide with the most checklist-like, benchmarked advice of the session: explicit TP decision framework, early-game targets, and rank-banded champion pool advice.

## Contributes

- **Wave habit**: read wave state every 30 s; ahead → tank 3–4 minions outside tower to freeze; behind → last-hit only and let it come to you; before recall → fast-push crash
- **3-question TP check**: fight already even? arrival flips numbers? absence punishable (2+ plates)?
- **TP by phase**: early = defensive only; 10–20 min = prime Dragon/Herald window; 20+ = teamfights/Baron
- **Champion pool by rank**: Iron–Silver Garen/Darius; Gold–Plat Darius/Sett; Diamond+ Camille/Fiora
- **Split-push triggers and anti-triggers** (never while Baron is alive)
- **Level 6 all-in checklist** (Darius example): enemy ≤50% HP, stacks rolling, ult up, their defensive cooldown burned
- **Five mistakes list**: roaming without clearing the wave, ego-kill TPs, over-chasing after won trades, skipping the 3:00 ward, splitting while Baron is up

## Caveats

Affiliated with a boosting/coaching service; win-rate figures cited (Garen 52.1%) are season-15-era and stale. Frameworks age better than numbers.
