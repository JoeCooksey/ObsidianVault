# Pascoe 2017 — Mindfulness Mediates the Physiological Markers of Stress

*Journal of Psychiatric Research* systematic review and meta-analysis of **45 randomized controlled trials** comparing meditation to **active** control groups (according to PubMed — Pascoe 2017, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpsychires.2017.08.004)).

Pooled across focused-attention, open-monitoring, and self-transcending meditation, practice **reduced cortisol, CRP, blood pressure, heart rate, triglycerides, and TNF-α**. Using active controls (not just waitlists) strengthens the inference that the effect is from the practice itself.

This is the cleanest interventional evidence that **stress reduction lowers hs-CRP**, and the flip side of the observational finding that **chronic psychological stress raises it**. Confidence is **medium** — heterogeneous interventions and outcomes, modest effect sizes. Contributes the claim that **chronic stress raises hs-CRP and structured stress reduction (meditation) lowers it**.
