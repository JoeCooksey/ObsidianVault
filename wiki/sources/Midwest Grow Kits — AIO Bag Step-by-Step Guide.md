# Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide

The most detailed end-to-end procedural guide found, from a U.S. grow-supply vendor. Provides the specific numbers the synthesis is built on.

## What it contributes

- **Inoculation**: clean the black self-healing port with alcohol; inject **5–7 cc spores or ~10 cc liquid culture** into the **grain layer**; store at 72–76°F.
- **Colonization timeline**: first mycelium days **7–10**; grain layer approaches full colonization days **25–45**; keep dark/indirect, avoid direct sun.
- **Break and shake**: at **50–75%** colonization, inflate via filter 5 min, break grain to **marble-sized** pieces, knead to distribute.
- **Fruiting setup**: open and fold/roll the top, paper-clip loosely for slow FAE; **light-mist inside walls once daily**; **12–16 h indirect light** (5000–6500K); hold **72–76°F**.
- **Harvest**: at maturity, cut near the base with a clean blade rather than pulling.
- **Contamination stop-signs**: do not proceed if **green, black, pink, or sour-smelling** growth appears.
