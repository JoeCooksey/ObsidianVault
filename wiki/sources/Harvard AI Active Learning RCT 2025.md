# Harvard AI Active-Learning RCT (2025)

## Key Claims
- A 2025 randomized controlled trial at Harvard found AI tutoring built on **active-learning principles** produced learning gains with **effect sizes of 0.73 to 1.3 standard deviations** over traditional instruction. (confidence: medium — large effect, single study, encountered via secondary reporting)

## Contribution to topic
This is the headline evidence that an LLM *configured as an active tutor* beats passive instruction by a large margin — the empirical case for building the [[ChatGPT ML Tutor Prompt (Zero to Pro)]] around active recall and Socratic questioning rather than lecture-style explanation.

> [!gap] Effect size cited via secondary coverage; primary paper not fetched. The 0.73–1.3 SD range is very large — verify against the original study before quoting as fact. Likely a controlled course setting, not a year-long solo learner.
