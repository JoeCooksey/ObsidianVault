# Compound Interest Starting Early

Pooled financial-education sources (Fidelity, credit unions, advisor blogs) on why building money habits before 25 pays off. high for the math, medium for the prescriptive rules.

- **Time > amount.** $500/mo from 25 at ~7% ≈ $1M+ by retirement; starting at 35 roughly halves it. Start-at-25 vs start-at-30 ≈ $450K difference for only $30K less invested.
- **Start small now** — even $25–$50/mo begins compounding and builds the habit.
- **Order of operations:** (1) build a 3–6 month emergency fund in HYSA; (2) pay off >~20% APR debt (guaranteed return); (3) automate ≥20% savings, "pay yourself first"; (4) capture any employer 401(k) match (free money).
