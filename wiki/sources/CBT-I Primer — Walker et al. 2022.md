# CBT-I Primer — Walker et al. 2022

Peer-reviewed primer (Walker, Muench, Perlis, Vargas; 2022) describing the standard multi-component structure and evidence base of CBT-I. Authoritative academic source.

## What it contributes

Defines the five components and their mechanisms → [[CBT-I (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia)]]:
- **Sleep Restriction Therapy** — limit time in bed to actual sleep ability, raising homeostatic drive → [[Sleep Restriction Therapy]].
- **Stimulus Control Therapy** — leave bed if awake >15–20 min → [[20-Minute Rule for Nighttime Awakenings]].
- **Cognitive Therapy** — reframe dysfunctional sleep beliefs.
- **Sleep Hygiene** — education; *minimal effect standalone*.
- **Relaxation/Mindfulness** — adjunctive, lowers cognitive arousal.

**Dosage**: 6–8 sessions, 30–90 min, weekly/bi-weekly.
**Efficacy**: effect sizes 1.0–1.2 (~50% reduction), stable to 24 months.
