# Bastos 2024 — Ultra-Processed Food, Dietary Indexes, and Systemic Inflammation

*Clinical Nutrition ESPEN* cross-sectional population study of **583 adults and older adults** (according to PubMed — Bastos 2024, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.clnesp.2024.02.010)).

A low-grade systemic inflammation (LGSI) score was built from **CRP, TNF-α, and adiponectin**. Both the **Dietary Inflammatory Index (DII)** and a **Mediterranean-style dietary pattern score** were significantly associated with the LGSI score; a general "healthy eating index" was not. Notably, separately adding an **ultra-processed-food ratio did not increase** the strength of the associations — suggesting that overall dietary *pattern* already captures most of the inflammatory signal.

Contributes support for the **[[Dietary Inflammatory Index]]** as a valid summary of a diet's effect on hs-CRP, and reinforces that a **pro-inflammatory (high-DII) pattern is associated with higher inflammation**. Confidence **medium** (cross-sectional, single population).
