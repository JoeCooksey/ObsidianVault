# Cortisol — Cleveland Clinic

Authoritative clinical reference on cortisol; the medical baseline for the [[Cortisol Complete Guide]].

## Key Facts

**Functions:** glucose/metabolism regulation (liver, muscle, fat, pancreas); sustains the **stress response** after the adrenaline wave; **anti-inflammatory in short bursts** (immune-suppressing when chronic); raises **blood pressure**; anchors the **sleep-wake cycle** (peaks at waking, lowest at bedtime).

**High cortisol — Hypercortisolism / Cushing syndrome**
- *Causes:* long-term high-dose corticosteroids (prednisone, prednisolone, dexamethasone); pituitary/adrenal tumors producing excess ACTH/cortisol.
- *Signs:* facial + abdominal weight gain, purple stretch marks, arm/thigh muscle weakness, high blood sugar → type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure, weakened bones, excess hair growth.

**Low cortisol — Hypocortisolism / Adrenal insufficiency**
- *Primary (Addison's):* autoimmune adrenal destruction, infection, tissue damage.
- *Secondary:* underactive pituitary, or **abruptly stopping long-term steroids**.
- *Signs:* fatigue, unintended weight loss, loss of appetite, low blood pressure (dizziness on standing); salt craving; skin darkening.

> [!warning] Addison's disease is **life-threatening without corticosteroid replacement**. Natural methods do not treat true adrenal insufficiency — see a healthcare provider for diagnosis.

**Testing:** blood, saliva, urine (24-hr), or hair (chronic). Timing matters because of the steep daily rhythm.
