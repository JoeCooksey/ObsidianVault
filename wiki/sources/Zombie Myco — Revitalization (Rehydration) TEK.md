# Zombie Myco — Revitalization (Rehydration) TEK

Vendor TEK on rehydrating spent substrate blocks to pull additional flushes, plus general fruiting troubleshooting.

## What it contributes

- **Dunking method**: submerge the block in non-chlorinated/distilled water at **65–70°F** for **4–6 hours** (max 12 h), then drain **30–60 min** before returning to fruiting.
- **AIO caveat**: AIO bags usually retain enough internal water for a second flush; a gentle mist + paper-clip humidity dome is often enough, and dunking adds stress + contamination risk.
- **Yield decay**: second flushes are typically **≤50%** of the first by weight; each subsequent flush is smaller.
- **Fruiting troubleshooting**: stalled pins ← under-misting/poor FAE (mist 2–3×/day, tent with a dome); side-only pins ← microclimate issue.

## Confidence

**Medium** — vendor content, internally consistent and matches forum consensus, but yield figures are typical-case anecdote, not measured.
