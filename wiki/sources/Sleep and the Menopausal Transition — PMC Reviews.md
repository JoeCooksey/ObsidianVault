# Sleep and the Menopausal Transition — PMC Reviews

A cluster of peer-reviewed NIH/PMC reviews on neuroendocrine mechanisms of sleep disturbance across the menopausal transition. Backs [[Menopause and Sleep Disruption]].

## What it contributes

- Hormonal decline (estradiol, progesterone) and reduced **melatonin** weaken circadian signaling and consolidation.
- Hormone markers predict **awakenings** independent of vasomotor and depressive symptoms.
- Hot flashes strongly associated with awakenings; the awakening may *precede* the flash (shared brain trigger).
