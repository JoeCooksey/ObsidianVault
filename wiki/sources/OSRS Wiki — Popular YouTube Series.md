# OSRS Wiki — Popular YouTube Series

The official OSRS Wiki's curated record of notable OSRS YouTube series — the authoritative list of bingeable account-progress and challenge content.

## Contribution to topic
Supplies the concrete "OSRS-on-OSRS" second-screen recommendations in [[OSRS Second-Screen Grinding Setup]]: progress series (Swampletics, One Man Army, By Release, Fully Mobile), restriction runs (Xtreme Onechunk, A Defence Saga, PVP but HCIM), completion (Collection Log Completionist, GenerateTask), and competitive (Gielinor Games, Max Cape Speedrun). These are long-form and low-stakes — ideal half-watch material.
