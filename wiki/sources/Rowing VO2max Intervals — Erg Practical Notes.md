# Rowing VO2max Intervals — Erg Practical Notes

Compiled practical guidance (Concept2 VO2max calculator, Hydrow stroke-rate/numbers explainers, and rowing-community references) for translating the 4x4 to a rowing erg.

## What It Contributes
The rowing-specific numbers the generic protocol lacks: split/power targets, stroke rate, and drag factor.

## Key Points
- **Target by power/pace, not just HR.** Watts is the best cross-session comparator (independent of damper/fatigue). VO2 max work ≈ **2K pace to ~2K + a few s/500m**; train above ~85% VO2 max (≈92% HRmax).
- **Stroke rate:** work ~**28–32 spm** (aerobic-capacity/threshold boundary; 30–34 = threshold, 36+ = anaerobic). Recovery ~18–20 spm. Power per stroke matters more than rate.
- **Drag factor ~120–130** (≈ damper 3–5) best simulates on-water and lets you hold 28–32 spm; if you can't, drag is too high. Reserve high drag for short power pieces.
- **Common erg 4x4 variant:** 4 × 4 min with 2 min rest, building pace each rep (negative split). Use 3 min rest to match the Norwegian standard.

## Confidence
**Medium** — synthesized from manufacturer training resources and community practice (forums used only as pointers, not cited as authority). Numbers are well-established rowing-training norms, not from a single RCT.
