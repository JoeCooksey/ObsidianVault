# Cleveland Clinic — Managing Sleep Maintenance Insomnia

Clinical consumer article featuring behavioral sleep medicine psychologist **Dr. Alicia Roth (PhD)**, outlining a three-step approach to insomnia where staying asleep is the problem.

## What it contributes

**Three steps**:
1. **Talk to a healthcare provider** — rule out underlying disorders (obstructive [[Sleep Apnea and Nose Breathing|sleep apnea]], snoring, restless legs) via a sleep study if needed.
2. **See a behavioral sleep specialist** — review diet, lifestyle, sleep patterns; identify circadian rhythm and sleep drive.
3. **Keep a sleep diary** — track diet, exercise, meds, naps, sleep efficiency, time-to-sleep, and total sleep.

**Treatment**: [[CBT-I (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia)|CBT-I]] is the gold standard; most improve in 4–8 sessions.

**Avoid**: late-day caffeine; daytime naps (reset sleep drive); sleeping pills (temporary, don't fix root cause).
