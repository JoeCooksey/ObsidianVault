# Supporting a Depressed Loved One — Mayo Clinic

Clinical guidance for family and friends supporting someone with depression.

## What it contributes
- **Listen, don't fix** — let them share feelings without advice, opinions, or judgment; being heard is a powerful healing tool.
- **Positive reinforcement** — remind them of their good qualities and worth (depressed people judge themselves harshly); acknowledge even small milestones.
- **Gentle structure** — encourage exercise, regular eating, and social contact; a **standing daily walk together** is a concrete, low-pressure example.
- **Manage expectations** — recovery takes time; meet them where they are.
- **Safety** — depression raises suicide risk; if severe or life-threatening, contact a provider or emergency services. In the U.S., **call or text 988**; SAMHSA **1-800-662-HELP**.
