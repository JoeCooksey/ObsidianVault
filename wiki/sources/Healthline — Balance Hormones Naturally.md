# Healthline — Balance Hormones Naturally

Medically-reviewed consumer-health article compiling evidence-based lifestyle strategies for hormonal balance, each mapped to the specific hormones it affects.

## What it contributes
- The "few levers move everything" framing: protein, exercise, lean body mass, gut health, low added sugar, stress reduction, healthy fats, sleep, fiber, Mediterranean-pattern diet.
- Per-strategy hormone mapping (e.g., protein → ghrelin↓; sleep → insulin/cortisol/leptin/ghrelin/GH; sugar → insulin resistance).

## Confidence
**Medium** — consumer-health aggregator that cites peer-reviewed studies but is not itself primary research. Directionally well-supported and consistent with endocrinology basics; specific magnitudes should be confirmed against primary trials (PubMed/Cochrane) before high-confidence claims.

> [!gap] Per the wiki research program, consumer-health aggregators are medium-confidence. The *direction* of each lever is well established; exact effect sizes are not verified here.
