# de Sousa Fernandes 2020 — Exercise and Neuroplasticity

According to PubMed. Systematic review (21 studies from ~20,000 screened) of how physical exercise reshapes the brain. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1155/2020/8856621)

## What it contributes

Direct evidence for **how to optimize [[Neurons]] through exercise.**

- Physical exercise **increases neuroplasticity via neurotrophic factors**: [[BDNF]], GDNF, and NGF, plus their receptors (TrkB, P75NTR). (Source: de Sousa Fernandes 2020, confidence high)
- This yields measurable improvements in **cognitive function — learning and memory** — in both humans and animals. (confidence high)
- Exercise promotes **neural cell growth and proliferation**, not just signaling.

A complementary 2023 study (Marino et al., *Science Advances*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adh1403)) shows intensive treadmill training restores striatal synaptic plasticity and rescues motor/cognitive deficits in a Parkinson's model via BDNF/TrkB and NMDA-receptor modulation — exercise repairs plasticity, not just prevents decline.
