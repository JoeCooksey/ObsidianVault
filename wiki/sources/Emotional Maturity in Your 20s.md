# Emotional Maturity in Your 20s (aggregated)

Therapist and counseling blogs on boundaries, saying no, and apologizing. Practitioner advice, not clinical trials — **medium confidence**.

- **Saying "no" without guilt** is a sign of emotional maturity; "no" needs no justification. Replace reflexive "sorry" with "that doesn't work for me."
- **Boundaries = self-respect**, not rudeness; guilt usually comes from internalized beliefs that needs don't matter — therapy/journaling helps challenge them.
- **A good apology** acknowledges your role *and* expresses genuine remorse.
- **Name feelings honestly** to model maturity and invite authentic conversation.
