# Maria Lucey — Ninja CREAMi Protein Ice Cream

Registered dietitian's recipe + health assessment for Ninja CREAMi protein ice cream.

## Nutrition (full serving with toppings)
- **393 cal · 38g protein · 35g carbs · 21g sugar · 5g fat (13g sat) · 722mg calcium (72% DV)**
- **Base alone:** ~240 cal, 26g protein

## The base "formula" (not a fixed recipe)
- **Liquid:** dairy milk, high-protein milk (Fairlife), or plant milk
- **Creaminess booster:** ½ banana, xanthan gum, pudding mix, cream cheese, or avocado
- **Protein:** protein powder (casein preferred for texture), Greek yogurt, or cottage cheese

## Health take
"High in protein, lower in calories and sugar than traditional ice cream"; nutrient-dense enough for regular (not just occasional) eating. Caveats: 21g sugar is still moderate; cold blunts sweetness (don't over-sweeten); eat fresh.
