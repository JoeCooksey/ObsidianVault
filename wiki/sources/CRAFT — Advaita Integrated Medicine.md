# CRAFT — Advaita Integrated Medicine

Clinical overview of **Community Reinforcement and Family Training**, the evidence-based alternative to ultimatums and confrontation for families of a drinking/using loved one.

## What it contributes
Concrete, behaviorally-grounded tactics a family member can use:
- **Positive reinforcement for sobriety** — praise, attention, quality time when sober, making sobriety *feel rewarding*.
- **Natural consequences for use** — let the negative results land; withdraw positive interaction during use; avoid enabling/rescue.
- **Communication skills** — engage constructively, no nagging/pleading/threatening; uses [[Motivational Interviewing]] to explore ambivalence.
- **Personal boundaries + self-care** — prioritize your own life and happiness; work with a CRAFT-trained therapist; avoid caregiver burnout.

The summarizing slogan: **"reward sobriety and ignore intoxication" without ultimatums or relationship damage.**
