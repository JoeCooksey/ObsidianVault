# Li, Huang & Zhu 2025 — Exercise for Cortisol (Network Meta-Analysis)

Published in *Sports* (2025). **Network meta-analysis of 44 RCTs, 3,284 participants** with psychological distress, ranking exercise modalities for **lowering cortisol**. The strongest single source for the exercise recommendation in [[Ways to Reduce Cortisol (Tier List)]].

## Key Findings

**Modality ranking (SUCRA):**
1. **Yoga** — 93% (SMD −0.59; 95% CI −0.90 to −0.28)
2. **Qigong** — 73% (SMD −0.42; CI −1.03 to 0.20)
3. **Multicomponent exercise** — 52%
4. Continuous aerobic — lower
5. **HIIT** — lowest; *tended to increase* cortisol

**Dose (inverted-U, not linear):**
- Overall optimum ~**530 MET-min/week** (yoga peak ~630; multicomponent ~640; aerobic ~770).
- **Session 30–60 min**; **>3 sessions/week** gave the greatest benefit.
- **Moderate intensity (3–6 METs) outperformed high-intensity.**

**Conclusion:** Mind-body practices (yoga, qigong) lower cortisol more than conventional exercise; benefits are **non-linear** — more/harder is not better, and very intense work can raise cortisol.

## Contribution
Turns "exercise lowers stress" into a specific prescription: **moderate mind-body movement, 30–60 min, 3–4×/week** — and explains why late/HIIT-heavy training is counterproductive for someone trying to *lower* cortisol.

## Reliability
**High** — recent network meta-analysis of RCTs. Caveat: population is people *in psychological distress*, so effect sizes may differ in already-calm individuals.
