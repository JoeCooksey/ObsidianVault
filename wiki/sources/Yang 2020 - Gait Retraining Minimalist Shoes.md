# Yang 2020 — Alterations in Running Biomechanics after 12-Week Gait Retraining with Minimalist Shoes

According to PubMed. RCT in *Int. J. Environmental Research and Public Health* 17(3):818. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/ijerph17030818)

30 male recreational runners randomized to gait retraining + minimalist shoe (GR) vs minimalist shoe only (MIN); 17 completed. Both groups lowered loading rate, but **GR lowered it more** and avoided the peak impact force; gait retraining converted **78%** of rearfoot strikers to forefoot strikers. Key caveat: **minimalist shoes alone had limited effect on reducing impact** — the benefit comes from the combined program.

Important nuance for the impact-reduction claim: barefoot shoes change *how* you run only modestly on their own; deliberate gait retraining does the heavy lifting. (Small completer sample → confidence: medium.)
