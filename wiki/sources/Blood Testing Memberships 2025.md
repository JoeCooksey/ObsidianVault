# Direct-to-Consumer Blood Testing Memberships (2025)

Pricing snapshot for comprehensive lab memberships, the cheapest way to get a wide biomarker panel without a physician referral.

| Service | Markers | Price/yr | Lab network |
|---------|---------|----------|-------------|
| Superpower | 100+ | ~$199 | Quest |
| Function Health | 160+ | ~$365 | Quest + LabCorp |
| Empirical Health (heart) | 100 | $190 one-off | — |
| Self-order single panels | varies | $30–200 | LabCorp/Quest/Ulta |

> [!note] NPR (Apr 2026) and Sherwood News caution these are partly **sales funnels** — interpreting 100+ results without a clinician can drive anxiety and over-testing. Medium confidence on optimal-use framing; pricing itself is solid.
