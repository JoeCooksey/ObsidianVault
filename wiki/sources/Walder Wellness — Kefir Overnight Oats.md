# Walder Wellness — Kefir Overnight Oats

Registered-dietitian-authored recipe; corroborated by several other RD/recipe sources (Eating Bird Food, Pinch of Wellness, Miss Allie's Kitchen).

## What It Contributes

- **Ratio:** ~1 cup kefir : ½ cup rolled oats (1:2) for a creamy, not-soupy base.
- **Soak time:** ≥5 hours (minimum 2; overnight ideal) so oats fully absorb.
- **Storage:** 4–5 days in an airtight container; best texture early. Use kefir not near its expiry if making the full batch.
- Top with frozen fruit and nuts/seeds.

## Confidence

**Medium** — RD-authored and consistent across multiple independent recipe sources; informal but reliable for a no-cook breakfast.
