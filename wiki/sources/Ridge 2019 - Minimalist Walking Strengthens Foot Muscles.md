# Ridge 2019 — Walking in Minimalist Shoes Is Effective for Strengthening Foot Muscles

According to PubMed. RCT (Brigham Young University; Irene Davis, Harvard) published in *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise* 51(1):104–113. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1249/MSS.0000000000001751)

57 runners randomized to: minimalist-shoe walking (MSW), progressive foot-strengthening exercise (FS), or control (C); all maintained running mileage. Foot muscle strength (custom dynamometers) and size (ultrasound) measured at weeks 0, 4, 8. **Both MSW and FS significantly increased every muscle size and strength measure; control did not.** Conclusion: minimalist-shoe walking is as effective as a dedicated foot-strengthening program, and the convenience of just changing footwear may drive better compliance.

This is the strongest single piece of evidence for the "barefoot shoes strengthen your feet" claim, and the lineage behind the popular "~60% foot-strength increase" figure.
