# Sun 2024 — Artificial Sweeteners and CVD/Mortality (UK Biobank)

Large second cohort corroborating [[Debras 2022 - Artificial Sweeteners and Cardiovascular Disease (NutriNet-Sante)|Debras 2022]], with a crucial mechanistic wrinkle. **133,285 UK Biobank participants** free of CVD and diabetes at baseline; intake from repeated 24-h recalls; Cox models + polygenic risk scores.

## Key Findings (According to PubMed)
- Per teaspoon-increase in artificial sweetener: overall CVD HR **1.012** (1.008–1.017), coronary artery disease HR **1.018**, peripheral arterial disease HR **1.035**, heart failure HR 1.018 (marginal).
- **~70% of the CVD association is mediated by prior type 2 diabetes** (68–80% across subtypes). Interpretation: the sweetener–CVD link runs largely *through* diabetes/metabolic status — strong hint that the association reflects who uses sweeteners (metabolically at-risk people) rather than direct cardiotoxicity.
- Effect **independent of genetic susceptibility** (no gene × sweetener interaction).

## What It Means
- Per-unit hazard ratios are **tiny** (~1–3.5% per teaspoon); the headline is the mediation finding, which supports the [[Reverse Causation in Sweetener Cohort Studies|confounding-by-indication]] reading.
- Same observational limitation as all cohorts here.
