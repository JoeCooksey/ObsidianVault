# Christopher Kimball — The Creamiest Scrambled Eggs

Substack piece from Christopher Kimball (founder of America's Test Kitchen / Milk Street) on maximizing creaminess in scrambled eggs.

## What it contributes
- **Mechanism:** fat physically coats egg proteins → slower coagulation → harder to overcook and creamier mouthfeel.
- **Dairy strategy:** ~1 Tbsp dairy per egg; *reduce* ½ cup heavy cream by simmering ~1 min to thicken, then blend with crème fraîche (tang) or mascarpone (sweet). Add a final spoon of crème fraîche near the end.
- **Tooling:** saucepan > skillet for easier stirring and even texture; silicone spatula, not a whisk (whisking → grainy curds, per Ramsay).
- **Salt timing:** mid-to-late; salting at the very end made eggs weep on the plate.
