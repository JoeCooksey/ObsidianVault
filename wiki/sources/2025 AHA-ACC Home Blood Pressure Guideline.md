# 2025 AHA/ACC High Blood Pressure Guideline

Multi-society 2025 guideline (JACC) endorsing home blood pressure monitoring and a sub-120 systolic target. Home BP outperforms office BP for mortality prediction and is highly cost-effective ($30–50 cuff). Basis for the S-tier rating of home blood pressure — the highest-yield cheap metric.
