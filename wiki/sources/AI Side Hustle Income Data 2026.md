# AI Side Hustle Income Data 2026

Aggregated 2026 income figures across the common AI money-making methods. Used to calibrate the tier list with realistic ranges rather than hype.

## Service rates (hourly)

- AI consulting: **$100–$300/hr**
- AI automation services: **$75–$200/hr**
- Prompt engineering: **$50–$150/hr**
- AI content creation: **$40–$100/hr**
- AI tutoring: **$50–$100/hr**

## Realistic ramps

- Freelancing beginners: **$500–$2,000/mo within 90 days**; experienced AI-augmented: **$5,000–$15,000/mo**.
- AI automation projects: **$2,000–$15,000** each + **$500–$2,000/mo** recurring per client.
- Micro-SaaS: median successful product reaches **~$1,200 MRR by 90 days**; casual utilities ~$600/mo; experienced operators $20K+/mo. 34% of Q1-2026 launches were by founders with zero prior programming.
- Faceless YouTube: **$50–$500/mo** early → **$500–$5,000/mo** by months 12–18; large channels $3K–$30K/mo (finance/tech RPM highest at $15–$40 per 1,000 views).
