# Kavyani 2022 — Efficacy of Omega-3 Supplementation on Inflammatory Biomarkers

*International Immunopharmacology* **umbrella meta-analysis** pooling **32 prior meta-analyses** of omega-3 (n-3 PUFA) supplementation (according to PubMed — Kavyani 2022, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.intimp.2022.109104)).

Omega-3 supplementation significantly reduced **CRP (ES −0.40**, 95% CI −0.56 to −0.24), **TNF-α (ES −0.23)**, and **IL-6 (ES −0.22)** across a range of health conditions. This is one of the most consistent supplement–inflammation signals in the literature, supporting a recommendation for n-3 PUFAs (EPA/DHA) as adjuvant anti-inflammatory agents.

Contributes the claim that **omega-3 (fatty fish or fish-oil supplementation) lowers hs-CRP** — the best-evidenced single supplement for this marker. (Note: high between-study heterogeneity, I²≈89%.)
