# CSBD and Problematic Pornography Use Treatments — Systematic Review 2023

Preregistered systematic review of treatments for compulsive sexual behavior disorder (CSBD) and problematic pornography use (PPU).

## What it contributes

The first systematic synthesis of *what actually helps* people distressed by their pornography/sexual behavior.

- **Scope:** 24 studies, 1,058 treated individuals (977 psychotherapy, 81 pharmacological), published 2000–2021. (Source: PMC9872540)
- **CBT — strongest evidence:** six studies synthesized; significant symptom-severity reductions vs waitlist, stable at 3–6 month follow-up. Components: psychoeducation, motivation, behavioral activation, cognitive restructuring, cue exposure/urge management, mindfulness. Samples *n*=6 to *n*=138.
- **ACT (Acceptance & Commitment Therapy):** three studies, significant symptom reduction; quality-of-life effects inconsistent.
- **Other psychotherapy:** 12-step programs and art/experiential therapy showed symptom and shame improvements but limited rigorous evaluation.
- **Pharmacological:** SSRIs (fluoxetine, sertraline, paroxetine) mixed — one study's placebo matched the drug; naltrexone showed some severity reduction.

## Limitations
Only **4 of 24 studies were RCTs**; "the quality of most studies was low or very low." Predominantly male, WEIRD samples; heterogeneous outcome measures. Authors: "first evidence for the effectiveness of...cognitive behavior therapy" but specificity conclusions need caution.

> [!gap] Treatment evidence is early-stage. CBT is the best-supported option but the field lacks large RCTs.

## Confidence
High for the synthesis itself (preregistered, rigorous method); the *underlying treatment evidence* it reviews is low/medium quality.
