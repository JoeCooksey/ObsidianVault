# Empowering Parents — 7 Reasons Why Nagging Doesn't Work

Practitioner article cataloguing **why repeated reminders/lectures backfire** and what to do instead. Generalizes directly from parent→child to adult-child→parent.

## The 7 reasons nagging fails
1. **Creates resentment** — produces anger and resentment toward the nagger.
2. **Gets tuned out** — the more you nag, the less they hear.
3. **Negative reinforcement** — they expect you'll just move to the next complaint anyway.
4. **Feels controlling** — being nagged makes people resist rather than cooperate (the [[Psychological Reactance]] mechanism).
5. **Words lose impact** — they learn you'll drop it (or do it yourself) if they wait you out.
6. **Models poor behavior** — they start communicating back the same nagging way.
7. **Focuses on failures** — fixates on what they're *not* doing; wears down instead of building up.

## What to do instead
- **State expectations clearly, once**, and let **consequences** (not repetition) do the teaching — this preserves the other person's agency.
- **Emphasize the positive** — "catch them being good," focus on strengths, not deficits.
