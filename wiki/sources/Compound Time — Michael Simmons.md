# Compound Time — Michael Simmons

Medium essay (Accelerated Intelligence) defining **compound time** — time invested in long-payoff activities — versus reactive linear time.

## Key claims

- High performers protect time to step away from urgent work and invest in long-payoff activities, *despite* having more responsibility. (confidence: medium — anecdotal, drawn from biographies)
- **The 5-hour rule**: ~1 hour/day of deliberate learning and reflection.
- Six compounding activities: journaling, napping, walking, reading, conversation, experimentation — each illustrated with figures (Franklin, Edison, Darwin, Buffett, Jobs/Wozniak, Bezos).
- Deliberate-practice math (corroborated by other career-habit sources): **15 min/day = 62.5 hr/yr = ~312 hr over 5 years.**
