# Hu et al. 2023 — Bankart vs Conservative for First-Time Dislocation

*European Journal of Medical Research* (2023). Systematic review + meta-analysis of **12 prospective trials, 786 participants** — young, active patients after a **first** traumatic anterior shoulder dislocation. According to PubMed. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40001-023-01160-0)

## Key Findings

| Outcome | Arthroscopic Bankart | Conservative | 
|---|---|---|
| Re-dislocation | **7.5%** | **53.0%** |
| Subluxation | 3.1% | 24.2% |
| Positive apprehension test | 7.3% | 25.8% |
| Subsequent instability surgery | 5.6% | 37.8% |
| Return to play | 83.5% | 66.0% |

- All differences statistically significant (p ≤ 0.03), with low heterogeneity (I² = 0% for most).
- **Functional scores did not differ** between groups at follow-up.

## Contribution
The anchor evidence that, in young active patients, surgery after a first dislocation cuts re-dislocation roughly **7-fold**. Authors' nuance: because long-term function is comparable, "immediate surgical stabilization might not be suitable for everyone" — supports shared decision-making, not blanket surgery.

## Reliability
**High** — pooled prospective trials, consistent direction, low heterogeneity. Population is young/active, so figures over-state risk for older or low-demand patients.
