# Moore4Power Launch — Semiconductor Today

News report on Infineon's launch of the EU flagship project **[[Moore4Power]]**.

## Key claims (confidence: high)

- Total project volume **€91M**, **3-year** runtime, **62 partners** across **15** European countries; led by **[[Infineon Technologies]] AG**.
- Funded by EU Horizon Europe — **Chips Joint Undertaking** (Innovation Action), co-funded by participating nations.
- Approach: **heterogeneous integration** combining Si, SiC, GaN with sensing, control, and communication.
- Named partners: ALSTOM, ABB Finland, Fraunhofer ENAS, INNOVATION DISCO, INGETEAM.
- Application targets: wind energy; e-mobility (**99% efficiency**, bidirectional charging); railway (**≥30%** propulsion-loss cut). Digital Product Passport for lifecycle transparency.
