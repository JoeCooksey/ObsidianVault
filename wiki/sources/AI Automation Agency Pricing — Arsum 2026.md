# AI Automation Agency Pricing — Arsum 2026

Pricing reference for AI automation agencies (build workflows/agents for businesses, often on [[AI Automation Agency|n8n]] or similar). Three models: project fee, monthly retainer, hybrid.

## Project fees by scope

| Tier | Scope | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | Lead routing, basic document flows, simple internal automation | $3,000–$10,000 |
| Mid-market | Sales/support/finance ops, internal assistants (multi-system) | $10,000–$35,000 |
| Enterprise | Multi-step AI workflows, regulated ops, custom agent systems | $35,000–$100,000+ |

## Monthly retainers

- Single straightforward workflow: **$500–$1,500/mo**
- Department workflow, multiple systems: **$1,500–$4,000/mo**
- LLM-heavy / compliance-sensitive: **$3,000–$8,000+/mo**

Retainers cover monitoring, bug fixes, prompt/workflow tuning, model swaps, small expansions, reporting.

## Why the model scales

5 clients on $3K–$5K/mo retainers ≈ **$15K–$25K/mo predictable revenue** without proportionally more time. n8n self-hosted is flat-rate (same infra serves 5 or 50 clients); startup tool cost < $200/mo.

## What it contributes

Supplies the **agency tier** of the money-making ladder — the highest-ceiling "services" play, sitting above solo freelancing. Cross-checks the consulting figures in [[How to Make Money with Claude Code — youcanbuildthings 2026]].
