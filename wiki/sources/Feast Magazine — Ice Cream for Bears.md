# Feast Magazine — Ice Cream for Bears

Profile of founder **Timothy Berg** and the brand's "farm-to-cone" approach. Berg conceived it as a NYC tennis trainer, then launched while doing an MBA at WashU St. Louis.

## The recipe philosophy
Premium all-natural ingredients instead of "low-calorie alternatives with synthetic components." Sweetened **only with raw, unfiltered honey**. Ingredients: organic grass-grazed dairy + organic free-range egg yolks. Local honey producer (via Cam Loyet of Honeymoon Chocolates); Honeymoon chocolate used in several flavors; all add-ins free of processed sugar.

Berg on honey: *"It's a product that we've eaten in that exact form for at least 8,000 years."*
