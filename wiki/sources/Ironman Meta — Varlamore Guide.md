# Ironman Meta — Varlamore Guide

Covers how the Varlamore expansion reshapes the classic ironman route. The classic guides (Oziris-descended) predate this content; this source patches them.

## What it contributes

**Hunter rumours** (from 46 Hunter): passive food, prayer supplies, blessed bone shards. Tiers: Novice 46 → Adept 57 → Expert 72 (sunlight antelopes) → Master 91 (needs At First Light). Hunters' sunlight crossbow (72 Hunter / 74 Fletching) called the "best 3-tick ranged weapon in the game."

**Blessed bone shards** (30 Prayer min): 5 XP/shard with blessed wine, 6 with sunfire wine at the Teomat libation bowl. Sources: hunter rumours, calcified deposits (41 Mining, AFK), chiseling bones. Replaces Wilderness/gold-heavy prayer methods for irons.

**Moons of Peril** (75+ combat stats, 70+ Prayer recommended): three bosses dropping full combat sets — Blood Moon (Bloodrager melee, "stepping stone to Bandos"), Blue Moon (Frostweaver magic), Eclipse Moon (Eclipse ranged). Better drop rates than Barrows. Blood Moon set notably good for Slayer.

**Fortis Colosseum** (late/endgame): Sunfire fanatic armour from Wave 4+, Dizana's quiver (BiS ranged back slot) + echo crystal on completion. Wave 12 wants ~95 Atk/Str, 90 Def, 94 Magic.

> [!gap] Royal Titans (Dead Eye / Mystic Vigor prayers) and Yama appeared in search snippets as mid-game and endgame content respectively but were not detailed in any fetched source. Low confidence — needs verification.
