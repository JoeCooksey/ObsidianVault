# Gottman — Small Things Often

Gottman Institute article distilling decades of longitudinal couples research into three daily marriage habits. The thesis: **"small things often" beats "big things rarely"** — everyday micro-connection predicts relationship success more than vacations or grand gestures.

## What It Contributes
Anchors the relationships domain in [[Research - Daily Habits Every Man Should Adopt]] with research-backed daily behaviors that generalize to friendship and family.

## The Three Daily Habits
1. **Small acts of kindness** — little extras (fixing a plate, taking a task off their plate) that signal "you're worth the effort."
2. **Turn toward bids for connection** — respond positively when a partner reaches for attention; the most successful couples do this consistently, building "a bank of love and trust."
3. **Relationship rituals** — regular shared activities unique to the partnership (e.g. a nightly show together).

## Supporting Research (from related Gottman/Gable work)
- **Six-second kiss** — long enough to feel intimate, short enough to sustain daily.
- **Active-constructive responding** (Shelly Gable) — genuine enthusiasm for a partner's good news correlates with higher satisfaction and trust.
- **Specific daily gratitude** correlates with higher relationship satisfaction.

**high confidence** — grounded in the Gottman Institute's longitudinal observational research, widely replicated in relationship science.
