# CGM for Non-Diabetics — Systematic Review (2025)

2025 systematic review (PMC) on continuous glucose monitoring in non-diabetic individuals for cardiovascular prevention. Verdict: **not worth routine lifelong use** for healthy people, but a useful **short n=1 learning tool**. Basis for the C-tier rating of non-diabetic CGM.
