# Karpathy — Neural Networks: Zero to Hero

Hands-on lecture series that builds neural networks and a language model from scratch, in code, line by line.

## Key Claims
- Karpathy (Stanford PhD, former Tesla AI director, OpenAI founding member) is repeatedly cited as the **single best free resource** for learning deep learning if you can pick only one. (confidence: high — strong consensus across resource lists)
- The series' pedagogy is **build-it-from-scratch**: micrograd (autograd from zero) → makemore (language models) → building GPT — which matches the "implement a neural net by hand, then a transformer" milestones in the prompt. (confidence: high)
