# Source - EE PhD vs MS Industry Value

Composite of practitioner discussion (Physics Forums, Quora) and career guides (Indeed, Peterson's, SMU GradArticles) on whether a PhD beats a master's for industry EE.

## What It Contributes

- **PhD pay:** ECE PhD avg ~$149K; research scientist ~$137,525 (PayScale); PhD starts $120K+, tops $150K+ in research/leadership.
- **Premium over MS:** only ~$5,000–$25,000/yr.
- **Opportunity cost:** ~5 years on a survival-level stipend while an MS-holder earns six figures, invests, and pays debt. Low end → may never recover; high end → break even in 8–10 yrs.
- **Verdict:** a master's is adequate for the majority of EE roles; a PhD opens a *different, narrower* set of doors (academia, research). **Doing a PhD for money is not recommended.**
- **Practice note:** for ordinary engineering practice a PE license is more useful than a PhD.

## Reliability Note

Forums and Quora are **pointers, not authorities** (per program.md exclusions). Used here for the *opportunity-cost reasoning* — which is sound and self-consistent across multiple independent threads — rather than as a primary salary source. PayScale figures cross-check with [[Source - Research.com EE Degree Levels and ROI]]. Treat dollar figures as `medium`; treat the qualitative "PhD is goal-driven, not money-driven" conclusion as `high` (broad agreement).
