# Xu 2016 — Alcohol Sensitivity, Alcohol Use, and hs-CRP

*Alcohol* — cross-sectional analysis of **2,903 older Chinese men** from the Guangzhou Biobank Cohort Study (according to PubMed — Xu 2016, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.alcohol.2016.10.011)).

After adjustment, alcohol use was associated with elevated **hs-CRP in a dose-response pattern**: **daily drinkers OR 1.38**, **excessive drinkers (≥210 g ethanol/week) OR 1.57**. The association was **stronger in alcohol-sensitive individuals** (those who flush, from ALDH2 polymorphisms): OR 1.82 for daily and **2.34 for excessive** users.

Confidence is **medium** (single cross-sectional cohort, older men, one ethnicity). The signal aligns with the broader literature that **heavy/daily drinking raises hs-CRP**, while some studies suggest *light* drinking has a flat or slightly lower association (a debated J-shape). Contributes the claim that **heavy and daily alcohol raises hs-CRP, especially in people who flush**.
