# Hurley et al. 2020 — Bankart vs Conservative (Arthroscopy)

*Arthroscopy* (2020). Meta-analysis of **10 prospective studies, 569 patients** comparing arthroscopic Bankart repair vs conservative management after a first-time anterior dislocation. Level II. According to PubMed. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.arthro.2020.04.046)

## Key Findings
- **Total recurrent instability: 9.7% (surgery) vs 67.4% (conservative)**, I² = 0, p < .0001.
- Further surgical treatment for instability: 5.9% vs 46.7%.
- Return to play: 92.8% vs 80.8% (p = .002).
- Conclusion: ~7-fold lower recurrence; routine arthroscopic Bankart repair "may be advisable" in first-time dislocators who play sports.

## Contribution
A second, independent meta-analysis converging on the same ~7× recurrence reduction as [[Hu et al 2023 — Bankart vs Conservative First-Time Dislocation Meta-analysis]]. Two concordant Level-I/II syntheses = high confidence for the young/active subgroup.

## Reliability
**High** — concordant with other meta-analyses. Same caveat: sports-active, mostly young cohorts.
