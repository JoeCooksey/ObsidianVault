# CISUR — Safer Tripping: Magic Mushrooms, LSD, and Other Hallucinogens

Harm-reduction infosheet from the **Canadian Institute for Substance Use Research** (University of Victoria), published through BC Partners for Mental Health and Substance Use / HeretoHelp.bc.ca. The most authoritative *institutional* source found for the explicit avoid-list.

## Recommendations

- **Environment:** safe, peaceful place; sober companion; plan for the full 8–12 hour arc
- **Avoid:** climbing, **mirror-gazing**, unfamiliar sexual encounters, **driving** — unpredictable negative outcomes
- **Substances:** one at a time; cannabis or alcohol on top creates unpredictable effects
- **Mindset:** embrace rather than resist; surrender minimizes distress
- **Contraindications:** personal or family mental-health history (especially psychosis), heart conditions, current depression/anxiety, patterns of excessive use
- **Difficult trips:** calmer surroundings, soothing music, relaxation; reassure that effects are temporary
