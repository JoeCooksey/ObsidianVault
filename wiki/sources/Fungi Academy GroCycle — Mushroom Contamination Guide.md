# Fungi Academy / GroCycle — Mushroom Contamination Guide

Cultivation-education guides on identifying and handling contamination (claims here are corroborated across Fungi Academy, GroCycle, and North Spore contamination pages).

## What it contributes

- **Trichoderma (green mold)**: white at first then bright green/blue-green; fast and aggressive; once green, **toss and isolate immediately**.
- **Bacterial / wet spot**: slimy, wet, sour-smelling patches; from too-wet substrate or under-sterilized grain; toss.
- **Cobweb mold**: grey, wispy, fast-spreading; if caught early can be knocked back with **3% hydrogen peroxide** + improved airflow.
- **Overlay**: cottony/fluffy white *mycelium* (not contamination) caused by high temp / low humidity / low FAE; fix the environment.
- **Prevention**: most contamination originates at inoculation (technique, airflow) or from over-wet substrate.
