# Cleveland Clinic — Hormones Overview

Authoritative patient-facing catalog of human hormones from a major academic medical center. Provides a comprehensive gland-by-gland table of hormones and their primary functions.

## What it contributes
- A near-complete enumeration of hormones tied to their producing gland/organ, including often-omitted ones: erythropoietin and renin (kidneys), IGF-1 and angiotensinogen (liver), leptin/adiponectin (fat), ghrelin and GLP-1 (gut).
- Confirms the **hypothalamus → pituitary → target gland** control cascade.
- Establishes that hormone production is not limited to "endocrine glands" — fat, gut, kidney, and liver are major endocrine organs too.

## Confidence
**High** — academic medical center, consistent with [[Cleveland Clinic — Organs in the Body]] and the Endocrine Society. Foundational physiology, not time-sensitive.
