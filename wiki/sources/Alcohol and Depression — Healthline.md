# Alcohol and Depression — Healthline

Overview of the bidirectional alcohol–depression link and what it implies when someone stops drinking.

## What it contributes
- **Self-medication** — depression frequently precedes drinking; alcohol gives temporary relief from anxiety/low mood, so the drinking problem grows *out of* the depression.
- **The diagnostic clue** — people with alcohol-*caused* depression often **feel better within the first few weeks** of stopping; if that happens, the alcohol was the driver.
- **Dual diagnosis** — when depression came first, sobriety alone won't fix it; the depression must be treated directly, or it keeps "plaguing you" after you're sober.
- **Safety** — co-occurring depression and alcoholism carry an **increased risk of suicide.**
