# Explainer: The Chemistry of Farts (Chemistry World, RSC)

Accessible RSC-published explainer that aggregates the Suarez/Levitt findings. ([source](https://www.chemistryworld.com/news/explainer-the-chemistry-of-farts/2500168.article))

## Key Points
- **Bulk composition (all odorless):** up to a quarter is swallowed N₂ and O₂; about three-quarters is CO₂, H₂, and CH₄ produced by gut flora.
- **The smell** comes from three sulfur gases, each present at only ~50 ppm:
  - Hydrogen sulfide — rotten egg
  - Methanethiol (methyl mercaptan) — rotten cabbage
  - Dimethyl sulfide — garlic/sweet
- Sulfate-reducing microbes react H₂ with sulfate to make H₂S; other bacteria (e.g., *Firmicutes*) produce the H₂ substrate.
- **Beans** raise flatulence frequency, but the effect **diminishes over several weeks** as the gut adapts.
