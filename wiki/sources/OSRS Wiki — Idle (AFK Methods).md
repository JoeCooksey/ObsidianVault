# OSRS Wiki — Idle (AFK Methods)

The official OSRS Wiki page cataloguing the most idle (AFK) activities, the authoritative reference for time-between-clicks across skills and combat.

## Contribution to topic
Provides the canonical, community-maintained list of longest-idle methods that underpins [[OSRS Second-Screen Grinding Setup]]:
- **Skilling:** Infected Roots (WC, 30 min), Crashed Stars (Mining, ~63 min total / 7 min per tier), Dark Crabs (Fishing, longest single stretch), Sailing (~30 min), and "Make All" production (Cooking/Crafting/Fletching/Herblore/Smithing).
- **Combat:** Gemstone Crab (~10 min, "most effective way of idling combat"), Ammonite Crabs (~10 min), Nightmare Zone (~20 min).

## Reliability
High confidence — official wiki, continuously updated, mechanics-based rather than economy-based (idle durations don't drift the way GP/hr does).
