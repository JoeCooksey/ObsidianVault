# Kaelen 2018 — The Hidden Therapist: Evidence for a Central Role of Music in Psychedelic Therapy

*Psychopharmacology*, 2018 (Kaelen et al., Imperial College London) — plus the surrounding research line (LSD-music studies 2015–2016, follow-up neuroimaging).

## Key findings

- In patients receiving psilocybin for treatment-resistant depression, the most-reported themes were **intensification of emotions and mental imagery by music** — the music materially shaped the therapeutic experience, hence "the hidden therapist"
- Earlier LSD studies: enhanced music-evoked **wonder and transcendence**, increased perceived **meaningfulness** of music, with corresponding changes in meaning-processing brain regions
- The **hippocampus** is implicated in the combined psychedelic + music effect
- Follow-up work found increased low-frequency brain responses to music after psilocybin therapy for depression
