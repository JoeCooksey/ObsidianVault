# Complete Health Screening Guide — Costs & Frequencies (BodySpec, 2026)

Consumer screening-cost reference (DEXA provider blog — note vendor bias, hence **medium** confidence on framing). Supplies the price + frequency columns for imaging/screening rows of the master tier list: DEXA $40–60, lipid $35–70, glucose/A1c $28–80, whole-body MRI $1,200–4,500.

Corroborated independently for whole-body MRI cautions: ACR and major radiology groups advise against routine screening in average-risk asymptomatic adults; ~1.6% confirmed-cancer yield with high false-positive cascade.
