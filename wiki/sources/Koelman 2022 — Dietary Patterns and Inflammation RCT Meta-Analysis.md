# Koelman 2022 — Effects of Dietary Patterns on Biomarkers of Inflammation

*Advances in Nutrition* systematic review and meta-analysis of **22 randomized controlled trials** on how whole dietary patterns move inflammatory biomarkers (according to PubMed — Koelman 2022, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/advances/nmab086)).

The **Mediterranean diet** stood out as the pattern with the most consistent anti-inflammatory effect, lowering **IL-6 (−1.07 pg/mL)**, **IL-1β (−0.46 pg/mL)**, and **CRP (−1.00 mg/L**, 95% CI −2.02 to 0.01; note the CI touches zero, and heterogeneity was very high, I²=100%). By contrast, the **DASH, vegetarian, and vegan** patterns showed **no substantial** effect on these markers in the pooled RCT data.

Contributes the key diet claim: among tested dietary *patterns*, the **Mediterranean diet has the strongest RCT evidence for lowering hs-CRP**. Pairs with the older-adult meta-analysis (Wu 2021, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/advances/nmaa116)) showing an inverse Mediterranean–CRP association.
