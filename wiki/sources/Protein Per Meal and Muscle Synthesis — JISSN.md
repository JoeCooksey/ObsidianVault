# Protein Per Meal and Muscle Synthesis — JISSN (Schoenfeld & Aragon 2018)

Peer-reviewed review in the *Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition* on how much protein the body can use per meal for muscle building.

## What It Contributes

- Recommends **~0.4 g/kg per meal** (≈30–40 g) across ~4 meals to maximize MPS — pragmatic upper target.
- **Leucine threshold** (~2.5–3 g/meal) underlies the response (~25–40 g high-quality protein).
- **Critical nuance:** acute studies suggesting a hard MPS plateau (~20 g) are not conclusive; per-meal *distribution* matters less than hitting an adequate **daily total**. The author calls the distribution evidence "fragile."

## Confidence

**High** — peer-reviewed, by leading researchers; the contested nuance is itself well-documented.
