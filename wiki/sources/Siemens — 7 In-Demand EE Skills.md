# Siemens — 7 In-Demand EE Skills

Siemens Academic hiring-manager survey naming the seven most in-demand skills for early-career electrical engineers. Primary source for the soft-skill and specialty tiers in [[EE Student Skill Stack (Ranked)]] and the survey data in [[Research - First-Year ASU EE Skills]].

## What It Contributes
- **Communication & collaboration is the #1 ranked skill** — ahead of any specific technical tool; >90% of employers prioritize teamwork.
- The seven skills: electrical wiring, electrical design, **control systems** (~69% job growth), commissioning & troubleshooting (~38% demand rise), systems design, **CAD tools** (~110k engineers), communication.

> [!note] Confidence: high — employer-side hiring data from a major industrial firm; corroborates [[Research.com — Most Valuable EE Degree Skills]].
