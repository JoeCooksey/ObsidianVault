# Leproult & Van Cauter — Sleep Restriction Lowers Testosterone (2011)

Landmark JAMA study (University of Chicago). 10 healthy young men, avg age 24, lean, restricted to 5 h sleep/night for one week in a lab.

## What it contributes
The clearest causal demonstration that **acute sleep loss lowers testosterone**: a **10–15% drop**, comparable to aging 10–15 years. Participants' mood and vigor fell each day as T declined. Establishes 7–9 h sleep as a primary T lever and short sleep as a top suppressor.

## Caveats
- Small sample (n=10), short duration, lab setting
- A later systematic review/meta-analysis (ScienceDirect, 2021) found **total** sleep deprivation has a larger effect than **partial** short sleep — the magnitude is real but condition-dependent (confidence: medium for the precise %).
