# Theoatrix — 20 Mid-Game Goals OSRS

A well-known OSRS creator's structured list of 20 mid-game account goals, ordered roughly by leverage.

## Contribution to topic
The backbone of [[OSRS Mid-Game Progression Goals]]: gear unlocks (Barrows Gloves, Dragon Defender, Fire Cape, Fighter Torso, Rigour/Augury), combat targets (base 90s, Slayer), account unlocks (grandmaster quests, spellbooks, achievement diaries, combat achievements), 83 Construction for a maxed POH, and passive engines (herb/birdhouse/tree runs, Farming). Answers the "what's worth doing next" framing rather than the money/AFK framing.

## Reliability
Medium confidence — established, well-regarded community creator (Theoatrix), but a single opinionated guide. Priorities are broadly the community consensus for account-building; specifics (exact GP costs, quest lists) should be checked against the OSRS Wiki for current values.
