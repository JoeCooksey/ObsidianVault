# Exercise Snacks Cardiometabolic Meta-Analysis (2025)

Systematic review + meta-analysis on the effectiveness of [[Exercise Snacks]] as a time-efficient treatment for cardiometabolic health in adults (PubMed/PMC, through April 2025).

## What it contributes

The strongest quantitative case that very short, intense activity bursts work.

- **Definition used:** "short bouts (≤1 min) of vigorous-intensity (≥64% VO₂peak, ≥77% of maximal heart rate) PA spread throughout the day."
- **Scale:** 27 trials, **970 adults** (33.8% male), 12 countries, mostly RCTs.
- **Cardiorespiratory fitness (VO₂max):** **SMD = 0.63** (95% CI 0.45–0.82; *P* < 0.001) — a substantial improvement.
- **Systolic BP:** SMD = −0.67 (−0.98 to −0.37). **Diastolic BP:** −0.34. **Body fat %:** −0.21. **Waist:** −0.21. Body mass: no change.
- **Modalities:** cycling (16 studies), stair climbing (4), running, brisk walking, resistance variants.
- **Dose:** 2–15 weeks, 1–5 sessions/week.
- **Certainty:** low-to-moderate (4 strong, 11 moderate, 12 weak studies per EPHPP). A separate BMJ-reported meta of 11 trials reached "moderate certainty" for the VO₂max benefit.

> [!note] A companion review notes the dominant forms differ by age: **stair climbing** for young/middle-aged adults; **leg-focused strength and tai chi** for older adults.

Anchors [[Exercise Snacks]] and the [[Daily Movement Routine (Flexibility, Mobility, Longevity)]]. Complements [[Breaking Up Sedentary Time]].
