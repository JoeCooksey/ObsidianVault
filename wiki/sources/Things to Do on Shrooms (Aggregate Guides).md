# Community Psilocybin Activity Guides (Aggregate)

Rollup of the community/commercial guide layer: healing-mushrooms.net "Best Things to Do on Shrooms" (fetched in full), Psychedelic Support "How to Take Shrooms Safely" (Guy Borgford; fetched in full), plus search excerpts from Tripsitter.com, PsyBear's trip-sitter guide, and similar sites. **Low confidence individually — filed for the consensus pattern, which is strong and matches the peer-reviewed practice set in [[Dutton 2025 - Psychedelic Harm Reduction Scoping Review]].**

## The consensus activity set

- **Music**: mellow, wordless — lyrics can steer the trip unintentionally
- **Nature**: powerful but only with safe location + sitter; "some people prefer nature, others find it anxiety-inducing"
- **Art / writing / journaling**: capture insights, expressive outlet
- **Trusted friends**: connection amplification
- **Movies/TV**: nature documentaries, gentle or trippy films — enjoyment-focused trips
- **Meditation / introspection**: for depth-focused trips

## Dose-dependent and cautionary notes

- **Sex**: light doses only, established partners
- **Dosing**: "low and slow," ≤1 g dried for first-timers, digital scale, wait 60–90 min before redosing
- **Avoid**: alcohol (one week before/after per Psychedelic Support), MDMA combos, tripping alone at meaningful doses, misidentified mushrooms, skipping integration
- **Phone**: airplane mode / Do Not Disturb before onset
- **Sitter scripts**: say "You're safe, I'm here" / "Just breathe"; avoid "Calm down," "You're fine," "What are you seeing?"
- **Integration**: use the post-trip neuroplastic window (days–weeks) deliberately
