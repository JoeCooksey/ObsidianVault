# The Almanack of Naval Ravikant

Free book compiling Naval Ravikant's writing and interviews on wealth and happiness, edited by Eric Jorgenson (2020). Primary reference for the leverage framework used across this research.

## Key claims

- **Four kinds of leverage**: labor and capital are *permissioned* (require people or gatekept money); **code and media are permissionless** — replicable at zero marginal cost, needing only a computer. (confidence: high — central, widely-quoted thesis)
- **Specific knowledge** is found by pursuing innate curiosity and passion, not chasing what's hot; it can't be conventionally taught, only learned via apprenticeship and self-exploration. *"Escape competition through authenticity."*
- **Productize yourself**: *"You're never going to get rich renting out your time"*; *"earn with your mind, not your time"*; build something that *"earns while you sleep."*
- **Long-term games**: *"All returns in life — wealth, relationships, knowledge — come from compound interest."* Work with integrity-driven people over decades; reputation and trust compound.

## Contribution to this topic

Supplies the *why* behind [[Compounding Daily Projects Tier List]]: the highest-tier daily projects (code repo, writing) are exactly the **permissionless** leverage types, and the payoff mechanism (reputation/skill/asset compounding) is the same compound-interest logic Joe already applies to [[Index Fund Investing]].

> [!note] Source quality: the book is a curated secondary compilation of Naval's own primary statements; quotes are his. Self-help framing, not empirical research — high confidence as a *framework*, not as quantified prediction.
