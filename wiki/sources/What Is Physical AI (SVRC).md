# What Is Physical AI (SVRC)

Guide framing **Physical AI / embodied intelligence** as the defining 2026 shift "from the software era into the age of physical intelligence."

## What It Contributes

- **Definition** of Physical AI vs. digital AI: perceiving and acting in the physical world.
- **The three convergence enablers**: simulation (NVIDIA Isaac, MuJoCo), transferable foundation models (RT-2, π0, Octo), affordable data-collection hardware.
- **The deployment-gap thesis**: the bottleneck is reliable, repeatable manipulation in messy environments — an engineering problem, not just a modeling one.
- Hardware/market data points: industrial robot installs at an all-time-high $16.7B; humanoids at $16k–150k.
