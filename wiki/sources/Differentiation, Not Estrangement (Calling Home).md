# Differentiation, Not Estrangement (Calling Home)

Argues most adults pulling away from parents don't need *estrangement* — they need **differentiation**: staying connected while stopping the project of changing the parent.

## What it contributes
- **Definition** — differentiation is the ability to keep your own self, values, and calm while staying in relationship; knowing where you end and the other begins.
- **The reframe** — *"the way out of this hurt is not to focus on the wrongdoings of the parent, but to put energy into changing your response toward the reality of who your parent is."*
- **Adult autonomy cuts both ways** — once children are adults they make their own choices; making love/acceptance conditional on compliance is the failure mode (true for a parent toward a child *and* a child toward a parent).
- **Collapsed boundaries** — feeling selfish/bad for self-care, and responsible for a parent's upset when you set a boundary, is the pattern to grow out of.
