# Hotspawn — How to Get Good at LoL

Recent (Apr 2026) general improvement guide. Useful as the consensus skeleton — its five pillars recur across every other guide found.

## What It Contributes
- **Five pillars:** (1) master one role until timings are automatic; (2) shrink champion pool to 2–3 champions you enjoy, not meta picks; (3) objectives over kills — towers and dragons win games; (4) map awareness — minimap checks as constant habit, cursor kept near champion; (5) perfect the laning phase (freezing/pushing).
- Champion choice by playstyle fit, not win rate; read a guide before practicing a champion.
- Vision: ward jungle entrances, objective pits, chokepoints; control wards to deny.
- Framing: "climbing the ranks is a marathon, not a sprint."
