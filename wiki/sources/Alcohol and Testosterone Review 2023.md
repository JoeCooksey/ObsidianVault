# Alcohol and Testosterone Synthesis in Men — Review (2023) + Santi Meta-analysis (2024)

Narrative review (Expert Review of Endocrinology & Metabolism, 2023) plus a 2024 *Andrology* meta-analysis (Santi et al.) on chronic alcohol and the gonadal axis.

## What it contributes
Dose-dependent picture:
- **Acute, low-to-moderate**: small transient T *increase*
- **Large acute dose**: T falls within ~30 min, troughs 12–16 h later, depressed up to 24 h
- **Moderate habitual**: ≈ **−6.8%**
- **Chronic heavy**: up to **−50%**

Mechanisms: increased HPA-axis (cortisol) activity, inflammation, oxidative stress, direct Leydig-cell toxicity, plus alcohol-driven weight gain. The 2024 meta-analysis confirms chronic consumption disrupts the whole hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal axis.

## Practical takeaway
≤1 drink/day appears low-risk; the harm is in chronic/heavy use. Supports alcohol as an F-tier suppressor in [[Testosterone-Lowering Habits to Avoid]].
