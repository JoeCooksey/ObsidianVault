# Harvard Health — Lifestyle Strategies for Testosterone

Plain-language clinical summary from Harvard Health Publishing; useful as a high-trust overview that aligns with the primary literature.

## What it contributes
A consolidated authoritative checklist.

**Raise / maintain T:**
- Weight management (up to +30% by reversing a deficit)
- Resistance training (largest gains at moderate-to-high intensity) + cardio
- Balanced diet with healthy fats and protein (onions, oysters, omega-3 fatty fish, extra-virgin olive oil)
- 7–9 h quality sleep (T peaks in REM)
- Stress management

**Lower T (avoid):**
- Excessive alcohol (limit to ≤1 drink/day)
- Smoking / nicotine
- Untreated sleep apnea
- Environmental toxins (BPA in plastics)
- Chronic stress (elevated cortisol)
