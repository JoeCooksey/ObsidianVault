# Coetzee 2017 — Conceptualizing Minimalist Footwear: An Objective Definition

According to PubMed. Review in *Journal of Sports Sciences* 36(8):949–954. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2017.1346816)

Reviews footwear-biomechanics studies and proposes a structural definition to end inconsistent usage: a minimalist shoe has **a highly flexible sole and upper, weighs ≤ 200 g, has a heel stack height ≤ 20 mm, and a heel-toe differential ≤ 7 mm.** The authors argue that conflicting findings on whether minimalist shoes help or harm stem largely from the absence of a common definition.

Anchors the definitional section of [[Barefoot (Minimalist) Footwear]]; note consumer "barefoot" brands market tighter specs (≈0 mm drop, <4 mm stack) than this broader minimalist threshold.
