# Deep Work — Cal Newport

Newport's 2016 book arguing that distraction-free concentration is both increasingly rare and increasingly valuable. Primary reference for the deep-work framing of full-focus daily projects.

## Key claims

- **Definition**: deep work = distraction-free concentration pushing cognitive capability to its limit; creates value, improves skill, hard to replicate. Shallow work = low-demand logistical tasks done distracted. (confidence: high)
- **Formula**: High-Quality Work Produced = Time Spent × Intensity of Focus.
- **Deep Work Hypothesis**: deep work is becoming rare as it becomes valuable → economic opportunity for those who cultivate it.
- **Two core abilities** the new economy rewards: master hard things quickly; produce at an elite level (quality + speed). Both require deep work.
- **Principle of Least Resistance** explains why deep work is rare — people default to the easiest in-the-moment behavior absent impact feedback.
- **Four scheduling philosophies**: monastic, bimodal, rhythmic, journalistic.
- Deep work is a **trainable skill** — overlaps with deliberate practice; "move beyond good intentions and add routines and rituals."
