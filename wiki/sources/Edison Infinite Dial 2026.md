# Edison Research — The Infinite Dial 2026

The longest-running survey of US digital audio behavior (annual since 1998). 2026 edition published March 2026. Establishes the *scale* side of "what is the point of podcasts."

## Key numbers

| Metric | 2026 value |
|---|---|
| Ever listened/watched a podcast (12+) | **80%** (~230M) — all-time high |
| Monthly podcast consumers | **58%** (167M) — record |
| Weekly podcast consumers | **45%** (~130M) |
| Have both listened to AND watched | 57% of ever-consumers |

## Structural shifts

- **Podcasting is becoming a video medium**: 39% of weekly consumers name YouTube their primary service; 32% of all Q4 2025 podcast time was on YouTube (Spotify 25%, Apple 20%)
- Growth is now driven by **55+** listeners (monthly online audio 52% → 70% between 2024 and 2026) — the medium has fully mainstreamed
- AI users over-index on podcast listening

## Notes

The Infinite Dial measures consumption, not motivation — pair with [[Tobin Guadagno 2022 — Why People Listen (PLOS One)]] for the *why*. "Learning new things" consistently ranks at/near the top of stated listening reasons in companion surveys (Edison Podcast Metrics), but the specific 2026 motivation breakout was not in the public release.
