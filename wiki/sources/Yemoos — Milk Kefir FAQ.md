# Yemoos — Milk Kefir FAQ

The most detailed grain-care reference among the major vendors, plus a strong budget-quality buying option (~$10 live grains).

## What it contributes

- **Pricing/quality:** ~$10 live heirloom grains, low shipping ($5 fresh / $2 dried), 2-business-day dispatch, international shipping — the best quality-for-price specialist vendor identified.
- **Storage/breaks:** fridge-in-milk (1–2 months), drying and freezing for long-term, two-method-backup recommendation.
- **Reviving:** rinse with non-chlorinated water, restart in small milk volumes, allow several cycles; grains survive 2–3 years cold.
- **Growth rates and reactivation ratios** for fresh vs. dried grains.
