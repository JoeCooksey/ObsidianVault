# Boosteria — LoL Macro Play Guide

Practical macro framework from a coaching/boosting site (Oct 2025). Single commercial source — medium confidence, but internally coherent and consistent with other guides found.

## What It Contributes
- **Five macro pillars:** Information (wards, tracking), Time (tempo, death timers, recall windows), Space (wave + vision control), Resources (gold, XP, ultimates, cooldowns), Conversion (turning advantages into objectives).
- **Wave states:** freeze, slow push, crash, bounce — definitions and use cases. Filed in [[Wave Management (LoL)]].
- **Tempo** defined as "the ability to act before the enemy can respond," created via wave priority, recall timing, health advantage, vision.
- **60-30-10 rule:** push lanes 60 s before an objective spawns, clear vision at 30 s, position at 10 s.
- **Win conditions taxonomy:** scale-and-teamfight, pick-and-snowball, split-push, siege, hard-engage.
- Core macro question: "what is the single most valuable thing I can be doing on the map right now?" — asked roughly every 20 seconds.
