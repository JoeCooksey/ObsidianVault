# Delgado et al. 2025 — Bankart vs Latarjet (Long-Term)

*Orthopaedic Journal of Sports Medicine* (2025). Matched-pair cohort, **80 patients (40 each)**, single institution, **mean 13.2-year** follow-up (range 10–17). Level III. According to PubMed. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1177/23259671241313474)

## Key Findings
- **Recurrence: 35% (Bankart) vs 10% (Latarjet)**, p = .009.
- Cumulative proportion of stable shoulders at 15 years: **64.4% (Bankart) vs 89.6% (Latarjet)**.
- Revision surgery for instability: 20% (Bankart) vs 5% (Latarjet).
- WOSI score and sports level better with Latarjet; **no difference in complication rate** (15% vs 17.5%).

## Contribution
Shows the durability gap widens over time: soft-tissue Bankart repair drifts toward ~1-in-3 recurrence by ~13 years, while the bone-block Latarjet holds ~90% stable. A matched companion study in **adolescents** ([[Delgado 2024 adolescent]] equivalent) found the same pattern (35.3% vs 5.9%). Key input for the Bankart-vs-Latarjet decision in higher-risk patients.

## Reliability
**Medium** — retrospective matched-pair (Level III), single center, modest n. Direction is consistent with the broader literature; absolute numbers should not be over-read.
