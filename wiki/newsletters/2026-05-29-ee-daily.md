# EE Daily — May 29, 2026

*Daily briefing on electrical engineering news, breakthroughs, and one topic worth a deep dive.*

## Top Stories

**1. Moore4Power Officially Launches: €91M to Heterogeneously Integrate Si, SiC, and GaN**

Infineon Technologies leads Moore4Power, an EU flagship semiconductor R&D initiative that officially launched May 20, 2026. The consortium unites 62 partners from 15 European countries with a €91M budget over three years. The program targets heterogeneous integration of silicon, silicon carbide (SiC), and gallium nitride (GaN) within a single power module. Key technical workstreams include 1200V SiC high-temperature module platforms, GaN device scaling toward higher voltage classes, double-sided cooling for SiC, and high-frequency switching optimization in GaN systems.

> **Why it matters:** Heterogeneous Si/SiC/GaN integration is the logical next step after single-material WBG optimization. By co-integrating Si gate drivers with SiC switches and GaN passives in one package, you can dramatically reduce parasitic inductance and improve switching transient behavior—directly impacting converter efficiency and EMI in EV powertrains and data center PSUs. Moore4Power funds the manufacturing research that private companies won't risk alone.

**2. AMD EPYC "Venice" Gen 6 Enters Production on TSMC 2nm — First 2nm HPC Part**

AMD has kicked off production of its 6th Generation EPYC server processors (codenamed "Venice") built on TSMC's N2 process node, making it the first high-performance computing product to enter volume production at 2nm. The move follows TSMC's N2 GAA nanosheet technology entering volume production earlier in 2026. AMD's Q1 2026 data center revenue hit $5.775B (+57% YoY), with total revenue at $10.3B (+38%), and the company's $10B Taiwan investment deepens its advanced packaging partnership with TSMC.

> **Why it matters:** 2nm GAA nanosheet transistors deliver an estimated 10–15% performance-per-watt improvement over N3E at equivalent clock speeds, alongside ~15% higher transistor density. For data center power engineers, this means the same compute workload draws measurably less power, shifting thermal and PDN design constraints downstream. Venice will also stress-test TSMC's N2 yield ramp at high volume.

**3. Soitec/NTU Singapore Report >50% PAE at FR3 Frequencies Using GaN — 6G Milestone**

Soitec and Nanyang Technological University Singapore announced results from their four-year joint 6G research program at MWC 2026 (March), demonstrating record Power Added Efficiency exceeding 50% at FR3 frequencies (7–24 GHz) using GaN devices on Soitec's advanced epitaxial wafers. Three technical papers document performance at both FR3 and mmWave bands relevant to 6G handsets and base stations.

> **Why it matters:** PAE is the critical figure of merit for RF power amplifiers—it measures the fraction of DC input power that becomes useful RF output. At >50% PAE in the FR3 band, this approaches the efficiency of sub-6GHz 5G amplifiers, which is not trivial at higher frequencies where transistor fT and fmax penalties are significant. For a handset, this directly translates to battery life and thermal headroom. GaN-on-SiC remains the dominant substrate; GaN-on-Si is cheaper but sacrifices ~5–8% PAE.

**4. 2D Fe₂Mo₃O₈ Ferroelectric Semiconductor: 230 µC/cm² Polarization, 4×10⁹-Cycle Endurance**

Published in *Advanced Materials* (2026), researchers synthesized a 2D Fe₂Mo₃O₈ ferroelectric semiconductor single crystal using an oxygen-self-supply CVD strategy. The material achieves polarization values up to 230 µC/cm²—10 to 100× larger than most existing 2D ferroelectric materials—alongside ultralong endurance of 4×10⁹ cycles. Complementary research establishes 2D van der Waals ferroelectric materials as transformative components for neuromorphic computing and AI hardware, with applications in brain-inspired CuInP₂S₆-based devices.

> **Why it matters:** Most 2D ferroelectrics top out at 2–20 µC/cm². At 230 µC/cm², Fe₂Mo₃O₈ enters the territory of bulk PZT (~40–80 µC/cm²) and exceeds many hafnium oxide ferroelectrics (~10–30 µC/cm²) used in commercial FeFETs. The 4×10⁹-cycle endurance is critical for embedded NVM applications where write-cycle lifetime has been the primary barrier to adoption. This is still a research-stage material, but the numbers are industry-relevant.

Hyperscalers (Google, Amazon, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI) are investing billions in purpose-built accelerators for their specific workloads.

> **Why it matters:** The shift from merchant GPUs to custom ASICs is an architectural inflection point. ASICs trade programmability for 2–4× efficiency on fixed workloads—critical when running transformer inference 24/7 at scale. For EEs, this means a growing market for chip architects, mixed-signal IC designers, and advanced packaging engineers who can build domain-specific silicon. The HBM shortage (Micron CEO: customers getting 50–66% of requirements) is the current bottleneck in this supply chain.

**6. Graphene-Based Bidirectional Neural Interface Published in *Nature Communications***

Published May 28, 2026, a new graphene transistor-based bidirectional neural interface achieves both high signal-to-noise ratio neural recording and precise electrical stimulation through the same electrode array. The graphene active sensing layer enables low 1/f noise at biologically relevant frequencies (1–500 Hz) while the gate-tunable transconductance supports stimulation artifact recovery within microseconds.

> **Why it matters:** Bidirectional neural interfaces require electrodes that both sense and stimulate without the stimulation artifact saturating the recording amplifier chain. Silicon-based MEAs suffer from charge injection limits; graphene's electrochemical window and biocompatibility solve both. This matters for closed-loop neuromodulation (epilepsy, Parkinson's) and brain-computer interfaces—a growing market for analog front-end IC designers.

## Daily Research Topic: Heterogeneous Integration — Mixing Si, SiC, and GaN in One Power Module

**What it is:** Heterogeneous integration (HI) in power electronics means packaging transistors made from different semiconductor materials—typically silicon gate drivers, SiC power switches, and GaN devices—into a single module. The goal is to exploit the optimal material for each function: Si for low-voltage logic/drivers (cheap, mature), SiC for high-voltage/high-temp switching (1200V+, 175°C), and GaN for high-frequency operation (1 MHz+, low switching loss).

**Why now:** The Moore4Power launch is a concrete €91M bet that heterogeneous integration is the path forward. It acknowledges that no single material wins at all operating points.

**5 Technical Points to Understand:**

1. **Die-attach and CTE mismatch**: SiC has a CTE of ~4.0 ppm/°C; Si is ~2.6 ppm/°C; GaN-on-Si is ~2.6 ppm/°C but GaN-on-SiC is ~4.0 ppm/°C. Mismatched CTEs cause solder fatigue under thermal cycling. Silver sintering (Ag sinter) at 250–300°C die-attach has replaced Pb solder in automotive SiC for this reason.

2. **Parasitic inductance in multi-chip power modules (MCPMs)**: The common-source inductance (L_CS) between the power loop and gate driver loop must be minimized; in a heterogeneous module, the bond wire + substrate routing between the Si driver and SiC switch introduces L_CS that adds a di/dt feedback voltage, effectively decelerating switching transitions and increasing losses. Target: L_CS < 1 nH.

3. **Gate drive timing coordination**: GaN's faster switching (t_r/t_f ~1–5 ns) vs. SiC (~20–50 ns) requires gate driver propagation delays matched to each device type. A 10 ns mismatch in a half-bridge with mixed GaN/SiC devices causes shoot-through risk.

4. **Thermal management**: SiC can operate at junction temperatures up to 200°C; GaN-on-Si is typically limited to 150–175°C due to buffer trap leakage. In a co-packaged module, the thermal interface material (TIM) and heatsink must be designed for the most thermally demanding device while protecting the more sensitive one.

5. **Substrate options**: DBC (Direct Bonded Copper) on AlN (aluminum nitride) is the dominant substrate for high-performance SiC modules—AlN's thermal conductivity (~180 W/m·K) far exceeds Al₂O₃ (~24 W/m·K) and nearly matches SiC itself (~120–200 W/m·K). AMB (Active Metal Brazing) on Si₃N₄ is emerging for highest-reliability automotive applications.

**Concrete Calculation to Work Through:**
A half-bridge SiC module switches 800V at 30A with L_CS = 3 nH. The gate drive current during turn-on is 2A over 50 ns. Calculate the voltage spike induced on the gate from the common-source inductance: V = L_CS × (dI/dt). Compare this to the threshold voltage of a 1200V SiC MOSFET (V_th ≈ 2.5V) and assess whether a negative gate turn-off voltage (-3V) is sufficient margin. Then recalculate with L_CS reduced to 0.8 nH using a flip-chip die-attach and see how the margin improves.
