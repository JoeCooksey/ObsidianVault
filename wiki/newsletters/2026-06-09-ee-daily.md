If SpaceX/Tesla vertically integrate advanced lithography — even at partial scale — it forces a rethink of who controls the chokepoint in the [[AI Five-Layer Cake]]. ASML remains the only company that ships High-NA EUV machines; Terafab would be a single-company anchor customer with enormous negotiating power. For EEs: watch the ASML EUV order book for fab lead times — that's where AI infrastructure capacity gets determined.

**4. 2D CuInP₂S₆ Ferroelectric: Brain-Inspired Memory for Neuromorphic Computing**

Published in *Advanced Functional Materials* (2026): Researchers demonstrate brain-inspired neuromorphic computing using two-dimensional CuInP₂S₆ (CIPS) — a van der Waals ferroelectric material. The CIPS ferroelectric tunnel junctions exhibit analog resistance states that mimic synaptic weight modulation, enabling in-memory computing with sub-picojoule switching energy. This extends the [[2D Materials]] ferroelectric family beyond Fe₂Mo₃O₈ (230 µC/cm² reported earlier this week) with a focus on synaptic functionality over raw polarization density.

> **Why it matters:** FeRAM and [[Ferroelectric Memory]] have long promised non-volatile, low-power storage with fast write speeds. The move to 2D van der Waals ferroelectrics removes the epitaxial substrate constraint — these films can be transferred to any surface, including flexible substrates and BEOL CMOS. For EEs tracking memory technology: the CIPS→neuromorphic path is the most promising route to in-memory AI inference at the edge, where both power density and weight storage are bottlenecks.

**5. Memristor Platform Closes Perception-Computation Gap**

New research from *Nature Electronics* (June 2026) demonstrates a memristor-based neuromorphic platform where environmental signals directly drive computation — eliminating the traditional ADC front-end between sensing and processing. The system operates at ultralow power (<10 nW average) by exploiting the memristor's inherent nonlinearity as both a sensor interface and compute element. The authors call this "perception-driven computation" and demonstrate it for tactile sensing in an autonomous micro-robot.

> **Why it matters:** Current edge AI systems waste power on the sensing→digitization→inference pipeline. This architecture is the [[Neuromorphic Computing]] equivalent of removing the fetch-decode overhead in a CPU — raw sensory analog signals directly update weight states. The 10 nW power envelope is orders of magnitude below the 1 µW floor of most ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers. Key [[EE-Software-on-Linux-Mint]] analog: this is hardware that programs itself through physics, not instructions.

**6. Microsoft Borosilicate Glass Storage: 10,000-Year Data Integrity**

Microsoft Research demonstrated a novel borosilicate glass laser storage technique using phase modifications to encode data at unprecedented write speeds for glass-based archival storage. The technique achieves multi-layer bit encoding at a claimed data integrity lifetime of 10,000 years — far beyond magnetic tape (30 years) or optical disc (100 years). Readout uses standard optical microscopy. The paper positions this as datacenter cold storage for the AI era, where training datasets and model weights must be preserved indefinitely.

> **Why it matters:** AI's insatiable data appetite is colliding with the economics of long-term storage. At current trends, datacenter cold storage is a growing capex line. Glass storage's density (no cooling required, no degradation mechanism at room temp) competes favorably with LTO tape for archives >20 years. The optoelectronics are familiar EE territory: femtosecond laser pulse control, laser-material interaction physics, and phase-change readout — all in a CMOS-compatible geometry.

## 🔬 Daily Research Topic: Silicon Photonics for AI Data Center Interconnects

**What it is:** Silicon photonics integrates optical components (modulators, photodetectors, waveguides, multiplexers) onto standard CMOS silicon wafers using 193 nm immersion lithography. For AI datacenters, it enables co-packaged optics (CPO) — moving the electrical-to-optical conversion from the transceiver cage directly onto or next to the GPU/ASIC die, cutting board-level electrical trace losses and enabling terabit-class bandwidth per package.

**Why now:** NVIDIA announced CPO integration at 400 Tbps for its next-gen Spectrum-X AI networking. Intel and Broadcom are competing for the same CPO standard (OIF co-packaged optics MSA). PsiQuantum's Omega chip (300mm GlobalFoundries process) demonstrated that silicon photonics can reach million-qubit scale. And the Terafab announcement (see Story 1) signals that new fabs may be optimized for photonics-enabled chips from day one.

**Technical bullets:**
- **Mach-Zehnder Modulator (MZM):** Vπ × L product ≈ 2–3 V·cm in silicon; 50 GBaud NRZ/PAM4 achievable at sub-1 V drive for short links
- **Micro-ring modulator:** Compact (≈ 5 µm radius), resonance wavelength thermally tuned ±0.1 nm/°C — requires active thermal stabilization to ±0.01°C in datacenter racks
- **Photodetector:** Ge-on-Si achieves >1 A/W responsivity at 1310/1550 nm; bandwidth limited by RC product (target: BW × R_load > 60 GHz at 50 Ω)
- **Wavelength Division Multiplexing (WDM):** CWDM4 (4 channels × 100G = 400G per fiber) is the current standard; LR8 (8 × 100G = 800G) emerging for AI GPU-to-GPU fabric
- **CPO thermal budget:** Electrical power dissipated in the co-packaged optical engine adds to the GPU TDP — current designs target <5 W optical per 800G port (6.25 mW/Gbps target vs. ~15 mW/Gbps for pluggable transceivers)

- IEEE Xplore search: `"co-packaged optics" AND "silicon photonics" AND "data center" 2024-2026`
- IEEE Xplore search: `"Mach-Zehnder modulator" AND "PAM4" AND bandwidth`
