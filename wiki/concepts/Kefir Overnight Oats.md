# Kefir Overnight Oats

No-cook, make-ahead breakfast: rolled oats soaked in [[Milk Kefir Making — Step-by-Step Guide|kefir]] overnight. Double-dips two S-tier health levers — live probiotic cultures ([[Gut Health Foods Tier List]]) and oat beta-glucan fiber. (Source: [[Walder Wellness — Kefir Overnight Oats]])

## The Ratio

**½ cup rolled oats : 1 cup kefir** (1:2). Too much liquid → soupy; too little → gluey. Add a pinch of salt and optional chia for thickness.

## Method

1. Combine oats + kefir in a jar; stir.
2. Refrigerate **≥5 hours** (minimum 2; overnight is ideal) so oats fully absorb.
3. Top before eating with **frozen fruit** (no thawing — it weeps juice in as it sits) and a spoon of nuts/seeds.

## Storage

- Keeps **4–5 days** in an airtight jar — perfect for jarring 4–5 at once on prep day.
- Best texture is days 1–3.
- Use kefir that **won't expire** within the keep window.

## Why It Fits Joe's Stack

- Uses two staples Joe already makes/buys: home kefir + oats.
- Frozen fruit topping retains 90–100% of nutrients (see [[Frozen vs Fresh Produce]]).
- Variant: blend kefir + frozen fruit + seeds for a **kefir smoothie** when short on time.
