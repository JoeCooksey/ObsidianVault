# Split Pushing (LoL)

Pressuring a side lane while your team plays mid or the opposite side. The goal is **not** the tower — it's forcing 2+ enemies to respond, which creates a numbers advantage somewhere else on the map. (Sources: [[Boosteria — Top Lane Fundamentals Guide]], [[EloAscend — Top Lane Guide 2026]])

## When to Split (all should be true)

- You win the 1v1 against whoever answers, or can escape whoever can kill you
- You're 1–2 items ahead or playing a duelist built for it
- Your team can hold a 4v4 (kite, disengage) without you
- **Baron/Elder is not about to spawn** — splitting while Baron is up hands the enemy a free 5v4 objective

## When to Group Instead

- Behind in items or levels
- Baron alive
- Your team has a reliable 5v5 engage (e.g. Malphite ult) that needs your body
- Enemy comp out-scales side lanes and wants you to split

## Pre-Split Checklist

1. Enemy summoners/ultimates tracked?
2. Two wards covering the approach paths (one deep in their jungle)?
3. Mid wave in a safe state for your team?
4. Escape plan if 2+ collapse?

(Source: [[Boosteria — Top Lane Fundamentals Guide]])

## Execution Loop

Push to Tier 2 → force a response → read how many came → if 2+: back off, let your team take a fight/objective 4v3 → reset and repeat. The 1-3-1 formation forces two-man responses on both sides. A surprise [[Teleport Usage (Top Lane)|TP]] flank converts split pressure into a won teamfight.
