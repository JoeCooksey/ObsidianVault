# Social Proof

[[Robert B. Cialdini|Cialdini's]] third principle: we decide what is correct by observing **what other people do** — especially under uncertainty and when those others are similar to us. "If everyone's doing it, it must be right."

It powers canned laughter, "#1 best-seller" labels, lines outside clubs, and seeded tip jars. Its dark side is **[[Pluralistic Ignorance]]** — the bystander effect, where everyone stays passive because everyone else is passive. Social proof is strongest under **uncertainty** plus **similarity**. **Defense:** treat obviously manufactured or manipulated social proof as a red flag, and check reality directly rather than outsourcing your judgment to the crowd.
