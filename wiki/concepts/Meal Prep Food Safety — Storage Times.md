# Meal Prep Food Safety — Storage Times

Reference for how long batch-cooked food stays safe. These limits set the meal-prep schedule: ~4 days fresh, then rely on the freezer. (Source: [[FoodSafety.gov — Leftovers and Cold Storage]])

## The Three Rules That Matter Most

1. **2-hour rule** — refrigerate cooked food within 2 hours of cooking (1 hour if room is >90°F). Cool large batches fast (shallow containers).
2. **Fridge ≤40°F, freezer 0°F.**
3. **Reheat to 165°F** — steaming hot throughout, every time.

## Refrigerator (≤40°F)

| Food | Fridge life |
|------|-------------|
| Cooked **ground beef** | 3–4 days |
| Cooked **salmon / white fish** | 3–4 days |
| Cooked **poultry** | 3–4 days |
| Cooked **rice** | ~4–6 days (treat as 4 when unsure) |
| Cooked **vegetables** | 3–4 days |
| **Hard-boiled eggs** | up to 1 week |
| Soups / stews | 3–4 days |

> [!note] Fish and salmon are the most perishable proteins here — **eat them first (days 1–2)**.

## Freezer (0°F) — quality, not safety limits

| Food | Freezer life |
|------|--------------|
| Cooked **ground beef** | 3–4 months |
| Cooked **fish / salmon** | 2–3 months |
| Cooked **rice** | ~3 months |
| Soups / stews / casseroles | 2–3 months |

(Source: [[Meal Prep Food Safety — Storage Times]] data via FoodSafety.gov / USDA FSIS / FDA storage chart)

## Practical Workflow

- Prep covers **Sun–Wed fresh**; **freeze half on prep day** for Thu–Fri.
- **Freeze portions flat** in bags so they stack like books and thaw fast.
- **Reheating fish without drying it:** oven 275–325°F, covered in foil with a splash of water/oil, ~10–15 min to 125–130°F internal. Microwave only at 50% power, covered, in 1–2 min bursts. (Source: reheating-salmon method aggregate)
- **Rice caution:** the real risk is *Bacillus cereus* toxin from rice left warm — cool and refrigerate fast, reheat hot.
