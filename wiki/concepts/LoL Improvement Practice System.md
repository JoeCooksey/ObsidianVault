# LoL Improvement Practice System

The meta-layer: *how* to practice LoL, as opposed to *what* to know ([[LoL Macro Play]], [[Laning Fundamentals (LoL)]]). Built from the consensus of improvement guides, then corrected by the research.

## The Research Correction (read first)
- A registered report found **deliberate practice had a null effect on rank** (r = -.01) while **plain volume of play predicted it** (r = .26 in LoL). ([[Martončik 2024 — Psychological Predictors of Esports Success]])
- Behavioral API data shows Challenger players' signature is **consistent daily volume — more games/day, fewer zero days, low variability** — not exotic training. ([[Bennett 2024 — Practice Behaviours of Expert LoL Players]])
- Reconciliation: in a game with built-in matchmaking, fast feedback, and forced variety, ordinary ranked play already contains most of what [[Deliberate Practice]] is supposed to add. Structure helps at the margin (focus, review, tilt control) but **never at the cost of games played**. Volume first, structure second.

## The System
1. **One role, 2–3 champions.** Benchmark: 30+ games per champion at ≥50% win rate; top players average ~33 games/champion. Cuts decision load so attention goes to macro. ([[Dignitas — LoL Ranked Climbing and Tilt Guides]], [[Hotspawn — How to Get Good at LoL]])
2. **Consistent schedule.** Mirror the Challenger pattern: similar hours daily, few zero days, no binge-and-burnout cycles. ([[Bennett 2024 — Practice Behaviours of Expert LoL Players]])
3. **5–10 min warm-up** before ranked: last hits, combos slow→fast, kiting. Daily 20-min drill block when laning is the weakness ([[Laning Fundamentals (LoL)]]).
4. **One focus goal per session**, quantifiable: CS@10, wards placed/cleared, deaths to ganks. One or two at a time — a long list fixes nothing.
5. **Loading-screen game plan:** identify both teams' win conditions and your plan for early/mid/late before minions spawn. Antidote to autopilot.
6. **Light VOD review** (1 game per session-block, ~10 min): own mistakes only, ignore teammates; for each fight ask first *"was the play a good idea?"* before *"did I execute it well?"*; output = 1–2 action points for the next session.
7. **Tilt protocol:** 3–5 game sets, stop-loss after 2 straight losses, chat muted. ([[Tilt (Esports)]])

## Pitfalls
- Champion-hopping with the meta (resets learning every patch).
- Reviewing teammates instead of yourself.
- Grinding past tilt — negative-quality reps.
- Over-structuring: drills replacing games inverts the evidence.
