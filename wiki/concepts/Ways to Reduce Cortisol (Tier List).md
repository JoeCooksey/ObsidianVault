# Ways to Reduce Cortisol (Tier List)

You reduce cortisol when it's **chronically elevated** or won't drop in the evening — the pattern behind poor sleep, "wired and tired," central weight gain, and early-phase burnout. The target is the **evening/night end of the rhythm and chronic baseline**, not the healthy morning peak (Source: [[Huberman — How to Control Your Cortisol and Overcome Burnout]]). See [[Cortisol Complete Guide]].

Ranked by evidence × effect size.

---

## S-Tier — biggest, best-supported levers

### 😴 Protect sleep + cut evening light
Sleep is the master regulator of the rhythm. In the evening, **dim overhead/bright lights** (use low, warm/red light) to avoid late cortisol spikes that wreck sleep onset (Source: [[Huberman — How to Control Your Cortisol and Overcome Burnout]]). Poor sleep → elevated next-day cortisol → worse sleep (the loop to break first).

### 🧘 Mind-body exercise (yoga / qigong) — the standout in RCTs
A 2025 network meta-analysis (44 RCTs, 3,284 people in psychological distress) ranked modalities for *lowering* cortisol: **yoga #1** (SUCRA 93%, SMD −0.59), **qigong #2** (73%), then multicomponent exercise. **Moderate intensity (3–6 METs) beat high intensity; HIIT tended to *raise* cortisol** (Source: [[Li Huang Zhu 2025 — Exercise for Cortisol Network Meta-analysis]]).
- **Dose:** 30–60 min sessions, **>3×/week**, ~530 MET-min/week (inverted-U — more is not better).

### 🌬️ Slow breathing / NSDR / meditation
Deliberate slow, exhale-emphasized breathing and Non-Sleep Deep Rest (NSDR/Yoga Nidra) down-shift the stress response and lower cortisol; Huberman recommends NSDR specifically for early-phase (high-cortisol) burnout (Source: [[Huberman — How to Control Your Cortisol and Overcome Burnout]]). A long exhale is the fastest acute lever you carry everywhere.

## A-Tier — strong supplement & lifestyle levers

### 🌿 Ashwagandha (take in the evening)
The best-evidenced cortisol-lowering supplement. Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs (873 participants): **significant cortisol reduction at 8 weeks**; landmark trials show ~**11–32% serum cortisol reduction** (one classic trial: 300 mg root extract 2×/day × 60 days → ~28% drop vs ~8% placebo) (Source: [[Bachour et al 2025 — Ashwagandha Cortisol Meta-analysis]]).
- **Dose/timing:** **300–600 mg/day**, late afternoon → night (it can be sedating; that's the point). Huberman cautions against taking it *continuously* long-term — cycle it.
- *Caveat:* it reliably lowers the cortisol *number*; effect on *perceived* stress is less consistent across analyses.

### 🐟 Omega-3 (fish oil)
An RCT in midlife adults: **2.5 g/day** omega-3 (mostly EPA) → **~19% lower cortisol** during a lab stress test vs placebo. Cheap, foundational, low-risk. See [[Foundational Health Supplements]].

### 🔥 Sauna / hot bath
A **~20 min sauna at 80–100 °C** or a hot bath lowers cortisol (and aids evening wind-down) (Source: [[Huberman — How to Control Your Cortisol and Overcome Burnout]]).

### 🌱 Rhodiola rosea
A double-blind RCT found rhodiola reduced the cortisol response to awakening stress and improved burnout/attention in chronic-fatigue participants. Situational adaptogen. See [[Rhodiola Rosea]].

## B-Tier — supportive / smaller

### 🥗 Diet pattern
An 18-month trial found a **polyphenol-rich Mediterranean diet** favorably lowered fasting morning cortisol. Note the flip side: **very low-carb diets can *raise* cortisol** in some men — a modest evening starch can help the night drop (Source: [[Huberman — How to Control Your Cortisol and Overcome Burnout]]).

### 🤝 Social connection & positive activity
Positive social contact and creative/enjoyable activity (e.g., art-making RCTs) reduce cortisol; chronic isolation/stress raises it. Magnesium and other calming supports (apigenin, L-theanine) help sleep, which indirectly lowers cortisol — see [[Sleep Optimization Supplements]].

### ☕ Cut afternoon/evening caffeine
Caffeine raises cortisol for hours; an afternoon dose keeps the evening number elevated. See [[Caffeine Timing Protocol]].

## What to AVOID (raises cortisol)
- Chronic short sleep / erratic wake times
- Bright overhead light at night
- Late-evening intense exercise / HIIT
- Afternoon–evening caffeine
- Chronic psychological stress with no recovery (suppresses testosterone too → [[Cortisol and Testosterone Suppression]])
- Alcohol close to bed (fragments sleep → higher cortisol)

---

## Quick Protocol (chronically high / poor sleep)
1. Fix sleep timing; dim warm light after sunset.
2. Yoga/qigong or moderate movement, 30–60 min, 3–4×/week (not late, not HIIT-only).
3. Slow-breathing or NSDR daily; one long exhale set when acutely stressed.
4. Ashwagandha 300–600 mg in the evening (cycle it) + omega-3 2.5 g/day.
5. No caffeine after early afternoon; no alcohol near bed.
6. Optional: evening sauna/hot bath; small evening starch if low-carb and wired at night.

> [!note] If symptoms suggest true **Cushing syndrome** (purple stretch marks, central weight gain, muscle wasting, new diabetes/hypertension), that's a medical evaluation — not a lifestyle fix. See [[Cortisol Complete Guide]].
