# Jealousy as a Compass

[[Mel Robbins|Robbins']] reframe of envy: instead of suppressing jealousy or feeling ashamed of it, **read it as data.** Jealousy is a signal pointing directly at something you secretly want but haven't admitted or pursued.

The move is: *let yourself feel it* ([[Let Them]] applied inward — let the feeling exist), then *let me* use it as direction — what does this envy reveal about my own goals? Comparison only damages you when you use it to feel small; used as a compass, the same emotion becomes a clue about where to aim next.

*Source: [[The Let Them Theory - Mel Robbins]].*
