# Component Meal Prep Method

A batch-cooking strategy: instead of assembling complete meals in advance, you **cook each component in bulk separately**, store them apart, and assemble different bowls on demand. It solves the two failure modes of meal prep — boredom and soggy texture. (Source: [[Component Meal Prep Bowls — Make-Ahead Method]])

## The Bowl Formula

```
Carb base + Protein + 1–2 cups vegetables + Sauce + Crunchy topper
```

- **Carb base:** rice, potato, sweet potato, quinoa.
- **Protein:** ground beef, salmon, white fish, eggs (batch 2–3 kinds).
- **Vegetables:** one big roasted sheet pan covers the week.
- **Sauce:** the variety lever — see below.
- **Topper:** toasted seeds, crushed nuts, herbs, avocado, citrus zest.

## Why It Works

1. **Sauce defeats monotony.** The same rice + beef + broccoli is a different meal with teriyaki vs. tahini vs. chimichurri. Keep 2–3 sauces ready and the repetition problem disappears. (Source: [[Component Meal Prep Bowls — Make-Ahead Method]])
2. **Separate storage extends freshness.** Keeping hot components (grains, protein) apart from fresh greens and wet toppings pushes usable quality from ~2–3 days to **4–5 days**. Assemble at mealtime for best texture and temperature.
3. **Assembly is <5 minutes** once components exist.

## Prep-Day Sequence

Run oven + stove + rice cooker in parallel:
1. Oven 425°F — sheet pan of starch (potato/sweet potato) + sheet pan of mixed veg, ~25–30 min.
2. Rice cooker — big batch of grain.
3. Pan 1 — brown ground beef with **neutral** seasoning (so it fits any cuisine).
4. Pan 2 — fish last (cooks fast, eat first).
5. Hard-boil eggs in any free pot.
6. Cool, portion, refrigerate days 1–4, **freeze the rest flat**.
