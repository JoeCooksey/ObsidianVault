# Gut Health Tests

How to actually measure gut health — ranked by **validity**, because the consumer market is full of expensive low-value panels. The honest summary: a few clinical tests are solid, microbiome sequencing is descriptive-not-diagnostic, and the popular "leaky gut" blood test is invalid.

## Tier 1 — Validated Clinical Tests (worth it when indicated)

| Test | What it measures | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **Fecal calprotectin** | GI inflammation; the noninvasive "test of choice" to distinguish IBS from IBD | High value — order through a doctor if you have alarm symptoms |
| **Lactulose:mannitol ratio (LMR)** | *Functional* intestinal permeability (drink 2 sugars, measure urine ratio) | Best validated permeability test. Healthy ~0.014, untreated coeliac ~0.133 (~10×); AUC 0.88 for coeliac. (Source: [[Lactulose-Mannitol Meta-analysis 2022]]) |
| **Fecal elastase** | Pancreatic exocrine function (maldigestion) | Validated for its specific purpose |
| **Coeliac serology (tTG-IgA) + endoscopy** | Coeliac disease | Gold standard if coeliac suspected |
| **Hydrogen/methane breath test** | SIBO, lactose/fructose malabsorption | Useful, some false positives |

## Tier 2 — Descriptive, Not Diagnostic

| Test | Reality |
|---|---|
| **Stool microbiome sequencing** (16S or shotgun metagenomics) | Genuinely shows *what's there* and diversity metrics — useful for research and curiosity. But there is **no validated "healthy reference"** to diagnose against, results vary by lab/method, and it can't yet prescribe a treatment. Interesting, not actionable for most. |
| **Secretory IgA (sIgA, stool)** | Reflects mucosal immunity; influenced by many factors; weak standalone signal |
| **SCFA stool panels** | Plausible marker of fermentation; not standardized for clinical decisions |

## Tier 3 — Avoid / Low Value

| Test | Why skip |
|---|---|
| **Zonulin ELISA (blood or stool)** | The headline "leaky gut" test — and it's **broken**. Commercial kits measure "unknown proteins, not zonulin," and correlate R≈0.03–0.17 with real permeability (Source: [[Zonulin Biomarker Critique 2021]]). Don't pay for it. |
| **IgG "food sensitivity" panels** | IgG indicates *exposure/tolerance*, not allergy or intolerance; flagged as non-validated by allergy societies |
| **At-home "gut health score" apps with supplement upsells** | Marketing wrapper around Tier-2 sequencing; conflict of interest |

## DIY / Free Signals (no lab needed)
- **Bristol Stool Chart** — type 3–4 is the target; tracks transit/consistency
- **Symptom + food journal** — cheapest way to find personal triggers
- **Transit time** (corn/sesame/blue-dye marker test) — ~12–48h is healthy
- **Plant-diversity count** — track toward 30/week (a proxy for likely diversity)

## Decision Guide
> [!tip] What to actually do
> - **No alarm symptoms:** skip lab tests. Track Bristol type, a food journal, and plant diversity. Optimize diet/lifestyle first.
> - **Persistent GI symptoms, blood, weight loss, anemia:** see a doctor for **calprotectin + coeliac serology** — do not self-diagnose with a consumer panel.
> - **Curiosity / quantified-self:** microbiome sequencing is fine as a snapshot — just know it can't (yet) tell you what to fix.
