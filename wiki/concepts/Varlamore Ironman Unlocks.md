# Varlamore Ironman Unlocks

The Varlamore expansion (2024–2025, three parts) patched the three historic pain points of ironman progression: prayer training cost, mid-game gear drought, and supply generation. Classic Oziris-style routes predate it; any modern route should weave these in (Source: [[Ironman Meta — Varlamore Guide]]).

## The four systems

**Hunter rumours** — from 46 Hunter (Novice → 57 Adept → 72 Expert → 91 Master, Master needs At First Light). Passive food, moonlight moths (prayer restore), blessed bone shards. At 72: sunlight antelopes + the Hunters' sunlight crossbow (74 Fletching to craft), "best 3-tick ranged weapon in the game." Slots into the early game alongside birdhouses.

**Blessed bone shards** — prayer training without the Wilderness or huge GP. 30 Prayer minimum; 5 XP/shard with blessed wine, 6 with sunfire wine at the Teomat. Shard sources: hunter rumours, calcified deposits (41 Mining, AFK), chiseling bones. This is now the default iron path to 43 → 70+ Prayer.

**Moons of Peril** — mid-game gear pipeline (75+ combat, 70+ Prayer recommended). Three bosses, three full sets:
- Blood Moon → Bloodrager (melee) — slayer workhorse, "stepping stone to Bandos"
- Blue Moon → Frostweaver (magic)
- Eclipse Moon → Eclipse (ranged)

Better drop rates than Barrows; fills "the awkward phase between quest gear and Barrows/Slayer drops."

**Fortis Colosseum** — late-game capstone (Children of the Sun to enter). Sunfire fanatic armour from Wave 4+; Dizana's quiver (BiS ranged back slot) + echo crystal on completion. Wave 12 wants ~95 Atk/Str, 90 Def, 94 Magic.
