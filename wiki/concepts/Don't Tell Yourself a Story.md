# Don't Tell Yourself a Story

[[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]]'s caution against the **narrative of destiny** that ego builds after a few wins. Success tempts you to construct a tidy story in which your genius and inevitability explain the outcome — erasing the role of luck, timing, and other people.

The danger is that the story blinds you: once you "know" why you won, you stop observing reality accurately, stop adapting, and start defending the myth. Holiday's antidote is to stay grounded in facts and process rather than self-mythology — to keep doing the work instead of narrating your own legend.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Success."*
