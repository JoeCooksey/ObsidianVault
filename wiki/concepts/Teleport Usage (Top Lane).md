# Teleport Usage (Top Lane)

Teleport is the top laner's defining macro tool — a **global tempo lever**. The rule: TP only when it *fixes your wave* or *turns a guaranteed numbers advantage*. Reactive, hopeful TPs to fights already lost waste the spell plus the two waves you miss. (Sources: [[Boosteria — Top Lane Fundamentals Guide]], [[EloAscend — Top Lane Guide 2026]])

## The 3-Question Check (before any play TP)

1. **Is the fight already even with no one low?** → Don't TP. Your arrival must change the outcome, not just join it.
2. **Does your arrival flip the numbers?** 2v3 → 3v3 with full HP = yes. 1v4 → 2v4 = no.
3. **Can the enemy top punish your absence?** If you lose 2+ plates or a tower for the play, the trade is usually negative.

(Source: [[EloAscend — Top Lane Guide 2026]])

## TP by Game Phase

| Phase | Use |
|---|---|
| **Early (0–10 min)** | Defensive: return to lane after a crash to avoid losing waves, hold a freeze via crash → recall → TP back |
| **Mid (10–20 min)** | The prime window: Dragon/Herald fights, cross-map plays, countering enemy TP |
| **Late (20+ min)** | Teamfight flanks and Baron plays; a TP from an unexpected angle creates the numbers advantage before the enemy can react |

## Specific Patterns

- **Freeze-hold TP**: crash the wave, recall, TP back to lane — the freeze stays intact and the enemy keeps bleeding CS. (Source: [[Boosteria — Top Lane Fundamentals Guide]])
- **Bot-timer sync**: if your slow push crashes in ~25 s and bot is setting up a dive, hold TP to counter or match the enemy top's TP to force a 4v4.
- **Objective TP**: only if your wave won't punish you for leaving, or you create a cross-map trade (they get a wave, you get a Dragon).

## The Cardinal Sins

- TPing to a fight that is already lost ("ego TP")
- Burning TP for a kill that gains less than the waves it costs
- Holding TP so long it never gets used — an unused summoner is also a waste
