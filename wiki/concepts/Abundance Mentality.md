# Abundance Mentality

Covey's term for the belief that there is enough success, recognition, and opportunity for everyone — the opposite of the **Scarcity Mentality** (zero-sum, I-win-means-you-lose thinking endemic in competitive environments); abundance thinking is the psychological prerequisite for genuine Win-Win negotiation and generosity-first relationships.
