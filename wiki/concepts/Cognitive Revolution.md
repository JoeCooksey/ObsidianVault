# Cognitive Revolution

The first of [[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] four revolutions, ~70,000 years ago: a change in Sapiens' cognition (likely a genetic mutation rewiring the brain) that produced **[[Fictive Language|fictive language]]** — the ability to talk about things that don't physically exist.

This let Sapiens gossip, plan, and above all believe in [[Shared Fictions|shared fictions]], enabling cooperation in unlimited numbers. It is why Sapiens, rather than the stronger Neanderthals or other human species ([[The Other Humans]]), came to dominate. The Cognitive Revolution is effectively the start of history as distinct from biology.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 1.*
