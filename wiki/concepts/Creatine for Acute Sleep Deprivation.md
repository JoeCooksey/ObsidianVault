# Creatine for Acute Sleep Deprivation

A high **bolus dose of creatine (10–20 g)** is proposed to blunt the cognitive effects of *acute* sleep deprivation — i.e. as a rescue strategy after a bad night, not a nightly sleep aid. The mechanism is thought to relate to creatine supporting brain energy (phosphocreatine) buffering when sleep loss has depleted it. This is distinct from creatine's day-to-day cognitive role covered in [[Creatine for Cognitive Performance]].

> [!caution] GI side effects at high doses
> Bolus doses of 10–20 g can wreak havoc on the gut and cause loose stools, even in people with normally good digestion — see [[Bristol Stool Scale]]. Not harmful, but uncomfortable. Use sparingly, on the specific bad-sleep day rather than routinely.

This sits in the supplement layer of the [[Sleepmaxxing Protocol]] as a situational tool, alongside the nightly stack in [[Sleep Optimization Supplements]].
