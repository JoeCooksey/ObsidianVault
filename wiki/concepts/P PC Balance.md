# P/PC Balance

Covey's Production / Production Capability balance: sustainable effectiveness requires investing in your capacity (the goose — your health, skills, relationships, tools) at least as much as you harvest output (the golden eggs) — killing the goose for short-term gain destroys long-term effectiveness.
