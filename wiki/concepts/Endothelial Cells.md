# Endothelial Cells

The single-cell-thick lining of every blood vessel — collectively one of the largest "organs" in the body (~hundreds of m² of surface). They control vascular tone, clotting, inflammation, and barrier function. **Ranked #4** in [[Research - Most Important Cells in the Human Body]].

## Why they matter
- **Master switch for cardiovascular health.** Endothelial dysfunction is the *earliest* and an independent predictor of cardiovascular disease — the #1 cause of death globally. (Source: [[Moreau 2023 — Exercise and Vascular Function with Aging]], confidence high)
- They produce **nitric oxide (NO)**, the key vasodilator that keeps arteries flexible and blood pressure healthy.
- They gate delivery of oxygen and nutrients to all other cells, including [[Neurons]] and [[Cardiomyocytes]].

## How to optimize them (actionable)
1. **Aerobic exercise** — blood-flow shear stress is the primary physiological stimulus for NO production; preserves endothelial function and reduces arterial stiffness. (Source: [[Moreau 2023 — Exercise and Vascular Function with Aging]], confidence high)
2. **Dietary nitrates** — leafy greens and beetroot raise NO availability (food-first).
3. **Don't smoke / avoid vaping** — directly destroys endothelial NO signaling.
4. **Control blood pressure and blood glucose** — both injure the endothelium mechanically and metabolically.
5. **Reduce oxidative stress + inflammation** — the upstream cause of NO loss; addressed by exercise, sleep, and a polyphenol-rich diet.

> [!gap] Aerobic-exercise endothelial benefits are blunted in estrogen-deficient postmenopausal women (Moreau 2023) — one protocol does not fit all.
