# Rejection-Then-Retreat

A compliance technique (also called the **door-in-the-face**) that weaponizes [[Reciprocity]]: make a large request you expect to be refused, then "retreat" to a smaller request — the one you wanted all along.

Because you appear to have made a concession, the target feels obligated to concede in return by saying yes to the smaller ask. [[Robert B. Cialdini|Cialdini]] found it not only boosts compliance but also increases the target's *satisfaction* and sense of responsibility for the outcome, making them more likely to follow through. It is the mirror image of [[Foot-in-the-Door]] (which starts small and grows).

*Source: [[Influence - Robert Cialdini]].*
