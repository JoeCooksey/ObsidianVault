# Maturity Continuum

Covey's three-stage model of human development: **Dependence** (you take care of me) → **Independence** (I can do it myself) → **Interdependence** (we can do it together) — most self-help stops at independence, but Covey argues that the highest effectiveness requires moving all the way to interdependence, which is where Habits 4–6 operate.
