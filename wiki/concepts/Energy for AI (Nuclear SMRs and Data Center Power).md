# Energy for AI (Nuclear, SMRs, and Data Center Power)

The binding constraint on AI in 2026 is **not chips — it's power**. Data centers powering AI are driving a surge in electricity demand, reviving **nuclear** and pulling massive investment into **power delivery**. This is the bottom layer of [[AI Five-Layer Cake|Jensen Huang's five-layer cake]] (energy → chips → platform → models → applications). (Source: search synthesis — Nature / Stanford SETR, confidence: medium)

## The 2026 Picture

(Source: search synthesis, confidence: medium)

- **Small Modular Reactors (SMRs)** are being developed rapidly specifically to feed AI data-center demand.
- **Nuclear fusion** progress is bringing abundant energy "closer," though not yet commercial.
- **Advanced batteries** — CATL deploying **sodium-ion** in 2026 across swap systems, vehicles, and grid storage, projecting sodium-ion + lithium-ion developing in parallel.
- Nature flagged **"AI and nuclear energy"** together as agenda-setting for 2026.

## Why It's An EE / Power-Electronics Story

The whole chain from reactor/grid to GPU is **power conversion** — Joe's declared specialty:

- **Grid → data center** — medium-voltage AC/DC, [[Silicon Carbide Power Electronics|SiC]]-based rectification.
- **Inside the rack** — 48V bus + [[Gallium Nitride Power Electronics|GaN]] VRMs feeding the chips (already a recurring [[EE Daily — June 02, 2026|EE Daily]] topic).
- **Storage** — sodium/lithium battery management, bidirectional conversion ([[V2G Bidirectional Charging]]).
