# Circle of Concern vs Circle of Influence

Covey's framework from Habit 1 (Be Proactive): your **Circle of Concern** contains everything you worry about (news, others' behavior, circumstances outside your control), while your **Circle of Influence** contains only what you can actually affect through direct action — and proactive people invest their energy exclusively in the latter, which gradually expands it.
