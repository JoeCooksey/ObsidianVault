# OSRS High GP-Per-Hour Money Makers (Active)

When you want maximum GP and are willing to give the game full attention, active PvM and trading dominate. These require gear, stats, and mechanical skill, but the ceiling is far above [[OSRS AFK Money-Making Methods|AFK methods]].

## Bossing (highest hourly)
| Method | GP/hr | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Theatre of Blood (raid) | very high | Scythe of Vitur / Justiciar drops worth hundreds of millions |
| Nex (group) | 12M–16M | Duo/trio, ~5–7 kills/hr |
| Phantom Muspah (solo) | 1.5M–5M | 85+ Slayer; Venator Shard / Ancient Icon spikes |
| Vorkath (solo) | up to 4.5M | Reliable; Dragon Hunter Lance/Crossbow |

(Source: [[Best GP Per Hour OSRS 2025]])

## Skilling (active, high)
- **Runecrafting blood/soul runes** — 1.5M–2M GP/hr (77 RC blood / 90 RC soul).
- **Runecrafting lava runes** — ~1.2M–1.8M GP/hr (82 RC, 23 Magic, Binding Necklace).

## Trading
- **Grand Exchange flipping** — scales with cash stack: ~1M/hr on 10M, ~3M–4M/hr on 100M. No combat or stats required; capital-gated, not skill-gated.

## Key trade-off
The strongest hourlies (raids, Nex) carry **learning curves and death risk**; Vorkath/Muspah are the usual "reliable solo" picks once base-90 combat and [[OSRS Mid-Game Progression Goals|mid-game gear unlocks]] are done.

> [!note] Top-end hourlies assume near-maxed stats and best-in-slot gear; mid-game accounts realise a fraction of these rates.
