# EE Career Fields by Degree Level

Which electrical engineering subfields need a bachelor's, reward a master's, or genuinely require a PhD. **Rule of thumb: most fields enter at the BS; the MS specializes; the PhD is for research.**

## Bachelor's Is Enough (Entry)

Most EE practice is open at the BS level — the master's *advances* rather than *gates* these:

- **Power systems / energy** — generation, transmission, distribution, renewables; utilities. Most jobs require only a BS. (Source: [[Source - Research.com EE Degree Levels and ROI]])
- **Communications & signal processing** — digital/analog comms, data transmission, networks.
- **Control systems & robotics** — automation, control theory, manufacturing/aerospace/automotive control.
- **Embedded systems** — entry at BS; deepened at MS.

## Master's Specializes (Career Advancement)

A master's takes you from generalist to specialist and unlocks lead/R&D roles. High-return concentrations:

- Power electronics & wide-bandgap (SiC/GaN) devices
- Semiconductor / solid-state device design
- Signal processing, machine learning, AI hardware
- Embedded systems, RF/microwave circuits

~72% of MS grads go directly to industry (design, microelectronics/semiconductors, application-specific HW/SW, CAD tools for digital/aerospace/defense). (Source: [[Source - Research.com EE Degree Levels and ROI]]) `confidence: high`

## PhD Is Needed or Strongly Rewarded (Research)

A doctorate is genuinely required, or a major advantage, only in research-heavy areas:

- **Semiconductor / device physics & nanoelectronics** — fundamental device behavior, fabrication research.
- **Photonics / optoelectronics & RF photonics** — optical signal processing, integrated photonics.
- **Machine learning / AI hardware research** — novel architectures and systems.
- **Academia** — tenure-track faculty (non-negotiable).
- **Research scientist** — national labs (e.g. LLNL/Sandia research tracks), corporate R&D labs.

~28% of MS grads continue to a PhD; the rest go to industry. (Source: [[Source - Research.com EE Degree Levels and ROI]]) `confidence: high`

## Highest-Paying Specializations (Any Level)

Aerospace product/parts manufacturing pays top (~$136,570 median); power systems, semiconductor design, communications, and control engineering offer the strongest returns. EE overall median: $111,910 (May 2024), 9% projected growth 2023–2033. (Source: [[Source - edX Is a Master's in EE Worth It]])
