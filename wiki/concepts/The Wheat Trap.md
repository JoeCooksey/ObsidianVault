# The Wheat Trap

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] signature inversion of the [[Agricultural Revolution]]: **"We did not domesticate wheat. Wheat domesticated us."**

A handful of plant species (wheat, rice, potatoes) spread across the globe by getting humans to clear land, haul water, weed, guard, and tend them — backbreaking labor that bodies evolved for foraging were not built for, producing slipped discs, arthritis, and hernias. In exchange, farming yielded more calories per acre, so populations exploded and there was no going back. The species "succeeded" (more humans, more wheat) while individual humans worked harder, ate worse, and lived in denser, disease-prone, hierarchical settlements. The wheat trap is the prime example of why, for Harari, evolutionary success and individual well-being can move in opposite directions.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 2.*
