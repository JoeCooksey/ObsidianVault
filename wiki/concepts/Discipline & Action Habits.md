# Discipline & Action Habits

Habits 1–20 from [[101 Habits — Dan Zakaria]]. The foundation: act regardless of mood, close loops, and build the identity of someone who follows through.

## The Habits
1. **Make your bed** every single morning.
2. **Wake & sleep at the same time** every day.
3. **Take a cold shower occasionally** — stimulates blood flow, "makes you feel alive."
4. **Do the hardest task first.**
5. **2-minute rule** (David Allen / GTD): if it takes <2 min, do it now.
6. **Finish what you start** — unfinished small things pile up mentally.
7. **Keep promises to yourself** — they matter more than promises to others.
8. **Set a daily non-negotiable** (his: community, meditation, journaling, reading, gratitude, gym, 1–2 deep-work sessions).
9. **Stop snoozing** — either sleep for real or get straight up.
10. **Track your habits** — "if it's measured, you can optimize it."
11. **Plan tomorrow the night before** (a few main things + when).
12. **Set tight deadlines** for yourself to force focus.
13. **Delayed gratification** — work, then reward. Always earn your vices.
14. **Work even when unmotivated** — don't tie emotions to work.
15. **Reduce decision fatigue** — plan first, then act; don't think and do at once.
16. **Keep your environment clean** — it reflects your internal state.
17. **Avoid excuses / take responsibility.**
18. **Show up consistently** — "consistency is key."
19. **Do one uncomfortable thing daily** — rewires the reward system so everything else feels easier.
20. **Discipline over motivation** — "motivation is temporary, discipline is forever"; discipline is tied to identity.
