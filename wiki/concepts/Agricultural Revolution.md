# Agricultural Revolution

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] second revolution, ~12,000 years ago: the shift from foraging to farming. Famously, he calls it **"history's biggest fraud"** — farming multiplied the *species* (more food → more people) while making the average individual's life harder: monotonous labor, worse diets, disease, and social hierarchy.

Key associated ideas: **[[The Wheat Trap]]** ("we didn't domesticate wheat; wheat domesticated us"), the **[[Luxury Trap]]** (conveniences silently become necessities), and the rise of large-scale **[[Imagined Orders]]** and **[[Writing and Numbers]]** needed to run agrarian societies. The revolution inverts the usual progress narrative: the metric that improved (population) is not the metric that mattered to a single human life.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 2.*
