# Religion as Unifier

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] third great unifying force: religion gives **superhuman legitimacy to fragile human laws and norms.** By rooting social rules in a transcendent source ("the gods command it," "it is sacred"), religion makes [[Imagined Orders|imagined orders]] feel absolute and binding rather than arbitrary, allowing large populations to share values and cooperate.

Crucially, Harari extends "religion" beyond gods to any system that confers universal, superhuman authority on a set of norms — including modern ideologies like **[[Humanism]]**, liberalism, communism, and capitalism, which he treats as functionally religious. This is one of the three forces (with [[Money as Trust|money]] and [[Empire]]) behind the [[Unification of Humankind]].

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 3.*
