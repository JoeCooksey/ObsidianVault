# Jungle Tracking for Laners

Knowing the enemy jungler's position (or probable position) is what licenses every aggressive decision in lane. Top lane is the longest lane with the fewest escape routes — the top laner who tracks the jungler trades freely; the one who doesn't plays scared or dies. (Source: [[Boosteria — Jungle Pathing Basics]])

## Read the Start

- **Leash read**: if the enemy top laner arrives late or has burned mana, the jungler started topside. Late bot laners = botside start.
- Most junglers path toward their **strong-side lane** or full-clear toward the opposite buff. Knowing the start side predicts the whole first clear.

## Count Camps via CS

Each jungle camp ≈ 4 CS. Check the scoreboard:
- **~12 CS at 2:45** = three-camp clear → level 3 → **gank window open now**
- **~24 CS at 3:30** = full clear → heading to scuttle/gank with level 4

(Source: [[Boosteria — Jungle Pathing Basics]] via search aggregate; medium confidence)

## The 3:00 Ward

Level 3 (≈2:45–3:00) is the earliest real gank timing, and it's exactly when most players are still trading carelessly. **Ward river/tri-brush at 3:00 every game.** If the jungler started topside, the ward matters even earlier. (Source: [[EloAscend — Top Lane Guide 2026]])

## Absence Is Information

If the enemy jungler hasn't shown anywhere for 30+ seconds and your lane is pushing with no vision: **assume the gank**. Prolonged invisibility is a threat signal, not safety. Track enemy summoners/ultimates too — a jungler whose ult is down rarely forces a play. (Source: [[Boosteria — Jungle Pathing Basics]])

## Defaults When Blind

- Wave pushing toward you + no vision = safe to be aggressive (gank must run past your tower)
- You pushing + no vision = the danger state; tether trades short, hold escape tools
- Lean toward the warded side of the lane
