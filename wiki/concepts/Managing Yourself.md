# Managing Yourself

[[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]]'s point that **scale comes from self-management, not just talent.** As responsibility grows, the binding constraint becomes your own discipline, organization, and restraint — your ability to manage your time, ego, finances, and relationships rather than letting success run away with them.

He cites figures (like the disciplined habits of generals and executives) who stayed sober, organized, and humble precisely when their power would have justified indulgence. Self-management is unglamorous and invisible, which is why ego neglects it — and why neglecting it is the common cause of post-success collapse.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Success."*
