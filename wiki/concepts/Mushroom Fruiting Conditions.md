# Mushroom Fruiting Conditions

**Fruiting** is the stage where fully colonized substrate switches from vegetative growth to producing mushrooms (fruit bodies). It is triggered by changing four environmental variables: **fresh air exchange, humidity, light, and temperature** (Source: [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]]).

## The four triggers

| Variable | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Fresh air exchange (FAE)** | Several exchanges/day | Colonization builds up CO₂; mushrooms need lower CO₂ / more O₂ to form caps instead of stalks |
| **Humidity** | ~90–95% at substrate surface | Fruit bodies are mostly water; low humidity aborts pins and dries caps |
| **Light** | 12–16 h/day indirect | A signal (not for photosynthesis); 5000–6500K LED or indirect daylight; orients growth |
| **Temperature** | 60–75°F (15–24°C) | Cooler than colonization; species-dependent |

(Sources: [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]], [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]])

## Setting up fruiting in an AIO bag

1. **Cut the bag open** a few inches above the substrate surface, then roll or fold the top down to create a "hood."
2. **Mist** the inside walls of the hood lightly **once per day** — mist the air and walls, not directly onto the substrate (oversaturation invites bacteria and mold).
3. **Create FAE**: fold the top loosely and hold it with a paper clip so air exchanges slowly, or open and fan the hood a few times a day. The fold-and-clip makes a passive **humidity dome** while still breathing.
4. **Provide light**: 12–16 h/day of indirect light above the bag.
5. **Hold temperature** in the species' fruiting band (60–75°F for most).

(Sources: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]], [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]])

## Stages you'll see

- **Pinning**: tiny mushroom primordia ("pins") appear, usually within days of opening.
- **Growth**: pins swell into full mushrooms over ~3–7 days.
- **Harvest**: pick when the **veil under the cap just begins to tear** — peak maturity, before spores drop. Twist gently at the base or cut with a sanitized blade close to the substrate.

(Source: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]])

## Troubleshooting fruiting

- **Stalled pins / no pins on top**: usually under-misting or poor FAE. Mist around the cut 2–3×/day and tent with a clear dome.
- **Long, leggy stems with small caps**: too much CO₂ → increase FAE.
- **Side/bottom pins only**: microclimate issue; the top is too dry or too still.

(Source: [[Zombie Myco — Revitalization (Rehydration) TEK]])
