# Laning Fundamentals (LoL)

The first ~14 minutes: last-hitting, trading, and wave control. Lanes are decided less by all-ins than by **repeated small trades that remove the opponent's ability to contest the wave**. (Source: [[Boosteria — LoL CSing Fundamentals]])

## CS Benchmarks
- Pros: 8–10 CS/min. Low elo average: 5–6 CS/min. Practical target: **~80 CS by 10 minutes**. (confidence: medium — multiple guide sources)
- Track **CS@10 and CS@15** — the same metrics pro analysts use to evaluate laners.
- Habit rule: stack certainty — take the guaranteed last hit over a speculative poke when you don't have priority.

## Last-Hit Mechanics
- Wind-up timing: start the auto slightly before the minion reaches kill range.
- Calibrate your auto damage at levels 1, 3, 5 for each item start.
- Settings: **Attack Move on Cursor** + **Target Champions Only** to prevent misclicks.
- Orbwalking (auto → step → auto) for spacing while farming.

## Trading Templates (by archetype)
1. **Ranged bully** (e.g. Caitlyn): auto the enemy when they go for a last hit, step back, repeat.
2. **Melee punish** (e.g. Darius): posture in your minions; engage the moment they step up for CS.
3. **All-in threat** (e.g. Zed): stack the wave, time the all-in to cannon timing.
4. **Support engage** (e.g. Nautilus): only when your ADC can follow and the wave favors you.

**Minion advantage rule:** trading with 6 minions vs their 3 borrows DPS from your wave — wave state is part of every trade calculation. Won trades convert to wave control, which is often worth more than the HP damage. (Source: [[Boosteria — LoL CSing Fundamentals]])

## Practice Drills
Practice Tool routine: 10-min no-pressure CS test · full wave under tower 10× consecutively · 30 cannons in a row · hold a freeze 2 full waves. Daily 20-min block: 5 min last-hit (track CS@5) → 5 min under-tower → 5 min freeze → 5 min reviewing one CS loss. (Source: [[Boosteria — LoL CSing Fundamentals]])
