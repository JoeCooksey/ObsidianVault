# Healthy Dopamine Protocols

Practical, evidence-supported ways to keep a **high, stable dopamine baseline** with intact receptor sensitivity. The goal is never to maximize peaks — it is to **raise the floor** (tonic dopamine) and ration the spikes (Source: [[Huberman Lab Episode 39 — Controlling Your Dopamine]]).

---

## Daily Baseline Builders (🟢)

| Protocol | Dose | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning sunlight** | 5–10 min outdoors, early | Triggers dopamine release; anchors the circadian dopamine rhythm |
| **Cold exposure** | cold shower / ~14 °C plunge, 1–3 min | Dopamine **~2.5× baseline, sustained for hours, no crash** (Source: [[Sramek 2000 — Cold Water Immersion and Catecholamines]]) |
| **Exercise** | most days; intensity you enjoy | ~2× during; long-term **D2 receptor upregulation** and higher baseline |
| **Music** | favorite tracks | Striatal dopamine on listening **and** anticipation |
| **Meditation** | 10–15 min | Raises dopamine-related activity; lowers cortisol that suppresses dopamine |
| **Social connection / flow** | daily | Sustainable, baseline-supporting reward |
| **Adequate protein** | tyrosine-rich foods | Supplies **tyrosine**, the dopamine precursor |
| **Sleep** | 7–9 h | Protects receptor density and baseline drive |

---

## The Rules of Use

1. **Don't stack.** Layering dopamine sources (phone + pre-workout + music + caffeine during a workout) spends tomorrow's motivation and makes the activity itself less rewarding. Do hard things *plain* (Source: [[Huberman Lab Episode 39 — Controlling Your Dopamine]]).
2. **Intermittent reward.** Don't reward yourself every time, and don't always reward to the same degree — randomize it. Constant predictable reward flattens the response ([[Reward Prediction Error]]).
3. **Earn your peaks.** A dopamine peak that follows effort sustains the baseline; a peak from passive consumption erodes it.
4. **Lean into the pain side.** Voluntary discomfort (cold, fasting, hard exercise) tips the [[Dopamine Baseline and the Pleasure-Pain Balance|balance]] toward pain first → it rebounds to durable, crash-free pleasure.
5. **Caffeine is a friend in moderation.** Unlike most stimulants it **upregulates** dopamine receptors → see [[Caffeine Complete Guide]] and [[Caffeine Cycling Protocol]].
6. **Phone discipline.** Keep the phone out of activities you want to keep enjoying (meals, workouts, conversations, sex).

---

## When the Baseline Is Already Low

If you're anhedonic / unmotivated, the fix isn't a bigger spike — it's **resting the biggest spike** for ~30 days while loading 🟢 builders. See [[Dopamine Detox (Evidence Review)]] and [[Dopamine Downregulation and Tolerance]].
