# Authority

[[Robert B. Cialdini|Cialdini's]] fifth principle: we obey credible authorities almost automatically — and, crucially, we often respond to mere **symbols** of authority rather than its substance. Three symbols reliably trigger deference: **titles**, **clothing** (uniforms, white coats, suits), and **trappings** (luxury cars, large offices) — all of which can be faked.

The principle is rooted in the [[Milgram Experiment|Milgram obedience studies]], where ordinary people delivered what they believed were dangerous shocks because an authority figure told them to. **Defense:** ask two questions — *"Is this person genuinely an expert on this matter?"* and *"How honest can I expect this expert to be here?"* (i.e., what is their incentive?).
