# Dichotomy of Control

The central [[Stoicism|Stoic]] distinction, from Epictetus: **some things are up to us and some are not.** Up to us are our judgments, intentions, and actions; not up to us are other people's choices, outcomes, reputation, the body, and external events. Suffering comes from investing emotional energy in the second category; peace comes from focusing effort on the first.

This is the philosophical backbone of Mel Robbins' *[[The Let Them Theory - Mel Robbins|Let Them Theory]]*: **[[Let Them]]** is the acceptance of what you can't control (other people), and **[[Let Me]]** is the redirection of energy to what you can (your own response). It also drives Ryan Holiday's counsel to do the work and release the need for recognition ([[The Effort Is Enough]]).
