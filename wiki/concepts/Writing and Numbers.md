# Writing and Numbers

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] account of why script and numerals were invented: not for poetry but for **bureaucracy** — tracking grain surpluses, taxes, debts, and property in agrarian states too large to run on memory alone. The earliest writing (Sumerian cuneiform) is accounting.

Writing let societies store information outside the human brain and manage [[Imagined Orders|imagined orders]] at scale. It also trained humans in **non-intuitive, categorical thinking** (catalogues, ledgers, partial scripts) that the foraging mind never needed. This administrative tooling is a direct consequence of the [[Agricultural Revolution]] and a prerequisite for empires and complex economies.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 2.*
