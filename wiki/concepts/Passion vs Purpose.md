# Passion vs Purpose

[[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]]'s warning that **passion is often unfocused intensity that substitutes for results.** The passionate person is loud, busy, and emotionally invested — but enthusiasm is not a plan, and it frequently masks a lack of realism and discipline.

He contrasts it with **purpose** (and its partner, realism): quiet, deliberate, directed action toward a defined end. "Passion" burns hot and flames out; purpose is what actually finishes things. The ego loves passion because it feels good and looks impressive; purpose is less glamorous but is what great work is actually built on.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Aspire."*
