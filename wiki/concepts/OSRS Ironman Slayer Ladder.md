# OSRS Ironman Slayer Ladder

Slayer is the spine of ironman mid-game: one skill that simultaneously trains all combat, generates GP and supplies, and gates the account's core weapons. Expect 100–200 hours from 58 to the low 90s (Source: [[Ironman Meta — Progression Guides]]).

## The ladder

| Slayer | Unlock | Why it matters |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| 58 | **Black mask** (Cave Horrors; needs Mos Le'Harmless quest) | +16.67% accuracy & damage on task → becomes slayer helm (i) |
| 75 | Gargoyles | GP + alchables + supplies engine |
| 85 | **Abyssal whip** (Abyssal demons) | primary melee weapon until raids |
| 87 | **Trident of the seas** (Kraken) | mandatory PvM magic weapon |
| 91 | **Primordial boots** (Cerberus) | BiS melee boots (+ pegasian/eternal crystals) |
| 93 | **Occult necklace** (Smoke Devils) | biggest single magic damage upgrade |
| 95 | **Dragon hunter lance** (Alchemical Hydra) | BiS vs dragons → Vorkath, DHL-meta bosses |

## Route notes

- Train on-task from ~58 onward; the black mask boost is why the route front-loads getting to 58 quickly (often via quest XP + bursting).
- Slayer points fund unlocks (block list, slayer ring craftable at 75 Crafting after Smoking Kills equivalent unlock).
- Blood Moon (Bloodrager) set from [[Varlamore Ironman Unlocks|Moons of Peril]] is a strong slayer melee set before Bandos.
