# AI Automation Agency

Build and maintain AI workflows/agents for businesses (lead routing, document flows, sales/support ops, internal assistants). Usually built on **n8n** (self-hosted, flat-rate) + LLM steps. The highest-ceiling *services* play because it converts one-off builds into recurring retainers. (Source: [[AI Automation Agency Pricing — Arsum 2026]])

## Pricing by scope

| Tier | Scope | Project fee | Retainer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | Single workflow (lead routing, doc flow) | $3K–$10K | $500–$1,500/mo |
| Mid-market | Department ops, multi-system, internal assistant | $10K–$35K | $1,500–$4,000/mo |
| Enterprise | Multi-step agents, regulated/compliance | $35K–$100K+ | $3,000–$8,000+/mo |

Retainers cover monitoring, bug fixes, prompt/workflow tuning, model swaps, expansions, reporting.

## Why it scales

- **n8n self-hosted is flat-rate** — same infra serves 5 clients or 50; startup tool cost < $200/mo.
- **5 clients × $3K–$5K/mo ≈ $15K–$25K/mo recurring**, decoupled from hours.
- Custom agents are **assets**: ~$3K–$10K build + $500–$2K/mo, ~90% margin.

## The real constraint

Not the tech — **niche selection + client acquisition + delivering measurable business results.** Generic "I automate stuff" loses to "I cut your support response time 60%." Specialize in one vertical (e.g. real-estate ops, clinic intake).
