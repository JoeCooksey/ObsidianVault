# GI Red Flag Symptoms

⚠️ The signs that are **never** "just gut imbalance" — alarm symptoms that functional disorders (IBS-type) cannot explain and that require a doctor, sometimes urgently (Source: [[IFFGD GI Alarm Symptoms]]).

## See a Doctor Promptly

| Red flag | Why |
|---|---|
| **Blood in stool** | Bright red on surface = likely anal source; mixed in = colon; **black/tarry (melena) = upper GI bleed — urgent** |
| **Vomiting blood** | Always structural: ulcer, varices, esophagitis, malignancy — urgent |
| **Unexplained weight loss** | 10+ lb over 3 months without trying → malignancy, IBD, or celiac until ruled out |
| **Difficulty swallowing (dysphagia)** | Suggests stricture, tumor, or achalasia — not functional |
| **Persistent fever with GI symptoms** | Functional disorders cause no inflammation; fever means something else |
| **Nighttime pain or diarrhea that wakes you** | Functional symptoms usually quiet at night |
| **Anemia / iron deficiency** | Implies occult GI blood loss until proven otherwise |
| **Persistent vomiting** | Obstruction, ulcer, or other structural cause |
| **New or sudden bowel-habit change after age 50** | Colorectal cancer screening trigger |
| **Palpable abdominal mass or swelling** | Structural by definition |

## Risk Modifiers (lower the bar for getting checked)

- Family history of **colorectal cancer** or **IBD**
- Symptoms persisting **>2 weeks** despite sensible diet/lifestyle changes
- Chest pain with GI symptoms — **rule out cardiac first**

## The Operating Rule

> [!tip] Two-track triage
> - **No red flags:** optimize diet and lifestyle first ([[Gut Optimization Actions Tier List]]); skip consumer testing.
> - **Any red flag:** doctor first — calprotectin + celiac serology are the validated starting tests ([[Gut Health Tests]]); a consumer microbiome panel cannot rule out cancer, IBD, or celiac.
