# Entrepreneurship and Innovation

The principles by which individuals create transformative new products, companies, and categories — synthesized across Isaacson's three subjects: Jobs's **product absolutism** (simplicity via subtraction, humanities × sciences intersection), Musk's **first-principles thinking** (physics as the only hard limit, The Algorithm's five-step engineering process), and Leonardo's **sfumato** (comfort with ambiguity as a prerequisite for creative breakthroughs); all three share a refusal to accept convention as a constraint.
