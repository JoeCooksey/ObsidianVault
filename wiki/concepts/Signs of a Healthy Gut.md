# Signs of a Healthy Gut

The positive checklist — what a well-functioning gut actually looks and feels like. A healthy gut is mostly **silent**: digestion happens without demanding attention.

## Bathroom Markers (the objective core)

| Marker | Healthy range |
|---|---|
| **Stool form** | [[Bristol Stool Scale]] type 3–4 — sausage-shaped, smooth or with surface cracks |
| **Frequency** | 3×/day to 3×/week — *consistency for YOU matters more than the number* |
| **Effort** | Easy passage; no straining, pain, or urgency; feeling of complete emptying |
| **Appearance** | Brown, formed; no blood, no mucus |
| **Transit time** | ~14–58 h healthy band; PREDICT 1 median 28.7 h (Source: [[Blue Poo Transit Time Study (PREDICT 1)]]) |
| **Rhythm** | Predictable pattern — regularity beats frequency |

> [!note] Frequency is the weakest of these signals — it correlates poorly with transit time, and transit time explains more microbiome variation than frequency or consistency (Source: [[Blue Poo Transit Time Study (PREDICT 1)]]).

## Digestion Markers

- **Minimal bloating and gas** after normal meals — some gas is normal and healthy (fermentation = the microbiome working), but it shouldn't cause pain, visible distension, or social distress
- **No frequent heartburn, reflux, or indigestion**
- **Comfortable eating across a varied diet** — fiber-rich and fermented foods tolerated without payback
- **Stable appetite** with normal fullness signals; no intense sugar cravings

## Systemic Markers (medium confidence — gut is one input among many)

- **Steady energy** through the day — no unexplained chronic fatigue
- **Clear-ish skin** — no persistent unexplained acne, eczema, or rosacea flares
- **Stable mood and mental clarity** — the gut produces/modulates serotonin and GABA precursors ([[Microbiota-Gut-Brain Axis Review]])
- **Solid sleep** — gut-derived signals feed circadian and sleep regulation
- **Robust immunity** — not catching every passing infection (~70% of immune tissue is gut-associated; [[Why Optimize Your Gut]])
- **Stable weight** without unexplained drift
- **Healthy mouth** — gums that don't bleed, few cavities; the oral microbiome is upstream of the gut (Source: [[Cleveland Clinic Dysbiosis Overview]])

## Under the Hood

A balanced microbiome has **high diversity with no single organism dominating** (Source: [[Cleveland Clinic Dysbiosis Overview]]). You can't feel diversity directly — the proxies are diet variety tolerated (30 plants/week, [[Gut Optimization Actions Tier List]]) and the bathroom markers above.

> [!gap] No single sign is diagnostic. The checklist works as a cluster: if the bathroom markers are in range AND systemic markers are quiet, gut health is very likely fine — no testing needed ([[Gut Health Tests]]).
