# Humanism

In *[[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari|Sapiens]]*, [[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari]] treats **humanism — the belief that human beings and human experience are the ultimate source of meaning and authority — as a modern religion.** Where traditional religions locate ultimate value in gods, humanism sacralizes humanity itself ("human rights," "the individual," "free will," "follow your heart").

By his definition of [[Religion as Unifier|religion]] (any system granting superhuman legitimacy to norms), humanism qualifies: it grounds law, ethics, and politics in the supreme value of the human, just as theistic religions ground them in the divine. Harari distinguishes liberal, socialist, and evolutionary humanisms — and notes that, like all [[Imagined Orders]], humanism's authority rests on collective belief rather than objective fact.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 3.*
