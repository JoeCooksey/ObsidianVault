# Finishing Oils (Cold-Use Oils)

**Finishing oils** are added *raw, off the heat* — drizzled over finished dishes for flavor, aroma, and nutrients. The best ones are exactly the oils you should **never cook with**, because heat destroys their delicate omega-3 fats and antioxidants. This is the "best for food toppings" half of the [[Cooking Oils — Master Guide]].

## Why use oil raw at all
- **Flavor & aroma:** good oils carry volatile compounds that vanish under heat.
- **Nutrient preservation:** polyphenols (EVOO) and omega-3 ALA (flax, walnut) survive only when not cooked. (Source: BodyBio; Whole Food Earth)
- **Fat-soluble absorption:** a little fat on vegetables/salads helps absorb fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) and carotenoids.

## The finishing-oil lineup
| Oil | What it adds | Use on | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Extra-virgin olive oil** | Peppery polyphenols, fruity flavor | Salads, soup, pasta, bread, roasted veg | The universal finisher; also fine to cook with. |
| **Flaxseed oil** | Highest plant omega-3 (ALA) | Salads, grain bowls, yogurt, smoothies, dips | Smoke point ~225°F — **never heat**. Refrigerate; rancid fast. (Source: BodyBio) |
| **Walnut oil** | Nutty, omega-3-rich | Salads, roasted veg, desserts | Heat-fragile; finishing only. |
| **Toasted sesame oil** | Strong nutty seasoning | Stir-fries (added at end), noodles, dressings | A *seasoning*, not a cooking fat — add off-heat. |
| **Avocado oil (unrefined)** | Mild, buttery | Salads, drizzling | Also usable for cooking when refined. |
| **Pumpkin seed, hemp, etc.** | Specialty nutty/grassy notes | Salads, dips | Niche gourmet finishers; keep cold. |

## Quality & storage rules
> [!tip] For raw use, buy **cold-pressed / unrefined** — no heat was used in production, so antioxidants and flavor are intact. (Source: Whole Food Earth)

- Store delicate oils (flax, walnut) in the **fridge**, in **dark glass**; they oxidize quickly.
- Buy small bottles you'll finish fast — these oils don't keep.
- Flax oil is the most fragile of all: refrigerate, use within weeks of opening, discard if it smells fishy/bitter (rancid).
