# Fiber Supplement Tier List

S–F ranking of fiber supplements by **weight of clinical evidence, breadth of benefit, and tolerability**. Built on the functional-fiber framework: the fibers that do the most are **gel-forming (viscous)** and **resist fermentation**, because they hold water all the way through the colon — normalizing stool in both directions and producing the gel-dependent metabolic benefits (cholesterol, glycemic control). See [[Functional Fiber Framework (McRorie)]].

> [!tip] One-line answer
> **Psyllium husk is the S-tier default for almost everyone.** It is the only widely available fiber that is gel-forming *and* minimally fermented, so it gets the metabolic benefits without much gas. Start everything else only for a specific reason.

## The two axes that decide the tier

| Property | What it buys | Examples |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Viscous / gel-forming** | Cholesterol ↓, glucose ↓, water-holding laxation | psyllium, β-glucan, glucomannan, methylcellulose |
| **Fermentable (prebiotic)** | Feeds bacteria, SCFA/butyrate — but **gas & bloating** | inulin, wheat dextrin, PHGG, acacia, resistant starch |
| **Best combo** | Gel-forming **+** low fermentation = benefits without gas | **psyllium**, methylcellulose, calcium polycarbophil |

## S-Tier — Best overall

| Supplement | Dose | Why it's S |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| **Psyllium husk** (*Plantago ovata*) | 5–15 g/day | Strongest, broadest evidence of any fiber. Gel-forming + minimally fermented → normalizes stool **both ways** (firms diarrhea, softens constipation). **Winner of the constipation meta-analysis** (RR 1.82; +~3 BMs/week). **FDA heart-health claim** (LDL ↓). Improves glycemic control and IBS abdominal pain. Low gas for a soluble fiber. (Source: [[Functional Fiber Framework (McRorie)]], [[Fiber for Chronic Constipation Meta-Analysis (2022)]]) |

## A-Tier — Excellent for a specific job

| Supplement | Dose | Why it's A |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| **Oat β-glucan** | 3–4 g/day | Viscous; **FDA cholesterol claim**; 3–4 g/day lowers LDL ~5–7%. Fermentable so some gas. Best *targeted* cholesterol fiber after psyllium. |
| **Methylcellulose** (Citrucel) | 2–6 g/day | Viscous **and non-fermentable** → effective regularity with the **least gas/bloating** of any soluble fiber. Semi-synthetic; weaker metabolic data. Best for gas-sensitive guts. |
| **Calcium polycarbophil** (FiberCon) | ~1–2 g/day | Non-fermentable water-holding resin; good laxation, minimal gas. Convenient caplets. Less metabolic benefit. |

## B-Tier — Good, with caveats

| Supplement | Dose | Why it's B |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| **PHGG** (partially hydrolyzed guar gum, Sunfiber) | 5–7 g/day | Fully fermentable prebiotic but **unusually low-gas**; well-tolerated. Strong for **IBS** (both C and D), normalizes stool form, ↑ Bifidobacterium/*Parabacteroides*. Not viscous → little cholesterol benefit. (Source: [[PHGG Microbiome Trials]]) |
| **Glucomannan** (konjac) | 1–4 g/day | Highly viscous → cholesterol ↓ and modest weight loss (~0.8 kg). **⚠️ Choking / GI-obstruction risk** — take with lots of water, never dry/as a tablet that can swell in the throat. |
| **Acacia / gum arabic** | 5–15 g/day | Gentle, very low-gas prebiotic; well-tolerated. But weak clinical-outcome evidence — mostly a "tolerable prebiotic" pick. |

## C-Tier — Prebiotic-only, gassy, narrow

| Supplement | Dose | Why it's C |
|-----------|------|-----------|
| **Inulin / FOS / chicory root** | 3–10 g/day | Non-viscous, **fully fermentable** → strongly bifidogenic but **high gas/bloating**; **no cholesterol benefit**; less effective for stool frequency. **Stanford 2022: 30 g/day caused inflammation** in most participants + liver-enzyme spikes in some. Fine as a *small-dose* prebiotic, poor as a primary fiber. (Source: [[Inulin High-Dose Inflammation Finding (Stanford 2022)]]) |
| **Wheat dextrin** (Benefiber) | 3–10 g/day | Non-viscous, fully fermentable → prebiotic only, **gas-prone**, minimal metabolic benefit. Mixes clear/tasteless = its main selling point. Celiac: caution. |
| **Resistant starch** (RS2, potato starch) | 10–40 g/day | Good butyrate/SCFA producer, glycemic benefits, but bloating-heavy and behaves more like a food than a clean supplement. Honorable mention. |

## D/F-Tier — Skip as a *supplement*

| Supplement | Why low |
|-----------|---------|
| **Wheat bran (isolated insoluble)** | Pure roughage; less effective than psyllium for constipation and **can worsen IBS** ("brick wall" effect). Get bran from whole grains instead. |
| **Polydextrose** | Synthetic, weak clinical evidence; mostly a food additive/bulking agent. |

## How to use this

1. **Default → psyllium.** Covers regularity, cholesterol, glucose, IBS pain. Start 5 g/day, build to 10–15 g, drink water.
2. **Very gas-sensitive → methylcellulose or calcium polycarbophil** (non-fermentable).
3. **IBS that flares on psyllium → PHGG** (low-gas, gut-friendly).
4. **Targeted LDL lowering → add oat β-glucan** on top of psyllium.
5. **Doses matter: >10 g/day and ≥4 weeks** are where constipation benefits show up.
6. **Supplements are the backstop, not the goal** — whole-food fiber (legumes, oats, fruit, 30+ plants/week) wins on the matrix. See [[Gut Health Foods Tier List]], [[Food Matrix Effect]].

> [!warning] Universal cautions
> Take any fiber with a full glass of water; separate from medications by ~2 hours (fiber can bind drugs); ramp slowly to limit gas. Glucomannan especially must never be taken dry.
