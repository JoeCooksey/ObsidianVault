# Vision Control (LoL)

The information pillar of [[LoL Macro Play]]: place vision where fights and objectives will happen, deny the enemy's. A team-wide job, not support-only. Removing enemy wards matters as much as placing your own — every cleared ward forces a face-check or a retreat. (Source: [[WeCoach — Vision Control Ward Guide]])

## Ward Types
- **Stealth (totem) ward:** 600-unit cast range, ~900-unit vision radius, 90–120 s duration depending on game phase. (confidence: medium)
- **Control ward:** permanent until killed; reveals and disables enemy wards; visible itself. Buy one nearly every back.
- **Oracle Lens:** sweeps and disables nearby wards — the denial tool.
- **Farsight trinket:** long-range ward to scout bushes without face-checking (late game).

## Where to Ward, by Phase
- **Early:** your jungle entrances (anti-invade), enemy jungle entrances (track their jungler), river pixel brush. Top: river + tri-brush. Mid: side brushes + raptor approach — then play toward your warded side. Bot: river, tri-brush, lane bushes vs hook supports.
- **Mid:** enemy buff camps (position + respawn timers), Dragon and Baron approaches, deep lane bushes; adapt depth to whether you're ahead or behind.
- **Late:** Baron/Elder pits, enemy base and jungle entrances, inhibitor lane bushes. Never ward alone — getting caught warding loses games at this stage.

## Six Common Mistakes
Warding without a purpose · weak vision before objectives · no defensive wards when behind · bad timing relative to spawns (see the 60-30-10 rule) · skipping control ward purchases · re-warding spots the enemy just cleared. (Source: [[WeCoach — Vision Control Ward Guide]])

## Minimum Viable Habit
Easy per-game floor: 3+ wards placed, 2+ enemy wards cleared, control ward on every relevant back. (confidence: medium — common coaching baseline)
