# Gut Optimization Actions Tier List

Behaviors and actions ranked by evidence strength × effect size for building a healthy gut. This is the **actions** companion to the **foods** list ([[Gut Health Foods Tier List]]). Ranks reflect human-evidence quality, not hype.

## S-Tier — Do These First (strongest evidence)

| Action | Why it works | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Eat fermented foods daily** | Raised microbiota alpha-diversity + lowered 19 inflammatory markers (IL-6) in 10 wks | Stanford RCT, *Cell* 2021 (high) |
| **30+ different plant foods/week** | Plant diversity is the strongest dietary predictor of microbiome diversity | ZOE/American Gut (high) |
| **Feed fiber + resistant starch** | Fuels SCFA (butyrate) production — the core payoff molecule | Multiple RCTs (high) |
| **Cut dietary emulsifiers** (CMC, P80) | Removing them stops non-reversible microbiota damage | [[Human Gut Emulsifiers Study 2021]] (high) |

## A-Tier — High Value

| Action | Why it works | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Eat polyphenol-rich foods** (berries, EVOO, dark chocolate, green tea) | Polyphenols feed Akkermansia & lower zonulin/permeability | Observational + RCT (medium-high) |
| **Regular moderate exercise** | Independently increases microbial diversity & butyrate producers | Cohort + small RCTs (medium) |
| **Protect sleep / fix circadian rhythm** | Circadian disruption raises permeability (occludin) | Mechanistic + animal (medium) |
| **Manage chronic stress** | Stress worsens barrier injury via glucocorticoid signaling | [[Gut-Harmful Foods and Actions Tier List]] (medium) |
| **Omega-3 intake** (fatty fish) | Reverses dysbiosis, raises SCFA producers | RCT (medium) |

## B-Tier — Worth Doing, Context-Dependent

| Action | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Time-restricted eating / overnight fast** | Gives the gut lining rest; modest microbiome benefit; evidence still maturing |
| **Targeted probiotic supplement** | Strain-specific; food (kefir, 61 strains) usually beats pills for general use; reserve supplements for specific indications |
| **Prebiotic supplement** (inulin, GOS) | Works, but whole-food fiber is cheaper and more diverse; can cause gas — titrate up |
| **Stay hydrated** | Supports mucus layer and motility; low-cost baseline |

## C-Tier — Minor / Situational

| Action | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Bone broth / collagen** | Plausible barrier support; thin human evidence |
| **L-glutamine** | Some data for barrier repair in stressed states; not needed for healthy people |
| **Apple cider vinegar** | Negligible direct microbiome evidence; mostly hype |

## D-Tier — Skip / Avoid

| Action | Why |
|---|---|
| **Direct-to-consumer "gut cleanse" / detox** | No evidence; can harm the microbiome |
| **Routine/unneeded antibiotics** | One course can depress diversity for months — only when medically required |
| **Buying a zonulin test to guide a protocol** | Test is invalid (Source: [[Zonulin Biomarker Critique 2021]]) |

## The Minimum Effective Protocol
1. Fermented food at one meal daily (kefir, yogurt, kimchi, sauerkraut)
2. Hit 30 plant foods/week; make fiber the default
3. Drop ultra-processed foods with emulsifiers
4. Sleep 7–8h, exercise 3–5×/week, keep stress in check
