# Protein Ice Cream (Ninja CREAMi) Health Profile

How healthy a "Ninja CREAMi" actually is depends entirely on the base, but the **typical high-protein recipe is a genuinely good dessert nutritionally** — more a protein-forward snack than a treat.

## Typical numbers (per serving)
| Build | Calories | Protein | Sugar |
|---|---|---|---|
| RD high-protein base | ~240 | ~26g | low |
| RD base + toppings | ~393 | 38g | 21g |
| Low-cal chocolate (per **pint**) | ~235 | 10g | 14g |
| Keto chocolate (per serving) | ~157 | 15.6g | ~1g net carb |

For contrast: 1 cup Häagen-Dazs vanilla = **478 cal, 6.7g protein, 42.8g carbs** (Source: [[Forks Over Knives — Ninja CREAMi Comparison]] search data). A CREAMi pint can beat that on every axis.

## Why it's "healthy"
- **High protein** (20–40g) from protein powder, Greek yogurt, or cottage cheese → satiety + muscle support (Source: [[Maria Lucey — Ninja CREAMi Protein Ice Cream]]).
- **You control sugar** — refined sugar swapped for fruit, dates, maple, or low/zero-cal sweeteners (allulose, monk fruit).
- **No mystery additives** — homemade avoids the gums and artificial blends in many store "protein ice creams."
- **Calorie control** — many full pints land 235–400 cal vs ~950 for a premium store pint.

## The honest caveats
> [!gap] "Healthy" is recipe-dependent — these caveats decide it.
- **Sugar isn't zero**: a topping-loaded serving still hits ~21g sugar.
- **Sweetener trade-offs**: sugar alcohols/artificial blends can cause GI upset or an aftertaste; some find them "overly sweet/artificial."
- **Protein-powder quality matters**: pick one that blends clean and skips junk fillers.
- **It's processed protein, not whole food** — fine as a daily snack, not a vegetable.
- Best **eaten fresh**; macros/texture degrade on refreeze.

## Bottom line
A Ninja CREAMi protein ice cream is **one of the better "have-your-cake" desserts** — RD-endorsed for regular eating — provided you choose a clean protein powder and don't drown it in toppings. The machine is neutral; the recipe is the health lever.
