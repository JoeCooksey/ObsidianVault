# Time Confetti

**Time confetti** = leisure shredded into tiny, scattered, low-quality scraps — the seconds and minutes lost to interruption and multitasking. Term coined by [[Brigid Schulte]], popularized by [[Ashley Whillans]]. (Source: [[Whillans - Time Confetti and the Broken Promise of Leisure]])

## The core idea

The modern problem is usually **not a shortage of free minutes** — it is that the minutes arrive shredded. A free hour cut by notifications becomes several useless 5–6 minute fragments. You can't rest, think, or enter [[Focused vs Diffuse Mode|flow]] in a fragment, so the time evaporates and you feel you had less of it than you did.

This reframes "I have no time" as **"my time is fragmented and contaminated."** The fix is structural, not motivational.

## Why fragments are worse than their length

- **The 10% tax**: one leisure hour interrupted by 14 notifications loses ~10% of the time to the interruptions themselves. (Source: [[Whillans - Time Confetti and the Broken Promise of Leisure]])
- **The contamination tax** (bigger): each interruption reminds you of everything else you could be doing, so even the surviving scraps feel anxious rather than restorative — the **autonomy paradox** (flexible tech = constant intrusion).
- **The perception tax**: fragmentation makes people *estimate they had less free time than they actually did* — it manufactures felt time-poverty.

## Relationship to killing time

"Killing time" *is* time confetti by choice: you take a small pocket and fill it with the slot machine ([[Digital Switching]]), guaranteeing it stays a useless fragment. The antidote is to **defend continuous blocks** and to put even small pockets to a single restorative or building use rather than reflexive switching.

## What to do about it

1. **Batch and shield**: cluster interruptions (notification windows, Do-Not-Disturb) to manufacture continuous blocks.
2. **Protect leisure like a meeting**: schedule it; treat it as non-negotiable, not as filler.
3. **Train time affluence daily** (Whillans): a deliberate 10–15 min of real leisure beats waiting for a rare big block.
4. **De-fragment the pocket**: in a genuine spare 10 minutes, do *one* thing fully (read, walk, call someone) instead of switching apps.
