# News Tools and Apps Stack

Ranked toolkit for consuming news efficiently and impartially. See [[Staying On Top of News - Complete Guide]] for the daily system.

---

## Tier List

### S-Tier — Use Daily

**Ground News** (ground.news)
- Aggregates 50,000+ sources; tags every story with bias (left/center/right) + factuality + ownership
- **Blindspot feature**: shows you stories being covered heavily by one side that the other is ignoring — the single best anti-echo-chamber tool available
- Cost: Free (limited) / $9.99/mo or $79.99/year
- Weakness: US-centric; left-right binary oversimplifies non-US stories

**AllSides** (allsides.com)
- Curates 33% Left / 33% Center / 34% Right daily — structurally balanced by design
- Free; 2,400+ sources rated
- Best free option; no algorithm manipulation
- Also check: allsides.com/media-bias to look up any outlet instantly

---

### A-Tier — RSS Reader (Power User Control)

**Feedly** (feedly.com)
- Most popular RSS reader; clean interface; iOS/Android/Web
- Subscribe to your curated 5–8 sources; all in one feed with no algorithm
- Free (up to 100 sources) / Pro at $8/mo
- **Best for**: users who know their source list and want zero algorithm influence

**Inoreader** (inoreader.com)
- Power-user RSS reader with automation, keyword alerts, and web page monitoring
- Free (150 sources) / Supporter at $2.50/mo / Pro at $5/mo
- **Best for**: following specific beats (EE/tech/academic) with keyword filtering

---

### A-Tier — Fast Daily Digests

**Particle** (particle.news)
- AI groups multi-source coverage into short summaries; source links visible; follow-up questions supported
- Free / Particle+ at $2.99/mo
- **Best for**: 5-min daily catch-up before the morning session

**Axios AM** (axios.com/newsletters)
- Free daily email briefing; "Smart Brevity" format (key sentence → why it matters → go deeper)
- ~5 min read; consistent quality
- **Best for**: morning briefing if you don't want to open an app

**NPR Up First** (podcast)
- 15–20 min daily morning podcast from NPR; three biggest stories
- Free; works offline
- **Best for**: commute or gym; center-rated factual reporting

---

### B-Tier — Supplemental

**Pocket** (getpocket.com)
- Save-for-later reading; distraction-free reading mode; free
- Use to save deep-read articles discovered in your morning scan for focused reading later

**Flipboard** (flipboard.com)
- Magazine-style aggregator; more curated but algorithm-influenced
- Good secondary browse tool; don't use as primary

**SmartNews** (smartnews.com)
- Free news aggregator; AllSides analysis rates it Lean Left (2026 update); use with that in mind

---

### C-Tier — Use as Reference Only

**Google News / Apple News** — algorithm-heavy; personalization creates filter bubbles over time; use for breaking news lookup, not as daily feed

**Twitter/X / Reddit / Facebook** — high engagement, low reliability; zero editorial standards for user-shared links; useful only as pointers to verify elsewhere

## Recommended Daily Stack (Minimal, High-Signal)

```
Morning (15 min):
  → Axios AM email (5 min headlines)
  → Feedly RSS feed — top 3 stories from curated sources (10 min)

Weekly cross-check (10 min):
  → Ground News — check 1 major story for blindspots
  → AllSides — verify your source stack is balanced
```

Cost: $0 (Axios free + Feedly free tier + AllSides free + Ground News free tier)
