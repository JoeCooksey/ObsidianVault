# The Expectations Treadmill

[[Morgan Housel|Housel's]] core mechanism in *[[The Art of Spending Money - Morgan Housel|The Art of Spending Money]]*: **happiness ≈ results minus expectations**, so lifestyle creep is really *expectation* creep. Every upgrade quietly resets your baseline of "normal," meaning the next upgrade is needed just to feel the same — and the relief you bought never actually arrives.

You can raise your income and possessions your whole life and never feel richer if expectations climb just as fast. The practical lever is the cheaper one: managing expectations beats endlessly increasing results. It's the financial cousin of Harari's [[Luxury Trap]] and a near-restatement of "enough" from *The Psychology of Money*.

*Source: [[The Art of Spending Money - Morgan Housel]].*
