# Reciprocity

The first of [[Robert B. Cialdini|Cialdini's]] weapons of influence: humans feel a deep, near-universal obligation to **repay what another person has given us.** A favor, a gift, a concession, even an unwanted free sample triggers a sense of indebtedness disproportionate to the original gift's value.

Compliance professionals exploit this by giving something small first (the free sample, the free trial, the "gift" in the mail) to create an obligation to buy or comply. A specialized form is **[[Rejection-Then-Retreat]]** (the door-in-the-face technique). **Defense:** accept genuine gifts and favors, but mentally redefine a manipulative "gift" as the sales tactic it actually is — you owe nothing in return for a trick.
