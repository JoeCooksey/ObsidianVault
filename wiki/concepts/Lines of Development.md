# Lines of Development

## Definition
**Lines of development** are the many independent dimensions you grow along, **each at its own altitude** on the [[Nine Levels of Thinking|levels]]. If the levels are your altitude of awareness, the lines are the different areas of life that each have their own altitude. **[[Ken Wilber]]** identified over a dozen; **[[Craig Perry]]** lists: cognitive, emotional, moral, interpersonal, spiritual, psychosexual, aesthetic, kinesthetic/physical, values, needs, self-identity/ego, and worldview.

## Why It Matters
You develop along **all lines simultaneously but at completely different rates** — so you can be **level 7 on one line and level 3 on another**. This is why "smart" people are often badly stuck: high development in one line plus neglect of the others produces explanations that work in one area and fail catastrophically in another. Naming the line a problem touches tells you **where to direct your growth** — "you cannot strengthen a line you have not defined."

## How It Works
Perry's examples of uneven lines:
- **The £100M founder:** cognitively level 7 (synthesizes, builds systems) but emotionally level 3. Sells the company; the cognitive challenge vanishes but the un-processed emotions don't → depression with no explanation ("I should be happy").
- **The "gym bro":** kinesthetic line maxed, interpersonal line at level 3 — "can get the girl but can't keep her," because attraction and connection are *different lines*. His explanation: "women are impossible to understand."
- **The artist:** aesthetic line at level 7, practical/business line at level 4 — can't market the work, can't pay rent, believes "art and business are incompatible" (itself a level-4 borrowed-culture explanation).

**Regression:** you don't hold a level permanently. When stressed, tired, hungry, or overwhelmed, you **drop several levels on whichever line is under pressure** (e.g., cognitive line collapsing to survival mode when sleep-deprived and caffeinated).

## Cross-Domain Connections
- Conceptual cousin of **Howard Gardner's multiple intelligences**, but framed developmentally (each line has a *level*, not just a presence).
- Completes the [[AQAL Model (Integral Theory)|AQAL]] trio: quadrants (what) + levels (how clearly) + lines (where you're uneven).
- Operationalized in the [[Explanation-Building Writing Practice]] question 3: "Which line of development does this touch?"
