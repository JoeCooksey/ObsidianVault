# The Effort Is Enough

[[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]]'s [[Stoicism|Stoic]] reframe for handling failure and lack of recognition: **do your work, do it as well as you can, and let the effort itself be the reward** — independent of whether anyone notices, praises, or credits you.

Recognition is outside your control ([[Dichotomy of Control]]); the quality and integrity of your effort is not. Tying your sense of worth to applause hands your peace to an audience that may never deliver. Tying it to the effort keeps it in your own hands. "The effort is enough" is how you keep working through obscurity, injustice, and defeat without ego curdling into bitterness.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Failure."*
