# Health Metrics Master Tier List — Price, Frequency & Worth-It

> [!important] Worth-It score combines **predictive power** (does it forecast disease/death?), **actionability** (can you change it?), and **cost-per-insight**. A metric is only worth tracking if a bad reading would change what you do.

Worth-It scale: **S** = track for life, near-free or high-yield · **A** = high value, worth the cost · **B** = useful, situational · **C** = marginal / hype-prone · **D** = skip for most healthy people.

---

## S-Tier — Track These First (cheap + powerful)

| Metric | What It Tracks | Price | Frequency | Worth-It | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Blood pressure (home cuff)** | Cardiovascular risk #1 modifiable | $30–50 one-time device | Weekly (daily if elevated) | **S** | Home BP predicts mortality *better* than office BP (Ohasama study). 2025 AHA/ACC guideline targets <120 systolic (Source: [[2025 AHA-ACC Home Blood Pressure Guideline]]) |
| **Waist-to-height ratio** | Visceral fat → CVD, diabetes, all-cause mortality | Free (tape measure) | Monthly | **S** | Beats BMI; only anthropometric that survives adjustment for all classic risk factors. Target **<0.5** (Source: [[Waist-to-Height Ratio CVD 2025]]) |
| **Resting heart rate** | Autonomic + cardiovascular fitness | Free (or wearable) | Daily | **S** | Free, trend-rich, rises with illness/overtraining; lower is generally better |
| **VO2 max (estimate)** | Cardiorespiratory fitness — strongest single mortality predictor | Free via wearable estimate | Continuous | **S** | Low fitness carries higher mortality risk than smoking/diabetes; each +1 MET = ~13–15% lower mortality (Source: [[VO2 Max Mortality Evidence]]) |
| **Body weight** | Energy balance baseline | $25 scale | Daily/weekly | **S** | Cheap context for everything else; pair with waist measure |
| **HbA1c + fasting glucose** | 3-month glucose average; metabolic health | $28–80 | Every 6–12 mo | **S** | Earliest diabetes predictor; cheap and decisive |

## A-Tier — High Value, Worth the Cost

| Metric | What It Tracks | Price | Frequency | Worth-It | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **ApoB** | LDL *particle count* — best heart-attack predictor | $35–70 (often in panel) | Every 6–12 mo | **A** | Single best blood biomarker for CVD; beats LDL-C. ESC/CCS now recommend over LDL (Source: [[Empirical Health Best CVD Biomarkers 2025]]) |
| **Lp(a)** | Genetic CVD risk, independent | $35–70 | **Once in life** | **A** | One-time test; if high, intensify everything else. Target <30 mg/dL |
| **hs-CRP** | Systemic inflammation | $20–50 | Every 6–12 mo | **A** | ACC 2025 names hs-CRP the best inflammation marker; now rivals LDL for prediction |
| **Fasting insulin / HOMA-IR** | Earliest insulin resistance | $15–40 | Every 6–12 mo | **A** | Most skip it; flags metabolic trouble years before glucose moves |
| **Lipid panel (full)** | HDL, triglycerides, LDL-C | $35–70 | Every 6–12 mo | **A** | Standard, cheap, insurance-covered; triglycerides track carb/sugar load |
| **Vitamin D (25-OH)** | Most common deficiency in young adults | $30–60 | Annually | **A** | ~36% of young adults deficient; cheap fix, broad effects |
| **Ferritin + iron panel** | Iron status; fatigue, cognition | $30–60 | Annually | **A** | Low ferritin = fatigue/hair loss; common in athletes & menstruating women |
| **Testosterone (total + free) + SHBG** | Energy, muscle, libido, mood (males) | $40–90 | Annually | **A** | High-yield for young males; free T + SHBG matter more than total alone |
| **CBC + CMP** | Blood count, kidney, liver, electrolytes | In most panels | Every 6–12 mo | **A** | RDW within CBC independently predicts all-cause mortality |
| **TSH + thyroid panel** | Metabolism, energy | $30–80 | Annually | **A** | First-line for unexplained fatigue/weight change |
| **Comprehensive lab membership** | 100–160+ biomarkers, trended | $199–365/yr | Annually | **A** | Superpower ~$199 (100+); Function ~$365 (160+); both via Quest/LabCorp (Source: [[Blood Testing Memberships 2025]]) |
| **Sleep duration (wearable)** | Recovery, total sleep time | $150–350 device | Nightly | **A** | Wearables nail *sleep vs wake* (>90% sensitivity); duration is the reliable number |
| **HRV (wearable, trend)** | Autonomic stress/recovery | $150–350 device | Nightly | **A** | Trend > absolute; Oura most accurate for RHR/HRV. Validated for stationary, not motion (Source: [[Wearable Health Metric Validity]]) |

## B-Tier — Useful, Situational

| Metric | What It Tracks | Price | Frequency | Worth-It | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **DEXA (body composition)** | Visceral fat, lean mass, bone density | $40–60 | Every 6–12 mo | **B** | Gold standard for body comp; ~95% cheaper than MRI; HSA/FSA eligible |
| **Grip strength** | Muscular strength → mortality | $20 dynamometer | Quarterly | **B** | Stronger CVD-death predictor than systolic BP in PURE study |
| **Omega-3 Index** | EPA+DHA in RBC membranes | $50–100 | Annually | **B** | CVD risk predictor; target >8%; actionable via diet/supplements |
| **Homocysteine** | Methylation, B-vitamin status | $30–70 | Annually | **B** | CVD + cognitive risk independent of LDL; fixable with B12/folate |
| **CAC score (coronary calcium)** | Actual coronary plaque | $100–400 (low-dose CT) | Once at 40+ (repeat 3–5 yr) | **B** | Best CVD test for intermediate-risk 40+; decides statin start. Radiation dose |
| **B12 / folate (RBC)** | Neurological, DNA synthesis | $20–60 | Annually | **B** | RBC folate > serum; high MMA confirms borderline B12 |
| **Magnesium (RBC)** | Often-low electrolyte | $30–60 | Annually | **B** | Serum Mg nearly useless; use RBC Mg |
| **VO2 max (lab test)** | Gold-standard fitness | $100–250 | Every 1–2 yr | **B** | Wearable estimate usually enough; lab test for athletes/baseline |
| **Gait speed** | Frailty, mortality (older adults) | Free | Yearly (50+) | **B** | <1.0 m/s = elevated risk; mostly relevant later in life |

## C-Tier — Marginal / Hype-Prone

| Metric | What It Tracks | Price | Frequency | Worth-It | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **CGM (non-diabetic)** | Glucose response to food | $50–100/mo | 2-week n=1 trials | **C** | Not recommended routinely for healthy people; useful as a *short* learning tool, not a lifelong metric (Source: [[CGM Non-Diabetic Review 2025]]) |
| **Sleep stage data (wearable)** | REM/deep/light split | (device) | Nightly | **C** | Multi-stage accuracy only 50–80% vs PSG; Oura best (~79%), Apple Watch ~51%. Don't over-trust stage breakdowns |
| **Epigenetic age clock** | Biological vs chronological age | $200–500 | Every 1–2 yr | **C** | Interesting, noisy, low test-retest reliability; rarely changes action |
| **Microbiome sequencing** | Gut bacterial composition | $150–300 | Once | **C** | Science immature; recommendations often weakly supported |
| **Telomere length** | Cellular aging proxy | $200–400 | — | **C** | High measurement noise; not actionable |

## D-Tier — Skip for Most Healthy People

| Metric | What It Tracks | Price | Frequency | Worth-It | Notes |
|--------|---------------|-------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **Whole-body MRI (Prenuvo etc.)** | Cancer/anomaly screen | $1,200–4,500 | — | **D** | ACR/major radiology groups advise against for average-risk asymptomatic adults; detects confirmed cancer in only ~1.6%; high false-positive cascade (Source: [[Whole-Body MRI Screening 2025]]) |
| **Routine genetic panels (beyond ApoE/MTHFR/Lp(a))** | Broad SNP profiling | $100–300 | — | **D** | Most results non-actionable; APOE4 and a few variants are the exceptions |
| **GDF-15 / Klotho / IL-6 (consumer)** | Emerging aging markers | varies | — | **D** | Research-stage; no validated action thresholds for individuals yet |

## The Minimal High-Yield Stack

> [!tip] If budget is tight, this covers ~90% of actionable findings:
> **Free/cheap, lifelong:** home BP cuff, waist-to-height ratio, body weight, resting HR + VO2 max estimate (any wearable).
> **One $200 annual blood panel** (Superpower-tier): ApoB, Lp(a) once, hs-CRP, HbA1c, fasting insulin, lipids, Vitamin D, ferritin, CBC/CMP, testosterone (males), TSH.
> **Add at 40+:** one CAC score; consider DEXA for body-composition trends.

## Frequency Cheat-Sheet

| Cadence | Metrics |
|---------|---------|
| **Daily** | Blood pressure (if elevated), resting HR, sleep duration, weight, VO2max estimate |
| **Weekly–Monthly** | Blood pressure (if normal), waist-to-height ratio |
| **Every 6–12 mo** | HbA1c, fasting glucose/insulin, lipids, ApoB, hs-CRP, CBC, CMP |
| **Annually** | Full hormones, Vitamin D, ferritin, B12, omega-3 index, homocysteine, DEXA |
| **Once in life** | Lp(a), ApoE genotype |
| **Once at 40+, repeat 3–5 yr** | CAC score |
