# Trip Sitter

A sober companion present during a psychedelic experience. The single most consistently recommended safety measure across clinical and community sources; emotional support during use was the most-cited during-trip harm-reduction practice (16 studies) in the 2025 scoping review (Source: [[Dutton 2025 - Psychedelic Harm Reduction Scoping Review]]).

## When

- Recommended for any moderate or high dose; higher doses increase sitter use in practice
- ~21% of users consider **remote sitting** (on call by phone) acceptable; in-person is the standard
- Professional fallback: Fireside Project psychedelic peer-support line (62-FIRESIDE) — de-escalated distress for 65.9% of callers (Source: [[Dutton 2025 - Psychedelic Harm Reduction Scoping Review]])

## What a good sitter does

- Conveys calm, security, and non-judgmental presence; experienced with non-ordinary states preferred
- Stays mostly invisible — present, not directive; clinical monitors do not talk to or guide participants during sessions (Source: [[Psilocybin Session Protocol (Johns Hopkins)]])
- Manages the environment, not the person: changes music, dims lights, offers water

## Scripts

- Say: *"You're safe. I'm here."* · *"You don't have to do anything. Just breathe."* · *"This will pass."*
- Avoid: *"Calm down"* · *"You're fine"* · *"What are you seeing?"* — these invalidate or pull the person out of the experience (Source: [[Things to Do on Shrooms (Aggregate Guides)]])

During a difficult stretch: reassure that effects are temporary, move to calmer surroundings, switch to soothing music, guide slow breathing (Source: [[CISUR Safer Tripping Infosheet]]).
