# Time Management Habits

Habits 71–82 from [[101 Habits — Dan Zakaria]]. Plan the week, batch by type, exploit leverage (80/20), and close open loops.

## The Habits
71. **Plan your week**, not just your day — plan for uncertainties; frontload the hardest tasks.
72. **Use a calendar** — put deadlines behind goals ("I will achieve X by doing Y in Z time").
73. **Audit your screen time** — 4 hrs/day ≈ 10 full years over a 60-year span; awareness → cutting it.
74. **Batch similar tasks** — batch shallow (admin) work together; batch deep work together.
75. **Follow the 80/20 principle** — 80% of results come from 20% of actions; give that 20% your attention.
76. **Set time limits for tasks** (Parkinson's Law) — work expands to fill the time you give it.
77. **Arrive early** — chronic lateness is disrespectful; "the way you do anything is how you do everything."
78. **Stop procrastinating small tasks** — the 2-minute rule; unfinished tasks become **open loops** that drain mental energy.
79. **Create a morning routine** — simple, consistent, sustainable (his: wake → freshen up → read 30 min → meditate 15 min → stretch → gratitude → pray, ~1 hr → deep work).
80. **Create an evening (wind-down) routine** — screens off, read 30 min, journal & plan tomorrow, gratitude → sleep.
81. **Schedule recovery time** — "no recovery, no growth" (muscles, brain, everything).
82. **Measure where your time goes.**

## Notes
- #78 "open loops" = the same idea as #6 ("finish what you start") in [[Discipline & Action Habits]].
- #75 (Pareto) + #74 (batching) are the highest-leverage items here.
- #79/#80 routines overlap with [[Daily Learning Habit (Operating System)]] and the sleep emphasis in [[Health & Energy Habits]] (#85).
