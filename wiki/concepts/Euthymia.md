# Euthymia

A [[Stoicism|Stoic]] term [[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]] borrows from Seneca, meaning **"tranquility" — the sense of knowing your own path and walking it without being distracted by the crisscrossing paths of others.**

It is the antidote to the comparison and restlessness that ego produces after success. The person with euthymia is not jealous of rivals, not derailed by others' wins, not constantly second-guessing their direction. They trust their course and keep moving. Seneca: "the most important thing is to set a course and stick to it without being lured aside."
