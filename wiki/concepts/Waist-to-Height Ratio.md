# Waist-to-Height Ratio

The cheapest high-value health metric: waist circumference ÷ height, in the same units. Requires only a tape measure (Source: [[Waist-to-Height Ratio CVD 2025]]).

## Why It Beats BMI

- Captures **central (visceral) adiposity** — the fat actually linked to heart disease and diabetes — which BMI cannot distinguish from muscle (confidence: **high**).
- In 2025 analyses, when adjusted for age, sex, smoking, exercise, diabetes, hypertension, and cholesterol, **only waist-to-height ratio held as an independent predictor** of cardiovascular disease (Source: [[Waist-to-Height Ratio CVD 2025]]).
- High ratio is positively associated with all-cause, cardiovascular, cancer, respiratory, and Alzheimer's mortality.

## Target

| Result | Risk |
|--------|------|
| **< 0.50** | Low |
| 0.50–0.59 | Elevated |
| ≥ 0.60 | High |

Rule of thumb: **keep your waist less than half your height.**

## How to Measure

Measure waist at the midpoint between the lowest rib and the top of the hip bone (roughly at the navel), relaxed, after exhaling. Same tape, same spot, monthly.

> [!tip] Pair with body weight and a wearable's resting HR for a free, lifelong baseline before spending anything on labs or scans.
