# Macronutrient Sourcing Guide (What to Buy)

A **shopping list by macronutrient** — the best-value carb, protein, and fat sources to actually buy, with the sourcing upgrade that matters for each. This is the "what to put in the cart" companion to the [[Food Health Tier List — Macronutrients|which-foods tier list]] and the sourcing-decision pages.

## 🍞 Carbohydrate Sources to Buy
| Buy | Sourcing note | Value pick |
|---|---|---|
| Oats (rolled/steel-cut) | Organic optional (not Dirty Dozen) | Bulk plain oats — cheapest whole carb |
| Potatoes / sweet potatoes | **Potatoes are Dirty Dozen → buy organic** if affordable | Sweet potato = high nutrient/$ |
| Lentils, beans, chickpeas | Dried or canned both fine | Dried in bulk = cheapest protein+carb |
| Rice (white for athletes, brown for fiber) | No sourcing premium needed | Big-bag white/jasmine rice |
| Berries | **Strawberries/blueberries/blackberries are Dirty Dozen → organic, frozen** | Frozen organic berries = best value |
| Bananas, pineapple, mango | **Clean Fifteen → buy conventional** | Conventional, no premium |

**Rule:** fiber is the through-line — if it's been refined (white bread, juice, cereal), it drops. Spend your organic dollars on the **berries, greens, and potatoes** (Dirty Dozen), buy the rest conventional.

## 🥩 Protein Sources to Buy
| Buy | Sourcing upgrade worth it? | Value pick |
|---|---|---|
| Eggs | ✅ **Pasture-raised + Certified Humane** | Best ROI upgrade; ~$0.30–0.50/egg |
| Small fatty fish (sardines, anchovies) | ✅ Wild, canned | ~$1–2/can, low mercury, high omega-3 |
| Salmon | 🟡 Wild or farmed both fine | Frozen wild fillets / canned |
| Chicken/turkey breast | 🟡 "Organic/air-chilled" minor; skip the hype | Bulk frozen breast |
| Greek yogurt, cottage cheese | No premium needed | Plain, full or low-fat |
| Lean beef | 🟡 Grass-fed marginal (taste/ethics only) | Lean ground; skip processed meat |
| Lentils/legumes, tofu/tempeh | No premium needed | Dried legumes = cheapest protein |

**Rule:** the **egg + small-wild-fish combo** is the highest-value sourcing upgrade. Get omega-3 from **fatty fish, not beef**.

## 🥑 Fat Sources to Buy
| Buy | Sourcing note | Value pick |
|---|---|---|
| Extra-virgin olive oil | **Buy real EVOO** — look for harvest date + single origin/PDO; much is adulterated | Mid-price single-origin EVOO |
| Avocado | **Clean Fifteen → conventional fine** | Conventional |
| Nuts/seeds (walnuts, almonds, chia, flax) | Raw/unsalted, bulk | Bulk bins / big bags |
| Fatty fish | (see protein) | Sardines/salmon |
| Whole eggs | (see protein) | Pasture-raised |
| Butter/full-fat dairy | Grass-fed marginal | Fine in moderation |

**Rule:** the one fat to genuinely vet for sourcing is **olive oil** (fraud is common) — buy EVOO with a **harvest date** and a single country of origin. Avoid heavy use of refined seed oils and *all* industrial trans fats (the one near-universal "avoid").

## The Three Sourcing Levers That Actually Matter
1. **Produce:** spend organic dollars on the [[EWG Shopper's Guide to Pesticides in Produce (2025)|Dirty Dozen]], buy Clean Fifteen conventional.
2. **Animal:** [[Animal Product Sourcing (Grass-Fed, Pasture-Raised, Wild-Caught)|pasture-raised eggs + small wild fish]] are the high-ROI upgrades; grass-fed beef is optional.
3. **Oils:** buy genuine **EVOO**; avoid trans fats. Everything else is noise.
