# Let Them

The first half of [[Mel Robbins|Robbins']] two-part tool: when other people reveal who they are, what they think, or how they want to behave — **let them.** Stop managing, convincing, fixing, and resenting other adults. Let them be annoyed, let them misunderstand you, let them make their own choices, let them pull away.

It is a release of the impossible job of controlling other people — a practical restatement of the Stoic [[Dichotomy of Control]]. On its own, though, "Let Them" can sound like apathy; Robbins insists it is only half the tool and is incomplete without **[[Let Me]]**, which redirects the freed-up energy back to your own response.

*Source: [[The Let Them Theory - Mel Robbins]].*
