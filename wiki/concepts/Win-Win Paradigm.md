# Win-Win Paradigm

Covey's Habit 4 interaction model: the belief that mutually beneficial solutions exist and are the only durable basis for ongoing relationships — built on three character traits (Integrity, Maturity, and [[Abundance Mentality]]) — and codified in Win-Win Agreements that replace top-down management with shared desired results, guidelines, resources, accountability, and consequences.
