# 4x4 Intervals for Rowing

How to execute the [[Norwegian 4x4 Interval Protocol]] on a rowing erg (Concept2). Rowing is one of the recommended modalities because the whole-body muscle mass drives HR high quickly and the monitor gives you precise, objective targets (split, watts, stroke rate). Structure is unchanged: 4 × 4 min hard / 3 min easy at 85–95% HRmax.

## The Right Target Number: Pace/Power, Not Just HR
On the erg, **watts (or 500m split) is the most reliable cross-session target** because it is independent of damper and fatigue drift. (Source: [[Rowing VO2max Intervals — Erg Practical Notes]])

- **Anchor to your 2K:** VO2 max intervals sit around **2K pace to ~2K+2–4 s/500m**. A practical 4x4 work target is roughly your **2K split plus 2–5 seconds per 500m** (≈85% of VO2 max power ≈ ~92% HRmax). Hold it for 4 min and repeat.
- Pace negative-split-able: rep 1 a touch easier, improving (lower split) each rep so rep 4 is fastest — same "pace for the 4th rep" rule as running.

## Stroke Rate (SPM)
- Work intervals: **~28–32 spm.** This sits at the aerobic-capacity / threshold boundary — high enough for VO2 max work, low enough to keep clean technique for 4 min. (Source: [[Rowing VO2max Intervals — Erg Practical Notes]])
- Don't chase rate over power: a strong drive at 28–30 spm beats flailing at 36. Power per stroke (length + leg drive) does the work; rate just modulates it.
- Recovery: drop to **~18–20 spm**, very light pressure.

## Drag Factor
- Set **drag factor ~120–130** (damper roughly 3–5 on most machines) — this best simulates on-water rowing and lets you sustain 28–32 spm. (Source: [[Rowing VO2max Intervals — Erg Practical Notes]])
- Higher drag is for short power pieces; for 4-min aerobic intervals, lower-to-moderate drag is correct. If you can't comfortably hold 28–32 spm, your drag is too high.

## Recovery Intervals
3 min of **very light paddling at ~18–20 spm** — keep the flywheel moving so HR settles to ~65–70% HRmax without stopping cold.

## Full Session Template
1. Warm-up: 6–10 min easy row at 18–22 spm, a few 10-stroke builds.
2. 4 min @ (2K + ~2–5 s/500m), 28–32 spm → 3 min light paddle @ 18–20 spm. **×4.**
3. Cool-down: 5 min easy row → stop; mobility after.
Total ≈ 35–40 min.

## Rowing-Specific Tips
- **Program it on the Concept2 monitor:** New Workout → Intervals: Time → 4:00 work / 3:00 rest, ×4. The monitor auto-splits and shows average pace per rep so you can verify even pacing.
- **Technique under fatigue is the failure point** — on rep 4, keep the sequence (legs → back → arms; arms → back → legs on the recovery). Don't shoot the slide or open the back early.
- A common simpler erg variant uses **4 × 4 min with 2 min rest, building pace each rep** — slightly shorter rest than the 3-min Norwegian standard but the same negative-split intent. Use 3 min rest to stay true to the protocol. (Source: [[Rowing VO2max Intervals — Erg Practical Notes]])
- Lower impact than running 4x4 — good option on days legs are beat up from running or lifting.
