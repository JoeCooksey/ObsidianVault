# Identity & Self-Respect Habits

Habits 41–55 from [[101 Habits — Dan Zakaria]]. The thesis core of the video: **habits follow identity** — act like the person you want to become and the behavior follows. Maps directly to [[Identity-Based vs Goal-Based Habits]].

## The Habits
41. **Vote for who you want to become** — every action is a vote for an identity (read 1 page = a vote for "reader").
42. **Act like your future self** — "to get there, you must already be there" (mentally first). *(Law-of-attraction framing — confidence: low.)*
43. **Keep your word.**
44. **Dress with intention** — "the way you see yourself is how others see you."
45. **Speak only positively about yourself** — change self-talk to change identity.
46. **Build confidence through action** — confidence = accumulated *evidence*, not videos or dreaming.
47. **Define your personal values** — journal what you can't live without; where you want to be in 5 years.
48. **Protect your reputation** — never act (publicly or privately) against your moral values.
49. **Be somebody who finishes things** — "the only way to fail is to quit"; stay in the game long enough to get lucky.
50. **Keep your standards high** — for partners, friends, and goals; aim as high as possible.
51. **Say no more often** — not rude, it's self-respect.
52. **Develop self-awareness** — through journaling; train emotional intelligence.
53. **Do the right thing when nobody's watching** — "live like there's a film crew following you."
54. **Focus on becoming, not having** — material success doesn't bring lasting joy; who you become does.
55. **Build an identity around growth** — grow, learn, share, create, conquer.

## Notes
- #41/#46/#55 are the operational form of [[Identity-Based vs Goal-Based Habits]] — habits *create evidence* for the desired self-image.
- #52 (self-awareness via journaling) recurs in #37 ([[Mindset & Growth Habits]]) and #99 ([[Success Multiplier Habits]]).
