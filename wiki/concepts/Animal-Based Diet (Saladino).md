# Animal-Based Diet (Saladino)

[[Paul Saladino]]'s post-carnivore diet (2022–present): **meat + organs + fruit + honey + raw dairy**, plants mostly excluded. This page is the practical how-to; the evidence assessment lives in [[Research - Paul Saladino Animal-Based Diet]].

## How to Follow It

### Daily Structure
1. **Meat** — ≥1 lb per 100 lbs bodyweight/day, prioritizing grass-fed ruminants (beef, lamb, bison, venison). Protein target: goal bodyweight × 1–1.2 g. Pork/chicken limited (grain-fed = "not as clean").
2. **Organs** — 0.5 oz liver/day (or 2–3 oz 2×/week) + a few oz of heart/kidney/marrow etc. His company sells desiccated-organ capsules as the squeamish path (note the conflict of interest).
3. **Carbs from fruit + honey** — scale to activity: goal bodyweight × 0.7 g (sedentary) → × 1.7 g (12+ hrs training/week). Saladino himself eats ~300 g/day: berries, melon, mango, oranges, coconut water, ~10 tbsp honey.
4. **Dairy (raw, in his version)** — milk, kefir, butter, cheese. ⚠️ The raw part is the diet's clearest safety problem post-2024 — see [[Raw Milk Safety and H5N1 (CDC FDA)]]; pasteurized full-fat dairy keeps virtually all the nutrition without the pathogen risk.
5. **Eggs** — pasture-raised, several/day.

### The Three Toxicity Tiers (Source: [[Heart and Soil Plant Toxins and Food List Guide]])
- **Eat freely:** ruminant meat, organs, eggs, dairy, nearly all fruit, avocado/olives/squash/cucumber, animal fats, honey/maple syrup, bone broth
- **Conditional:** pork, chicken, wild seafood, white rice, sweet potato, carrots, sourdough, fermented vegetables, coffee/tea
- **Avoid:** seed oils, grains, legumes, nuts, seeds, crucifers (kale/broccoli/cauliflower), nightshades (tomato/potato/pepper), leafy greens, processed food

### Transition Tips (from his guides)
Start by swapping seed oils → butter/tallow; add fruit/honey rather than cutting carbs (this is NOT keto — that's the lesson of his own carnivore failure); add liver gradually; "no exact formula — individual tolerance varies."

## What It Actually Is, Structurally

Strip the branding and it's: **whole-food, zero-ultra-processed, high-protein, ~paleo-with-dairy, with vegetables and grains removed**. Most reported benefits (energy, body comp, skin) are explainable by what it shares with ANY whole-food cleanup — protein, satiety, no UPF, no seed-oil-laden junk — not by the vegetable removal, which is the part the evidence opposes ([[Carnivore and Animal-Based Diet Risk Reviews (PMC)]]).

## Vs. Strict Carnivore

The fruit/honey/dairy additions are not cosmetic: they restore carbs (his testosterone/sleep fix — see [[Saladino Diet Critiques (Gene Food + Hone Health)]]), vitamin C, potassium, folate (partially), and some prebiotic substrate. Several standard anti-carnivore critiques no longer fully apply; the zero-fiber, high-saturated-fat, and high-LDL critiques still do.
