# Protein Per Meal Target

How much protein to put in each meal-prep bowl to maximize muscle protein synthesis (MPS). (Source: [[Protein Per Meal and Muscle Synthesis — JISSN]])

## The Target

- **~30–40 g protein per meal** (≈ **0.4–0.6 g/kg bodyweight**) maximally stimulates MPS.
- Mechanism = the **leucine threshold**: ~2.5–3 g leucine per meal triggers the strongest MPS response, which works out to ~25–40 g of high-quality protein.

## What That Looks Like in Joe's Foods

| Food | Protein / 3 oz (or unit) |
|------|--------------------------|
| Salmon | ~25 g |
| Ground beef | ~22 g |
| White fish (cod/tilapia) | ~18–22 g |
| 2 eggs | ~12 g |

Pairing one protein with the starch/veg in a bowl clears 30 g easily. (Values via [[Food Health Tier List — Meats and Seafood]].)

## The Honest Caveat

The "30–40 g per meal" figure is **contested**:
- Some controlled studies show MPS plateaus near **20 g** with no further gain to 40 g.
- Recent reviews argue per-meal *distribution* matters little if **total daily protein** is adequate — the evidence for the per-meal plateau is "fragile."

**Takeaway:** treat 30–40 g as a sensible upper target per meal, but prioritize hitting your **daily** protein total. (Source: [[Protein Per Meal and Muscle Synthesis — JISSN]])
