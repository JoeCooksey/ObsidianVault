# Character Ethic vs Personality Ethic

Covey's central distinction: the **Character Ethic** (pre-1920 success literature) grounds effectiveness in deep principles — integrity, humility, courage, justice, patience — while the **Personality Ethic** (post-1920) peddles surface techniques — social skills, communication hacks, image management — which Covey argues are secondary to character and ultimately hollow without it.
