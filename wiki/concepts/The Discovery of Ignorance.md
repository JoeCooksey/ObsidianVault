# The Discovery of Ignorance

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] account of what actually launched the [[Scientific Revolution]]: not a new fact, but a new attitude — the willingness to **admit "we do not know."**

Pre-modern traditions assumed all important truths were already revealed in scripture or ancient wisdom; ignorance was a gap to be filled from authority. Modern science instead begins by confessing collective ignorance and treating no theory as sacred or final. That humility opened the door to systematic observation, experiment, and mathematics — and to the assumption that new knowledge yields new power. It is the intellectual seed of the [[Science, Empire, Capitalism Feedback Loop]].

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 4.*
