# Liking

[[Robert B. Cialdini|Cialdini's]] fourth principle: we prefer to say yes to **people we like**, and likability is built from predictable, exploitable levers:

- **Physical attractiveness** (halo effect)
- **Similarity** (shared background, interests, dress)
- **Compliments** and praise
- **Familiarity** through repeated contact
- **Cooperation** toward shared goals
- **Association** with good things (good news, attractive models, food)

The Tupperware party is the archetype — you're buying from a friend, not a salesperson. **Defense:** separate the deal from the dealer; if you notice yourself liking someone unusually fast, treat that as the cue to evaluate the offer strictly on its own merits. Distinct from but related to **[[Unity]]** (shared identity rather than mere affection).
