# Maintain Your Sobriety

[[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]]'s term for the **clear-headed restraint that keeps you grounded** under both pressure and praise. "Sobriety" here is metaphorical: not being drunk on success, not being thrown into blame and excuse-making by failure, not being intoxicated by your own importance.

Under stress the ego seeks scapegoats; under success it seeks indulgence. Sobriety is the steady, honest middle — owning your part, staying disciplined, and refusing the emotional binges (entitlement, self-pity, grandiosity) that ego offers. It is closely tied to [[Managing Yourself]] and is what allows clear judgment when it matters most.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Failure."*
