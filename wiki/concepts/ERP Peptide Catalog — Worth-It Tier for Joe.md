# ERP Peptide Catalog — Worth-It Tier for Joe

Tier-ranking of **every product on the [[ERP Peptide Price List (Jun 2026)]]** by how worth-it it is *for Joe specifically*. Prices shown are the PDF's per-kit (10-vial) price.

> [!important] **Joe's context:** ~19yo male, 5'9"/156 lbs, cyclist 6×/week + strength training, lean-bulk goal, EE student, peak natural hormones, healthy BMI. Possibly competes → **WADA rules apply**. "Worth it" = evidence × safety × *relevance to a healthy young athlete* − (risk + legal/anti-doping exposure). For most of this list the honest answer is **not worth it**.

> [!danger] **Read first — the source matters more than the molecule.** This is a grey-market vendor (crypto payment, WhatsApp contact, no quality assurance). Even the peptides that rank well *in principle* (GHK-Cu, BPC-157, Thymosin Alpha-1, Semax) should come **pharmaceutical-grade from a licensed compounding pharmacy with a prescription**, not from an unverified injectable kit. Grey-market peptides routinely test under-dosed, contaminated, or mislabeled. Self-injecting unsterile product carries infection/abscess/sepsis risk. Many items here are **WADA-banned** (disqualifying for a competitive cyclist) and several require a prescription or are outright unsafe to self-administer. **Bottom line for Joe: ~3 things on this entire list are worth pursuing, and none of them from this vendor.**

## S-Tier — Genuinely worth it (but not from this vendor)

- **GHK-Cu** (`CU50` $39 / `CU100` $59) — best-evidence cosmetic peptide, but **use it topically**, not as this injectable vial. Buy a finished 1–2% serum instead. Skin/hair, near-zero risk, fits his existing skincare routine. See [[Peptide Tier List Complete]].
- **Thymosin Alpha-1** (`TA5` $100 / `TA10` $180) — strongest human evidence base of any research peptide (Phase III, immune modulation). Genuinely useful for an endurance athlete's post-training immune dip — but get it **compounded/prescribed**, not grey-market.

## A-Tier — Worth considering, specific use, medical oversight

- **BPC-157** (`BC5` $39 / `BC10` $59) — best peptide for tendon/gut repair; **use on an overuse injury, not daily**. Animal-heavy evidence, thin human data. WADA-legal. Oral capsule is the safer entry point.
- **Semax** (`XA5` $39 / `XA10` $69) — BDNF-upregulating nootropic; reasonable for EE study sprints. Russian clinical evidence, minimal side effects. Nasal route.

## B-Tier — Situational / file for later

- **Selank** (`SK5` $39 / `SK10` $69) — clean anxiolytic for exam stress; pairs with Semax.
- **KPV** (`KP10` $69) — gut anti-inflammatory during heavy training blocks.
- **PT-141 / Bremelanotide** (`P41` $69) — FDA-approved for sexual desire; **not indicated at 19** unless libido issues emerge.
- **Tesamorelin** (`TSM5` $99 …) — best-evidence GH-axis peptide, but for **30s+ body comp**, not now.
- **Ipamorelin** (`IP5` $49) / **CJC-1295 no-DAC** (`CND5` $79) / **CJC+Ipa blends** (`CP10` $109 / `CP20` $179) / **Sermorelin** (`SMO5` $69) — the GH-pulse stack. **Redundant at 19** (peak natural GH); file for 25–30+. WADA-legal but pituitary tinkering with no upside now.
- **AHK-Cu** (`AU50` $59) — topical hair copper peptide; weaker evidence than GHK-Cu. Optional add-on.

2. **The GH-axis column is redundant at 19.** Your natural GH is at its lifetime peak; HGH itself is banned and risky. File the *peptide* GH-pulse stack (CJC-1295 no-DAC + ipamorelin) for your late 20s–30s if recovery ever becomes limiting.
3. **Anything WADA-banned is disqualifying if you race** — HGH, IGF-1 LR3, TB-500 (and the GLOW/KLOW/BB blends), AOD-9604, MGF, HCG, melanotans, myostatin agents.
4. **The only genuinely worth-it items** are GHK-Cu (topical), Thymosin Alpha-1 (training immunity), BPC-157 (injury repair), and Semax/Selank (study) — **and you'd want those pharmaceutical-grade via a prescriber, not from this vendor.**
