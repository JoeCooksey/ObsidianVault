# Costco Vegetable Soup Recipes

Seven soup recipes buildable entirely from Costco produce and spices. All use the ingredient set in [[Costco Pantry for Homemade Soup]].

---

## 1. Classic Vegetable Soup (Everyday Staple)
**Time**: 40 min | **Difficulty**: Easy | **Servings**: 6–8

**Costco Ingredients:**
- 1 large onion, diced
- 3 carrots, sliced
- 3 celery stalks, sliced
- 2 medium russet potatoes, cubed
- 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 cups Kirkland Stir-Fry Frozen Blend (or any frozen mixed veg)
- 6 cups Kirkland Organic Chicken Stock
- 2 tbsp Kirkland EVOO

**Spices:** salt, black pepper, thyme (1 tsp), bay leaf (1–2), Kirkland No-Salt Seasoning (1 tsp)

**Method:**
1. Heat EVOO in large pot over medium heat.
2. Add onion, celery, carrots — sauté 5–7 min until softened.
3. Add garlic, cook 1 min.
4. Add potatoes and broth. Bring to boil.
5. Reduce heat, add thyme, bay leaf, salt, pepper. Simmer 15 min.
6. Add frozen vegetables. Simmer 10 more min.
7. Remove bay leaf, adjust seasoning, serve.

**Pro tips:** Add a parmesan rind while simmering for depth (remove before serving). Dice potatoes small (½ inch) so they cook fully.

## 2. Roasted Tomato Basil Soup
**Time**: 50 min | **Difficulty**: Easy | **Servings**: 4–6

**Costco Ingredients:**
- 3 lbs fresh tomatoes (Roma or Campari), halved
- 1 large onion, quartered
- 6 garlic cloves, whole
- 3 tbsp Kirkland EVOO
- 2 cups Kirkland Chicken Stock

**Spices:** salt, black pepper, 1 tsp dried basil, ½ tsp oregano, pinch red pepper flakes

**Method:**
1. Preheat oven to 450°F.
2. Toss tomatoes, onion quarters, and garlic with EVOO, salt, pepper on a large baking sheet.
3. Roast 30 min, stirring once at 15 min, until caramelized and slightly charred at edges.
4. Transfer everything (including all juices) to a blender. Add basil, oregano, red pepper.
5. Blend smooth. Pour into pot.
6. Add chicken stock to desired consistency. Simmer 10 min.
7. Season to taste. Finish with a drizzle of EVOO.

**Pro tips:** Roasting concentrates sugars and removes excess water — critical for flavor depth. Don't skip. For creamy version: blend in 3 tbsp heavy cream or a drizzle of coconut milk.

## 3. French Onion Soup
**Time**: 75 min (45 min hands-off caramelizing) | **Difficulty**: Medium | **Servings**: 4

**Costco Ingredients:**
- 5–6 large onions (~3 lbs), thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp Kirkland EVOO
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 6 cups Kirkland Chicken Stock (or beef-style if available)

**Spices:** 1 tsp fresh or dried thyme, 2 bay leaves, salt, black pepper, 1 tsp Worcestershire (optional), 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar

**Method:**
1. Melt butter + EVOO in heavy-bottomed pot over medium-low heat.
2. Add sliced onions. Cook, stirring every 5 min, for 40–45 min until deep golden brown and jammy. Do not rush this step. Add salt early to draw out moisture.
3. Add garlic, cook 1 min. Add balsamic, stir to deglaze.
4. Add broth, thyme, bay leaves, Worcestershire, pepper.
5. Bring to simmer, cook 15 min.
6. Remove bay leaves. Taste and adjust.

**Classic topping** (optional): ladle into oven-safe bowls, top with a slice of toasted baguette + shredded Gruyère or Swiss, broil 2–4 min until bubbly.

**Key rule**: Low and slow on the onions — caramelizing is not optional. Rushing this step produces a totally different (inferior) soup.

## 4. Minestrone
**Time**: 45 min | **Difficulty**: Easy | **Servings**: 6–8

**Costco Ingredients:**
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 carrots, sliced
- 3 celery stalks, sliced
- 1 zucchini or 2 bell peppers, diced
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 can (28 oz) Kirkland diced tomatoes
- 6 cups Kirkland Chicken Stock
- 1 can white beans or kidney beans (or use dried, cooked)
- 1 cup small pasta (ditalini, elbow) — optional

**Spices:** 1 tsp Italian seasoning, ½ tsp oregano, ½ tsp basil, ¼ tsp red pepper flakes, 1–2 bay leaves, salt, pepper, Kirkland No-Salt Seasoning

**Method:**
1. Sauté onion + carrot + celery in EVOO, 5–7 min.
2. Add garlic, cook 1 min.
3. Add bell peppers/zucchini, cook 3 min.
4. Add diced tomatoes (with liquid), broth, all spices, and bay leaves.
5. Bring to boil, reduce to simmer 20 min.
6. Add beans. If using pasta, add in last 10 min.
7. Remove bay leaves. Finish with fresh herbs or Kirkland No-Salt Seasoning.

**Key rule**: Add pasta last — it absorbs broth quickly and turns to mush if it sits. If making ahead, keep pasta separate and add to individual bowls.

## 5. Curried Lentil Vegetable Soup
**Time**: 40 min | **Difficulty**: Easy | **Servings**: 6

**Costco Ingredients:**
- 1 large onion, diced
- 4 carrots, diced
- 2 medium potatoes, cubed
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup red lentils (Costco bulk)
- 6 cups Kirkland Chicken Stock
- 1 can Kirkland coconut milk (optional — adds creaminess)

**Spices:** 2 tsp curry powder, 1 tsp Kirkland Ground Turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp coriander, ½ tsp garam masala, pinch cayenne, salt

**Method:**
1. Sauté onion in EVOO over medium heat, 5 min.
2. Add garlic, cook 1 min.
3. Add all spices (curry, turmeric, cumin, coriander, garam masala, cayenne). Cook 1–2 min — "blooming" spices in oil unlocks full flavor.
4. Add carrots, potatoes, lentils, broth.
5. Bring to boil, reduce heat, simmer 25–30 min until lentils are fully soft and beginning to dissolve.
6. Stir in coconut milk if using. Simmer 5 more min.
7. Season with salt. Finish with a squeeze of lemon.

**Pro tips:** Red lentils dissolve as they cook, naturally thickening the soup. Green lentils hold shape better if you want distinct pieces. Blooming spices in Step 3 is critical — the soup tastes flat if you skip it.

## 6. Quick Frozen-Vegetable Broth Soup (15-Min Weeknight)
**Time**: 15 min | **Difficulty**: Very easy | **Servings**: 4

**Costco Ingredients:**
- 3 cups Kirkland Stir-Fry Frozen Blend (broccoli, snap peas, green beans, carrots, mushrooms, peppers, onions, water chestnuts)
- 1 onion, diced (or use the onions already in the blend)
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 6 cups Kirkland Chicken Stock

**Spices:** 1 tsp Kirkland No-Salt Seasoning, salt, pepper, thyme or Italian seasoning (½ tsp)

**Method:**
1. Sauté diced onion in EVOO, 3 min.
2. Add garlic, 1 min.
3. Pour in chicken stock. Bring to boil.
4. Add frozen vegetable blend straight from freezer. Return to boil.
5. Simmer 8–10 min until vegetables are tender.
6. Season with No-Salt Seasoning, salt, pepper.

**Upgrade options:** Add beaten egg at the end (stir in while simmering for egg-drop style), add ginger for Asian-style, add coconut milk for Thai-style. This is the "no time, no energy" default soup.

## 7. Mushroom Garlic Soup
**Time**: 35 min | **Difficulty**: Easy | **Servings**: 4

**Costco Ingredients:**
- 1.5 lbs Chef's Blend mushrooms (sliced — button, cremini, shiitake mix)
- 1 large onion, diced
- 6 garlic cloves, minced (more is better here)
- 4 cups Kirkland Chicken Stock
- 2 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp EVOO

**Spices:** 1 tsp thyme, ½ tsp smoked paprika, salt, black pepper

**Method:**
1. Melt butter + EVOO in pot over medium-high heat.
2. Add sliced mushrooms — do NOT stir for first 3–4 min; let them sear and brown.
3. Stir, cook until all moisture evaporates (~8 min total). Mushrooms should be dark and concentrated.
4. Add onion, sauté 3 min. Add garlic, cook 1 min.
5. Add thyme, paprika, salt, pepper.
6. Add chicken stock, simmer 15 min.
7. Optional: blend ½ the soup, return to pot — creates creamy texture with chunky mushroom pieces.

**Key rule**: Mushrooms must sear, not steam. Don't crowd the pan and don't stir immediately — water needs to evaporate first or you get soggy gray mushrooms instead of golden umami bombs.

## Universal Soup Principles
1. **Mirepoix (onion + carrot + celery) = the base** of most soups. Sauté in fat before adding liquid.
2. **Bloom spices in oil** before adding liquid — 1–2 min of heating spices in fat dramatically intensifies flavor.
3. **Salt in layers**, not all at the end — season the vegetables while they cook, not just the finished broth.
4. **Acid finishes** (lemon juice, balsamic, wine) added at the end brighten all soups.
5. **Soups are better the next day** — make double batches; freeze half in quart containers.
