# Consumer Purchase Value Tier List

Organized by category with worth-it / not-worth-it calls across all price ranges. Principle: **cost per use beats sticker price**. A $200 item used daily for 5 years costs $0.11/day. A $30 item used 3 times costs $10/use.

---

## The Master Framework

**Buy it**: daily use + long lifespan + real health/productivity impact + no free substitute  
**Skip it**: low frequency + single-purpose + subscription required + brand premium > quality premium

---

## By Price Tier

### Under $20

**Worth Every Penny**
- Microfiber cloths 10-pack ($6–10) — replaces paper towels permanently
- Digital kitchen scale ($10–15) — precision cooking, coffee, portioning
- Jump rope ($8–12) — full cardio in 15 min; no subscription
- Sleep mask, contoured ($8–15) — light blocking → better sleep architecture
- Tongue scraper ($5–8) — removes bacteria toothbrush misses; daily hygiene
- Microplane zester ($12–18) — restaurant-grade; used weekly for years
- Kitchen scissors ($10–15) — replaces 20% of cutting board work
- Generic ibuprofen/acetaminophen ($5–8) — identical molecules to brand names at 2–4× less

**Skip These**
- Garlic press — harder to clean than mincing; takes 10 sec with a knife
- Banana/avocado slicer — knife + spoon; one-trick plastic clutter
- Paper towels (ongoing) — microfiber cloths are better and cheaper at scale
- Premium hand sanitizer ($12+) — basic formula kills identical 99.9% of germs

---

### $20–$100

**Worth It**
- Budget fitness tracker (Fitbit Inspire 3, ~$70) — 10-day battery; steps/sleep/HR; saves $300+ vs. flagship
- Electric kettle ($25–40) — daily use; 60-second heat; auto shutoff
- Insulated water bottle ($30–45) — cold 24h; daily carry; replaces hundreds of plastic bottles
- French press ($25–35) — better coffee than K-cup; $0.15/cup vs. $0.70–1.20/pod
- Keychron entry mechanical keyboard ($50–80) — years of daily typing; PBT keycaps
- Quality no-show socks 6-pack ($25–40) — stay on; 2–3 year lifespan
- Foam roller high-density ($25–40) — daily active recovery; replaces many massage gun use cases
- Aquaphor 32oz ($15–20) — replaces 5 single-purpose creams (lips, dry skin, minor cuts, chafing)
- Magnesium glycinate ($15–25) — sleep + muscle recovery; one of few supplements with clean RCT evidence
- Bitwarden password manager (free or $10/year) — best ROI per dollar in security

**Skip These**
- K-cup machine ($60–90) — $0.70–1.20 per pod; worse coffee than a $25 French press; plastic waste
- Extended warranties on cheap electronics — often 50% of replacement cost; most items don't fail
- WHOOP subscription ($30/month) — same data as $70 Fitbit or free apps
- Name-brand grocery staples — Consumer Reports blind tests show store brands identical
- Smart appliances with app dependency (Wi-Fi kettle, etc.) — apps die in 2–3 years
- Budget DSLR in 2026 — phone cameras surpassed entry DSLRs; mirrorless is the new standard

---

### $100–$500

**Worth It**
- Seiko 5 Sports ($180–280) — automatic movement; sapphire; 100m water resistance; decades of use
- Ergonomic desk chair ($150–300) — 6–8 hrs/day seated; back pain ROI is enormous
- Oscilloscope, Rigol DS1054Z ($350) — EE essential; can't simulate real circuit debugging; career tool
- Standing desk converter ($100–200) — reduces back pain; increases afternoon energy
- Quality mattress topper ($100–200) — 2,500+ hrs/year use; sleep quality = highest ROI health lever
- MacBook Air M-series refurbished ($400–600) — 3–5 year lifespan; best battery in class
- Running shoes (Brooks/ASICS proper fit, $120–160) — injury prevention worth 10× their cost
- Second monitor 1080p ($100–180) — 20–40% workflow speed increase in productivity studies
- Chef's knife, Victorinox Fibrox 8" ($45–60) — outperforms $300 knives in Cook's Illustrated tests; 10+ year lifespan

**Skip These**
- Smart home starter kit ($150–300) — 70% of features unused after 2 months
- DSLR camera body without lens investment plan ($300–500) — dead-end ecosystem; mirrorless replaced it
- Premium protein powders ($60–80/month) — identical macros to $25 generic; marketing is the product
- Budget robot vacuum under $250 — struggles with edges, carpet, pet hair; worth it only at $350+
- Juice cleanse programs ($150–300) — no clinical evidence; high sugar; no advantage over eating less sugar
- Peloton app subscription without the bike — YouTube has equivalent free classes; high abandonment

---

### $500–$2,000

**Worth It**
- Quality laptop (MacBook Air 15" M3, ThinkPad X1, $700–1,200) — 5–7 year daily tool; single best ROI purchase for knowledge workers
- Quality road/gravel bike ($800–1,500 used) — health + transport + hobby; 10+ year lifespan; daily use
- Good mattress ($500–1,000) — 2,500+ hrs/year; foundational health investment
- Noise-canceling headphones (Sony WH-1000XM5, $300–380) — worth it for daily commuters/open office workers; concentration multiplier
- Dental work (preventive + early intervention) — $800 crown now vs. $5,000–10,000 bridge later; asymmetric ROI
- Quality monitor 27" IPS 4K ($350–600) — 8+ hrs/day; eye strain + productivity

**Skip These**
- New flagship iPhone every year (~$1,000) — 3-year-old phone does 95% identically; $300/year savings compounds to $3,600 over a decade
- Peloton Bike+ ($2,500 + $44/month) — same units sell for $200–400 on Facebook Marketplace 2 years post-launch
- Apple Vision Pro ($3,500) — majority of users stop using within 4 weeks (2025 surveys); technology not ready
- Gaming PC "future-proofed" at $1,500–2,000 — $700–800 handles 95% of current games; architecture changes faster than future-proofing
- Luxury designer items at retail — same brands 50–70% cheaper used; brand premium rarely matches quality premium

---

### $2,000+

**Worth It (High Leverage)**
- Index fund investments (any amount, started early) — S&P 500 beats 92% of active managers; $200/month at 19 = $1.87M at 65 at 10% avg
- Home gym setup (barbell + plates + rack + bench, $800–1,500) — replaces $500–1,200/year gym membership; 20+ year lifespan
- Therapy / professional coaching ($100–200/session) — compounding ROI; prevents career/relationship derailments costing 100× more
- STEM university education — EE median starting salary $75–90k; break-even on tuition in 2–5 years
- High-quality kitchen appliances (KitchenAid stand mixer, $300–500) — 30+ year lifespan; used daily

**Skip These (Capital Destroyers)**
- New luxury car off the lot — 20% value loss day one; 2–3 year old used car is the play
- Timeshares ($15,000–30,000) — notoriously impossible to exit; maintenance fees compound indefinitely
- Whole life insurance (young healthy person) — term life + invest the difference outperforms universally; heavy commission product
- Stacking all streaming subscriptions simultaneously ($100+/month) — rotate 1–2 at a time; share or use library
- Luxury gym membership (Equinox, $200–300/month) — 80% of goals achievable at $30/month Planet Fitness

## Category-Specific Verdicts

### Coffee
- **Worth it**: French press ($25), quality burr grinder ($50–80), electric kettle ($30)
- **Not worth it**: K-cup machines, $7/day Starbucks habit ($2,500/year), pod subscriptions
- **Best upgrade**: French press + burr grinder + good beans = $0.40/cup café-quality at home

### Tech
- **Worth it**: Mechanical keyboard, second monitor, quality laptop (4+ year hold), good headphones (if daily use)
- **Not worth it**: Annual flagship phone upgrade, smart home hubs, DSLR in 2026, Apple Vision Pro

### Health/Fitness
- **Worth it**: Running shoes (proper fit), foam roller, fitness tracker (basic), home gym long-term
- **Not worth it**: WHOOP subscription, fancy ab gadgets, juice cleanses, luxury gym memberships vs. home gym

### Food/Kitchen
- **Worth it**: Chef's knife (Victorinox), kitchen scale, microplane, electric kettle, kitchen scissors
- **Not worth it**: Garlic press, single-use gadgets, name-brand packaged food vs. store brand, K-cups

### Personal Care
- **Worth it**: Quality toothbrush (Oral-B iO), SPF 50 sunscreen, Aquaphor, tongue scraper
- **Not worth it**: Most "detox" skincare systems, expensive supplements with no RCT evidence, multi-step kits vs. simple 4-step routine

### Sleep
- **Worth it**: Good mattress ($500–1,000), sleep mask, magnesium glycinate, blackout curtains
- **Not worth it**: Sleep tracking subscriptions (phone apps do it free), $400+ "smart" mattresses with app dependency

### Fashion
- **Worth it**: Quality basics (good jeans, neutral t-shirts, versatile jacket) — cost per wear math dominates
- **Not worth it**: Luxury designer at retail (buy used), trendy fast fashion (doesn't last), buying for occasions then never wearing

---

## The Ultimate Single Rule

> **Spend more on the things you use every single day. Spend less on everything else.**

Daily items where an upgrade matters: chair, mattress, shoes, keyboard, water bottle, coffee method, headphones.

Items where brand premium rarely pays: groceries, supplements, fashion, warranties, subscriptions.
