# Imagined Orders

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] term for the **shared rule-systems that large societies organize themselves around** — Hammurabi's Code, the Declaration of Independence, money, hierarchy, human rights. They feel objective and natural to those inside them, but they are *intersubjective* inventions: real only because enough people believe in them together.

Three features make an imagined order durable: it is embedded in the material world (architecture, dress, ritual), it shapes our desires from birth, and it is **inter-subjective** (it survives the death of any individual believer because it lives in the collective imagination). Harari notes the first rule of sustaining one: *never admit it is imagined.* It is the institutional form of [[Shared Fictions]], and large-scale imagined orders became necessary after the [[Agricultural Revolution]].
