# Multi-Flush Rehydration (Dunking)

A colonized substrate block usually produces mushrooms in **flushes** (waves) separated by a week or two of rest. **Rehydration** — replacing the water the block lost growing the first flush — coaxes out additional flushes. The aggressive version is **dunking** (full submersion); for AIO bags it is often unnecessary (Source: [[Zombie Myco — Revitalization (Rehydration) TEK]]).

## Between flushes

1. **Harvest the entire flush** down to the substrate surface — remove every mushroom, including aborts, to prevent rot and contamination.
2. **Re-establish humidity**: mist the hood interior several times over a few hours, then close it to let CO₂ and moisture rebuild.
3. Wait. A second flush of pins typically appears within ~1–2 weeks.

(Source: [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]])

## Dunking (cold-water soak) — the stronger method

If the block has visibly shrunk and dried, fully rehydrate it:

1. Submerge the whole block in **non-chlorinated / distilled water at ~65–70°F**.
2. Soak **4–6 hours** (do not exceed 12 hours — too long drowns the mycelium and invites bacteria).
3. **Drain thoroughly** for 30–60 minutes, then return to fruiting conditions.

(Source: [[Zombie Myco — Revitalization (Rehydration) TEK]])

> [!note] For AIO bags there is usually enough water already inside for a second flush. A gentle mist + humidity dome is often all that's needed, and a full dunk adds stress and a contamination vector. Dunk only if the block is clearly dried out.

## What to expect

Second flushes are typically **50% or less** of the first by weight, and each subsequent flush is smaller and more contamination-prone. A common AIO outcome is one large first flush (~2–3 oz dried from a 5 lb bag) plus a smaller second flush. Once flushes shrink and contamination risk climbs, retire the block (Source: [[Zombie Myco — Revitalization (Rehydration) TEK]]).
