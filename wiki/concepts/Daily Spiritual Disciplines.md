# Daily Spiritual Disciplines

The set of repeatable practices that form the faith domain of a daily habit system. In the Christian tradition surveyed here, daily disciplines build character and deepen relationship with God; "good habits — prayer, gratitude, obedience — shape our character" (Source: Crossway, Bible verses on spiritual discipline).

## The Core Practices

- **Scripture reading** — making daily Bible reading a priority is treated as non-negotiable for growth (Source: GrowGodly).
- **Prayer** — across distinct modes, each with a purpose (Source: FaithTime):
  - *Adoration* — praise centered on God's character
  - *Confession* — clears conscience, restores humility
  - *Thanksgiving* — cultivates gratitude
  - *Supplication / intercession* — requests for self and others
  - *Contemplative* — silent listening
- **Gratitude** — naming specifics; a powerful aspect of discipline.
- **Solitude / silence** — short daily listening, away from noise.
- **Community & service** — relational practices that, while not solo-daily, anchor the weekly cadence.

## How To Start (7-Day On-Ramp)
A graded entry that uses the [[Four Laws of Behavior Change]] (start tiny, make it easy):
- **Days 1–2:** 2 minutes of adoration + gratitude each morning.
- **Days 3–4:** add one specific request + one minute of listening in the evening.
- **Days 5–7:** share one prayer request with a friend; record an answered prayer.

(Source: Cru / FaithTime seven-day jumpstart.)

## Weekly & Beyond
Daily disciplines roll up into the [[Multi-Level Review Cadence]]: weekly corporate worship and an act of service; quarterly evaluation of which disciplines held; an annual study or growth focus.

> [!note] The sources surveyed are predominantly evangelical Christian (Cru, The Gospel Coalition, Crossway, ReFrame). Daily practices in other traditions (e.g. Islamic salah, Jewish shacharit, Buddhist meditation) were not covered here. **medium confidence** within the Christian frame.
