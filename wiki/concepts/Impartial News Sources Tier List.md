# Impartial News Sources Tier List

Ratings sourced from Media Bias/Fact Check (MBFC), AllSides, and Ad Fontes Media — three independent methodology systems. See [[Media Literacy and Bias Detection]] for how to use these ratings.

> **Critical caveat**: No source is truly unbiased. Every outlet reflects institutional incentives, editorial judgment, and financial pressure. This list identifies sources with the *lowest measurable bias* and *highest factuality* — not zero bias.

---

## S-Tier — Least Biased, Very High Factuality

These are wire services and public broadcasters. Their business model structurally incentivizes accuracy (wire services are sold to outlets across the political spectrum; a one-sided wire service loses clients). Use these as your factual baseline.

| Source | MBFC Rating | AllSides Rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reuters** | Least Biased / Very High Factuality | Center | Gold standard wire service; structural incentive to be trusted by all outlets |
| **Associated Press (AP)** | Least Biased / Very High Factuality | Center | Most widely distributed wire; US domestic and international coverage |
| **PBS NewsHour** | Least Biased / Very High Factuality | Center | Public broadcaster; no commercial advertising; strong long-form reporting |

**Best use**: Read wire-service summaries for the factual skeleton of any story, then use other sources for context.

---

## A-Tier — Center-Rated, High Factuality

Respected outlets with strong editorial standards and slight-to-center bias ratings across independent systems.

| Source | MBFC Rating | AllSides Rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BBC News** | Least Biased / Mostly Factual | Center-Left (slight) | Global reach; UK public broadcaster; slight lean right per MBFC, slight lean left per AllSides — aggregate = center |
| **NPR** | Center-Left / Very High Factuality | Lean Left | Strong journalism; slight progressive lean in story selection, but high factuality |
| **The Economist** | Center / High Factuality | Center | UK-based; classical liberal (free market + socially centrist); analytical depth |
| **Bloomberg News** | Center / High Factuality | Center | Finance and policy focus; owned by Bloomberg LP (Michael Bloomberg); strong data journalism |
| **Christian Science Monitor** | Least Biased / High Factuality | Center | One of the most consistently low-bias outlets; read by media literacy educators |
| **Al Jazeera English** | Center-Left / High Factuality | Center-Left | Qatar state-funded (source of potential bias on Gulf geopolitics); strong otherwise |

---

## B-Tier — Slight Lean, High Factuality

These outlets have a moderate lean in story selection or framing but maintain high journalistic standards. Good for depth and analysis — read alongside sources from the other direction to balance.

| Source | Bias Direction | AllSides Rating | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Wall Street Journal (News)** | Lean Right | Lean Right | News section ≠ Opinion section; news reporting is high-quality; editorial pages are conservative |
| **Financial Times** | Center-Left | Center | UK-based; global economics and policy; strong on finance; slight globalist lean |
| **Washington Post** | Lean Left | Lean Left | Owned by Jeff Bezos; strong investigative reporting; noticeable progressive lean in story selection |
| **New York Times (News)** | Lean Left | Lean Left | Elite standard-setting paper; high factuality but clear lean in coverage framing |
| **The Guardian** | Left | Left | UK-based; strong long-form; clear progressive perspective; pair with WSJ for balance |

## C-Tier — Clear Bias (Still Factual, Use Carefully)

| Source | Bias Direction | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Fox News** | Lean Right–Right | Mix of high-quality news reporters and opinion hosts; distinguish clearly |
| **MSNBC** | Lean Left–Left | Opinion-dominant; pair everything from this source with Right-leaning or Center coverage |
| **Vox** | Left | Explanatory journalism; progressive perspective is baked into framing |
| **The Daily Wire** | Right | Conservative perspective; check factuality per-article |
| **HuffPost** | Left | Tabloid tendencies; check claims independently |

## Tools for Checking Any Source

- **mediabiasfactcheck.com** — search any outlet; returns bias rating + factuality score + specific error history
- **AllSides.com/media-bias** — 2,400+ sources rated; multi-partisan editorial reviews + blind bias surveys
- **Ground News** — real-time bias overlay on current stories; shows ownership and funding; see [[News Tools and Apps Stack]]
- **Ad Fontes Media Bias Chart** — visual chart placing outlets on bias (L↔R) and reliability (high↔low) axes simultaneously
