# Stoicism

An ancient Greek and Roman philosophy (Zeno, Seneca, Epictetus, Marcus Aurelius) centered on living in accordance with virtue and reason. Its practical core is the **[[Dichotomy of Control]]**: focus only on what is up to you — your judgments, choices, and actions — and accept everything else (other people, outcomes, reputation, the past) with equanimity.

Modern popularizers like [[Ryan Holiday]] (*[[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday|Ego Is the Enemy]]*, *The Obstacle Is the Way*) translate it into a toolkit for ambition, adversity, and emotional self-management. It underlies much of contemporary self-help — including the control/response split at the heart of *[[The Let Them Theory - Mel Robbins|The Let Them Theory]]*.

**Key practices:** premeditation of adversity, the view from above, [[Euthymia|tranquility through self-knowledge]], treating obstacles as material for virtue, and separating events from our opinions about them.
