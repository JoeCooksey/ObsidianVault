# All-in-One Mushroom Grow Bag

An **all-in-one (AIO) grow bag** is a single sealed, pre-sterilized bag that combines the grain-spawn and bulk-substrate stages of mushroom cultivation into one container, so the entire grow — inoculation, colonization, and fruiting — happens without ever opening the bag until harvest. It is the lowest-effort, lowest-contamination entry point to cultivation (Source: [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]]).

## Anatomy

A standard AIO bag contains, bottom to top:

- **Grain layer** — sterilized grain (rye, milo, or birdseed) at the bottom; the nutrient-dense layer the culture colonizes first.
- **Bulk substrate layer** — a low-nutrient water-holding layer the colonized grain spreads into for fruiting. Usually **CVG** (coco coir + vermiculite + gypsum) or a manure-based blend (Source: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]]).
- **Self-healing injection port** — a rubber/silicone patch on the front that reseals after the needle is withdrawn, allowing sterile inoculation without opening the bag.
- **0.2-micron filter patch** — a microporous patch that lets the mycelium breathe (gas exchange) while blocking airborne contaminant spores.

## Why it works

The filter patch and injection port let the bag stay sealed through the two most contamination-vulnerable phases (inoculation and colonization). The grower only opens it once — to start fruiting — which is why AIO bags have a much higher beginner success rate than open-air methods (Source: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]]).

## AIO bag vs. monotub

- **AIO bag**: passive, self-contained, hands-off, smaller yield (typically 1 large flush of ~2–3 oz dried from a 5 lb bag). Best for beginners.
- **Monotub**: larger plastic bin, more substrate, higher yield, but requires manual fresh-air exchange and humidity management and offers more contamination surface. Better for experienced growers who want volume and control (Source: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]]).

> [!note] The technique below is identical for gourmet species (oyster, lion's mane) and dung-loving species. The bag does not know or care what culture is injected — see [[Psilocybin Legal Status (US)]] for the legal layer.

## Full workflow (the four phases)

1. **Inoculate** through the port → [[Sterile Inoculation Technique]]
2. **Colonize** in the dark at 72–76°F → [[Mycelium Colonization Phase]]
3. **Break and shake** at 50–75% to speed full colonization → [[Break and Shake (Spawn Distribution)]]
4. **Fruit** by opening the bag and creating FAE + humidity + light → [[Mushroom Fruiting Conditions]]

Then harvest, optionally rehydrate for a second flush ([[Multi-Flush Rehydration (Dunking)]]), and dry for storage ([[Drying and Storing Psilocybin Mushrooms]]).
