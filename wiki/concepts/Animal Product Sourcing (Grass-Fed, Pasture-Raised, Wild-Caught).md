# Animal Product Sourcing (Grass-Fed, Pasture-Raised, Wild-Caught)

Which animal-product upgrades are worth the premium and which labels are marketing. **Ranked by how much the label actually changes what you get.**

## The Worth-It Ranking

| Upgrade | Worth it? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Pasture-raised + Certified Humane eggs** | ✅ **Yes** (best value upgrade) | Real welfare + measurable nutrition edge; cheap per gram of protein |
| **Small wild fish** (sardines, anchovies, wild salmon) | ✅ **Yes** | Low mercury + high omega-3 + cheap — the cheat code |
| **Grass-fed beef** | 🟡 **Marginal** | Real but tiny nutrition gain; buy for taste/welfare/environment, not health |
| **"Free-range" / "cage-free" eggs** | 🟡 **Minor** | Modest welfare step; little nutrition difference |
| **"Natural" / "farm-fresh" anything** | ❌ **No** | Unregulated marketing words, mean nothing |

## Beef: Grass-Fed vs Grain-Fed
- Grass-fed has **2–5× more omega-3** and **~2× CLA** — but the **absolute amount is tiny** (~0.015 g vs 0.003 g per 3 oz). You'd need ~100× the beef to match one salmon serving (Source: [[Grass-Fed vs Grain-Fed Beef (Healthline + PMC Review)]]).
- **All beef** is excellent for B12, iron, zinc, protein regardless of feed.
- **Verdict:** buy grass-fed for taste/leanness/ethics if you want; don't pay for it expecting an omega-3 benefit. The bigger lever is **lean cuts + avoiding processed meat** (see [[Food Health Tier List — Macronutrients]]).

## Eggs: The Label Hierarchy
**Pasture-raised > free-range > cage-free > conventional/caged** (Source: [[Egg and Fish Sourcing Labels (GoodRx + FDA)]]).
- **"Pasture-raised" has no legal USDA definition** — only meaningful with **Certified Humane** or **Animal Welfare Approved** third-party certification (108 sq ft/hen, real foraging).
- Pasture-raised eggs measured at ~**2× omega-3, 3× vitamin D, 4× vitamin E, 7× beta-carotene** vs. caged-feed eggs.
- **"Cage-free"** can be 1 sq ft/hen indoors — better than cages, not "good."

## Fish: Wild vs Farmed
- **"Small + wild" wins** on mercury, omega-3, and price simultaneously: sardines, anchovies, herring, wild salmon, trout.
- **Farmed** can be *lower* in mercury (controlled feed) but often skews higher omega-6; quality varies wildly by country/operation.
- **FDA "Best Choices" (2–3×/wk):** salmon, sardines, anchovies, trout, herring, shrimp, pollock.
- **Avoid (high mercury):** shark, swordfish, king mackerel, tilefish, bigeye tuna; limit albacore tuna.

## Key Principles
1. **Welfare and nutrition are different reasons to upgrade** — be clear which you're paying for.
2. **Certification beats the adjective** — "Certified Humane / Organic / MSC-certified" carries legal weight; "natural / farm-fresh / cage-free" mostly don't.
3. **The eggs-and-small-fish combo is the highest-ROI sourcing upgrade** — cheap, real nutrition gain, no downside.
4. **Don't let perfect sourcing block eating the food** — conventional eggs and farmed salmon are still excellent foods.
