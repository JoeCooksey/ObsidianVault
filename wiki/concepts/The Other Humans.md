# The Other Humans

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] reminder that Homo sapiens was once **just one of several human species** sharing the planet — Neanderthals in Europe, Homo erectus in Asia, Homo floresiensis ("hobbits") in Indonesia, and others. For most of our existence we were unremarkable, middle-of-the-food-chain animals.

After the [[Cognitive Revolution]], Sapiens spread out of Africa and the other human species vanished — whether by interbreeding (modern non-Africans carry some Neanderthal DNA) or by being out-competed and eliminated. Harari uses this to puncture human exceptionalism: our solitary dominance is recent and was not inevitable, and it may have been built partly on the extinction of our cousins.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 1.*
