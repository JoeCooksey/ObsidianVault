# Credit and the Future

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] insight that **modern capitalism runs on belief in growth.** Credit is trust in a larger tomorrow: lenders advance money today because they expect the economy — and the borrower — to be bigger later.

Before the [[Scientific Revolution]], people assumed the total amount of wealth was roughly fixed (a zero-sum pie), so credit was scarce and distrusted. The new faith that the future would be richer than the present made large-scale credit possible, which funded the [[Science, Empire, Capitalism Feedback Loop]] that then *delivered* the growth — a self-fulfilling cycle. Credit is thus an extension of [[Money as Trust|money-as-trust]], projected forward in time.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 4.*
