# Break and Shake (Spawn Distribution)

**Break and shake** is breaking up the colonized grain inside a sealed bag and kneading it through the substrate so the active mycelium is distributed into hundreds of new growth points, instead of advancing from one front. It **dramatically speeds up full colonization** by giving the mycelium many simultaneous starting points (Source: [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]]).

## When to do it

When the grain layer is **mostly colonized — roughly 50% to 75%**. Too early (under ~30%) risks distributing too little mycelium relative to contaminant odds; too late wastes the speed benefit (Source: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]]).

> [!warning] Do **not** break and shake if you see any green, black, pink, gray-cobwebby, or sour-smelling growth. Shaking a contaminated bag spreads the contaminant everywhere. Inspect first → [[Mushroom Contamination Identification]].

## Procedure (bag stays sealed)

1. Let the bag sit ~5 minutes so the filter inflates it with fresh air (cushions the grain and reduces tearing).
2. From the outside, **break up the colonized grain with your fingers until pieces are roughly marble-sized.**
3. **Knead and shake** thoroughly to mix the colonized grain evenly with any uncolonized grain and the bulk substrate.
4. Flatten the substrate back into an even layer and return the bag to **72–76°F** in the dark.

(Sources: [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]], [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]])

## After break and shake

The bag re-colonizes the redistributed grain and the bulk substrate in roughly **7–14 days**, finishing as a solid white block. Once **100% colonized** (fully white top to bottom) — and ideally after a short consolidation period — it is ready to open and fruit → [[Mushroom Fruiting Conditions]].
