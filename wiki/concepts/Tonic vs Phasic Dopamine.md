# Tonic vs Phasic Dopamine

Dopamine neurons fire in two modes, and conflating them is the source of most dopamine confusion. **Tonic = your baseline / motivation. Phasic = the bursts and dips that signal learning.** (Source: [[Schultz Dopamine Reward Prediction Error Hypothesis]])

---

## The Two Modes

| | Tonic | Phasic |
|---|---|---|
| **Pattern** | slow, steady background firing | fast, transient bursts (and pauses) |
| **Timescale** | seconds–minutes–hours | milliseconds–seconds |
| **Function** | **motivation**, baseline drive, mood, sense of well-being | **learning** signal — encodes [[Reward Prediction Error]] |
| **Maps to** | your "[[Dopamine Baseline and the Pleasure-Pain Balance|baseline]]" | the "dopamine hit" everyone talks about |
| **What raises it** | sleep, sunlight, exercise, connection (slow inputs) | unexpected reward, novelty, drugs |

Fast phasic fluctuations support **learning**; slow tonic changes drive **motivation** (Source: [[Schultz Dopamine Reward Prediction Error Hypothesis]]).

---

## Why This Distinction Is the Whole Game

- The activities that **help you** (cold, sunlight, exercise, meditation) mostly raise **tonic** dopamine — a higher, steadier floor with no crash.
- The activities that **hook you** (drugs, gambling, doomscrolling) mostly drive **phasic** spikes — and big phasic spikes *drag the tonic baseline down* afterward (Source: [[Huberman Lab Episode 39 — Controlling Your Dopamine]]).
- Addiction is, in part, a **shift from a healthy tonic baseline to a spike-and-crash phasic economy**, ending in a low tonic baseline (dopamine deficit).

> [!key] Aim to **raise tonic, ration phasic.** A high, stable baseline is the goal; spikes are spice, not the meal.
