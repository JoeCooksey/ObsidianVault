# Four Quadrants (AQAL)

## Definition
The **four quadrants** are the foundation of the [[AQAL Model (Integral Theory)|AQAL model]]: four irreducible perspectives you can take on *any* situation, formed by crossing two axes — **subjective ↔ objective** and **individual ↔ collective**:

| | Individual | Collective |
|---|---|---|
| **Subjective (interior)** | **I** (Q1) — thoughts, emotions, beliefs; your inner world (mind) | **We** (Q3) — shared culture, values, group beliefs |
| **Objective (exterior)** | **It** (Q2) — brain, nervous system, body; the hard sciences | **Its** (Q4) — systems, structures, institutions |

## Why It Matters
The quadrants tell you **what to look at** when building an [[Good Explanations (Hard-to-Vary)|explanation]]. Perry's rule: *"Your explanation of any situation is only as good as the number of quadrants you've actually looked through,"* and *"you cannot solve a four-quadrant problem from a one-quadrant level of thinking."* **Stupid thinking** is defined here as looking through one quadrant and thinking you see the whole picture. Every quadrant is **constantly influencing every other** at every moment.

## How It Works
Worked example — a "motivation problem":
- **Q1 (I):** "I'm lazy, I have no discipline, something is wrong with me." (Wipes most people out on its own.)
- **Q2 (It):** Weeks of bad sleep, doom-scrolling till 1 a.m., no training → high [[Cortisol Complete Guide|cortisol]], a dysregulated nervous system, no energy.
- **Q3 (We):** Inherited a belief that "real effort = studying 8 hours every day," so you devalue yourself against an arbitrary cultural standard.
- **Q4 (Its):** A smartphone engineered by top scientists to capture attention, draining your limited daily focus.

Mix all four and the "motivation problem" dissolves into a multi-quadrant reality — none of which a single-quadrant explanation could see.

## Cross-Domain Connections
- Maps neatly onto perennial mind/brain and individual/society splits: Q1≈phenomenology, Q2≈neuroscience/biology, Q3≈culture/anthropology, Q4≈systems/economics.
- A practical antidote to **specialist tunnel vision** — why deeply "smart" people make dumb decisions outside their home quadrant (see [[Nine Levels of Thinking]]).
- The four daily questions in the [[Explanation-Building Writing Practice]] are one-per-quadrant.
