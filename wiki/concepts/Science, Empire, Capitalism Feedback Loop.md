# Science, Empire, Capitalism Feedback Loop

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] explanation for why the [[Scientific Revolution]] produced such explosive, sustained power: three forces reinforced one another in a loop.

- **Science** needs funding and problems to solve.
- **Empire** needs new technologies, maps, and knowledge to conquer and administer.
- **Capitalism** needs growth, new markets, and returns on investment.

Each fed the others: empires and capitalists financed science; science gave empires and businesses better ships, weapons, and methods; the resulting conquests and profits funded yet more science. This mutually amplifying triangle — running on the belief in future growth ([[Credit and the Future]]) — is, for Harari, the engine of the modern world.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 4.*
