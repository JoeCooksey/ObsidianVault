# Shared Fictions

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] central thesis in *[[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari|Sapiens]]*: Homo sapiens dominate the planet because we can **cooperate flexibly in massive numbers**, and we do that by collectively believing in things that exist only in our shared imagination — gods, nations, money, laws, corporations, human rights.

A shared fiction is real in its effects even though it has no physical existence: a $20 bill, a nation-state, or a limited-liability company has power only because millions of strangers agree to treat it as real. This ability to create and believe common myths is what broke the ~150-person cooperation ceiling and let humans build cities, armies, religions, and economies. It is the engine beneath [[Imagined Orders]], [[Money as Trust]], [[Empire]], and [[Religion as Unifier]].

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]].*
