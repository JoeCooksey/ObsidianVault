# Signs of an Unhealthy Gut

The warning-sign catalog, ordered from direct (digestive) to indirect (systemic). Pattern to look for: **persistent, recurring, or new-onset** — any single symptom occasionally is normal.

## Digestive Signs (high confidence — direct readout)

- **Persistent bloating** after meals; visible distension; uncomfortable trapped gas
- **Excessive or foul gas** — disproportionate fermentation from an imbalanced microbial population (Source: [[Cleveland Clinic Dysbiosis Overview]])
- **Diarrhea, constipation, or alternating between them**; [[Bristol Stool Scale]] types 1–2 or 5–7 as the norm
- **Ongoing abdominal discomfort** — cramping, burning, pressure
- **Frequent heartburn / indigestion**
- **Stool changes** — new color, mucus, greasy/floating stools (malabsorption), pellet-like or ragged form
- **Straining, urgency, or feeling of incomplete emptying**

## Food-Response Signs (medium-high confidence)

- **New food intolerances** — foods once well tolerated now cause symptoms; suggests altered immune response or increased permeability ([[Leaky Gut and Intestinal Permeability]])
- **Intense sugar/refined-carb cravings** — gut bacteria influence appetite signaling; medium confidence on the mechanism
- **Post-meal systemic reactions** — brain fog, headache, fatigue, itching around meals

## Systemic Signs (medium confidence — associative, many causes possible)

- **Chronic fatigue** despite adequate sleep — impaired absorption + low-grade inflammation
- **Sleep disturbance / insomnia** — gut produces sleep-relevant neurotransmitter precursors
- **Mood changes** — anxiety and depression associate with dysbiosis ([[Microbiota-Gut-Brain Axis Review]]); direction of causation runs both ways
- **Brain fog** — inflammatory byproducts affect cognition
- **Skin problems** — acne, eczema/atopic dermatitis, rosacea, psoriasis flares via the gut–skin axis
- **Unexplained weight changes** in either direction — energy extraction and fat-storage signaling shift with microbial composition
- **Frequent infections** — gut-associated immune tissue underperforming
- **Migraines** — increasingly linked to gut–brain inflammation; medium-low confidence
- **Autoimmune flares** — compromised barrier + dysregulated immune signaling; association documented, causality unproven
- **Bad breath, bleeding gums, frequent cavities** — oral dysbiosis, the upstream end of the same tube (Source: [[Cleveland Clinic Dysbiosis Overview]])

## Common Drivers (what to suspect first)

Antibiotics and NSAIDs · emulsifiers and ultra-processed food · high-sugar low-fiber diet · alcohol · chronic stress · poor sleep — full ranking in [[Gut-Harmful Foods and Actions Tier List]].

> [!warning] Escalation rule
> Any of these PLUS blood in stool, unexplained weight loss, fever, trouble swallowing, anemia, or symptoms persisting >2 weeks → [[GI Red Flag Symptoms]]. Don't self-treat past red flags.

> [!gap] Most systemic signs are non-specific — fatigue, poor sleep, and brain fog have a dozen causes. The gut becomes the leading suspect when systemic signs *cluster with* digestive signs.
