# Focus & Deep Work Habits

Habits 56–70 from [[101 Habits — Dan Zakaria]]. Engineering your attention: remove distraction, protect focus blocks, single-task. Pairs with [[Deep Work]].

## The Habits
56. **Silence unnecessary notifications.**
57. **Time-block your day** — make yourself "actively unavailable" to unimportant things.
58. **Work in focus sprints** — Pomodoro / 90-minute timers, then a proper break.
59. **Single-task, never multitask.**
60. **Keep your phone out of reach.**
61. **Create a distraction-free workspace.**
62. **Prioritize your top 3 tasks.**
63. **Schedule deep-work sessions.**
64. **Avoid context switching** — constant task-flipping is worse than multitasking.
65. **Set clear goals/intentions before working** — "no direction means you go nowhere."
66. **Use website/screen-time blockers** (he uses a "brick" + Unhook for YouTube).
67. **Take intentional breaks.**
68. **Protect your peak energy hours.**
69. **Finish important work before entertainment.**
70. **Review your priorities daily.**

## Notes
- #58/#64 → see [[Deep Work]] and [[Focused vs Diffuse Mode]] for the mechanism (attention residue, switching cost).
- #60/#66 are friction-adding tactics against bad habits — the [[Atomic Habits - James Clear|"make it hard"]] law.
- #62/#65/#70 overlap with [[Time Management Habits]] (#75 80/20, #71 weekly plan).
