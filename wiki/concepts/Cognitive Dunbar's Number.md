# Cognitive Dunbar's Number

The observation (associated with anthropologist Robin Dunbar) that humans can maintain stable, trust-based relationships with only about **150 individuals** through personal acquaintance and gossip alone. Beyond that, groups bound only by who-knows-whom fragment.

In *[[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari|Sapiens]]*, this ceiling is the problem that [[Shared Fictions]] solve: [[Fictive Language|believing the same myth]] — a god, a nation, a brand, a body of law — lets total strangers trust and cooperate without ever meeting. Breaking the 150-person barrier is, for Harari, the precondition for cities, armies, and states.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 1.*
