# Autophagy

The cell's recycling and quality-control process: damaged organelles and misfolded proteins are packaged and degraded for reuse. **Mitophagy** is the autophagy of damaged [[Mitochondria]] specifically.

## Why it matters
- It is the **shared upstream mechanism** behind several optimization plans in [[Research - Most Important Cells in the Human Body]] — it keeps [[Mitochondria]] and [[Stem Cells]] healthy.
- Activation of autophagy is one of the core cellular benefits of fasting, alongside enhanced mitochondrial health and DNA repair. (Source: [[Mattson 2016 — Intermittent Fasting on Health and Disease]], confidence high)
- Declining autophagy is a hallmark of aging; restoring it improves cellular resilience.

## How to activate it (actionable)
1. **Fasting / time-restricted eating** — the strongest physiological trigger (nutrient/energy scarcity). (Source: [[Mattson 2016 — Intermittent Fasting on Health and Disease]])
2. **Exercise** — induces autophagy and mitophagy in muscle and other tissues (via PGC-1α; see [[Mitochondria]]).
3. **Avoid chronic overnutrition** — constant feeding (high insulin/mTOR) suppresses autophagy.
4. **Sleep** — supports cellular clearance cycles.
