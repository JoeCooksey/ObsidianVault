# Dopamine Detox (Evidence Review)

What's real and what's hype about "dopamine detox" / "dopamine fasting." **The name is wrong but the protocol can work** — for the right reason (Source: Cameron Sepah, *Dopamine Fasting 2.0*, 2019).

---

## The Name Is Misleading

You **cannot** fast from dopamine — it's a constantly active neurotransmitter required for movement, motivation, and basic function. Cameron Sepah, who coined "dopamine fasting," **agrees the name is misleading**: the goal is not to lower dopamine but to **reduce impulsive behaviors** that the reward system reinforces (Source: [[Cameron Sepah — Dopamine Fasting 2.0]]). Harvard Health called the viral version "a misunderstanding of science spawning a maladaptive fad."

---

## What It Actually Is

Sepah's original concept is plain **cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) / stimulus control**: scheduled abstinence from a small set of **problem behaviors** (e.g., compulsive phone/social media, gaming, porn, junk food, novelty-seeking) — not sitting in a blank room avoiding all stimulation. The internet warped it into "avoid all pleasure," which is not the protocol.

---

## Does It Work? The Honest Answer

| Claim | Verdict |
|---|---|
| "Resets/lowers your dopamine" | ❌ Not literally — you can't deplete or reset total dopamine by abstaining for a day |
| "Specific dopamine-fasting guides are proven" | ⚠️ Evidence is **anecdotal**; no trials validate the specific fad protocols |
| "Abstaining from a compulsive behavior helps" | ✅ Plausible — it's CBT/stimulus control, which **is** evidence-based for behavioral addictions |
| "Receptors can recover during abstinence" | ✅ Supported — downregulated receptors upregulate over time → [[Dopamine Downregulation and Tolerance]] |

> [!key] What works is **behavioral**, not chemical: stop feeding the over-stimulated circuit so receptors re-sensitize and your [[Dopamine Baseline and the Pleasure-Pain Balance|baseline]] re-levels. Lembke's clinical heuristic is **~30 days** of abstinence from the problem stimulus (Source: [[Anna Lembke — Dopamine Nation]]).

---

## How to Do It Properly

1. **Name the one or two problem behaviors** (not "all pleasure"). Usually the variable-reward 🔴/🟠 items from [[Dopamine Increases by Activity (Tier List)]].
2. **Abstain ~30 days**, using **self-binding** (delete apps, remove access) to make it easy.
3. **Replace, don't just remove** — load 🟢 baseline builders from [[Healthy Dopamine Protocols]].
4. Expect the first **~1–2 weeks to feel worse** (the pain side rebounding) before the baseline recovers.
