# Scrambled Eggs — Masterclass

The runnable guide. Pick a style by the texture you want, then follow the universal rules. All texture decisions trace back to [[Scrambled Egg Science]] (stop before the protein over-tightens).

## Universal rules (apply to every style)
1. **Fresh eggs, room temp** if you can.
2. **Use a nonstick or well-seasoned pan** + a silicone spatula.
3. **Add fat.** Butter in the pan, and optionally cream/crème fraîche in the eggs.
4. **Cook gentler than feels right** and **pull the eggs while still glossy and slightly underdone** — they finish cooking off-heat (carryover).
5. **Season at/near the end** to avoid weeping (or salt 15 min ahead — see [[Scrambled Egg Science]]).
6. **Never overcook.** Dry, gray, weeping eggs = too hot or too long.

---

## Style 1 — Soft & creamy (French / Gordon Ramsay) ★ best-tasting
Custardy, small curds, restaurant-grade.
1. Crack **3 eggs into a cold nonstick pan** with **1 Tbsp butter** (cut into pieces). Don't pre-whisk.
2. Put on **medium-low** heat. Stir constantly with a spatula.
3. **Every 30–60 s, lift the pan off the heat** for ~30 s while still stirring, then return. Repeat ~4–5 min. This keeps the eggs under coagulation temp.
4. When they're a mass of soft, just-set curds, **kill the heat. Stir in 1 Tbsp cold crème fraîche** (cools instantly + adds tang) and season.
5. Plate immediately. (Source: [Gordon Ramsay / MasterClass](https://www.masterclass.com/articles/how-to-make-the-perfect-scrambled-eggs-with-gordon-ramsay))

## Style 2 — Light & fluffy (American diner)
Bigger, airier curds; fast.
1. Beat **2–3 eggs** with **~1 Tbsp water per egg** and a pinch of salt until uniform and slightly frothy.
2. Heat butter in a nonstick pan over **medium**.
3. Pour in, let set ~10 s, then push folds gently from the edges to the center.
4. Pull while still moist; carryover finishes them. (Source: [Food Republic](https://www.foodrepublic.com/1592835/water-versus-milk-fluffiest-scrambled-eggs/))

## Style 3 — Kenji's fast-but-creamy (starch hack)
Soft-scramble texture at weekday speed.
1. Whisk **3 eggs** with **1 tsp cornstarch (or potato/tapioca starch) dissolved in 1 Tbsp water**, plus salt.
2. Cook over **medium-high** with butter, stirring constantly — the starch blocks over-coagulation so higher heat is safe.
3. Done in ~2 min, still creamy. (Source: [The Kitchn on Kenji](https://www.thekitchn.com/kenji-lopez-alt-scrambled-eggs-review-23143709))

> [!tip] Cream-stream variation (Kenji): slowly stream beaten eggs into ~½ cup *simmering heavy cream* (borrowed from egg-drop soup) for silky ribbons in a custard sauce.

|---|---|---|

| Big fluffy curds | Style 2 | medium |
| Creamy + fast | Style 3 | med-high |
