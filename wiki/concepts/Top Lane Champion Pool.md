# Top Lane Champion Pool

Build the pool by **archetype and mastery, not tier list**. Tier lists for the same patch contradict each other (one 26.12 list has Darius S+, another has him D-tier — see [[Patch 26.x Top Lane Tier Lists (Aggregate)]]), but the archetype structure and the mastery benchmark are consensus across every source. (Sources: [[EloAscend — Top Lane Guide 2026]], [[Boosteria — Top Lane Fundamentals Guide]])

## The 3-Champion Structure

| Slot | Archetype | Job | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Blind pick** | Tank / juggernaut with no terrible matchups | First-pick safe, teamfight value when behind | Malphite, Ornn, Garen, Mordekaiser |
| **Comfort carry** | Bruiser or duelist you can snowball on | Win lane, win sides, 1v9 games | Darius, Sett, Jax, Fiora, Camille |
| **Pocket counter** | Answer to your blind pick's worst matchups | Counterpick situations only | Teemo/Kennen (into melee), Poppy (into dashers) |

Two mains + one pocket pick; five-champion pools kill pattern mastery. (Source: [[Boosteria — Top Lane Fundamentals Guide]])

## By Rank (skill-floor guidance)

- **Iron–Silver**: Garen, Darius, Malphite, Mordekaiser — simple kits that punish the mistakes these ranks make constantly; stats win over mechanics
- **Gold–Plat**: Darius, Sett — snowball pressure plus teamfight presence
- **Diamond+**: Fiora, Camille — mechanical expression and 1-3-1 split carry

(Source: [[EloAscend — Top Lane Guide 2026]], medium confidence)

## Mastery Benchmark

30–50+ games per pool champion at ≥50% win rate before adding another. Same finding as the general improvement research: top players average ~33 games/champion, and champion-hopping with the meta resets learning every patch. (Source: [[LoL Improvement Practice System]])

## Archetype Playstyle Cheat Sheet

- **Bully bruisers** (Darius, Renekton): stack waves, zone on last-hits, crash for cheater recalls and dives
- **Scaling tanks** (Ornn, Sion, Malphite): minimize early losses, hold freezes, TP to preserve good states, win the 5v5
- **Split carries** (Jax, Fiora, Camille): control long lanes, avoid auto-pushing into ganks, threaten 1-3-1

(Source: [[Boosteria — Top Lane Fundamentals Guide]])

## Current Meta Snapshot (patch 26.11–26.12, June 2026 — expires fast)

Broadly agreed strong: **Garen, Malphite, Mordekaiser** (and the new champion Zaahen appears on multiple lists). Contested: Darius (S+ on aggregate stat sites, D on one editorial list after bruiser nerfs). AP juggernauts trended up after 26.x fighter nerfs.

> [!gap] Tier placements are low confidence by nature — re-check a stats aggregator (u.gg, lolalytics) on the current patch before locking a pool.
