# AI Training Data Work (Online Income)

Paid, remote, async work **training and evaluating AI models** — writing and grading code, solving STEM problems, ranking model responses, and producing reference solutions that become training/RLHF data. For someone with coding + STEM skills, this is currently one of the highest $/hr online income sources that requires **no client acquisition and no portfolio**.

## Why this is S-tier *for Joe specifically*

- **Pays for his existing skills:** the highest-paying tasks "consistently require coding skills, STEM knowledge, or domain expertise" (Source: [[AI Training Platforms for Coders 2026 — aitrainer.work]]). Python + Calc/Physics/EE is exactly the in-demand profile.
- **Async + flexible:** no fixed schedule — fits a heavy course load and 6×/week training. Work an hour between classes.
- **It is itself AI literacy:** doing the work teaches *how LLMs are trained and where they fail* — and AI skills carry a documented **56% EE wage premium** ([[Research - EE AI Skills and Applications]]). Résumé-legible as "AI/ML model evaluation."

## Pay reality

| Tier | Rate | Who |
|---|---|---|
| General annotation (beginners) | ~$15–20/hr | DataAnnotation entry tasks |
| National avg AI trainer | **~$31/hr** | early 2026 benchmark |
| STEM / coding experts | **$40–$100+/hr** | what Joe qualifies for |
| Top niche (advanced code, specialized domains) | $50–$300+/hr | senior/PhD-level tasks |

(Source: [[AI Training Platforms for Coders 2026 — aitrainer.work]])

## The platforms

- **DataAnnotation.tech** — accepts beginners; good first door; coding tasks pay more.
- **Outlier (Scale AI)** — subject-matter experts in coding/STEM; easy onboarding, project-based, flexible.
- **Mercor** — vetted talent marketplace; longer-term project work; **highest rates outside direct employment** for developers who land projects.
- **Alignerr / Mindrift / RemoExperts** — additional options; most accept applicants globally.

## How to start
1. Apply to **DataAnnotation + Outlier** with the coding/STEM track selected.
2. Onboarding usually involves a graded coding/reasoning assessment — Joe's Python + math is the qualifier.
3. Once trusted, request higher-paying coding/STEM project queues.

> [!gap] Pay rates and project availability on these platforms fluctuate month-to-month and vary by queue; treat the table as a 2026 snapshot, not a guarantee. Hours can be inconsistent — it is a supplement, not a salary.

## Honest caveats
- Work volume is **not guaranteed** — queues dry up; income is lumpy.
- Some platforms have opaque pay/rating systems; read a current review before committing many hours.
- Lower-tier "general annotation" pays poorly — Joe should hold out for the **coding/STEM queues** where his edge actually prices in.
