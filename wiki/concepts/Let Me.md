# Let Me

The second — and, per [[Mel Robbins|Robbins]], the more important — half of the Let Them tool. Once you've released control of others with **[[Let Them]]**, you say **Let Me**: *let me* decide how I respond, where I spend my energy, what boundaries I set, and what I do next.

This is the active, ownership step that prevents "Let Them" from collapsing into passivity or resignation. Letting go of other people is only valuable because it returns your power to the one person you can actually change — yourself. Robbins argues most people remember the catchy first half and forget that the whole point is the second.

*Source: [[The Let Them Theory - Mel Robbins]].*
