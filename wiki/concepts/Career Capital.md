# Career Capital

**The stock of rare and valuable skills you have built — the currency you trade for the things that make a job great** (autonomy, impact, money, meaning). Coined by [[Cal Newport]] in *So Good They Can't Ignore You* (2012).

## The logic

1. The traits that define great work (autonomy, mastery, purpose) are **rare and valuable**.
2. By basic economics, to get them you must offer something rare and valuable in return — i.e. **rare and valuable skills**.
3. Therefore: **become good first, then cash in.** "Follow your passion" is backwards; passion follows mastery. (Source: [[Deep Work — Cal Newport]])

## How it's built

Career capital is accumulated through **[[Deliberate Practice]]** — stretching just beyond current ability, with feedback, on things that feel hard — not through comfortable repetition of what you already do well.

## Why it matters for self-investment

Career capital is the **non-financial asset with the highest leverage**: it compounds (each skill makes the next easier — see [[Compound Time (5-Hour Rule)]]), it can't be taxed or taken from you (Buffett's point), and it converts into both income and freedom. It is the engine behind the skills dimension of [[Research - Best Way to Invest in Yourself]] and pairs with [[Permissionless Leverage]] (code/media) to multiply its output.
