# Draw the Line

[[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]]'s principle that **failure is an event, not an identity.** Ego is what converts "I failed" into "I am a failure" — and that conversion is what turns a recoverable setback into a permanent self-image wound.

Drawing the line means refusing to let a defeat metastasize into your sense of self. You acknowledge the loss, extract the lesson, and move on — without writing it into the story of who you fundamentally are. The event is bounded; you decide where it ends.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Failure."*
