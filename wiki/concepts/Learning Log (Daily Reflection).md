# Learning Log (Daily Reflection)

A short written record kept *during or right after* each learning session — the closing move that converts time-spent into encoded, examined knowledge. Per [[NIU - Reflective Journals and Learning Logs]], **reflection turns experience into learning; experience alone does not.**

## Log vs. Journal

- **Learning log** — documentary: *what I did, what I learned, what ideas/questions it raised.*
- **Reflective journal** — subjective: *my reactions, where I got stuck, what it connected to.*

For a daily learning habit, a hybrid of three lines is enough.

## The Three-Line Daily Entry

1. **What I learned** (one sentence, in my own words — engages the [[Generation Effect (Handwriting)|generation effect]]).
2. **What's still fuzzy** (the open question → becomes tomorrow's first target, making the practice self-feeding).
3. **What it connects to** (a link to something I already know — builds the schema that drives [[Near vs Far Transfer|transfer]]).

## Why It Works

- **Metacognition:** writing forces you to notice the gap between *familiarity* and *understanding* — the same correction the [[The Feynman Technique|Feynman technique]] and [[Metacognition in Problem Solving|post-mortem]] exploit.
- **Generation over recognition:** producing a sentence from memory encodes far better than re-reading ([[Generation Effect (Handwriting)]]).
- **Visible progression:** seeing the log grow is itself motivating and reinforces the [[Never Miss Twice|streak]].
- **Question harvesting:** the "still fuzzy" line gives every next session a ready, specific entry point — no blank-page friction.

## Relation to the Vault's Note Systems

This is the lightweight daily layer beneath the heavier [[Zettelkasten]] / [[How to Take Smart Notes (Ahrens)|smart-notes]] practice: the log is the fast capture at the moment of learning; durable atomic notes are written later from the best of it.
