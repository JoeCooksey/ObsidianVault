# Non-Nutritive Sweeteners Overview

Quick reference to the sweeteners that actually go into diet sodas, so the study findings can be mapped to the right molecule. "Non-nutritive" / "non-sugar" sweeteners (NNS/NSS) provide sweetness with ~zero calories.

## The Ones in Diet Sodas
| Sweetener | Sweetness vs sugar | Where used | Study signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[[Aspartame]]** | ~200× | Diet Coke, Diet Pepsi (classic) | IARC 2B "possible carcinogen" (limited evidence); ADI generous; weakest gut signal |
| **Acesulfame-K (Ace-K)** | ~200× | Almost always blended with aspartame/sucralose | Linked to CHD in Debras 2022; bacteriostatic in vitro |
| **Sucralose** | ~600× | Diet drinks, Splenda | Impaired glycemic response in Suez 2022; reduces butyrate bacteria |
| **Saccharin** | ~300–400× | Tab, Sweet'N Low, some sodas | Clearest microbiome/glycemic signal (Suez) |
| **Stevia (steviol glycosides)** | ~200–300× | "Natural" zero sodas (Zevia, some Coke/Pepsi lines) | Neutral glycemic in Suez; still alters microbiome |
| **Cyclamate** | ~30–50× | Outside US (banned in US since 1969) | — |

## Not Usually in Diet Sodas (but confused with them)
- **Erythritol / xylitol** (sugar alcohols) — used in keto/stevia blends and baking, **not** mainstream diet sodas. Erythritol carries a thrombosis signal ([[Witkowski 2023 - Erythritol and Cardiovascular Risk (Nature Medicine)|Witkowski 2023]]). Important not to blame diet soda for erythritol findings.

## Key Point
"Artificial sweeteners" is not one thing. The evidence differs by molecule: **saccharin/sucralose** carry the clearest metabolic signal, **aspartame** the cancer-headline (but reassuring on dose), **erythritol** the clot signal (but not a soda ingredient).
