# Scrambled Egg Pairings and Add-Ins

What goes well with scrambled eggs. Two categories: **stir-ins** (added to the eggs) and **on-the-side** (the plate).

## The golden timing rules
- **Add cheese at the very end**, just as the eggs set — too early and it turns oily/rubbery. (Source: [ATK](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/articles/5989-10-ways-to-dress-up-simple-scrambled-eggs))
- **Add fresh herbs off the heat** so they stay bright.
- **Pre-cook watery/raw add-ins** (onion, pepper, ham, mushrooms) separately first — dumping them in raw releases water and makes eggs weep.

## Stir-ins (into the eggs)
- **Cheese:** goat cheese, Gruyère, sharp cheddar, pepper jack, mascarpone, feta.
- **Fresh herbs:** chives (the classic), parsley, dill, tarragon, basil.
- **Cooked aromatics/proteins:** sautéed onion + bell pepper, browned ham, smoked salmon (with chives), crumbled bacon, [[Ground Beef Browning — Masterclass|browned ground beef]] for a breakfast scramble.
- **Heat/umami:** red pepper flakes, hot sauce, a dab of miso or soy, sun-dried tomatoes.
- **Finishing fat:** a knob of butter or spoon of crème fraîche off the heat.
(Source: [ATK](https://www.americastestkitchen.com/articles/5989-10-ways-to-dress-up-simple-scrambled-eggs); [Greatist](https://greatist.com/eat/scrambled-egg-recipes-to-keep-breakfast-interesting))

## On the side (build a plate)
- **Bread/carb:** buttered sourdough toast (tangy counterpoint), brioche (rich), everything bagel, fresh baguette (French-style), or a tortilla for a breakfast taco/burrito.
- **Vegetables:** avocado/smashed avo, roasted cherry tomatoes, sautéed greens, hash browns or crispy potatoes.
- **Proteins:** bacon, breakfast sausage, smoked salmon.
- **Brighteners:** salsa/pico, hot sauce, a squeeze of lemon, fresh herbs.
(Source: [Live Simply](https://livesimply.me/what-to-eat-with-scrambled-eggs/); [The Happier Homemaker](https://thehappierhomemaker.com/what-to-serve-with-scrambled-eggs/))

## Three plug-and-play combos
1. **French simple:** soft scramble + chives + buttered baguette.
2. **Southwestern:** eggs + black beans + green chiles + pepper jack + tomato + scallion, in a warm tortilla with salsa.
3. **Brunch toast:** goat cheese + arugula + sun-dried tomato scramble on sourdough, red pepper flakes on top.
