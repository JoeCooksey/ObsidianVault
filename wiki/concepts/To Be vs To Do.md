# To Be vs To Do

A decision point [[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]] borrows from Air Force strategist **John Boyd**, who would tell promising young officers: *"To be somebody or to do something — in life there is often a roll call. You have to decide which way you're going to go."*

- **To be** = chase titles, recognition, promotions, the appearance of importance.
- **To do** = chase real work, real impact, the result itself — even if someone else gets the credit.

Ego pulls toward *to be*. The people who actually change things choose *to do*, and accept that doing meaningful work often costs you status, comfort, and recognition in the short term.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Aspire."*
