# BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor)

A neurotrophin protein that promotes the survival, growth, and connectivity of [[Neurons]] — often called "fertilizer for the brain." Acts mainly through the **TrkB receptor**.

## Why it matters
- It is the **main molecular mediator of how exercise improves the brain**. Higher BDNF → more synaptic plasticity, neurogenesis support, and better learning/memory. (Source: [[de Sousa Fernandes 2020 — Exercise and Neuroplasticity]], confidence high)
- BDNF/TrkB signaling can **restore impaired plasticity**, not just maintain it (shown in a Parkinson's model, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adh1403), confidence medium — animal model).
- Low BDNF is associated with depression and neurodegeneration.

## How to raise it (actionable)
1. **Aerobic exercise** — the most reliable acute and chronic BDNF stimulus.
2. **High-intensity intervals** — strong acute spikes.
3. **Quality sleep** and **learning novel skills** — support BDNF-driven plasticity.
4. **Intermittent fasting** — ketone β-hydroxybutyrate upregulates BDNF (confidence medium).
