# Vegetable Soup Guide

A flexible, one-pot vegetable soup built around what you have on hand: **celery, broccoli, carrot, onion, bell pepper, Costco spring mix, sweet potato, and Brussels sprouts.** The method below works for any mix — the key is adding vegetables in the right order so each one finishes cooked but not mushy.

**Total time:** ~50 minutes (15 prep + 35 cook). Makes ~6 servings.

---

## The Core Principle: Layer by Cook Time

Vegetables cook at very different rates. Add them in stages so everything is perfectly tender at the same moment. Dense roots go in first; delicate greens go in last.

| Stage | Vegetables | When to add | Simmer time after adding |
|-------|-----------|-------------|--------------------------|
| 1. Aromatics | Onion, celery, carrot | Sauté at start | 6–8 min sauté |
| 2. Dense roots | Sweet potato | After broth goes in | 15 min |
| 3. Medium | Brussels sprouts, bell pepper | Midway | 10 min |
| 4. Quick | Broccoli | Near end | 5–6 min |
| 5. Delicate greens | Spring mix | Off heat / last min | 1–2 min |

---

## Ingredients

**Your vegetables:**
- 1 onion, diced
- 2–3 celery stalks, sliced
- 2 carrots, sliced into half-moons
- 1 sweet potato, peeled and cubed (½-inch)
- 8–10 Brussels sprouts, halved or quartered
- 1 bell pepper, chopped
- 1 head broccoli, cut into small florets
- 2–3 big handfuls Costco spring mix

**Base:**
- 2 tbsp olive oil (or butter)
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 6–8 cups vegetable or chicken broth (or water + bouillon)
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes — optional but adds body
- Salt and black pepper to taste

**Optional additions (highly recommended):** see [Legume & Other Additions](#legume--other-additions) below.

---

## Spice Blends

Pick one direction. All amounts are starting points for a 6-cup pot — taste and adjust.

### 1. Classic Herb (everyday, comforting)
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 bay leaf
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- Finish with fresh parsley + a squeeze of lemon

### 2. Italian Minestrone
- 1.5 tsp Italian seasoning
- ½ tsp fennel seed (crushed)
- 1 bay leaf
- Pinch red pepper flakes
- Parmesan rind dropped in while simmering (remove before serving)
- Finish with basil + grated Parmesan

### 3. Curried / Warming (great with sweet potato)
- 1.5 tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp turmeric
- ¼ tsp cinnamon
- Add 1 cup coconut milk at the end for a creamy version

### 4. Smoky Southwest
- 1.5 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp chili powder
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- Finish with cilantro + lime

> **Tip:** Bloom dried spices in the oil with the aromatics (Stage 1) for 30–60 seconds before adding liquid. This deepens the flavor far more than dumping them into broth.

---

## Step-by-Step Method

1. **Prep everything first** (~15 min). Soup moves fast once it starts. Cube the sweet potato small so it cooks through; keep broccoli florets bite-sized.

2. **Sauté aromatics** (6–8 min). Heat oil in a large pot over medium. Add onion, celery, and carrot with a pinch of salt. Cook until the onion is translucent and softened.

3. **Garlic + spices** (1 min). Add garlic and your chosen dried spice blend. Stir constantly until fragrant — don't let the garlic brown.

4. **Add liquid + sweet potato** (15 min). Pour in broth (and diced tomatoes if using). Add the sweet potato and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer.

5. **Add medium veg** (10 min). Stir in Brussels sprouts and bell pepper. Keep at a gentle simmer.

6. **Add broccoli** (5–6 min). Add the florets. They should be bright green and just tender — don't overcook or they turn dull and soft.

7. **Finish with greens** (1–2 min). Turn off the heat. Stir in the spring mix; residual heat wilts it instantly. Remove the bay leaf.

8. **Season and finish.** Taste for salt and pepper. Add the appropriate fresh finish for your spice blend (lemon/parsley, basil/Parmesan, cilantro/lime, or coconut milk).

## Legume & Other Additions

Adding a legume or grain turns this from a light soup into a full meal and boosts protein and fiber.

**Legumes (add canned & drained with the broth in Step 4, or pre-cooked):**
- White beans (cannellini, great northern) — classic, creamy
- Chickpeas — hearty, holds shape; pairs well with curry blend
- Lentils — red lentils thicken the soup (add dry with broth, +20 min); green/brown hold shape
- Kidney beans — good with the Southwest blend

**Grains (add with broth; check package time):**
- Small pasta (ditalini, orzo) — +8–10 min, classic minestrone
- Rice — +18 min, or stir in pre-cooked
- Barley — hearty, +40 min (add early with sweet potato), or pre-cook
- Quinoa — +15 min, adds protein

**Other vegetables that fit well:**
- Zucchini or yellow squash (add with Stage 3, the medium veg)
- Green beans (add with Stage 3)
- Mushrooms (sauté with aromatics in Stage 1 for depth)
- Spinach or kale (kale needs ~5 min; spinach goes in last like the spring mix)
- Corn (add in the last 5 min)
- Cauliflower (add with Stage 2–3, ~12 min)
- Tomatoes / tomato paste (paste in Stage 3 for richer body)

**To make it heartier still:**
- Stir in a Parmesan rind while simmering for umami
- A splash of soy sauce or miso paste deepens savory flavor
- A spoonful of pesto on top at serving

## Notes & Tips

- **Don't overcook the broccoli and greens** — they're the most overcooking-prone. The staged method exists mostly to protect them.
- **Sweet potato thickens naturally.** As it softens, some breaks down and gives the broth body. For a thicker soup, mash a few cubes against the pot.
- **Make ahead:** flavor improves overnight. If reheating, the greens and broccoli soften more — consider adding fresh greens when you reheat.
- **Freezing:** freezes well *without* pasta/rice (grains turn mushy). Add cooked grains fresh when serving.
- **Salt last with broth-based soups** — store-bought broth is often salty, so taste before adding more.
