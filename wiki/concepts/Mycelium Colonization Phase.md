# Mycelium Colonization Phase

**Colonization** is the period after inoculation when the fungal **mycelium** (the white, root-like vegetative network) spreads through the grain and then the bulk substrate, consuming nutrients and claiming the territory before any contaminant can. The bag stays sealed and in the dark throughout (Source: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]]).

## Timeline

- **Days 3–10**: first visible white mycelium ("fuzz") radiating from the injection point.
- **Days 25–45**: grain layer approaches full colonization. Exact timing depends on genetics, temperature, inoculant type, and dose. Spore syringes run slower than liquid culture (Source: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]]).

## Conditions

- **Temperature: a stable 72–76°F (22–24°C).** This is warmer than the fruiting temperature — mycelium grows fastest in this band. Avoid swings.
- **Light: none needed.** Keep in the dark or low indirect light; avoid direct sun (heat + UV stress).
- **Do not open the bag.** The filter patch supplies all the gas exchange the mycelium needs.

(Sources: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]], [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]])

## What healthy colonization looks like

Dense, **rope-like white** mycelium with a fresh, mushroomy smell. Some bluing on dung-loving species is bruising, not contamination.

## What to watch for

Inspect daily without opening. Stop and isolate the bag if you see **green, black, gray-cobwebby, pink, or slimy** growth, or smell anything **sour/foul** — these are contamination, not mycelium → [[Mushroom Contamination Identification]].

## Next step

Once the grain layer is **50–75%** colonized, perform the [[Break and Shake (Spawn Distribution)]] to distribute mycelium evenly and dramatically shorten the time to full colonization.
