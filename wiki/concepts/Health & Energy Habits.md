# Health & Energy Habits

Habits 83–95 from [[101 Habits — Dan Zakaria]]. The physical base for everything else — the creator calls **sleep the single most important habit on the whole list**.

## The Habits
83. **Walk every day** — after meals (digestion), morning (circadian rhythm); walk *without your phone* to limit options.
84. **Strength train regularly** — calisthenics, martial arts, or weights; key for longevity, sleep, performance.
85. **Prioritize sleep** — *"probably the most important habit in this whole list."* Stop glorifying 5-hr nights; optimal sleep → focus, recovery, dopamine regulation, less distraction. Same sleep/wake time daily.
86. **Drink more water** — most people are chronically dehydrated.
87. **Eat more protein with meals** — for muscle *and* brain function; modern diets under-deliver.
88. **Get morning sunlight** — straight into the eyes, first thing; "a game changer."
89. **Stretch every day** — releases mental tension; "the best habit is the one you can stick to for 10 years."
90. **Improve your posture** — body language shapes self-image; confident posture → confident feeling; ergonomic chair / standing desk.
91. **Limit ultra-processed foods** — flip the package, read ingredients (~10 sec). "Your health is your wealth."
92. **Take movement breaks** — stretching, "lymphatic hops" (bounce on tiptoes) to get blood flowing.
93. **Practice proper breathing** — nasal inhale, mouth exhale; 5 deep belly breaths reset the nervous system.
94. **Reduce excessive sugar** — prefer fruit/honey over processed; avoids the dopamine spike-and-crash.
95. **Exercise even when busy** — move ~5×/week; active recovery on rest days.
