# Nine Levels of Thinking

## Definition
The **levels of thinking** are your **altitude of awareness** — how clearly you can see each of the [[Four Quadrants (AQAL)|four quadrants]]. Where the quadrants say *what* to look at, the levels say *how clearly* you see it: two people viewing the same inner world can perceive completely different things depending on their level. **[[Craig Perry]]** organizes nine levels into three memorable chunks (a developmental-psychology / Spiral-Dynamics-style ladder filtered through [[AQAL Model (Integral Theory)|Integral Theory]]).

## Why It Matters
**Lower levels cannot perceive higher ones; higher levels can look down and see the lower ones.** The higher you climb, the more you can see — and the better your [[Good Explanations (Hard-to-Vary)|explanations]] become, which is Perry's "whole game." This is why he invokes **[[Albert Einstein]]**: *"We cannot solve our problems with the same thinking we used when we created them."* Increasing awareness *is* the entire point of the video.

## How It Works
**Chunk 1 — Subconscious (levels 1–3, ~5% of people, mostly children and "adult children")**
1. **Survive** — pure desire ("I want / I need"); no awareness others have desires.
2. **Connection** — others have minds; do the right thing for them so they reciprocate.
3. **Control** — you sit in a web of relationships and can direct them; status and dominance ("the world is mine; everyone is a tool or obstacle").

**Chunk 2 — Conscious (levels 4–5, ~75%; the ceiling of formal education)**
4. **Belonging** — rules exist for a reason; conform so the tribe doesn't expel you ("my group is right, other groups are wrong" — starts most wars).
5. **Achieve** — question the rules, build your own path (science, entrepreneurship, the American dream). Useful, but works mostly in the systems quadrant (Q4) and ignores body/emotions/culture.

**Chunk 3 — Integrated (levels 6–9, ~15%; only via deliberate self-development)**
6. **Included** — every perspective is valid (enlightening, but its trap is that if no one can be wrong, you can't decide — Perry's critique of postmodernism).
7. **Harmonize** — hold and synthesize multiple perspectives ("truth is a synthesis, not a position"); the start of **second-tier thinking**.
8. **Construct-aware** — watch your own mind build explanations in real time, *before* you believe them → genuine choice over what to believe.
9. **Unitive** — the boundary between self and reality dissolves; egoless awareness ("the explanation and the explainer become the same"), pointed to by **[[Alan Watts]]** and **[[Terence McKenna]]**.

## Cross-Domain Connections
- Strongly parallels **Spiral Dynamics** (Beck/Cowan) and constructive-developmental models (Kegan, Cook-Greuter); the "tier 1 vs second-tier" language is Spiral Dynamics.
- You're not fixed at one level — see [[Lines of Development]] (you can be level 7 on one line, level 3 on another) and **regression** under stress.
- Population percentages are illustrative, not measured — see the confidence note in [[How To Increase Your Intelligence — Craig Perry]].
