# Scarcity

[[Robert B. Cialdini|Cialdini's]] sixth principle: opportunities seem **more valuable when they are limited or disappearing**, and the *fear of loss* motivates us more strongly than the prospect of an equivalent gain. Tactics: limited quantity ("only 3 left"), deadlines ("offer ends tonight"), and competition for the scarce item.

The psychological engine is **[[Psychological Reactance]]** — when a freedom or choice is restricted, we want it more. Scarcity bites hardest for things that are *newly* scarce and scarce *because of competition*. **Defense:** scarcity creates arousal that clouds judgment, so pause and ask whether you want the item for its *utility* (still useful when rare) or merely for the *thrill of owning* something hard to get.
