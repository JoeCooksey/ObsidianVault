# Investing in Yourself (Self-Investment)

**Deliberately allocating today's scarce resources — time, money, attention, energy — into your own capabilities, health, and relationships, so they compound into a larger future "self."** The asset is *you*; the return is realized over decades.

## Why it's the highest-leverage asset

- **It compounds and can't be confiscated.** Warren Buffett's framing: "By far the best investment you can make is in yourself … nobody can tax it away or steal it from you." Skills and knowledge appreciate and travel with you. ([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/warren-buffett-says-the-most-critical-investment-lies-within-ourselves)) — **medium** (widely-attributed quote)
- **The financial return is large but conditional.** A bachelor's degree carries a net ~$655K (men) lifetime earnings premium; ~14%/yr return on education is typical — but returns concentrate by ability and field, not automatic. (Source: [[Tamborini Kim Sakamoto 2015 — Education and Lifetime Earnings]]) — **high**
- **The non-financial returns can exceed the financial ones.** The economic value of education for longer, healthier life is "comparable to or greater than" its earnings value. — **high**

## The four buckets (where the returns are)

1. **Skills / knowledge** → [[Career Capital]], compounding income and autonomy.
2. **Health** → healthspan; ~80% of longevity is lifestyle, not genes.
3. **Relationships / social capital** → the #1 predictor of happiness and health (Harvard Study).
4. **Mindset / habits / financial base** → the operating system that keeps the other three running.

## The mechanism: compounding

Self-investment behaves like compound interest — small, consistent inputs dominate large sporadic ones. See [[Compound Time (5-Hour Rule)]] (deliberate learning) and [[Compounding Wealth]] (money). The full prioritized framework lives in [[Research - Best Way to Invest in Yourself]].
