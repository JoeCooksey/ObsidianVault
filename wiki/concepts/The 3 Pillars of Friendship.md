# The 3 Pillars of Friendship

A framework [[Mel Robbins|Robbins]] cites (from friendship research) for why adult friendships form and fade. Sustained closeness needs three things:

1. **Proximity** — repeated physical or regular closeness.
2. **Timing** — being in compatible life stages with available bandwidth.
3. **Energy / shared effort** — mutual investment and reciprocated attention.

When any pillar shifts — someone moves, has a kid, changes jobs — friendships naturally drift. Robbins' point, via [[Let Them]]: this drift is normal, not betrayal. **Let them** pull away as life stages change; **let me** invest in the people who currently share all three pillars, instead of forcing relationships the circumstances no longer support.

*Source: [[The Let Them Theory - Mel Robbins]].*
