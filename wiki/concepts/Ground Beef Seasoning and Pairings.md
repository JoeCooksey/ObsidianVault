# Ground Beef Seasoning and Pairings

How to season browned ground beef and what to serve it with.

## The seasoning technique (do this, not just "sprinkle on")
1. **Salt** the beef (kosher) to enhance the meat itself.
2. **Cook aromatics in the rendered fat** — finely chopped onion + minced garlic — once the beef releases its fat.
3. **Bloom the dry spices in the fat for 30–60 s** before stirring through. Heating spices in fat releases fat-soluble flavor compounds and spreads flavor through the whole dish — "you'll taste the difference in every bite."
4. **Crush dried herbs** (oregano) between your fingers as you add them to release more aroma.
(Source: [RawSpiceBar](https://rawspicebar.com/blogs/spices-101/best-ground-beef-seasoning-12-easy-blends-for-tacos-bowls-and-skillets); [The Seasoned Mom](https://www.theseasonedmom.com/taco-seasoning-for-ground-beef/))

## Core seasoning blends
- **Taco / Mexican:** cumin, chili powder, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, oregano, salt; finish with lime + cilantro. (DIY beats most store packets — no fillers.)
- **Italian / bolognese:** garlic, onion, oregano, basil, fennel seed, tomato, splash of red wine; parmesan to finish.
- **Burger/all-purpose:** salt, pepper, garlic powder, Worcestershire.
- **Umami boosters (any blend):** Worcestershire, soy sauce, tomato paste, fish sauce, a little miso, beef bouillon.

## What to serve it with (by dish)
- **Tacos / burritos / bowls:** warm tortillas or rice; toppings = guacamole, pico de gallo, shredded cheese, sour cream, lettuce, lime, hot sauce; black beans on the side.
- **Pasta:** spaghetti, pappardelle, or baked ziti; parmesan, garlic bread, a green salad.
- **Comfort plates:** mashed potatoes (Salisbury / cottage pie), buttered egg noodles, rice.
- **Veg sides:** roasted or sautéed vegetables, a crisp salad to cut the richness, sautéed peppers and onions.
- **Breakfast crossover:** fold browned beef into [[Scrambled Eggs — Masterclass|scrambled eggs]] or a breakfast burrito.

## Three plug-and-play combos
1. **Taco night:** taco-seasoned beef + warm tortillas + guac + pico + cotija + lime.
2. **Weeknight bolognese:** beef + soffritto + tomato + red wine over pappardelle, parmesan + garlic bread.
3. **Cottage pie:** beef + onion/carrot + Worcestershire + peas under mashed potato, baked.
