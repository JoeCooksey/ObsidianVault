# YouTube Channels for Creative and Professional Skills

Ranked for self-directed learners in **music, visual art/design, writing, languages, and business/marketing** — the practical and creative subjects not covered by the STEM and Humanities tier lists. Same criterion: depth × accuracy × accessibility.

---

## Music Theory & Production

| Channel | Best For |
|---------|----------|
| **Rick Beato** | The #1 music-education channel; deep theory + "What Makes This Song Great?" song breakdowns; ear training |
| **Adam Neely** | Bassist/composer; video essays on jazz harmony, rhythm, microtonality; the most intellectually rigorous music channel |
| **David Bennett Piano** | "Songs that use [concept]" — theory taught through famous pop/rock examples; very accessible |
| **12tone** | Animated theory analysis of how songs work; ~500K+ subs |
| **Andrew Huang** | Production + creativity; DAW-friendly harmony, sound design, creative challenges |
| **Music Matters** | Structured theory, composition, sight-reading, and piano (Gareth Green) |

**Single best starting point:** Rick Beato for breadth, Adam Neely for depth.

---

## Visual Art, Drawing & Design

| Channel | Best For |
|---------|----------|
| **Proko** | The fundamentals standard — figure drawing, anatomy, gesture, perspective; Stan Prokopenko breaks complex skills into beginner steps |
| **Bobby Chiu (Schoolism)** | Concept and character design; professional industry insight |
| **Kyle T. Webster** | Digital art and illustration from a Photoshop-brush legend (now Adobe) |
| **Chloe Rose Art** | High-energy painting/drawing/illustration; great for younger/newer artists |
| **Marco Bucci** | Color, light, and painting theory — exceptional for understanding *why* art reads well |

**Single best starting point:** Proko for fundamentals (anatomy + figure drawing).

---

## Writing & Storytelling

| Channel | Best For |
|---------|----------|
| **Brandon Sanderson** | The closest thing to a free creative-writing degree — his full BYU lecture playlists (plot, character, worldbuilding, publishing) |
| **Hello Future Me** | The most educational craft channel; structure, theme, and "On Writing" deep dives |
| **Reedsy** | Practical craft + the business of publishing — editing, querying agents, self-publishing, marketing |
| **Overly Sarcastic Productions** | "Trope Talk" series — narrative tropes and mythology; sharp analysis |
| **Terrible Writing Advice** | Teaches craft by satirizing bad writing; trains your self-editing eye |

**Single best starting point:** Brandon Sanderson's lecture series (free university course); Hello Future Me for craft analysis.

---

## Language Learning & Linguistics

| Channel | Best For |
|---------|----------|
| **Langfocus** | Paul Jorgensen; deep dives into how individual languages work — history, grammar, culture; best for *understanding* languages |
| **Easy Languages** | Street interviews with real native speakers + subtitles; immersion / comprehensible input across many languages |
| **NativLang** | Animated history and evolution of languages and writing systems |
| **Story Learning (Olly Richards)** | Language-learning *strategy* — the short-stories / comprehensible-input method |
| **Dreaming Spanish** | The flagship comprehensible-input channel (Spanish); model for input-based acquisition |
| **The Ling Space** | Academic linguistics made accessible — phonology, syntax, semantics |

> [!note] For actually *acquiring* a language, pair a "comprehensible input" channel (Dreaming Spanish, Easy Languages) with spaced repetition (Anki) — see [[Daily Learning — Sites and Activities Toolkit]]. Langfocus/NativLang/Ling Space are for *understanding* language, not fluency.

## Business, Entrepreneurship & Marketing

| Channel | Best For |
|---------|----------|
| **Y Combinator** | The highest-signal startup channel — Startup School lectures + founder talks from the top accelerator; theory-to-practice |
| **The Diary of a CEO (Steven Bartlett)** | Long-form founder/leader interviews; strategy, marketing, leadership, personal development |
| **HubSpot** | Practical, tactical marketing tutorials (SEO, content, funnels) |
| **GaryVee (Gary Vaynerchuk)** | Personal branding, social-media marketing, hustle mindset (high energy, lower signal-per-minute) |
| **Tim Ferriss** | Productivity, business systems, and tactics-focused interviews |

**Single best starting point:** Y Combinator's Startup School for substance; HubSpot for hands-on marketing.

> [!note] Confidence: each channel recurs across multiple independent 2026 "best of" lists (medium confidence individually, higher in aggregate). Business/marketing channels skew more motivational than rigorous — Y Combinator is the exception and the highest-substance pick.
