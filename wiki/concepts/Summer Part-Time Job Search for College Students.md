# Summer Part-Time Job Search for College Students

A system for finding and landing a summer part-time job as a college student, with Livermore / Tri-Valley specific context.

## The Core Framework

### 4 Job Categories

| Category | Examples | Pay Range | Competition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Local in-person** | Retail, food service, grocery | $16.50–$28/hr | Medium |
| **Local institutional** | LARPD, City of Livermore, school districts | $16–$26/hr | Medium-low |
| **Skilled local** | Tutoring, camp counselor, research aide | $25–$60/hr | Low |
| **Remote / flexible** | Online tutoring, virtual assistant, freelance | $15–$50/hr | Low–medium |

### The Application Rule
Apply to **minimum 20–30 positions** simultaneously. Expect 5–10% response rate from cold online applications. In-person walk-ins convert at 20–30%+ for retail/food service — always bring a printed resume.

## Key Local Resources (Livermore)

- **LARPD** — larpd.org/job-opportunities; best hourly rate for entry-level ($23.42/hr Park Aide)
- **Tri-Valley Career Center** — jobs.trivalleycareercenter.org; free coaching + local job board; 5860 Owens Dr, Pleasanton; 925-416-5100
- **City of Livermore Jobs** — governmentjobs.com/careers/livermoreca
- **LVJUSD Summer Programs** — edjoin.org

## Platform Tier List
- **S-Tier**: Handshake (student-specific) + Indeed (volume) + walk-in
- **A-Tier**: LinkedIn + ZipRecruiter + SimplyHired
- **B-Tier**: Glassdoor + College Recruiter + CollegeGrad

## Resume for No Experience
Lead with Education > Skills > Projects/Activities. One page. Tailor keywords from each job description. Include GPA if ≥ 3.0. List city (Livermore, CA) to signal you're local.

## Timeline
- **April–May**: Highest odds; most summer roles fill early
- **Late May (NOW)**: Still viable; apply immediately
- **June**: Late; focus shifts to non-seasonal retail/service roles
