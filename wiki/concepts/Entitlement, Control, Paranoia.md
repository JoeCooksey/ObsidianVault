# Entitlement, Control, Paranoia

[[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]]'s diagnosis of the **three diseases that success breeds through ego**:

- **Entitlement** — believing you deserve more, that the rules don't apply to you, that the world owes you.
- **Control** — the compulsion to micromanage everything and everyone, unable to delegate or trust.
- **Paranoia** — seeing threats everywhere, hoarding, suspecting rivals, unable to enjoy what you've built.

Each is ego converting a real win into a distorted relationship with reality. They are why successful people so often self-destruct: the disease is internal, not external. The remedy is sobriety, humility, and [[Managing Yourself|managing yourself]] before trying to manage everything else.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Success."*
