# Psilocybin Trip Activities Tier List

What to do (and not do) during a psilocybin experience, ranked by evidence and safety. Harm-reduction framing throughout.

> [!warning] Context first
> Psilocybin is federally Schedule I ([[Psilocybin Legal Status (US)]]). Skip entirely with personal/family history of psychosis or schizophrenia, heart conditions, or while on lithium (seizure risk) or MAOIs (Source: [[CISUR Safer Tripping Infosheet]], [[TripSafe Psilocybin Guide]]). Difficult-trip support: Fireside Project 62-FIRESIDE (free, confidential); crisis: 988.

## The One Rule That Reorders Everything: Dose

**At moderate-to-high doses, the best "activity" is no activity.** The clinical gold standard is lying down, eyeshades on, headphones playing a curated playlist, attention turned inward (Source: [[Psilocybin Session Protocol (Johns Hopkins)]]). The tier list below mostly applies to **low-to-moderate doses**; above ~3 g dried, collapse it to S-tier only.

## S-Tier — Evidence-Backed Core

| Activity | Why |
|---|---|
| **Curated music playlist** (lyric-free: classical, ambient) | The single most evidence-backed activity. Music intensifies emotion and imagery under psychedelics and functions as a "hidden therapist"; real-time music adjustment is a documented in-trip emotional regulation tool (Source: [[Kaelen 2018 - The Hidden Therapist (Music in Psychedelic Therapy)]], [[Dutton 2025 - Psychedelic Harm Reduction Scoping Review]]) |
| **Eyeshades + headphones inward journey** | The Johns Hopkins clinical protocol for medium/high doses; maximizes introspective benefit, minimizes environmental risk (Source: [[Psilocybin Session Protocol (Johns Hopkins)]]) |
| **Safe, familiar nature spot** (with sitter) | Nature relatedness rises after psychedelic use and persists ≥2 years; natural surroundings during the acute state amplify the effect — though clinical rooms work too, so nature is enhancement, not requirement (Source: [[Psychedelics and Nature Relatedness Studies (Imperial College)]]) |

## A-Tier — Strong Harm-Reduction Consensus

- **Meditation / slow breathing** — the universal difficult-moment tool; "surrender, don't resist" is the most-cited mindset strategy (Source: [[Dutton 2025 - Psychedelic Harm Reduction Scoping Review]])
- **Journaling** — captures insights that otherwise evaporate; doubles as the start of integration (Source: [[Dutton 2025 - Psychedelic Harm Reduction Scoping Review]])
- **Talking with trusted friends** — psilocybin amplifies feelings of connection; trusted company is also the safety layer
- **Gentle walk in familiar green space** — combines the nature effect with light movement; sitter recommended
- **Stargazing** — low-risk awe; awe is plausibly a working ingredient of the experience

## B-Tier — Good, Lower Ceiling

- **Drawing / painting / free-form art** — expressive outlet; widely recommended, evidence informal (Source: [[Things to Do on Shrooms (Aggregate Guides)]])
- **Nature documentaries or visually rich, calm films** — enjoyment-focused trips; pre-screen content
- **Stretching / gentle yoga** — body-based grounding

## C-Tier — Situational, Dose-Capped

- **Video games / regular movies** — distraction from the experience rather than engagement with it; low doses only
- **Sex** — established partner + light dose only; higher doses impair focus and consent clarity (Source: [[Things to Do on Shrooms (Aggregate Guides)]])
- **Mirror-gazing** — the famous lore says avoid; a 2024 Reddit-corpus study found mostly *positive* self-perception outcomes, so the warning is probably overstated — but visuals make reflections unpredictable (Source: [[Montclair Mirror-Gazing Psilocybin Study (2024)]] vs [[CISUR Safer Tripping Infosheet]])

## F-Tier — Do Not

- **Driving** — illegal (DUI applies even where decriminalized) and impairment outlasts the felt trip
- **Climbing, swimming, heights, biking** — impaired judgment + physical danger (Source: [[CISUR Safer Tripping Infosheet]])
- **Crowds, clubs, public places** — high-stimulation settings escalate anxiety (Source: [[TripSafe Psilocybin Guide]])
- **Phone / social media / texting** — one stray message can hijack the state; airplane mode before onset
- **Mixing substances** — alcohol, cannabis, stimulants make effects unpredictable; lithium is genuinely dangerous (Source: [[Dutton 2025 - Psychedelic Harm Reduction Scoping Review]])
- **Big decisions, important conversations, work** — judgment is altered; decide nothing, send nothing

> [!gap] Evidence asymmetry
> S-tier rests on clinical protocols and peer-reviewed studies; B/C tiers rest on harm-reduction consensus and community guides (medium-low confidence). No controlled study compares *activities* head-to-head.
