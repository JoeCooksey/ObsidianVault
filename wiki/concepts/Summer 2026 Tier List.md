# Summer 2026 Tier List
**Joe's personalized summer activity & project rankings — May–August 2026**

This tier list is calibrated to Joe's exact context: 19-year-old EE freshman at ASU, going into sophomore year (Fall 2026: MAT 343, EEE 202, PHY 131), FURI goal, WBG/power electronics track, summer = strength phase, Catholic, Spanish learner.

See full step-by-step guides in [[FURI Outreach Campaign Guide]], [[Summer 2026 Strength Program]], [[Roth IRA Setup Guide]], and [[Buck Converter Build Guide]] (existing).

---

## S-Tier — Do These. No Debate.

These four compound far beyond summer. Missing them has measurable long-term cost.

### 1. FURI Faculty Outreach Campaign
**Why S-tier:** The October FURI deadline is 90–120 days away. Professors who know you before the deadline accept 3–5× more often than cold applications. This campaign is the single highest-leverage academic action of the summer. → See [[FURI Outreach Campaign Guide]]

**Minimum viable commitment:** 30 min/day, 5 days/week. Read 1 paper/week, 1 cold email sent per week.

### 2. Buck Converter Build (STM32 + PCB)
**Why S-tier:** A working hardware artifact on your FURI application is the single clearest differentiator between you and every other EE freshman who applied. 12V→5V, 1A, 100 kHz, STM32 digital control, KiCad PCB, JLCPCB fabrication. → See [[Buck Converter Build Guide]]

**Key facts:** D = 41.7%, L = 100 µH, C_out = 44 µF, total cost ~$55–70, 12-week build, three FURI-reportable measurements (efficiency curve, load transient, Bode plot).

### 3. MIT 18.06 Linear Algebra (Strang, OCW)
**Why S-tier:** Direct preparation for Fall 2026 MAT 343. Also the math foundation for ML, signals, control theory, and eigenvalue-based circuit analysis. 35 lectures × 40 min/day = done in 87 days, comfortably within summer. → See [[MIT 18.06 Summer Schedule]]

**Stack:** 3B1B "Essence of Linear Algebra" first (visual intuition, ~3 hrs) → then Strang lectures sequentially with problem sets.

### 4. Open Roth IRA + Automate
**Why S-tier:** 2-hour action with a 40-year compounding tail. $100/month at age 19 at 8% avg = ~$525k at 65. Every month delayed costs $5,000–$10,000 in compounding tail. 2026 Roth IRA limit = $7,500. Must have earned income (job, tutoring, etc.) → See [[Roth IRA Setup Guide]]

## A-Tier — High Value. Do 2–3.

These are strong multipliers on the S-tier work and Joe's physical/mental baseline.

### 5. Python EE Project Ladder (Phase 0–3)
12 projects in 13 weeks, one per week. Ohm's Law calculator → RLC impedance visualizer → Bode plot generator → FFT spectrum analyzer → FIR filter designer → PID controller → closed-loop buck converter simulator in Python. Directly complements the hardware build; gives you software to demo alongside the board. → See [[Python EE Project Ladder]]

**Time:** 45 min/day. No prerequisites beyond Python basics.

### 6. Summer Strength Program (Upper/Lower 4-Day)
Per [[Joe's Custom Seasonal Training Plan]]: Summer = strength phase. Upper/Lower 4–5 days/week, 3–5 rep range, 85–90% 1RM, 2 Zone 2 cycling days, maintenance calories. → See [[Summer 2026 Strength Program]]

**Why now:** Physical baseline (T, HGH, dopamine, insulin sensitivity) is the meta-habit that makes everything else easier. A productive summer is built on a functioning body.

### 7. Spanish: Language Transfer + Anki
Phase 1 of the [[Spanish Fluency Complete Masterclass]]. Language Transfer Complete Spanish (free, 40 episodes, 7.5 hrs total) + Anki Spanish Core 1000 deck (10 new cards/day). 15–20 min/day. Target: complete Language Transfer by end of summer (A1 → early A2).

**Week 1 action:** Download Anki → add Spanish Core 1000 deck → do 10 cards. Walk while listening to Language Transfer Episode 1.

### 8. Comfort Zone Daily Stack
Per [[Comfort Zone Habits Tier List]]: Cold shower (2–5 min) + one daily social challenge (talk to a stranger, ask for something uncomfortable). Takes 5–10 min. Trains PFC → amygdala circuit that transfers to all domains. This is the minimum viable version — no extra time needed.

### 9. Morning Mobility Routine (7 min AM)
Per [[Daily Mobility Routine]]: Cat-Cow → 90/90 Hip Switch → World's Greatest Stretch → Ankle Wall Stretch → Deep Squat Hold. Critical for hip flexors (sitting + cycling = shortened iliopsoas). Prevents injury during strength phase. Do this before every Upper or Lower session.

## B-Tier — Worthwhile. Pick 1 That Fits Your Energy.

### 10. Roblox Game Dev (Clicker Idle Empire)
Per [[Roblox Game Development with AI]]: Studio went agentic in April 2026. Beginner with Studio Assistant can ship a working Clicker Idle game in 4–6 weeks. Teaches: Luau scripting, monetization pipeline, product iteration, DevEx economics. Realistic summer earnings: $50–300 total (learning, not income). Worth doing if you want to understand game development economics and have a coding artifact outside EE.

**Why not S-tier:** ROI vs. FURI/18.06 is much lower for Joe's specific track.

### 11. Catholic Spiritual Practice Stack
Per [[Catholic Spiritual Growth Steps]]: S-tier foundation = Sunday Mass + monthly Confession + 15 min daily prayer. A-tier add: Ignatian Examen (Hallow app, 10 min) + 15 min *Imitation of Christ* reading. This isn't separate from everything else — it's the frame that makes everything else hold together. Summer is the easiest time to establish the routine before the chaos of sophomore year.

### 12. Technical Blog / GitHub Portfolio
Document the buck converter build publicly. Forces teaching, catches errors, builds a portfolio for FURI applications and future internship interviews. GitHub Pages (free). One post per major build milestone (design → breadboard → PCB → measurements). 30 min per post, ~5 posts total over summer.

### 13. DoD SMART Scholarship Prep
Per [[Stackable EE Scholarships - ASU Second Year]]: DoD SMART opens **August 1** and closes first Friday of December. Full tuition + $30,000–$46,000 stipend + job offer post-graduation. Application requires: personal statement, 3 LoRs, GPA, citizenship. Summer = perfect time to draft the personal statement and identify LoR writers. → opens Aug 1 at smartscholarship.org

### 14. Podcast Learning Stack (No Extra Time)
Layer on top of workouts, commutes, and meal prep. Per [[Podcast Learning Tier List]]:
- Workouts: Huberman Lab Essentials (30 min, evidence-based protocols)
- Long walks: Hardcore History (4–6 hr deep dives, WWI/Mongols)
- Dishes/commute: Lex Fridman (tech/AI/science)
- Philosophy: Philosophize This! (canon, free)
