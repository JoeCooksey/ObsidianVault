# The Canvas Strategy

[[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]]'s tactic for the early, unproven stage of a career: **"find canvases for other people to paint on."** Instead of competing for credit, do the unglamorous work that makes the people around you look good — clear obstacles, find opportunities for others, do more than your share without demanding recognition.

The ego rebels because the work is anonymous and feels beneath you. But the strategy compounds: you learn from those you help, you build relationships and reputation, and you accumulate skills while others are busy posturing. It is generosity deployed as a long-term self-interest strategy — the opposite of grabbing for status before you've earned it.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Aspire."*
