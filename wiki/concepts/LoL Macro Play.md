# LoL Macro Play

The map-wide game: wave control, objective timing, vision lines, rotations, and tempo — as opposed to micro (mechanics in the fight). Macro reduces to one question asked roughly every 20 seconds: **"What is the single most valuable thing I can be doing on the map right now?"** (Source: [[Boosteria — LoL Macro Play Guide]])

Macro is the main separator between mid elo and high elo; mechanics separate low elo from platinum/diamond, decision-making separates everything above. (confidence: medium — community consensus, multiple informal sources)

## Five Pillars
1. **Information** — wards, jungle tracking, enemy position inference
2. **Time** — tempo, death timers, recall windows
3. **Space** — wave position and vision control define where it's safe to stand
4. **Resources** — gold, XP, ultimates, summoner cooldowns
5. **Conversion** — turning any advantage into towers, dragons, Baron, inhibitors

(Source: [[Boosteria — LoL Macro Play Guide]])

## Key Ideas
- **Tempo** = the ability to act before the enemy can respond. Created by wave priority, better recall timing, health advantage, and vision.
- **Objectives > KDA.** Kills are a means; towers, dragons, herald/grubs, and Baron are permanent advantages that end games. The hardest low-elo lesson. (Source: [[Hotspawn — How to Get Good at LoL]])
- **60-30-10 rule** for objective setup: push lanes 60 s before spawn, clear vision at 30 s, be positioned at 10 s.
- **Win conditions:** every comp falls into scale-and-teamfight, pick-and-snowball, split-push, siege, or hard-engage. Identify yours on the loading screen and play toward it ([[LoL Improvement Practice System]]).
