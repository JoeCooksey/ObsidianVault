# Dash Stirring Popcorn Maker — Guide and Recipes

How to get the best popcorn out of the **Dash SmartStore Stirring Popcorn Maker** — an electric, **oil-based stirring popper** (not an air popper). The rotating stir rod keeps kernels and sugar moving so they coat evenly and don't scorch, which is why sweet recipes (kettle corn, caramel) work in it.

## Machine facts that change how you cook
- **Needs oil to work** — oil carries heat and lets the stir rod move kernels. Not an air popper.
- **The stir rod is the advantage** — enables kettle corn / caramel corn without burning.
- **The lid is a butter-melting tray** — put butter in the recessed lid during popping; rising heat melts it.
- **Flip to serve** — when pops slow to ~2–3 sec apart, unplug and flip so popcorn drops into the clear bowl.
- **Capacity up to 24 cups**; lid measuring cup marked for 8 / 16 / 24-cup batches. (Source: bydash.com, Williams-Sonoma manual)

## Base method (every batch)
1. Level surface, lid off. Wipe **~1 tsp neutral oil** across the nonstick plate.
2. Add oil + kernels together. Ratio ≈ **1 tbsp oil per ¼ cup kernels**. High-smoke-point neutral oil (canola, **refined coconut** = movie-theater flavor, refined avocado).
   - ¼ cup kernels ≈ **8 cups popped**; 2 tbsp kernels ≈ **4 cups popped**.
3. Optional: 1–3 tbsp butter in the lid tray to melt while popping.
4. Press power; don't open mid-pop.
5. **Flip + unplug when pops slow to every 2–3 seconds** (the #1 factor for avoiding burnt/chewy popcorn).
6. Season immediately while hot and slightly oily — that's when seasoning sticks.

## 7 tips for best results
1. **Refined coconut oil** for theater flavor.
2. **Don't overload** — stay at/under the measuring line or stirring suffers and duds increase.
3. **Use fine popcorn salt** (or grind salt) — coarse salt falls to the bottom.
4. **Fat first, then powders** — toss with oil/butter, then seasoning, so it clings.
5. **Fresh, airtight kernels** pop better (less moisture loss = fewer duds).
6. **Pour off promptly** to stay crisp; trapped steam = soggy.
7. **Kernel shape:** mushroom kernels for heavy coatings (caramel/cheese), butterfly for buttered/salted.

## Recipes

### Classic Movie-Theater Buttered
¼ cup kernels + 1 tbsp coconut oil; 2–3 tbsp butter in the lid. Pop, flip, drizzle butter in 2–3 layers tossing between each; fine salt.

### Kettle Corn (stir-popper specialty)
¼ cup kernels + 2 tbsp oil + **¼ cup sugar** + ½ tsp salt. Start machine, after ~1 min sprinkle sugar **evenly** over kernels (stir rod prevents burning). **Keep lid on — hot sugar spits.** Flip when pops slow; salt right after. Wash the plate promptly (caramelized sugar is the only tough cleanup).

### Caramel Corn
Pop 8–10 cups plain. Saucepan: ½ cup butter + 1 cup brown sugar + ¼ cup corn syrup, boil 4 min; off heat add ½ tsp baking soda + 1 tsp vanilla. Toss over popcorn, spread on lined sheet, bake **250°F 45 min**, stir every 15.

### White Cheddar
Hot popcorn + 1 tbsp melted butter + 2–3 tbsp powdered white cheddar (or fine parmesan).

### Cinnamon-Sugar "Churro"
Mist with melted butter; toss with 3 tbsp sugar + 1 tsp cinnamon.

### Spicy Chili-Lime
1 tbsp butter + ½ tsp chili powder + ¼ tsp smoked paprika + ¼ tsp cumin + pinch cayenne + lime juice & zest.

### Ranch
1 tbsp melted butter + 1 tbsp dry ranch seasoning.

### Garlic-Parmesan-Herb
2 tbsp butter steeped with 1 minced garlic clove; toss with 3 tbsp parmesan + 1 tsp Italian herbs.

### Cookies & Cream (dessert)
Plain popcorn drizzled with melted white chocolate + crushed Oreo; let set.

### Everything Bagel
1 tbsp butter + 1–2 tsp everything bagel seasoning.

## Troubleshooting
- **Many unpopped kernels** → old kernels or stopped too early.
- **Chewy/soggy** → too much oil, steam trapped under lid, or didn't pour off promptly.
- **Burnt** → ran too long after popping slowed; always flip/unplug at the 2–3-second mark.
