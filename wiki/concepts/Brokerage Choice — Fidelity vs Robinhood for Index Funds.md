# Brokerage Choice — Fidelity vs Robinhood for Index Funds

> The decisive difference: **Fidelity offers index mutual funds (including 0.00% ZERO funds); Robinhood offers only ETFs.** Both let you fully index for free — they just use different vehicles.

## The Core Distinction (confidence: high)

| | Fidelity | Robinhood |
|---|----------|-----------|
| Index **mutual funds** | ✅ Yes — incl. 0.00% ZERO line | ❌ None |
| Index **ETFs** (VOO/VTI/VXUS) | ✅ Yes | ✅ ~2,300 ETFs |
| Fractional shares | ✅ | ✅ from $1 |
| Commissions | $0 | $0 |
| Cheapest core | FZROX / FNILX @ **0.00%** | VOO / VTI @ **0.03%** |
| Best for | Roth IRA, set-and-forget mutual funds, ZERO funds | ETF-only investing, fractional ETF DCA, one app |

(Sources: [[NerdWallet — Robinhood Review 2026]], [[Fidelity Index Funds Lineup (Fidelity.com)]])

## "Index Fund" Means Different Tickers on Each

- **On Fidelity:** buy mutual funds — FZROX (total US, 0%), FNILX/FXAIX (S&P 500), FZILX (international). Or buy the same ETFs as Robinhood.
- **On Robinhood:** buy ETFs — **VOO** (S&P 500, 0.03%), **VTI** (total US, 0.03%), **VXUS** (international, 0.05%), **BND** (bonds). These are the ETF equivalents and are fully portable between brokers (Source: [[NerdWallet — Top S&P 500 ETFs 2026]]).

## Practical Verdict

- **For a long-term Roth IRA / serious nest egg → Fidelity.** Free ZERO mutual funds, mature retirement-account tooling, automatic recurring mutual-fund investing in exact dollar amounts, no temptation toward options/crypto/prediction markets.
- **Robinhood is fine for index ETFs** and great for fractional, app-first DCA — but its product gravity pulls toward active trading and (as of 2026) [[Agentic Trading (Robinhood)|agentic trading]], which is the opposite of the index discipline. Keep it for the satellite, not the core.
