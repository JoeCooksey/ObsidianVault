# Neurons

The electrically excitable cells that compute, store, and transmit information across the nervous system. **Ranked #1 most important cell type** in [[Research - Most Important Cells in the Human Body]] — they carry identity, cognition, and control of every organ.

## Why they top the ranking
- **Largely non-regenerating.** Most CNS neurons exit the cell cycle permanently after differentiation; once lost they are mostly not replaced. (confidence high) Adult neurogenesis exists but is limited (mainly hippocampus) and debated in scope.
- **Irreplaceable function.** They encode memory, personality, and motor/autonomic control. Loss → dementia, paralysis, death.
- **High energy + vulnerability.** Among the most [[Mitochondria|mitochondria]]-dense cells; sensitive to oxidative stress, glucose dysregulation, and sleep loss.

## How to optimize them (actionable)
1. **Aerobic exercise** — strongest lever. Raises [[BDNF]], GDNF, NGF → neuroplasticity, learning, memory; can *restore* plasticity, not just preserve it. (Source: [[de Sousa Fernandes 2020 — Exercise and Neuroplasticity]], confidence high)
2. **Sleep 7–9 h** — glymphatic clearance of metabolic waste (incl. amyloid-β) happens mainly in deep sleep; consolidates memory.
3. **Learn hard, novel things** — effortful, novel learning is the direct stimulus for synaptic plasticity.
4. **Protect metabolic health** — chronic high glucose/insulin resistance damages neurons; the brain is metabolically fragile.
5. **Omega-3 (DHA)** — structural component of neuronal membranes; supports synaptic function.
6. **Avoid neurotoxins** — chronic alcohol, chronic stress (cortisol), and head trauma.

> [!gap] Adult human neurogenesis magnitude is contested; "grow new brain cells" claims are weaker than "grow new connections."
