# The Arrow of History

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] claim that, despite countless local reversals, history shows a **long-run directional trend toward greater unity** — separate tribes, cultures, and civilizations steadily merging into larger and more interconnected wholes, driven by [[Money as Trust|money]], [[Empire|empire]], and [[Religion as Unifier|religion]].

He is careful to say this is not because unity is "good" or inevitable in any moral sense, only that it is the observable pattern: cultures are never static, are full of internal contradictions that drive change, and over millennia have collapsed into fewer, broader human worlds. The near-endpoint is today's single global civilization. This is the connective theme of the [[Unification of Humankind]].

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 3.*
