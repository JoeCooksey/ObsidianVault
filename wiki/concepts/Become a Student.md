# Become a Student

[[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]]'s call to adopt the posture of a **perpetual apprentice**: actively seek teachers, criticism, and gaps in your own knowledge. Ego hates being a beginner because beginners look incompetent — but mastery is impossible without the humility to keep learning.

The same principle returns after success as **"always stay a student"**: the most common cause of decline is believing you have arrived and therefore no longer need to learn. The people who keep winning keep treating themselves as students no matter how accomplished they become.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Aspire" and "Success."*
