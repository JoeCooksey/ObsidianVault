# T-Shaped Knowledge

A model for structuring knowledge across disciplines. The metaphor uses the letter "T":
- The **vertical bar**: deep expertise in one primary domain
- The **horizontal bar**: broad familiarity across many adjacent and non-adjacent domains

## Variants

| Model | Shape | Description |
|-------|-------|-------------|
| T-shaped | T | One deep domain + broad general awareness |
| π-shaped | π | Two deep domains + broad general awareness |
| Comb-shaped | ⊓⊓⊓ | Multiple deep domains (advanced polymath) |
| I-shaped | I | Pure specialist — deep but zero breadth |

## Why T-Shaped Beats I-Shaped

1. **Cross-pollination**: The horizontal bar exposes specialists to frameworks from other fields that solve problems in their primary domain
2. **Communication**: Broad knowledge enables working effectively with specialists from other fields
3. **Innovation**: Breakthrough ideas emerge at domain intersections — impossible without the horizontal bar

## Building the T

**The vertical bar (depth)**:
- Target your anchor domain — formal education, intensive study, deliberate practice ([[Deliberate Practice]])
- Reach the level where you can identify non-obvious problems and generate original solutions

**The horizontal bar (breadth)**:
- Apply the [[5-Hour Rule]]: 1 hr/day across adjacent and non-adjacent fields
- Read broadly: philosophy, history, economics, biology, psychology, mathematics
- Prioritize fields with high **transfer value** — mathematics, probability, physics, and philosophy of science transfer into almost every discipline

## The π-Model for Polymaths

[[Michael Simmons]]'s three-domain model maps to π-shaped knowledge: two vertical bars (two deep domains) connected by broad horizontal awareness. The goal is not to have 10 deep domains but to have 2–3 with enough horizontal reach to connect them.

## Choosing Complementary Domains

The most valuable combinations are:
1. **Adjacent but not obvious** — neuroscience + machine learning (both study complex adaptive systems)
2. **Tools + application** — mathematics as a tool domain combined with any application domain
3. **Technical + humanistic** — engineering + communication, science + philosophy

> [!note] The goal is not symmetry across the T. The horizontal bar needs enough depth to recognize analogies, not enough depth to publish. Breadth serves depth.
