# Sleepmaxxing Protocol

"Sleepmaxxing" is the practice of stacking every available lever to maximize sleep quality and quantity, framed in fitness circles as a prerequisite for muscle growth, fat loss, and recovery. As presented by [[Hypertrophy Academy]], the protocol organizes interventions into five categories so they can be tackled systematically rather than piecemeal.

> [!key-insight] Health solutions are usually multiple things, not one
> Most sleep problems have several stacking causes (e.g. [[Sleep Apnea and Nose Breathing|apnea]] *and* insomnia together). The protocol's logic is additive — glycine alone helps a little, but the full stack of environment + lifestyle + supplements + food compounds.

## The Five Categories

1. **Environment** — cool (~19–21°C), dark, comfortable, single-purpose bedroom. See [[Sleep Environment Optimization]] and [[Bedroom Stimulus Control]].
2. **Lifestyle** — caffeine before midday, no work/screens 90 min before bed, a consistent sun-aligned schedule, screening for [[Sleep Apnea and Nose Breathing|apnea]], and general fitness. See [[Circadian Rhythm Alignment]] and [[Pre-Bed Light Management]].
3. **Supplements** — glycine, magnesium, L-theanine, optional melatonin/cherry juice, bolus creatine for bad nights. See [[Sleep Optimization Supplements]].
4. **Food before bed** — easy-to-digest meals ≥1 hr before bed; kiwi + honey to raise melatonin and stabilize [[Liver Glycogen and Overnight Hypoglycemia|liver glycogen]].
5. **Tracking** — log total hours; wearables optional and best used sparingly to avoid score anxiety ([[Wearable Health Metric Validity]]).

## Why It's Framed as Priority #1

The argument is that sleep is uniquely unforgiving among health pillars: you can train sloppily and still progress, but lost sleep has "no second chances." Because it underpins hypertrophy, fat loss, cravings (worse sleep ≈ ~350 extra calories eaten next day), and injury avoidance, it's positioned as the first thing to optimize.

> [!caution] Creator protocol, not clinical guidance
> This framework comes from a fitness creator and mixes cited science with personal anecdote and selective dosing (e.g. 800 mg magnesium, 10–20 g creatine). Useful as a checklist; not a substitute for medical advice. Compare with the evidence-graded [[Sleep Optimization Supplements]].
