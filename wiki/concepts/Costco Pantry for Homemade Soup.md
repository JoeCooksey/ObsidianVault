# Costco Pantry for Homemade Soup

Master ingredient list — what Costco reliably stocks that feeds a soup habit cheaply.

## Fresh Vegetables (Bulk)

| Item | Format | Approx. Price | Soup Use |
|------|--------|--------------|----------|
| Organic carrots | 6 lb bag | ~$3.50 | All soups — mirepoix base |
| Russet potatoes | 10 lb bag | ~$5 | Hearty soups, chowders |
| Onions | 5–10 lb bag | <$1/lb | Every soup — foundational |
| Celery | Bundle | ~$3–4 | Mirepoix — trinity with carrot+onion |
| Mixed bell peppers | 6-count | ~$5–7 | Minestrone, ratatouille soup |
| Garlic | Bulk heads or pre-peeled jar | ~$4–8 | Every soup |
| Roma/Campari tomatoes | 2–4 lb pack | ~$5–7 | Tomato soup, minestrone |
| Cocktail cucumbers | 1.5 lb greenhouse | ~$5 | Cold soups (gazpacho) |
| Chef's Blend mushrooms | 1–2 lb | ~$5–8 | Mushroom soup, umami boost |

**Best value base vegetables**: onions + carrots + potatoes = the three always-worth-buying items. Together they anchor ~80% of all vegetable soups.

## Frozen Vegetable Blends

| Product | Contents | Weight | Best Soup Use |
|---------|----------|--------|---------------|
| Kirkland Stir-Fry Special Blend | Broccoli, snap peas, green beans, carrots, yellow carrots, mushrooms, red bell peppers, water chestnuts, onions | 5.5 lbs | Quick weeknight soup; minestrone |
| Path of Life Garden Vegetable Blend | Mixed seasonal vegetables (individually frozen) | Varies | Drop-in broth soup (20-min dinner) |

**Key advantage of frozen blends**: pre-cut, pre-portioned, nutritionally equivalent to fresh (frozen at peak ripeness), 0 prep time.

## Broth / Stock

| Product | Format | Price |
|---------|--------|-------|
| Kirkland Organic Chicken Stock | 6 × 32 oz cartons | ~$8.99 |
| Kirkland Organic Chicken Bone Broth | 6 × 32 oz cartons | ~$12–14 |

**Note**: No standalone vegetable broth in Kirkland brand confirmed, but the chicken stock works for all soup types. For fully vegetarian soups, use water + 1 tbsp Kirkland No-Salt Seasoning as a substitute broth base, or buy separate vegetable broth in-store.

## Spices & Seasonings

| Product | Key Use | Price |
|---------|---------|-------|
| Kirkland Organic No-Salt Seasoning | 21-spice blend — all-purpose finishing season | ~$9.99 / 14.5 oz |
| Kirkland Granulated Garlic | Depth without saltiness | ~$5–7 |
| Kirkland Ground Turmeric | Lentil/curry soups, anti-inflammatory | ~$19.99 / 2×12 oz |
| Kirkland Organic Paprika | Tomato soups, Spanish-style soups | ~$8–10 |
| Generic bulk: bay leaves, thyme, oregano, cumin, coriander | Standard pantry spices | Available in warehouse |

**The two non-negotiable spice purchases**: Kirkland No-Salt Seasoning (replaces 8+ individual spice bottles) + Granulated Garlic. Together they cover most soup seasoning needs.

## Other Pantry Items (Useful for Soups)

- **Kirkland EVOO** (2-liter tin) — sautéing vegetables at the start of every soup
- **Kirkland canned diced tomatoes** — minestrone, lentil, any tomato-based soup
- **Dried lentils / dried beans** — Costco sells bulk bags; essential for protein-rich soups
- **Barley / pasta** — bulk or individual packages; thickener for hearty soups
- **Kirkland coconut milk** (canned) — Thai-style soups, curried lentil

## Shopping Strategy

**Minimum viable Costco soup pantry** (~$40–50):
1. Onions (5 lb) + carrots (6 lb) + potatoes (10 lb) = base for 10+ soups
2. Kirkland Organic Chicken Stock (6-pack) = broth covered
3. Kirkland No-Salt Seasoning = seasoning covered
4. Kirkland Stir-Fry Frozen Blend (5.5 lb) = quick-soup shortcut

With those four items, you can make every soup in [[Costco Vegetable Soup Recipes]] at least once.
