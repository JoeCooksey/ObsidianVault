# OSRS Ironman Early Game

Tutorial Island through roughly the first 50–100 hours (Source: [[Ironman Meta — Progression Guides]]). The goal: quest past slow training, unlock the account's logistics layer (teleports, run energy, passive resources), and build a cash/supply engine. Quests are the most efficient early skill training for irons (Source: [[OSRS Wiki — Ironman Guide]]).

## Unlock checklist

### Logistics & utilities
- [ ] Games necklace + ring of dueling (Ferox Enclave bank/restore) — first session
- [ ] Ardougne cloak 1 (Easy Ardougne diary) — unlimited monastery teleports
- [ ] Fairy rings — Lost City → Fairytale I (+ Fairytale II started)
- [ ] Graceful outfit (Marks of Grace; 60+ Agility makes it fast)
- [ ] Boots of Lightness (Temple of Ikov cellar)
- [ ] Spirit trees (Tree Gnome Village)
- [ ] Kingdom of Miscellania (Throne of Miscellania; keep 500–750k in coffer, herbs or teaks)

### Cash & supply engines
- [ ] Wintertodt to 50+ Firemaking → bulk supplies, seeds, early cash
- [ ] Blackjacking to 45–50+ Thieving → early GP
- [ ] Bone Voyage → birdhouse runs (passive Hunter + seeds)
- [ ] Tempoross from 35 Fishing → fish, planks, jewellery
- [ ] Hunter rumours from 46 Hunter → food + bone shards ([[Varlamore Ironman Unlocks]])
- [ ] Herb farming runs as soon as seeds allow

### Combat & prayer foundation
- [ ] Quest XP chain: Waterfall Quest (30 Atk/Str), Fight Arena, Tree Gnome Village, The Grand Tree, Witch's House, Vampyre Slayer — skips slow low-level melee *(confidence: medium — classic route; wiki names Waterfall + The Feud explicitly)*
- [ ] 43 Prayer (protection prayers) — blessed bone shards now the iron-friendly method (30+ Prayer)
- [ ] Iban's staff (Underground Pass) — early-game magic weapon
- [ ] 55 Magic — High Level Alchemy
- [ ] Tai Bwo Wannai Trio — karambwans (combo food)
- [ ] Barbarian fishing from 58 Fishing — passive Agility + Strength (~74/74 by 99 Fishing)

## Exit criteria

Base ~50–60 stats, fairy rings + graceful + Ardy cloak, Kingdom running, Wintertodt cash spent on a Herblore base. Then start the RFD push → [[OSRS Ironman Mid Game]].
