# Udemy Personal Plan

Udemy's consumer subscription — what people mean by "Udemy premium membership." There is no product literally named "Premium"; **"Premium" is the badge Udemy prints on a course card to mark that the course is inside the Personal Plan collection.**

## What you get

**28,000+ courses**, curated by Udemy staff from the full 250,000-course marketplace using engagement data from 75,000 learners. Roughly **11% of the catalog**, selected for rating and demand (Source: [[Udemy — Personal Plan Page and FAQ (July 2026)]]).

Beyond video:
- 20,000+ practice exercises
- Coding exercises, labs, and certification exam practice tests
- Udemy AI Assistant (in-course Q&A) and AI Role Play simulations
- Certificates of completion

## The three constraints that decide everything

**1. Access is rented, not owned.** Cancel and the entire library disappears the same day. Individually purchased courses are yours for life. This makes the plan a **burst tool**, not a library — subscribe for a defined sprint, extract hard, cancel.

**2. Subscriptions have no refund.** Individual course purchases carry Udemy's 30-day money-back guarantee; the subscription does **not** (confidence: medium — location-based exceptions apply, and a 7-day free trial is offered in some regions/promos, though the live page during the July 2026 promo showed the discount instead of a trial).

**3. The curation bar is ~4.5 stars, and it cuts against niche technical topics.** A small-audience course can be excellent and still be excluded for having 50 reviews. This is why [[Udemy Personal Plan EE Coverage Map|LTspice is essentially absent]] while Python is total.

## Who it's for

Worth it when you will genuinely finish **more than one course a month** in the curated set, across several subjects. Bad value for someone who wants one specific course — buy that one on sale for $10–20 and keep it forever.

> [!important] The marketing copy names web development, IT certification, data science, design, marketing, and leadership. It never says "engineering" — but engineering coverage is real and substantial. Do not judge the plan by the copy; judge it by the catalog.
