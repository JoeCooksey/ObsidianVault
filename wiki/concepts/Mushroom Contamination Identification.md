# Mushroom Contamination Identification

Contamination is a competing mold or bacterium out-growing the desired mushroom mycelium. The single most useful skill in cultivation is telling **healthy white mycelium** (rope-like, fresh smell) from a contaminant. The three common offenders are *Trichoderma*, bacteria, and cobweb mold (Source: [[Fungi Academy / GroCycle — Mushroom Contamination Guide]]).

## Identification table

| Contaminant | Appearance | Smell | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trichoderma (green mold)** | White at first, then **bright green/blue-green** patches | Musty/coconut | **Toss.** Fast, aggressive, unsalvageable once green |
| **Bacterial (wet spot)** | **Slimy, wet, grey/yellow** patches; no fuzz | **Sour, foul** | **Toss.** Usually from too-wet substrate or under-sterilized grain |
| **Cobweb mold** | **Grey, wispy, fast-spreading** fuzz (looks like dust/spiderweb) | Faint/musty | Sometimes salvageable if caught very early |
| **Overlay (not contam)** | Cottony, fluffy white *mycelium* | Normal | Not a contaminant — it's the mycelium gone fluffy from high heat / low humidity / low FAE |

(Sources: [[Fungi Academy / GroCycle — Mushroom Contamination Guide]], [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]])

## Action by contaminant

- **Green (Trichoderma) or any sour/slimy patch**: the bag is lost. **Isolate it immediately**, seal it in a trash bag away from your grows, and dispose outside. Do not open it indoors — opening releases millions of spores that infect future grows.
- **Cobweb mold, caught early**: mist the spot with **3% hydrogen peroxide** and increase airflow; it can sometimes be knocked back.
- **Overlay**: not dangerous. Lower the temperature, raise humidity, and improve FAE. If it's a thick mat blocking pinning, scratch/disturb the surface to encourage fruiting.

(Source: [[Fungi Academy / GroCycle — Mushroom Contamination Guide]])

## Prevention beats cure

Most contamination traces back to **inoculation** (non-sterile technique, moving air) or **too-wet substrate**. Nail [[Sterile Inoculation Technique]], keep substrate at field capacity (squeezes a drop or two, not a stream), and never break-and-shake or open a bag that already shows any off-color or off-smell growth.

> [!warning] When in doubt, throw it out. A single contaminated bag opened indoors can spore-bomb every other grow in the room.
