# Independence as the Dividend

[[Morgan Housel|Housel's]] claim that the **highest-return use of money is autonomy** — control over your time and choices — which research consistently shows beats material purchases for well-being. The best thing money buys is the ability to wake up and do what you want, with whom you want.

The corollary for spending: purchases that *erode* independence (debt, status upkeep, obligations, golden handcuffs) are usually bad trades even when affordable, while spending that *protects or expands* freedom is almost always worth it. It is the spending-side application of [[Financial Freedom vs Wealth]] — wealth is what you don't spend, and what it buys you is options.

*Source: [[The Art of Spending Money - Morgan Housel]].*
