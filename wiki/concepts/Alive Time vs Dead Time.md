# Alive Time vs Dead Time

A distinction [[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]] takes from [[The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene|Robert Greene]]: in any down period — a setback, a wait, a demotion, a forced pause — you are spending one of two kinds of time.

- **Dead time** — passive, resentful waiting; biding the hours, resenting the situation, learning nothing.
- **Alive time** — active use of the interval to learn, build, observe, and improve.

The circumstances are identical; the only variable is what you do with them. This is the single most portable idea in *Ego Is the Enemy*: every rejection, layoff, injury, or delay becomes raw material the moment you decide to make the time *alive*.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Failure" (concept from Robert Greene).*
