# Talk Depletes Us

[[Ryan Holiday|Holiday]]'s argument (drawing on research by Peter Gollwitzer) that **announcing a goal gives the brain much of the same satisfaction as actually achieving it**, which quietly drains the drive to do the work. Talking about a plan feels like progress while producing none.

The prescription: *do, don't talk.* Keep your head down, let the work accumulate in silence, and let results — not declarations — be the proof. This is the opposite of building a brand or "manifesting" out loud; the ego wants the applause of intention, and giving it that applause early is how ambitions die quietly.

*Source: [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday]] — "Aspire."*
