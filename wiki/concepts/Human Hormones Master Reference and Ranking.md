# Human Hormones Master Reference and Ranking

Catalog of every major human hormone: producing gland/organ, primary function, and how to optimize it. Hormones are chemical messengers; the body makes **50+** of them. This page covers the ~40 that matter most. Importance tier is at the bottom.

> [!info] Key principle
> You rarely optimize a single hormone in isolation. **Sleep, resistance exercise, protein, fiber, sunlight, and stress control** move most of the high-impact hormones in the right direction at once (Source: [[Healthline — Balance Hormones Naturally]]). Chase the levers, not the molecules.

## Hypothalamic & Pituitary (the master controllers)

These sit at the top of the cascade — the hypothalamus signals the pituitary, which signals the downstream glands.

| Hormone | Source | Function | Optimize via |
|---|---|---|---|
| Releasing hormones (CRH, GnRH, GHRH, TRH) | Hypothalamus | Tell the pituitary which hormones to release | Sleep, circadian light, stress control |
| **Growth hormone (GH)** | Anterior pituitary | Growth, tissue repair, fat metabolism | Deep sleep, fasting, intense exercise |
| **ACTH** | Anterior pituitary | Triggers adrenal cortisol release | Stress management (drives [[Cortisol Complete Guide]]) |
| **TSH** | Anterior pituitary | Triggers thyroid hormone release | Adequate iodine/selenium, manage stress |
| **LH & FSH** | Anterior pituitary | Drive testes/ovaries → sex hormones | Sleep, body-fat in range, avoid overtraining |
| **Prolactin** | Anterior pituitary | Milk production; suppresses libido when high | Manage stress; high levels often pathological |
| **ADH / vasopressin** | Posterior pituitary | Water & electrolyte balance | Hydration; alcohol suppresses it |
| **Oxytocin** | Hypothalamus → pituitary | Bonding, labor, social connection | Social contact, touch, warmth |

## Metabolic & energy hormones (highest daily leverage)

| Hormone | Source | Function | Optimize via |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Insulin** | Pancreas (β-cells) | Lowers blood glucose; nutrient storage | Resistance + cardio exercise, fiber, less added sugar, stay lean (Source: [[Healthline — Balance Hormones Naturally]]) |
| **Glucagon** | Pancreas (α-cells) | Raises blood glucose between meals | Protein, avoid constant snacking |
| **Thyroid (T3/T4)** | Thyroid | Sets baseline metabolic rate of every cell | Iodine, selenium, sleep, avoid extreme dieting |
| **Cortisol** | Adrenal cortex | Stress response, glucose mobilization, wake signal | Morning light, sleep, manage stress → see [[Cortisol Complete Guide]] |
| **Leptin** | Fat tissue | "I'm full / energy stores are high" signal | Sleep, lower added sugar, avoid leptin resistance from obesity |
| **Ghrelin** | Stomach/gut | "I'm hungry" signal | Protein per meal lowers it; sleep ≥7h (Source: [[Healthline — Balance Hormones Naturally]]) |
| **GLP-1** | Gut | Slows gastric emptying, boosts insulin, satiety | Fiber, protein, whole foods |
| **Adiponectin** | Fat tissue | Improves insulin sensitivity, anti-inflammatory | Stay lean, exercise |

## Stress & catecholamines

| Hormone | Source | Function | Optimize via |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Adrenaline (epinephrine)** | Adrenal medulla | Acute fight-or-flight: heart rate, energy | Useful acutely (exercise, cold); avoid chronic activation |
| **Noradrenaline (norepinephrine)** | Adrenal medulla / neurons | Alertness, vasoconstriction, focus | Exercise, cold exposure |
| **Cortisol** | Adrenal cortex | (see metabolic) chronic elevation is harmful | [[Ways to Reduce Cortisol (Tier List)]] |

## Sex & reproductive hormones

| Hormone | Source | Function | Optimize via |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Testosterone** | Testes (men), ovaries/adrenals (small) | Muscle, bone, libido, drive, RBC production | Sleep, resistance training, lean body fat, zinc/vit-D if deficient → [[Testosterone-Boosting Habits to Include]] |
| **Estrogen** | Ovaries, fat tissue, placenta | Female reproduction, bone & cardiovascular protection | Mediterranean diet pattern, healthy body fat (Source: [[Healthline — Balance Hormones Naturally]]) |
| **Progesterone** | Ovaries, placenta | Maintains pregnancy, balances estrogen | Manage stress (cortisol "steals" the pathway) |
| **DHEA / androgens** | Adrenal cortex | Precursor to sex steroids | Sleep, manage chronic stress |

## Calcium, fluid & blood hormones

| Hormone | Source | Function | Optimize via |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Parathyroid hormone (PTH)** | Parathyroid | Raises blood calcium | Adequate calcium + vitamin D |
| **Calcitonin** | Thyroid | Lowers blood calcium (minor in humans) | — |
| **Active vitamin D (calcitriol)** | Kidneys | Calcium absorption, immune & bone health | Sunlight, supplement if deficient |
| **Aldosterone** | Adrenal cortex | Sodium retention, blood pressure | Sodium/potassium balance, hydration |
| **Renin / angiotensin** | Kidneys / liver | Blood pressure regulation | Manage BP, sodium |
| **Erythropoietin (EPO)** | Kidneys | Stimulates red blood cell production | Altitude, healthy kidneys |
| **IGF-1** | Liver (via GH) | Mediates growth/repair effects of GH | Protein, sleep, exercise |

## Sleep & circadian

| Hormone | Source | Function | Optimize via |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Melatonin** | Pineal gland | Signals biological night; initiates sleep | Dim light at night, morning sunlight → [[Circadian Rhythm Alignment]], [[Pre-Bed Light Management]] |

> [!note] Neurotransmitters that act hormone-like
> **Dopamine** and **serotonin** are primarily neurotransmitters but have systemic signaling roles. Dopamine also functions as a pituitary-controlling hormone (it inhibits prolactin). See [[Dopamine Complete Guide]].

## Importance ranking

Ranked by how fast a serious disruption threatens life or whole-body function. Most hormones are individually survivable with replacement therapy — the tiering reflects physiological centrality, not "you die instantly."

> [!tip] S-Tier — life-threatening if acutely deranged
> - **Insulin** — without it, fatal diabetic ketoacidosis; controls all fuel metabolism
> - **Cortisol** — acute deficiency (Addisonian crisis) is fatal; governs stress, BP, glucose
> - **Thyroid (T3/T4)** — sets metabolic rate of every cell; severe excess/deficiency is life-threatening
> - **Aldosterone** — electrolyte/BP collapse if absent
> - **ADH/vasopressin** — absence → dangerous water loss (diabetes insipidus)

> [!tip] A-Tier — central to daily function & long-term health
> - **Growth hormone / IGF-1**, **glucagon**, **PTH**, **adrenaline**, **melatonin**, **parathyroid/calcium axis**

> [!tip] B-Tier — major quality-of-life & long-horizon impact
> - **Testosterone**, **estrogen**, **progesterone** (reproduction, bone, body composition), **leptin/ghrelin** (appetite), **EPO**, **active vitamin D**

> [!tip] C-Tier — important but more redundant or narrow
> - **Oxytocin**, **prolactin**, **DHEA**, **calcitonin**, **adiponectin**, **GLP-1**

> [!gap] "Importance" is context-dependent. For a young healthy person optimizing performance, the *practical* leverage order is closer to: insulin sensitivity → cortisol rhythm → thyroid → testosterone/estrogen → growth hormone → melatonin. The S-tier above is about survival, not optimization payoff.

## The five levers that move everything

1. **Sleep 7–9h** — corrects cortisol, GH, leptin, ghrelin, insulin, testosterone at once
2. **Resistance + cardio exercise** — insulin sensitivity, GH, testosterone, adiponectin
3. **Protein 25–30g/meal + fiber** — ghrelin down, GLP-1/satiety up, insulin stable
4. **Morning sunlight + dim nights** — cortisol rhythm + melatonin (circadian backbone)
5. **Chronic stress control** — keeps cortisol from suppressing thyroid, sex hormones, and immunity

(Source for levers: [[Healthline — Balance Hormones Naturally]])
