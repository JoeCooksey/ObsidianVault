# Time Management Matrix

Covey's four-quadrant task prioritization framework from Habit 3: tasks are categorized by **Urgent vs. Not Urgent** and **Important vs. Not Important** — Q1 (crises), Q2 (prevention/planning/relationships — the quadrant of effectiveness), Q3 (interruptions — the quadrant of deception), Q4 (trivia); high-effectiveness people deliberately live in Q2, which prevents Q1 crises before they occur.
