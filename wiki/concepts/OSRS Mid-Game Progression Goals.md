# OSRS Mid-Game Progression Goals

The mid-game is the bridge from "trained the basics" to "ready for end-game raids and bossing." If the question is *what's worth doing next*, these are the highest-leverage goals — each unlocks more content than it costs. (Source: [[Theoatrix — 20 Mid-Game Goals OSRS]])

## Gear unlocks (do first)
- **Barrows Gloves** — strong all-round bonuses; gate for Ferocious Gloves and many quests.
- **Dragon Defender** — used almost everywhere; required to build the Avernic Defender (BiS offensive shield).
- **Fire Cape** (Fight Caves) and cape upgrades (Ava's Assembler, Mage Arena 2).
- **Fighter Torso** (Barbarian Assault) — free high strength bonus; needed for master clues.
- **Rigour & Augury** prayers — ~11M GP at 77 Prayer; major DPS for ranged/magic.

## Combat & skills
- **Base 90s combat** (90 Att/Str/Def) — the practical entry stat line for most end-game PvM.
- **Slayer** — trains most combat stats and gates many money-makers; required for max cape.
- **83 Construction** — maxed player-owned house (~50M GP), big quality-of-life jump.

## Account-wide unlocks
- **Grandmaster quests** — Monkey Madness 2, Song of the Elves, Dragon Slayer 2, Desert Treasure 2, While Guthix Sleeps — open new combat methods and areas.
- **Spellbooks** — Ancients (DT1), Lunars (Lunar Diplomacy / Dream Mentor), Arceuus (A Kingdom Divided).
- **Achievement Diaries** (hard tier+) and **Medium Combat Achievements** — quality-of-life rewards (e.g. no prayer drain at Barrows).
- **Mid-game bosses** — Scurrius, Perilous Moons — teach mechanics while paying GP/XP.

## Passive engines to start early
- **Herb/birdhouse/tree runs** and **Farming** — set up the daily passive income + XP loop ASAP (overlaps [[OSRS AFK Money-Making Methods]]).
