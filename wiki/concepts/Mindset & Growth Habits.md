# Mindset & Growth Habits

Habits 21–40 from [[101 Habits — Dan Zakaria]]. The inner game: learn relentlessly, reframe failure, stay curious, and control your self-talk.

## The Habits
21. **Read every day** — "the one habit that changes your life"; absorb an author's life in a few weeks.
22. **Reframe failure as lessons** — failure is a lesson, not a setback; unavoidable and necessary.
23. **Ask better questions** — better questions get better answers; people like you for it.
24. **Practice gratitude** — write it down, be specific; "what is vague is ignored." → [[Gratitude Practice (Evidence Review)]]
25. **Assume responsibility** — "make everything your fault" so you can fix it.
26. **Stop comparing yourself** — comparison is the thief of joy; social media drives it.
27. **Progress, not perfection** — get 1% better; don't chase perfect.
28. **Learn something new weekly** — the best skill is *learning how to learn*. → [[Daily Learning Habit (Operating System)]]
29. **Think long term** — success is built in decades; "if it won't matter in 2 years, it shouldn't matter now."
30. **Seek feedback** — learn from successful *and* average people.
31. **Challenge negative thoughts** — "your life heads in the direction of your strongest thought."
32. **Celebrate small wins** — with friends/family.
33. **Adopt a beginner's mindset** — "if you're the best in the room, you're in the wrong room."
34. **Stay curious** — opportunities come from anywhere.
35. **Learn from people better than you** — drop the ego.
36. **Replace complaints with solutions.**
37. **Journal your thoughts** — capture great ideas before they vanish.
38. **Reflect before reacting** — "when angry don't respond, when sad don't decide, when happy don't promise."
39. **Practice optimism** — "optimism is a skill you train, happiness is a habit you practice."
40. **Become a lifelong student** — seek mastery and self-mastery.

## Notes
- #21/#28/#40 are the learning core → overlaps [[Daily Learning Habit (Operating System)]] and [[Research - How to Learn Something Every Day (The Daily Learning Habit)]].
- #24 has the strongest evidence base in this set → [[Gratitude Practice (Evidence Review)]].
