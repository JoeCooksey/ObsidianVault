# Pluralistic Ignorance

The dark side of [[Social Proof]]: in an ambiguous emergency, **everyone looks to everyone else to gauge whether action is needed — and because each person sees the others staying calm, they all conclude nothing is wrong.** The collective passivity is mutually reinforcing.

This is the mechanism behind the **bystander effect** (illustrated by the Kitty Genovese case [[Robert B. Cialdini|Cialdini]] discusses): the more witnesses present, the *less* likely any one of them is to help, because responsibility is diffused and no one wants to overreact. The practical antidote: if you ever need help in a crowd, **single out one specific person** ("You, in the blue jacket — call 911"), which removes the ambiguity and the diffusion of responsibility.
