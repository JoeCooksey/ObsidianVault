# Emotional Bank Account

Covey's relational trust metaphor: every relationship operates like a bank account where deposits (keeping promises, showing kindness, clarifying expectations, being loyal) build a reserve of trust, and withdrawals (broken promises, disrespect, betrayal) drain it — high-balance accounts tolerate mistakes and enable direct communication; low-balance accounts require constant management overhead.
