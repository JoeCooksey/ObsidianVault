# Trading Stances and Windows (LoL)

Trading is a repeatable skill built on wave awareness, spacing, and timing — not raw mechanics. In top lane's melee-vs-melee environment, trading matters *more* than CS: one lost trade can zone you off the wave for the rest of laning. (Source: [[Boosteria — Trading in Lane Guide]])

## The Three Stances

| Stance | Position | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Aggressive** | Slightly ahead of your casters, angled toward the enemy's last-hit line | You have a level/HP/cooldown edge and want to punish CS attempts |
| **Defensive** | Behind your casters, CS only | Behind, gank threat, or losing matchup |
| **Cheater** | Playing for the slow push and crash | Executing a cheater recall — trade only to protect the wave plan |

Good players shift stance constantly based on wave state, cooldowns, and jungle info. (Source: [[Boosteria — Trading in Lane Guide]])

## Trading Windows (in order of reliability)

1. **Cooldown windows** — the most reliable rule. The enemy just used their trade tool (poke, shield, dash, stun): their ability to answer is down for a fixed number of seconds. Track 1–2 key ability timers, not the whole kit.
2. **Last-hit punishes** — step forward as they animation-lock on a minion; land Auto→Ability→Auto, disengage behind your minions.
3. **Level spikes** — levels 2, 3, and 6 grant new abilities or combos. The real skill is *preparing the wave so you hit the level first*, not just knowing the levels matter. (Source: [[Boosteria — Trading in Lane Guide]])
4. **Item timings** — returning from a crash with a component advantage (e.g. a long sword they don't have) is a window.

## Spacing: Tethering and Minion Aggro

- **Tethering**: enter the edge of your damage range, hit once, immediately step back outside *their* effective range. Threaten without being punishable.
- **Minion aggro**: autoing an enemy champion draws nearby enemy minions onto you. Avoid trading into 6+ enemy casters; trade when *your* wave is thicker. Brush drops minion aggro.
- Match trade length to your kit: burst champions take short trades; sustain/extended fighters want long ones.

## Convert the Win

A won trade is wasted unless you immediately convert: **crash and recall** (item advantage), **freeze** (zone them off the wave while they're weak), or **slow push** (build the next crash). (Source: [[Boosteria — Trading in Lane Guide]])

## Common Mistakes

- Trading inside the enemy wave early (minion damage loses the trade)
- Burning your best cooldown for minor poke instead of saving it for a window
- Winning a trade and doing nothing with it
- Trading with no jungle information — lean safe and keep escape tools up
