# Fictive Language

The capability unlocked by the [[Cognitive Revolution]]: the uniquely human ability to use language to talk about **things that do not physically exist** — gods, legends, money, nations, corporations.

Other animals can communicate real information ("lion by the river"); only Sapiens can convey and collectively believe fiction. This is the mechanism behind [[Shared Fictions]] and is what lets humans cooperate beyond the [[Cognitive Dunbar's Number|~150-person]] ceiling that limits groups bound only by personal acquaintance. Gossip plus shared myth is, in Harari's account, the foundation of all large-scale human society.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 1.*
