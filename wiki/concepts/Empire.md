# Empire

In *[[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari|Sapiens]]*, [[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari]] argues that empire has been **the most common and most successful form of political organization** for the last 2,500 years — and one of the three great unifiers of humankind.

His provocative claim: empires, for all their brutality and conquest, have been the chief engines of cultural diffusion — spreading law, language, technology, religion, and ideas across previously separate peoples, and absorbing the conquered into larger shared identities. Most of the cultures people today defend as "authentic" are themselves products of past empires. Empire is thus, alongside [[Money as Trust|money]] and [[Religion as Unifier|religion]], a major driver of the [[Unification of Humankind]].

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 3.*
