# Reducing Flatulence Odor (Protocol)

**The runnable answer.** Smell and volume are separate problems — fix the one that bothers you. Smell is the sulfur gases; volume is the bulk carbs gases. Most people want *less smell*.

## Layer 1 — Diet (the root cause of smell)
- **Cut the sulfur load**, especially large servings at once: red meat, eggs, and big plates of cruciferous (broccoli/cabbage/Brussels sprouts) + alliums (garlic/onion). See [[High-Sulfur Foods and Flatus Odor]].
- Spread protein across the day rather than one huge meat serving.
- **For volume/bloating too:** trial a **low-FODMAP** approach (less wheat, onion, beans, sugar alcohols). (Source: [[AAFP 2009 - Effective Management of Flatulence]])
- Don't abandon fiber/beans — the gut **adapts over ~weeks**; ramp gradually ([[Dietary Fiber and Gut Health]]).
- Limit **sorbitol/mannitol/xylitol** (sugar-free gum/candy) — fermentable polyols.

## Layer 2 — Eating behavior (cuts swallowed-air volume)
- Eat slower, smaller meals; chew with mouth closed.
- Cut **carbonated drinks** and gum chewing (both add swallowed air).
(Source: [[Cary Gastroenterology - Why Do My Farts Smell So Bad]])

## Layer 3 — On-demand odor control (for events)
- **Bismuth subsalicylate** (Pepto-Bismol), 524 mg up to 4×/day → **>95% less H₂S**. Episodic use only. See [[Bismuth Subsalicylate for Flatus Odor]]. *(Bismuth subgallate / Devrom is the dedicated "internal deodorant" version.)*
- **External charcoal garments** (briefs/pads) adsorb >90% of escaping sulfur gas. **Oral charcoal does NOT work** (saturates in transit — [[Suarez 1999 - Failure of Activated Charcoal to Reduce Gas]]).
- **Alpha-galactosidase (Beano)** before bean/bran/veg meals → cuts *volume* of carb-driven gas.

## What does NOT help (despite popularity)
- **Oral activated charcoal** — no benefit (SOR B).
- **Simethicone (Gas-X)** — no benefit for common flatulence (SOR B); it's for trapped bubbles, not odor.

## Priority order if you only do one thing
1. Reduce the biggest single sulfur source in your diet.
2. Slow down eating + drop carbonation.
3. Keep bismuth on hand for events.

## When to see a doctor
Persistent foul gas with **pain, bleeding, fever, diarrhea, or unexplained weight loss** → evaluate for food intolerance (lactose/gluten), **SIBO**, IBS/IBD, malabsorption. (Source: [[Cary Gastroenterology - Why Do My Farts Smell So Bad]])
