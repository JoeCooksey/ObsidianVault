# Are We Happier?

The question [[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari]] uses to puncture the progress narrative of *[[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari|Sapiens]]*: across all four revolutions our collective **power** has compounded enormously — but it is far from clear that individual **happiness** has risen with it.

Foragers may have had more varied, less stressful lives than the peasants of agrarian states ([[The Wheat Trap]]); modern comfort coexists with anxiety, loneliness, and meaning-loss. Harari notes that happiness depends heavily on expectations and biochemistry, not just material conditions, so more wealth and technology need not translate into more contentment. His unsettling conclusion: *"We are more powerful than ever before, but have very little idea what to do with all that power"* — the moral hanging over [[The End of Homo Sapiens]].

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 4.*
