# Money as Trust

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] framing of money as **"the most universal and most efficient system of mutual trust ever devised."** Money has no intrinsic value — a banknote, a gold coin, a number in a database is worth something only because everyone believes everyone else will accept it.

It is the purest [[Shared Fictions|shared fiction]]: it converts everything (land, labor, honor, time) into a common unit, letting total strangers — even enemies who share no language, religion, or law — cooperate and trade. For Harari this makes money one of the three great forces behind the [[Unification of Humankind]], alongside [[Empire]] and [[Religion as Unifier|religion]].

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 3.*
