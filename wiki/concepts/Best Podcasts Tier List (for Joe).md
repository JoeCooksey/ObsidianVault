# Best Podcasts Tier List (for Joe)

"Best" is preference, not fact — this list is **ranked for Joe's tracks** (EE/WBG power, AI, learning science, books/self-improvement, health, finance/quant, faith) using 2026 best-of lists as the candidate pool and the vault as the fit filter. Confidence: low-medium by nature; demote freely after sampling.

**Selection rule** (from [[Learning from Podcasts (Evidence Review)]]): prefer shows that are *interesting-demanding* over *enjoyable-easy* ([[Interest vs Enjoyment]]) — and prefer hosts whose **questions** you trust, since claims need verifying anyway ([[Parasocial Relationships (Podcast Hosts)]]).

## S-tier — core rotation

- **Dwarkesh Podcast** ([[Dwarkesh Patel]]) — the best-prepared interviewer in tech; frontier AI, compute, economics of AGI. Directly feeds Joe's AI×EE thesis (the [[Darkesh-Patel-Jensen-Huang-Nvidia-2025]] episode is already a vault source).
- **Huberman Lab** ([[Andrew Huberman]]) — already the backbone of the vault's dopamine/habits/cortisol clusters. Use Essentials episodes for density; verify supplement claims independently.
- **The Knowledge Project** (Shane Parrish) — mental models, decision-making, learning; the podcast equivalent of the vault's Books domain.
- **Lex Fridman Podcast** — marathon conversations with AI researchers, engineers, mathematicians, and the occasional theologian; uneven, but the hit rate on technical guests is unmatched.

## A-tier — high fit, rotate by season

- **Acquired** — deep company histories (TSMC, NVIDIA, ASML episodes are essentially audio courses in the semiconductor industry Joe tracks in [[EE Daily — June 11, 2026|EE Daily]]).
- **Founders** (David Senra) — one founder biography per episode; pairs with the vault's book-summary habit.
- **Latent Space** — the AI-engineer podcast; practical complement to Dwarkesh's frontier view.
- **The Amp Hour** (Chris Gammell & Dave Jones) — the long-running EE podcast: electronics design, industry, careers. The most directly on-major show on this list.
- **In Our Time** (BBC) — three academics + Melvyn Bragg on one topic (history/science/philosophy); the gold standard for podcast-as-seminar.
- **Hardcore History** (Dan Carlin) — 4–6 hr narrative history; the strongest case that audio can carry serious depth.

## B-tier — good, specific uses

- **EconTalk** (Russ Roberts) — weekly economist interviews since 2006; steelmanning practice.
- **99% Invisible** — design and engineered-world stories; light but consistently interesting.
- **The Daily Stoic** (Ryan Holiday) — short; pairs with the vault's [[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday|Ego]]/Stoicism cluster.
- **Embedded.fm** — embedded systems engineering; relevant once Joe's firmware track (C++/STM32) spins up.
- **Practical AI** — approachable ML/MLOps; B until Joe's ML phase, then promote.
- **The Peter Attia Drive** — longevity/health deep dives; the vault already encodes Attia's exercise pillars; episodes are reference-grade but long.
- **The Bible Project** — the strongest theology-domain podcast; literary-structural walkthroughs of Scripture (the vault's Theology domain is otherwise empty).

## C-tier — fine, but displaced by the above

- Stuff You Should Know, Radiolab, Freakonomics — pleasant generalist shows; for Joe they're *enjoyable-easy* cognitive snacks that crowd out A-tier time.
- Daily AI-news shows (The AI Daily Brief etc.) — useful only if AI news becomes a daily job input; otherwise [[EE Daily — June 11, 2026|EE Daily]] already covers it.

## Anti-recommendations

- Shows whose episodes are mostly ad reads + banter padding (the 20-min-content-in-90-min format) — the fluency illusion with extra steps.
- Health/finance shows monetized by the products they discuss — parasocial trust transfer is the business model.

> [!gap] All rankings are pre-sampling priors from best-of lists ([Discover Pods 2026](https://discoverpods.com/educational-podcasts/), Arize/TLDL AI lists) + vault fit. Re-rank after Joe actually listens; a tier list of unheard podcasts is a hypothesis.
