# Unity

The seventh weapon of influence, added by [[Robert B. Cialdini|Cialdini]] in the 2021 expanded edition of *Influence*. The strongest "yes" comes not from merely liking someone but from sharing an **identity** with them — being of the *same* family, hometown, ethnicity, team, religion, or surviving the same ordeal.

"We" categories make persuasion feel like self-persuasion: helping one of *us* is helping ourselves. It is distinct from **[[Liking]]** — unity is about shared identity ("one of us"), not affection or similarity of taste. Acting together toward a common goal, and co-creation, deepen the sense of unity. It explains the persuasive force of kinship appeals, regional pride, and in-group loyalty.

*Source: [[Influence - Robert Cialdini]] (expanded edition).*
