# Unification of Humankind

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] third revolution: the long process by which thousands of separate human cultures merged toward a single global civilization. Three universal orders did the pulling:

- **[[Money as Trust|Money]]** — the most universal system of mutual trust, letting strangers cooperate.
- **[[Empire]]** — the most successful (if brutal) political form, spreading law, ideas, and culture.
- **[[Religion as Unifier|Religion]]** — superhuman legitimacy for fragile human laws (extended by Harari to ideologies like [[Humanism]]).

The throughline is **[[The Arrow of History]]**: cultures constantly change and, over millennia, trend toward greater unity. Today's "global culture" is the near-endpoint of this process.

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 3.*
