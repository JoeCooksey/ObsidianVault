# Dopamine Increases by Activity (Tier List)

How much different activities and substances raise dopamine above baseline — and, crucially, **what it costs you afterward**. This is the page Joe asked for: a masterclass on "how much certain healthy and unhealthy activities provide."

> [!important] Two numbers matter, not one. The **multiplier** (how high the peak goes) tells you the rush. The **crash profile** (how far below baseline you fall, and for how long) tells you the price. A 2× spike that crashes hard is worse for you than a 2.5× spike that sustains with no crash. **Steepness and reliability of the spike — not just its height — drive addictive potential.** (Source: [[Huberman Lab Episode 39 — Controlling Your Dopamine]])

---

## The Master Table

Multipliers are **fold-increase above baseline** (1.5× = +50%). They are popularized magnitudes traceable to rat microdialysis ([[Di Chiara and Imperato 1988 — Drugs of Abuse and Dopamine]]) and small human studies — directional, not precise human constants.

| Activity / Substance | Peak (× baseline) | Onset | Crash below baseline | Net verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Morning sunlight** | small, slow | minutes | none | 🟢 Builds baseline; sets daily dopamine rhythm |
| **Cold water immersion (~14°C)** | **~2.5×** | minutes | **none** — sustained ~3 h | 🟢 Best ratio: big, durable, crash-free |
| **Exercise** | **~2×** | during/after | minimal; net positive | 🟢 Raises baseline long-term; D2 receptor upregulation |
| **Music (favorite tracks)** | modest | seconds | none | 🟢 Free, safe, anticipation-driven |
| **Meditation** | modest, sustained | over session | none | 🟢 Stabilizes baseline; lowers cortisol |
| **Social connection / flow** | modest | gradual | none | 🟢 Sustainable, baseline-supporting |
| **Caffeine** | **modest** | ~30–45 min | mild | 🟡 *Upregulates* dopamine receptors (net helpful in moderation) |
| **Chocolate / palatable food** | **~1.5×** | seconds–min | small | 🟡 Transient; fine occasionally |
| **Sex** | **~2×** | during | mild post-coital dip | 🟡 Healthy; pursuit + act both dopaminergic |
| **Sugar / ultra-processed food (frequent)** | ~1.5–2× | fast | yes, escalating | 🟠 Engineered for repeat spikes → tolerance |
| **Video games / doomscrolling / social media** | moderate, *variable-ratio* | seconds | yes; erodes baseline | 🟠 Intermittent reward = maximally habit-forming |
| **Pornography (chronic)** | moderate–high, novelty-driven | seconds | yes; desensitization | 🔴 Escalation + downregulation; flattens real-world reward |
| **Nicotine (smoked)** | **~2.5×** | seconds | sharp, short | 🔴 Fast spike + fast crash = high addictiveness |
| **Alcohol** | ~2× | minutes | yes, prolonged | 🔴 Spike then depressive trough |
| **Cocaine** | **~2.25–2.5×** | seconds | steep | 🔴 Blocks reuptake; strong downregulation |
| **Amphetamine / methamphetamine / Adderall (recreational doses)** | **~10×** | minutes | severe, long | 🔴 Largest spike → largest deficit; high neurotoxic risk at high doses |

Legend: 🟢 healthy/baseline-building · 🟡 fine in moderation · 🟠 erodes baseline with frequency · 🔴 strongly downregulating/addictive.

## Why the Same Multiplier Can Be Healthy or Harmful

Cold water and nicotine both peak around **2.5×**, yet one is a tool and one is a trap. The difference is **kinetics and crash**:

- **Cold immersion**: slow rise, *tonic* elevation, dopamine still ~150–200% of baseline **two hours later**, gentle return — **no receptor downregulation** (Source: [[Sramek 2000 — Cold Water Immersion and Catecholamines]]).
- **Nicotine**: near-instant *phasic* spike, gone in minutes, baseline undershoots — the steep up/down is exactly what trains compulsive re-use.

> [!key] Healthy dopamine sources are **slow, earned, and self-limiting**. Unhealthy ones are **fast, passive, and intermittently rewarded**. The fastest, most reliable spikes are the most addictive. (Source: [[Dopamine Downregulation and Tolerance]])

---

## The Hidden Tax: Baseline Erosion

Each potent spike borrows from your future baseline (Source: [[Dopamine Baseline and the Pleasure-Pain Balance]]). Stack several at once (phone + pre-workout + music + caffeine) and you blunt the **next day's** motivation. The clinical end-state of chronic high spikes is a **dopamine deficit state**: anhedonia, low drive, and needing the stimulus just to feel normal.

This is why 🔴 substances trend toward "you need it to feel okay" while 🟢 activities trend toward "you feel better in general."

---

## Practical Takeaways

1. **Favor 🟢 sources daily** — sunlight, cold, exercise, music, connection. They raise the floor.
2. **Don't stack spikes.** Do hard things *without* the dopamine garnish so the thing itself stays rewarding.
3. **Treat 🔴/🟠 as occasional, never default.** Their cost is paid in baseline.
4. **If anhedonic, rest the biggest spike for ~30 days** → [[Dopamine Detox (Evidence Review)]].

> [!gap] Precise human dopamine multipliers are hard to measure (most data are PET/microdialysis in animals or small samples). Use these as **relative tiers**, not lab-grade constants.
