# Mitochondria

The organelles that generate ~90% of cellular ATP via oxidative phosphorylation. Also gatekeepers of apoptosis, calcium buffering, and reactive-oxygen-species (ROS) signaling.

> [!note] Honest caveat: a mitochondrion is an **organelle, not a cell.** It's included in [[Research - Most Important Cells in the Human Body]] as the **highest-leverage sub-cellular optimization target** because it powers every other cell on this list — but it is ranked separately for that reason.

## Why they matter
- **Every energy-demanding cell depends on them** — [[Neurons]], [[Cardiomyocytes]], and skeletal muscle are the most mitochondria-dense.
- **Mitochondrial dysfunction is a hallmark of aging** and a driver of insulin resistance, sarcopenia, and neurodegeneration. (Source: [[Halling 2020 — PGC-1alpha and Mitochondrial Function]], confidence high)
- Quality matters as much as quantity: damaged mitochondria leak ROS and must be cleared by **mitophagy**.

## How to optimize them (actionable)
1. **Zone 2 endurance training** — sustained moderate cardio is the canonical stimulus for **mitochondrial biogenesis** via PGC-1α. (Source: [[Halling 2020 — PGC-1alpha and Mitochondrial Function]], confidence high)
2. **High-intensity intervals (e.g. 4×4)** — potent PGC-1α activator; raises mitochondrial density and VO₂max efficiently. (See [[4x4 Intervals for Running]] / [[4x4 Intervals for Rowing]].)
3. **Fasting / time-restricted eating** — triggers [[Autophagy]] and mitophagy, clearing damaged mitochondria. (Source: [[Mattson 2016 — Intermittent Fasting on Health and Disease]], confidence high)
4. **Avoid chronic overnutrition** — nutrient excess suppresses mitophagy and raises ROS.
5. **Adequate protein + micronutrients** — B-vitamins, CoQ10, magnesium are electron-transport-chain cofactors (food-first).
6. **Cold/heat exposure** — emerging evidence for biogenesis signaling (lower confidence).
