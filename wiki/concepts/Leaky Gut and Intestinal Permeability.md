# Leaky Gut and Intestinal Permeability

"Leaky gut" is the popular name for **increased intestinal permeability** — when the tight junctions between gut-lining cells loosen and let bacterial products, toxins, and undigested food particles cross into the bloodstream, triggering immune activation and systemic inflammation.

> [!important] Real science vs. marketing
> *Intestinal permeability* is a legitimate, measurable physiological state well-established in coeliac and Crohn's disease. "**Leaky gut syndrome**" as a stand-alone diagnosis you can buy a $200 test-and-supplement bundle for is **not** a validated clinical entity. Hold both truths: the mechanism is real; the wellness-industry packaging is over-sold.

## The Mechanism

The single-cell gut lining is sealed by **tight junction proteins** (occludin, claudins, zonula occludens). When these disassemble, the barrier becomes permeable. Bacterial **lipopolysaccharide (LPS)** and **flagellin** translocate into the portal circulation → low-grade systemic inflammation (Source: [[Human Gut Emulsifiers Study 2021]], emulsifier IBD reviews).

Zonulin (pre-haptoglobin 2) is a physiological *modulator* of tight junctions; gluten and bacterial exposure are its strongest triggers. But see the testing caveat below.

## Commonly Reported Symptoms

> [!warning] Symptoms are non-specific
> None of these *prove* leaky gut — they overlap with IBS, food intolerance, stress, and ordinary life. Treat them as reasons to investigate diet and lifestyle, not as a diagnosis.

- **Digestive:** bloating, gas, cramping, diarrhea/constipation, food sensitivities
- **Systemic:** chronic fatigue, brain fog, low mood/anxiety (gut–brain axis)
- **Immune/inflammatory:** frequent low-grade inflammation, joint aches, skin issues (acne, eczema)
- **Associations in research:** elevated permeability biomarkers appear in IBD, coeliac, type-1 diabetes, depression/suicidal behavior cohorts, and even healthy aging — but **association ≠ causation** in most of these.

## What Damages the Barrier

The biggest evidence-backed disruptors (full ranking in [[Gut-Harmful Foods and Actions Tier List]]):
- **Dietary emulsifiers** (CMC, polysorbate 80) — non-reversibly disturb human microbiota (Source: [[Human Gut Emulsifiers Study 2021]])
- **Alcohol** — modulates tight-junction proteins, raises gut leakiness and endotoxemia
- **NSAIDs** (ibuprofen, aspirin) — COX inhibition damages gut epithelium; effect common to the whole drug class
- **Chronic psychological stress** — exacerbates NSAID injury via glucocorticoid-receptor signaling
- **Circadian disruption / poor sleep** — increases permeability via occludin in animal models

## The Zonulin Testing Caveat

> [!warning] Don't buy a zonulin test
> Commercial zonulin ELISAs "do not reflect actual zonulin levels, but concentrations of unknown proteins," and correlate poorly (R ≈ 0.03–0.17) with real permeability (Source: [[Zonulin Biomarker Critique 2021]]). The validated functional test is the **lactulose:mannitol ratio** — see [[Gut Health Tests]].
