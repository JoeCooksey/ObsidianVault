# Stem Cells

Undifferentiated cells that can both self-renew and differentiate into specialized cell types. Include **tissue-resident adult stem cells** (intestinal, muscle satellite, neural) and **hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs)** that produce all blood and immune cells. **Ranked #3** in [[Research - Most Important Cells in the Human Body]].

## Why they matter
- **They regenerate everything else.** Skin, gut lining, blood, and immune cells are continuously rebuilt from stem-cell pools.
- **Their decline IS aging.** Falling stem-cell number and function underlies slower healing, immune decline, and tissue degeneration.
- They are the upstream source for [[Immune Cells]] (via HSCs) and tissue repair.

## How to optimize them (actionable)
1. **Periodic fasting / fasting-mimicking diet (FMD)** — the strongest lever. Promotes **stem-cell-based regeneration** and renewal of HSCs. (Source: [[Mattson 2016 — Intermittent Fasting on Health and Disease]], confidence medium — robust in rodents, human RCTs limited)
2. **[[Autophagy]] activation** (fasting, exercise) — autophagy maintains stem-cell quality and self-renewal capacity.
3. **Regular exercise** — increases muscle satellite cell activity and supports HSC niche health.
4. **Sleep + circadian regularity** — HSC output is circadian-gated; disruption impairs it.
5. **Minimize chronic inflammation** — "inflammaging" exhausts stem-cell pools; manage via diet, exercise, sleep.

> [!gap] Most regeneration evidence is from animal models. Human fasting/FMD trials show metabolic benefit but stem-cell regeneration endpoints are not yet confirmed by RCT.
