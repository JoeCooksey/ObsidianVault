# Summer 2026 Strength Program
**Upper/Lower 4-day split — Summer strength phase for Joe**

Per [[Joe's Custom Seasonal Training Plan]] and [[5 Pillars of Fitness]]:
**Summer = strength phase.** Upper/Lower 4–5 days/week, 3–5 rep range, 85–90% 1RM, maintenance calories, 2 Zone 2 cycling days.

Joe-specific adjustments (from [[Muscle Group Priority Guide]]): extra posterior chain emphasis every week; extra lat/upper back sets; OHP + rows + face pulls are priority every session.

---

## Weekly Schedule

| Day | Session | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Upper A — Horizontal Push/Pull Strength | 55–65 min |
| Tuesday | Lower A — Squat-dominant | 55–65 min |
| Wednesday | Zone 2 Cycling | 45–60 min |
| Thursday | Upper B — Vertical Push/Pull + Arms | 55–65 min |
| Friday | Lower B — Hip-dominant (most important day) | 55–65 min |
| Saturday | Zone 2 Cycling or Active Rest | 45–60 min |
| Sunday | Rest + PM Mobility (12 min) |  |

Before every Upper and Lower session: **7-min AM mobility routine** (per [[Daily Mobility Routine]]) — non-negotiable for hip flexor health.

---

## Upper A — Horizontal Push/Pull Strength (Monday)

**Focus:** Bench strength + horizontal row strength

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Barbell Bench Press | 4 × 3–5 | Main strength lift; add 2.5 lb/session when you hit top of range |
| Barbell Row (Pendlay or Bent-over) | 4 × 4–6 | Must match bench volume exactly |
| Incline Dumbbell Press | 3 × 6–8 | Slightly higher rep — upper chest emphasis |
| Cable Row (neutral grip) | 3 × 8–10 | Squeeze at contraction; full stretch |
| Face Pulls | 3 × 15–20 | Rear delt + rotator cuff; lighter, controlled |
| Lateral Raises (cable or DB) | 3 × 12–15 | Slow eccentric; width priority |

**Total time:** ~60 min

---

## Lower A — Squat-Dominant (Tuesday)

**Focus:** Quad strength + anterior chain

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Barbell Back Squat | 4 × 3–5 | Main strength lift; full depth; add 5 lb/session |
| Romanian Deadlift | 3 × 6–8 | Hip hinge pattern; hamstring priority |
| Walking Lunges (DB or BW) | 3 × 10/leg | Glute activation; good for Joe's glute weakness |
| Leg Press | 3 × 8–10 | Supplementary quad volume |
| Lying Leg Curl (machine) | 3 × 10–12 | Hamstring isolation |
| Calf Raises | 4 × 12–15 | High rep; squeeze at top |

**Total time:** ~60 min

---

## Upper B — Vertical Push/Pull + Arms (Thursday)

**Focus:** OHP strength + lat strength + arms

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Barbell Overhead Press (OHP) | 4 × 3–5 | Standing; most important shoulder exercise |
| Weighted Pull-ups or Lat Pulldown | 4 × 4–6 | Neutral-grip preferred; full hang at bottom |
| Dumbbell Arnold Press | 3 × 8–10 | Rotational component hits all delt heads |
| Chest-Supported Row | 3 × 8–10 | Eliminates lower back fatigue; pure upper back |
| Bayesian Cable Curl (incline or cable) | 3 × 10–12 | Stretch-focused; S-tier bicep exercise |
| Overhead Cable Tricep Extension | 3 × 10–12 | Long head stretch = most hypertrophy signal |
| Face Pulls | 2 × 20 | Every upper session, no exception |

**Total time:** ~60 min

---

## Lower B — Hip-Dominant / Posterior Chain (Friday)

**Most important session of the week for Joe (cyclist background = weak posterior chain)**

| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Conventional Deadlift | 4 × 3–5 | Main strength lift; add 5 lb/session; brace hard |
| Hip Thrust (barbell or machine) | 4 × 6–8 | Best glute exercise; full extension at top |
| Bulgarian Split Squat | 3 × 8/leg | Unilateral posterior chain; brutal but effective |
| Nordic Hamstring Curl | 3 × 5–8 | ACL prevention; hardest hamstring exercise |
| Back Extension (machine or 45°) | 3 × 12–15 | Lower back + glute tie-in |
| Adductor Machine | 3 × 15 | Groin stability; often neglected |

**Total time:** ~60 min

---

## Zone 2 Cycling Sessions (Wednesday + Saturday)

Per [[Cardio Complete Masterclass]]: Zone 2 = heart rate 130–150 bpm (conversational pace), minimum 45 min to trigger mitochondrial biogenesis. Cycling preferred over running for interference effect during a strength phase.

**Protocol:**
- Warm up 5 min easy
- 40–50 min at Zone 2 (can hold a conversation, slightly breathless)
- Cool down 5 min easy
- Podcast/music: Hardcore History or Huberman Lab Essentials

---

## Progressive Overload Rules

**Add weight when:** You hit the top rep target on ALL sets with good form for 2 consecutive weeks.

| Lift | Weight jump |
|---|---|
| Squat / Deadlift / Hip Thrust | +5 lb (or 2.5 kg plate each side) |
| Bench Press / OHP | +2.5 lb |
| Row / Pull-up | +2.5 lb (or 1 extra rep per set) |
| Accessory work | +5 lb or +1 rep — more flexible |

**Never add weight if:** Form broke down, you didn't sleep well, or you're at the top of a rep range on only 1 out of 4 sets.

---

## Nutrition (Maintenance Calories)

Per [[Joe's Custom Seasonal Training Plan]]: Summer = maintenance (not bulk, not cut).

**Target:**
- Calories: Estimated TDEE (track with Cronometer for 2 weeks to find your number)
- Protein: 1.8–2.2 g/kg bodyweight/day. Non-negotiable during strength phase.
- Creatine: 5 g/day post-workout. Most evidence-backed supplement for strength.
- Pre-workout: Optional; if used, just black coffee or caffeine — skip the blends.

**Meal timing for strength:**
- Pre-training: Light meal with protein + carbs, 60–90 min before
- Post-training: 30–50 g protein meal within 2 hrs (muscle protein synthesis window)

---

## Progress Tracking

Every 2 weeks, log:
- 1RM estimates for Bench, Squat, Deadlift, OHP (calculated from working sets)
- Body weight (morning, after bathroom, before eating) — should stay flat ±2 lbs
- Note any form cues or issues

After 12 weeks (end of summer): take stock. For Fall 2026, transition to hypertrophy (8–12 rep range) per [[Seasonal Fitness Periodization]].

## Joe-Specific Priority Notes

From [[Muscle Group Priority Guide]]:
> "Joe's priority stack (cyclist background — strong quads, weak posterior chain and upper body): Lats + upper back → Lateral + rear delts → Hamstrings + glutes → Chest → Quads (maintenance)."

**Practical translation:**
- Lower B (deadlift + hip thrust) is the most important day — do not skip
- Add 1 extra rear delt/face pull set to every upper session
- OHP takes priority over bench if you can only do one
- Quad work is maintenance only — your legs are already strong from cycling
