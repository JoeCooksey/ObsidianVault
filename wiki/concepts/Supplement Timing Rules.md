# Supplement Timing Rules

Practical rules for *when* to take supplements so they actually absorb. Timing matters most for fat-soluble compounds, amino acids, and minerals.

## Take WITH a fat-containing meal (fat-soluble)

These need dietary fat to absorb — taking them fasted wastes them and can cause GI upset.

- **Vitamins A, D, E, K** — up to ~32% better vitamin D absorption with fat (Source: body-science / cooper reviews 2025)
- **Vitamin K2 (MK-7)** — pair with D3
- **Omega-3 fish oil** — with meal; reduces fishy reflux too
- **Turmeric / curcumin** — fat-soluble; add **black pepper (piperine)** for up to ~20× absorption
- **Astaxanthin** — fat-soluble carotenoid
- **Bacopa Monnieri** — fat-soluble herb; fasted dosing causes nausea/cramping and poor uptake (Source: PMC12158153)
- **CoQ10**, **vitamin E**

## Best on an EMPTY stomach (amino acids, some herbs)

Free-form amino acids compete with dietary protein for the same intestinal transporters — protein in the same window cuts uptake ~30–40%.

- **L-tyrosine** — fasted or with black coffee; not with a protein meal/shake (Source: Life Extension)
- **L-theanine** — neutral amino-acid transporter; protein reduces uptake (Source: PMC5546063). Note: pairing with caffeine is fine and intended.
- **Rhodiola rosea** — commonly taken fasted in the morning for faster onset
- **Most single amino acids** (glycine, taurine, NAC) absorb fine fasted

## Timing-flexible (take whenever consistent)

- **Creatine** — chronic saturation matters, not acute timing; marginally better with carbs (insulin) but fasted is fine (Source: Huberman Lab)
- **TMG / betaine**, **boron**, **vitamin C** (water-soluble), **electrolytes**

## Take at NIGHT (sleep-supporting)

- **Magnesium glycinate**, **glycine**, **apigenin**, **L-theanine** — four independent sleep mechanisms; synergistic, no tolerance risk (Source: sleep-stack reviews 2025)
- **Low-dose melatonin** (0.1–0.5 mg, not 5–10 mg)

## Avoid in the EVENING (stimulating / sleep-disrupting)

- **Alpha-GPC** — daytime/pre-study only; cholinergic activity can disrupt sleep architecture (Source: Pierre Health)
- **Caffeine** — respect your sleep cutoff (see [[Caffeine Timing Protocol]])
- **Rhodiola, L-tyrosine** — morning/early afternoon

## Separate these (competition)

- **Minerals compete:** don't co-dose **zinc + magnesium + calcium + iron** in the same pill/meal — space them out
- **Amino acids vs. protein meals** — see empty-stomach rule above

> [!key-insight] The two rules that matter most
> 1. Fat-soluble → always with a fatty meal.
> 2. Free amino acids → away from protein.
> Get those two right and the rest is fine-tuning.
