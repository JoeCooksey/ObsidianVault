# Master Chunky Vegetable Soup Recipe

11 vegetables. One pot. 35 minutes. Covers most major plant families in a single meal.

---

## Ingredient List (Serves 6–8)

| Ingredient | Amount | Costco Source |
|------------|--------|---------------|
| Onion | 1 large, diced | Bulk bag |
| Garlic | 4–5 cloves, minced | Bulk heads or pre-peeled jar |
| Celery | 3 stalks, sliced | Bundle |

## Equipment

- Large pot or Dutch oven (6 qt minimum — this is a big soup)
- Cutting board + knife
- Wooden spoon
- Can opener
- Ladle

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Prep all vegetables before you start (10 min)
Cut everything before turning on the heat. Once you start cooking, the timing moves fast.

- **Onion**: dice into ½ inch pieces
- **Celery**: slice into ½ inch pieces
- **Garlic**: mince or press
- **Carrot**: slice into ¼ inch rounds
- **Sweet potato**: peel and cube into ½ inch pieces (uniform size = even cooking)
- **Brussels sprouts**: slice off stem end, halve through the core
- **Broccoli**: cut into small florets (1–1.5 inch) — smaller than you think; they shrink
- **Bell pepper**: remove seeds, dice into ½ inch pieces
- **Mushrooms**: slice ¼ inch thick
- **Chickpeas**: drain and rinse in a colander
- **Spinach**: leave whole or rough chop if leaves are large

### Step 2 — Start aromatics (3 min)
Heat 2 tbsp EVOO in pot over medium heat.
Add onion, celery. Cook 3 min, stirring occasionally — just until slightly softened.
Add garlic. Cook 1 min until fragrant.

> This step is optional but improves depth. If you want zero effort: skip it and add everything to cold broth in Step 3.

### Step 3 — Add hard vegetables + broth (15 min)
Add carrot, sweet potato, and brussels sprouts to the pot.
Pour in 6–8 cups chicken stock.
Add thyme, bay leaves, salt, pepper, and red pepper flakes if using.
Bring to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a steady simmer.
Cook **15 minutes** — test a sweet potato cube with a fork; it should meet slight resistance (not fully soft yet).

### Step 4 — Add medium vegetables + chickpeas (8 min)
Add broccoli, bell pepper, mushrooms, and chickpeas.
Return to simmer. Cook **8 minutes**.
Broccoli should be bright green and just tender. Mushrooms will have softened. Chickpeas are already cooked — they just need to heat through and absorb flavor.

### Step 5 — Add spinach (2 min)
Turn off heat (or reduce to lowest setting).
Add spinach. Stir it under the hot broth.
It wilts completely in **1–2 minutes**. Do not overcook — spinach goes grey and loses nutrients fast.

### Step 6 — Final seasoning
Remove bay leaves.
Taste the broth. Add salt if needed.
Stir in 1 tsp Kirkland No-Salt Seasoning as a finishing layer.
Optional: squeeze of lemon juice brightens the whole pot.

## Thickness Control

| Broth Amount | Result |
|-------------|--------|
| 6 cups | Thick, stew-like — vegetables packed together |
| 7 cups | Standard soup — good broth-to-vegetable ratio |
| 8 cups | Brothier — more liquid per bowl |

Start with 6 cups. Add more after blending if it's too thick. You can always add broth; you can't remove it.

## Storage & Meal Prep

**Fridge**: lasts 4–5 days in a sealed container. Tastes better on day 2 — flavors meld overnight.

**Freezer**: freeze in quart containers for up to 3 months. Leave 1 inch headspace. Thaw overnight in fridge or reheat from frozen on low with a splash of water.

**Meal prep move**: make a full batch Sunday → 4–5 lunches for the week, zero daily cooking.

## Spice Variations
The base recipe uses Classic seasoning (thyme + bay + salt + pepper). Swap for any of these without changing anything else:

| Profile | What to Add | What It Tastes Like |
|---------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Curry** | 2 tsp curry powder + 1 tsp turmeric + 1 tsp cumin | Warm, aromatic, slightly spicy |
| **Italian** | 1 tsp Italian seasoning + ½ tsp oregano + basil | Herbal, familiar |
| **Smoky** | 1.5 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp cumin | Robust, Spanish-style |
| **Ginger** | 1-inch fresh ginger added in Step 3 + lemon at end | Bright, warming |

## Nutrition Notes
This soup covers the following plant families in one bowl — important for gut microbiome diversity:
- **Alliums**: onion, garlic
- **Brassicas**: broccoli, brussels sprouts
- **Root vegetables**: carrot, sweet potato
- **Fungi**: mushroom
- **Legumes**: chickpeas
- **Leafy greens**: spinach
- **Nightshades**: bell pepper
- **Apiaceae (umbellifers)**: celery

That's 8 distinct plant families. The 30-plant-foods-per-week gut diversity target counts every distinct plant species — this soup alone gets you to ~11 (including onion, garlic, and any spices as separate counts).
