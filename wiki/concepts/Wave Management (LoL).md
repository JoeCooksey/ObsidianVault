# Wave Management (LoL)

The most controllable lever of macro in solo queue: minion wave state determines where you can safely be on the map and when. Wave state controls **tempo** — every roam, recall, dive, and objective setup is downstream of what the wave is doing. (Source: [[Boosteria — LoL Macro Play Guide]])

## The Four Wave States

| State | What it is | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| **Freeze** | Hold the wave just outside your tower range so the enemy must overextend to farm | Deny CS/XP, set up ganks, punish weak laners |
| **Slow push** | Kill enemy casters early, then only last-hit — wave grows into a 2–3 wave "megawave" | Build a timing window: roam, dive with the wave, set up an objective |
| **Fast push / Crash** | Clear fast and slam the wave into the enemy tower to reset it | Safe recall, rotation to objectives, plate damage before the 14:00 plating expiry |
| **Bounce** | After a crash, the wave pushes back toward you | Free safety window and freeze setup |

(Sources: [[Boosteria — LoL Macro Play Guide]], [[Boosteria — LoL CSing Fundamentals]])

## Decision Rule
Choose the state from lane strength + map state: ahead and enemy jungler shown elsewhere → push; weaker or expecting ganks → freeze; objective spawning soon → slow push timed to crash as it spawns (the 60-second arm of the [[LoL Macro Play]] 60-30-10 rule).

## Practice
Freeze-hold drill: maintain a freeze for 2 full waves in Practice Tool without losing XP. Under-tower drill: catch a full wave under tower 10× in a row. (Source: [[Boosteria — LoL CSing Fundamentals]])
