# Let Them Judge You

[[Mel Robbins|Robbins']] application of [[Let Them]] to the prison of other people's opinions. You cannot control what people think of you, and trying to is exhausting and futile — so **let them** judge, doubt, or misunderstand you.

The key insight: other people's opinions are formed by *their* experiences, fears, and limitations, not by the truth about you. Energy spent managing your image is energy stolen from actually living. The **Let Me** complement: let me focus on who I want to be and how I want to act, rather than on policing a perception I can never fully control.

*Source: [[The Let Them Theory - Mel Robbins]].*
