# The End of Homo Sapiens

[[Yuval Noah Harari|Harari's]] closing argument: the [[Scientific Revolution]] may make Sapiens **the last "natural" humans**, because we are gaining the power to redesign life itself — through biological engineering, cyborg engineering (merging organic bodies with machines), and the engineering of inorganic life (AI).

For ~4 billion years life evolved by natural selection; we are crossing into an era of *intelligent design* — by us. This could end Homo sapiens not through extinction but through transformation into something else entirely. Harari pairs the prospect with a warning that frames the whole book: we are acquiring godlike powers of creation and destruction while remaining confused about what we actually want — see [[Are We Happier?]].

*Source: [[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari]] — Part 4.*
