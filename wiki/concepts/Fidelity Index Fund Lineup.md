# Fidelity Index Fund Lineup

> Fidelity is the only major broker with genuinely **0.00% expense ratio** index *mutual funds* (the ZERO line). For a Fidelity Roth IRA / brokerage, this is the whole menu you need.

## The Funds That Matter (confidence: high, primary source)

| Job | ZERO (0.00%) | Indexed (0.015–0.06%) | Tracks |
|-----|--------------|------------------------|--------|
| **Total US market** | FZROX | FSKAX (0.015%) | ~3,800–4,000 US stocks |
| **S&P 500 / large cap** | FNILX | FXAIX (0.015%) | 500 large-caps |
| **International** | FZILX | FTIHX (0.060%) / FSPSX (0.035%) | non-US developed + EM |
| **US bonds** | — | FXNAX (0.025%) | total US bond market |
| **Extended (mid/small)** | FZIPX | FSMAX (0.035%) | US ex-S&P 500 |

FXAIX vs FSKAX is a near-tie — same 0.015% ER, near-identical returns; FSKAX adds the mid/small-cap slice (Source: [[White Coat Investor — FSKAX vs FXAIX]]).

## The ZERO Funds Catch (the one real trade-off)

FZROX / FNILX / FZILX / FZIPX are **0.00% but not portable**:
- They exist only at Fidelity and use Fidelity's proprietary indexes.
- You **cannot transfer them in-kind** to another broker — leaving Fidelity means *selling*, which is a taxable event in a regular brokerage account (no issue inside a Roth IRA).
- Trade-off vs FSKAX/FXAIX (0.015%) is ~$1.50/yr per $10k. Decide on **portability, not the pennies** (Source: [[Fidelity Index Funds Lineup (Fidelity.com)]]).

> [!tip] Practical rule
> **Inside a Fidelity Roth IRA:** ZERO funds are perfect — free, and the no-portability problem never bites (you won't sell-to-transfer a retirement account casually). **In a taxable account you might move someday:** prefer FSKAX/FXAIX or the portable ETFs (VTI/VOO).

## A Simple Fidelity Portfolio (young, aggressive)

- **One-fund:** 100% FZROX (or FXAIX). Done.
- **Two-fund:** ~80% FZROX + ~20% FZILX (US + international).
- **Three-fund:** add a small FXNAX bond sleeve later as the horizon shortens — see [[Asset Allocation by Age]].
