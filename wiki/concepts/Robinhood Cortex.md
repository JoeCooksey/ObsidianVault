# Robinhood Cortex

Robinhood's **in-house** AI assistant (launched **Mar 2025**, for Gold subscribers). It is the "brain" behind Robinhood's AI features — distinct from [[Agentic Trading (Robinhood)]], which connects a user's *external* agent.

## Features
- **Stock Digests** — short AI summary answering "why is this ticker up/down today?"
- **Portfolio Digests** — personalized plain-language insights on the user's holdings.
- **Trade Builder** — translates a thesis into a specific (often options) strategy.
- **AI Chat** — chat through ideas and place orders; has real-time market data + analyst research (its pitch vs generic ChatGPT).

## Cortex vs Agentic Trading
| | Cortex | Agentic Trading |
|---|---|---|
| Whose AI | Robinhood's own | Your external agent (Claude/ChatGPT/etc.) |
| Connection | Built into app | Robinhood Trading MCP |
| Role | Insights + assisted orders | Autonomous execution in a dedicated account |

> [!note] Cortex is analysis/assist; Agentic Trading is delegation of execution. Same caveat applies to both: AI summaries can be wrong or stale, and convenience ≠ edge ([[Efficient Market Hypothesis]]).
