# Empathic Listening

Covey's Habit 5 skill: listening with ears, eyes, and heart to genuinely understand another person's frame of reference — not merely waiting to reply — by reflecting back both content and feeling before moving to problem-solving; the antidote to autobiographical listening (filtering everything through your own experience, then evaluating, probing, advising, or interpreting).
