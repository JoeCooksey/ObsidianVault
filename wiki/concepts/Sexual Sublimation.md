# Sexual Sublimation

Channeling sexual energy/libido into a non-sexual outlet — exercise, creative work, intellectual or social pursuits — instead of (or alongside) physical release.

## Origin

The psychoanalytic concept traces to **Freud**, who held that libido could be redirected into creativity, intellectual work, and social activity. It also appears in older contemplative/eastern traditions (yogic, monastic) as "transmutation." (Source: search synthesis)

## Evidence status

> [!warning] Weak scientific evidence
> Most material on sublimation/"transmutation" is **philosophical, anecdotal, or spiritual** (Freudian theory, NoFap communities, tantra) rather than controlled research. The *general* mechanism it leans on is sound — **redirecting attention and energy into a competing absorbing activity reduces dwelling on the urge** — which overlaps with mainstream "healthy distraction" and behavioral-replacement findings. Treat strong claims about stored "power" as `confidence: low`.

## Practical form (the defensible version)

When aroused at an inconvenient time, **start a demanding, absorbing activity**: hard exercise, a focused work/creative block, a cold shower, or social contact. This is essentially [[Habit Breaking via Replacement Behavior]] applied to arousal, and pairs naturally with [[Urge Surfing]].
