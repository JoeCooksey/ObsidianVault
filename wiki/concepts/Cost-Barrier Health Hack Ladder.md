# Cost-Barrier Health Hack Ladder

A framework for ranking health interventions by **barrier to entry** rather than by impact. Useful because it makes the central truth of healthspan visible: cost and benefit are largely **inverted**.

## The barrier axis

Barrier combines four components, not just price:

1. **Money** — one-time or recurring cost.
2. **Effort / access** — time, consistency, equipment, physical access.
3. **Knowledge** — how much you must understand to do it safely/correctly.
4. **Medical gatekeeping / risk** — does it need a prescriber, and how unproven/risky is it.

## The ten tiers

| Tier | Levels | Cost band | Defining barrier |
|------|--------|-----------|------------------|
| I | 1–10 | $0 | Just doing it |
| II | 11–20 | <$50 once | Minor purchase/habit |
| III | 21–30 | $10–60/mo | Buying + remembering |
| IV | 31–40 | $30–150/mo | Time + technique |
| V | 41–50 | $200–1,500/yr | Money + acting on data |
| VI | 51–60 | $2k–15k once | Capital + space |
| VII | 61–70 | $500–5k/yr | Needs a prescriber |
| VIII | 71–80 | $8k–50k/yr | Wealth + clinic access |
| IX | 81–90 | $5k–100k/yr | Experimental + risk |
| X | 91–100 | $100k–$1M+/yr | Ultra-wealth + unproven |

## Core principle

> [!important] ~90% of measurable healthspan gains live in Tiers I–V (under ~$2,000/year). Higher tiers buy **data and marginal optimization**, not foundational health. Climb only after the cheap tiers are habitual. (Source: [[Bryan Johnson Immortals DIY — Empirical Health]])

## How to use it

- Audit yourself bottom-up: never pay for Tier VI+ while Tiers I–III are inconsistent.
- The **$2k/yr sweet spot** = lifestyle (free) + core supplements + a wearable + one good annual blood panel.
- Treat everything Tier VIII and above as **low confidence** until human trials mature.
