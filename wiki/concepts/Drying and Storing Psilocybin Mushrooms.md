# Drying and Storing Psilocybin Mushrooms

Fresh mushrooms are ~90% water and spoil in days. Drying to **"cracker dry"** (~5% moisture or less — brittle enough to **snap cleanly like a cracker**) is what makes them shelf-stable and preserves the active compounds **psilocybin** and **psilocin** (Source: [[Healing Maps — Cracker Dry: Drying and Storing Mushrooms]]).

## The four degradation pathways

Potency is destroyed by **moisture, heat, oxygen, and light**. Every storage decision is about minimizing all four (Source: [[Healing Maps — Cracker Dry: Drying and Storing Mushrooms]]).

## Drying method (two stages)

1. **Fan-dry first.** Lay mushrooms on a rack in front of a fan in low humidity until leathery/surface-dry. Keep temperature **below 95–115°F (35–45°C)** — excessive heat degrades psilocybin. Air-drying or a dehydrator on low both work.
2. **Finish with desiccant.** Move them into an airtight container with **food-safe silica gel** (or anhydrous calcium chloride), not touching the desiccant directly, until they **snap when bent**. The desiccant pulls out the last residual water that fan-drying can't.

(Sources: [[Healing Maps — Cracker Dry: Drying and Storing Mushrooms]], easy-cultivation desiccant guide)

> [!warning] Do not oven-dry at high heat or microwave — heat is one of the four degradation pathways and will cut potency.

## Storage (best → worst)

| Method | Shelf life / potency |
|---|---|
| **Cracker-dry + vacuum-sealed + frozen + dark** (gold standard) | 80–95% potency retained for **1–2 years** |
| Airtight jar + silica + cool dark pantry | ~6 months |
| Loose in a jar at room temp | Degrades fastest |

(Source: [[Healing Maps — Cracker Dry: Drying and Storing Mushrooms]])

## Storage rules

- **Keep them whole**, not ground. Grinding multiplies surface area → far more oxygen exposure → faster degradation.
- **Airtight + dark + cold.** Vacuum-seal (removes oxygen), freeze (halts reactions), keep out of light.
- Add a fresh silica packet to the storage container to catch any residual moisture.

(Source: [[Healing Maps — Cracker Dry: Drying and Storing Mushrooms]])
