# Pre-Bed Light Management

Bright light in the evening — especially overhead and blue-rich light — suppresses melatonin and signals "daytime" to the [[Circadian Rhythm Alignment|circadian clock]], delaying sleep. Managing light exposure in the hours before bed is one of the cheapest, highest-leverage sleep interventions.

## Evening (wind-down)

- Avoid bright **overhead "hospital lights."** Prefer **lamps** for a dimmer, warmer evening environment.
- Wear **red-light (blue-blocking) glasses** if available.
- Put phones, tablets, and laptops on **night mode / warm color mode**.
- Avoid big bright light sources (camera lights, tablet screens) close to bedtime.

## Morning (wake-up)

- **Sunlight is best** for setting the clock; if sun isn't available, red light helps somewhat.
- Pairs with the consistent wake time of [[Circadian Rhythm Alignment]].

> [!note] Where it fits
> Combine with a dark sleeping environment ([[Sleep Environment Optimization]]) so light is high in the morning, low in the evening, and near-zero during sleep. Part of the [[Sleepmaxxing Protocol]].
