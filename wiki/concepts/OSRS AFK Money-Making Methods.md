# OSRS AFK Money-Making Methods

AFK methods earn GP and XP with minimal clicks, ideal for multitasking or [[OSRS Second-Screen Grinding Setup|second-screen play]]. The defining trait is **time-between-clicks**: the longer the idle window, the more attention you can spend elsewhere. GP/hour figures fluctuate with the Grand Exchange — always price-check before starting.

## Passive / highest-AFK
- **Kingdom of Miscellania** — ~50k–100k GP/day for one daily click-in, after *Throne of Miscellania* + *Royal Trouble*. The most hands-off income in the game. *(confidence: medium — single guide source)*
- **Herb & birdhouse runs** — a few minutes every ~80 min / per herb cycle; among the best GP-per-effort in the game and trains [[OSRS Mid-Game Progression Goals|Farming/Hunter]]. *(Source: [[Theoatrix — 20 Mid-Game Goals OSRS]])*

## F2P AFK options
- Iron ore mining (~40k–60k, 15 Mining), trout fishing (~30k–50k, 20 Fishing), gold amulet crafting (~50k–80k, 8 Crafting), Ogress Shamans (~50k–80k, 40 combat + Corsair Curse). *(Source: [[OSRS Money Making Guide — Best AFK Methods 2025]])*

> [!note] GP/hr ranges are guide estimates and drift with the live economy; treat exact numbers as medium-confidence.
