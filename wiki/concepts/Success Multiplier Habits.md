# Success Multiplier Habits

Habits 96–101 from [[101 Habits — Dan Zakaria]]. The compounding layer — invest, network, teach, and the closing 1%-better thesis.

## The Habits
96. **Invest in yourself** — courses, coaches, mentorships; "you aren't where you want to be because you don't yet have the knowledge/skill set of the version of you who does."
97. **Save a percentage of your income** — allocate and save/invest every month; build wealth over time.
98. **Network intentionally** — go to events/communities; "your network is your net worth."
99. **Review your goals monthly** — reconnect with your *why*; journal goals, gratitude, plans, desires-as-if-achieved.
100. **Teach what you learn** — "the best way to learn is to teach"; learn specialized knowledge, then teach it. ("Mastery requires 10,000 hours of deep focus.")
101. **Improve by 1% every day** — the [[Atomic Habits - James Clear]] close: reduce friction to good habits, add friction to bad ones; 1% daily ≈ **37× in a year**. "Consistently good beats occasionally great."

## The Closing Thesis
> [!key-insight] "You don't have a discipline problem, you have an identity problem."
> Discipline becomes easy when you (1) use **systems** to lower friction and (2) align your **identity/self-image** with the person who already has the habits you want. Habits create evidence for that identity, and identity drives future habits — the loop of [[Identity-Based vs Goal-Based Habits]].
