# Gut-Harmful Foods and Actions Tier List

What damages the gut microbiome and barrier, ranked **worst-first** by evidence × harm. This is the destructive counterpart to [[Gut Optimization Actions Tier List]]. "F-tier" = most damaging.

## F-Tier — Most Damaging (avoid / minimize hard)

| Harm | Mechanism | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Dietary emulsifiers** — carboxymethylcellulose (CMC/E466), polysorbate 80 (P80/E433), carrageenan | Non-reversibly alter human microbiota, thin the protective mucus layer, raise permeability & LPS translocation → inflammation | [[Human Gut Emulsifiers Study 2021]]; FRESH RCT 2022 (high) |
| **Chronic heavy alcohol** | Modulates tight-junction proteins, disrupts flora, drives endotoxemia & "gut leakiness" | Multiple human/animal (high) |
| **Chronic / frequent NSAID use** (ibuprofen, naproxen, aspirin) | COX inhibition damages gut epithelium — an effect common to the *entire* drug class; causes NSAID enteropathy | [[Leaky Gut and Intestinal Permeability]] (high) |
| **Unnecessary antibiotics** | Collapse microbial diversity; recovery takes months, sometimes incomplete | High |

## D-Tier — Strongly Detrimental

| Harm | Mechanism | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Ultra-processed foods (UPFs)** | Bundle of emulsifiers, additives, low fiber, high refined sugar/fat → dysbiosis | High (associational + mechanistic) |
| **Artificial sweeteners** (sucralose, saccharin; aspartame less clear) | Reduce butyrate-producers, induce glucose intolerance & low-grade inflammation in some people | [[Artificial Sweeteners Gut Review]] (medium) |
| **Added/refined sugar excess** | Feeds pro-inflammatory taxa, crowds out fiber fermenters | Medium-high |
| **Chronic psychological stress** | Worsens barrier injury (esp. with NSAIDs) via glucocorticoid-receptor signaling | Mechanistic + animal (medium) |

## C-Tier — Moderately Harmful / Context-Dependent

| Harm | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Low-fiber diet** | Starves SCFA producers; microbes literally eat the mucus layer instead — a harm of *omission* |
| **Circadian disruption / poor sleep** | Raises permeability via occludin; shift-work & jet-lag models show endotoxemia |
| **Sedentary lifestyle** | Lower diversity and fewer butyrate producers vs. active people |
| **Carrageenan (thickener)** | Increases permeability & inflammation in models; classed with emulsifiers but somewhat weaker human data |

## B-Tier — Minor / Overstated

| Harm | Reality check |
|---|---|
| **Gluten (in non-coeliacs)** | Triggers transient zonulin release, but barrier impact in healthy people is modest & debated — not the universal villain it's marketed as |
| **Lectins / "antinutrients"** | Largely deactivated by cooking; harm overstated for most people — see [[Antinutrients-Plant-Defense-Compounds]] |
| **Dairy (non-intolerant)** | Fermented dairy is *protective*; only relevant if genuinely intolerant |

## Practical Worst-Offender Watchlist
Read labels for: **carboxymethylcellulose, polysorbate 80, carrageenan, sucralose, mono- and diglycerides**. These cluster in ice cream, packaged baked goods, protein bars, sauces, and "diet" products.

> [!note] Harm is dose- and context-dependent
> An occasional emulsifier-containing food is not a catastrophe. The damage comes from *chronic, daily* exposure displacing fiber and fermented foods. Fix the base diet first.
