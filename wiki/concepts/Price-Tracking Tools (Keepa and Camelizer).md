# Price-Tracking Tools (Keepa and Camelizer)

Browser extensions / sites that chart an item's **historical price** so you can tell whether a sale-day "deal" is a genuine low or an inflated-then-discounted markup. Essential during convergence weeks like [[Retail Deal-Event Convergence (2026)]] when every price is labeled a deal.

## Tools
- **Keepa** — Amazon price-history charts (extension + site); the most-cited tool for verifying Amazon lows.
- **The Camelizer** (CamelCamelCamel) — Amazon price history + price-drop alerts.
- **Amazon's built-in price history** — visible on product pages via the "Alexa for shopping" window (Source: search — NerdWallet/NBC).

## Rule of thumb
Before checkout: (1) confirm the price is at/near its historical low, (2) check the same item at a competing retailer that week, (3) compare shipping fees and cashback. A discount off an inflated "list price" is not a deal.
