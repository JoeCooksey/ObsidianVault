# Anna Lembke

**Anna Lembke, MD** is an American psychiatrist, Professor and Chief of the Stanford Addiction Medicine Dual Diagnosis Clinic at Stanford University School of Medicine. She is the leading popularizer of the **pleasure–pain balance** model of dopamine and addiction.

## Significance

- Author of *[[Anna Lembke — Dopamine Nation|Dopamine Nation: Finding Balance in the Age of Indulgence]]* (2021), a bestseller that brought the **dopamine deficit state** and **pleasure–pain homeostasis** concepts to a wide audience → [[Dopamine Baseline and the Pleasure-Pain Balance]].
- Earlier work *Drug Dealer, MD* examined the role of prescribers in the opioid epidemic.
- Frames modern compulsive overconsumption (phones, porn, food, drugs) as a single mechanism: high-potency, high-frequency dopamine overwhelming the brain's homeostatic balance.

## Key Contributions to the Dopamine Masterclass

- The **seesaw metaphor** of pleasure and pain, and the "gremlins on the pain side" comedown.
- **Self-binding** and ~**30-day abstinence** as clinical tools for recovery → [[Dopamine Detox (Evidence Review)]].
- "Pressing on the pain side" (voluntary discomfort) to gain durable, crash-free well-being.
