# Robert J. Meyers

Psychologist (PhD) who **developed CRAFT** — Community Reinforcement and Family Training — the evidence-based, non-confrontational method that teaches families to help a treatment-resistant drinking/using loved one through positive reinforcement and natural consequences rather than ultimatums.

Co-author of the popular CRAFT handbook *Get Your Loved One Sober: Alternatives to Nagging, Pleading, and Threatening* (with Brenda Wolfe). His clinical trials established CRAFT's roughly **2× advantage** over confrontation- and detachment-based approaches at engaging resistant drinkers in treatment.
