# WOD Nation

Value-focused fitness brand whose speed rope is the recurring **best-value** pick in 2026 roundups.

## Key models
- **Adjustable Speed Jump Rope (~$15)** — *best budget speed rope.* 10' adjustable, ~6.5 oz, nylon-coated steel, patented four-ball-bearing system for double-unders; includes a backup cable. A trainer who tested 12+ speed ropes called it a favorite. (Sources: [[Yahoo Health - Best Jump Ropes 2026 (Trainer-Tested)]], [[BarBend - Best CrossFit Jump Ropes (2026)]])
- **Atlas Weighted ($27.99)** — *best mid-range weighted / best grip.* Contoured rubber handles, removable ½-lb handle weights, no-cut cable adjustment, two cables for progression. Heavier, indoor-only. (Source: [[BarBend - Best Jump Ropes for Beginners (2026)]])

> [!note] Price discrepancy: roundups list WOD Nation speed ropes at both ~$15 (entry adjustable model) and ~$32.67 (a different SKU/bundle). Target the ~$15 adjustable model for best value.

## Take
The best answer to "cheap but good" — WOD Nation covers both the budget speed and mid-range weighted slots.
