# Sandi Mann

Senior psychology lecturer, University of Central Lancashire (UK). Author of *The Upside of Downtime: Why Boredom Is Good* (also published as *The Science of Boredom*). The main popularizer of the boredom-breeds-creativity position.

- Co-author of [[Mann and Cadman 2014 - Boredom and Creativity]] — the phone-book studies; passive boring tasks improved subsequent creative idea generation (finding later contested)
- Defines boredom as "a search for neural stimulation that isn't satisfied"
- Practical protocol: to harness boredom, do something requiring little or no concentration (walk a familiar route, swim laps, sit with eyes closed) with **no phone, no music**, and let the mind wander
- On phones: our attachment to them is "destroying our ability to be bored, yet preventing us from ever being truly entertained" — if you want mind-wandering, "switching off your phone is a must"
