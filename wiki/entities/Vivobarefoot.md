# Vivobarefoot

UK-founded premium barefoot shoe brand with a "barefoot-first" design philosophy and a cult following. Generally regarded as the benchmark premium brand for ground feel. (Source: [[Research - Barefoot Shoes Benefits and Best Brands]])

- **Flagship lifestyle/gym:** Primus Lite IV — ultra-thin ~3 mm sole, anatomically wide toe box, breathable knit upper; works for casual wear, light trails, gym (~$150).
- **Best boot:** Tracker II FG — waterproof, strong trail traction, durable (~$240).
- **Other lines:** Gobi (casual leather), Motus Strength (training), Primus trail.
- **Positioning:** premium price, very minimal stack; excellent for experienced barefoot users wanting maximum ground feel.
- **Trade-offs (per reviews):** higher price; some models have limited breathability and can be slippery on wet surfaces.

Frequently compared head-to-head with [[Xero Shoes]] (Vivobarefoot = premium/ground-feel, Xero = value).
