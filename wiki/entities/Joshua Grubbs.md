# Joshua Grubbs

Clinical psychologist and addiction researcher, among the most influential contemporary scientists studying pornography use and self-perceived "porn addiction."

## Significance
- Developed and popularized the **moral incongruence** framework and the **Pornography Problems due to Moral Incongruence (PPMI)** model → [[Moral Incongruence (Pornography)]].
- Co-author (with Shane W. Kraus) of the widely cited review [[Pornography Use and Psychological Science — Grubbs Kraus 2021]].
- His work shifted the academic conversation away from a simple "porn addiction" model toward distinguishing **genuine behavioral dysregulation** from **values-conflict distress**.

## Core contributions
- Showed that **religiosity and moral disapproval**, not frequency of use, are the strongest predictors of *self-reported* porn addiction.
- Demonstrated these effects in cross-sectional, nationally representative, and longitudinal U.S. samples.
- Argued for psychological nuance: pornography's effects range from positive to neutral to negative depending on context.

His research underpins the ICD-11's decision to exclude purely moral-judgment-based distress from the [[Compulsive Sexual Behavior Disorder (CSBD)]] diagnosis.
