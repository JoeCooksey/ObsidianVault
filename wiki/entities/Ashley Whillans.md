# Ashley Whillans

Behavioral scientist; Volpert Family Associate Professor at **Harvard Business School**. Her research studies how **time, money, and subjective wellbeing** relate — in particular how people mis-spend time and how to "buy" happier time.

## Why she matters here

She is the authority behind the **[[Time Confetti]]** framing and the practical claim that **time affluence is trainable**. Author of *Time Smart: How to Reclaim Your Time and Live a Happier Life* (2020).

## Signature findings

- People who **use money to buy time** (outsourcing dreaded tasks, shortening commutes) report higher life satisfaction — yet most people, even the wealthy, fail to do it. (Source: HBR / *PNAS* "Buying Time Promotes Happiness," 2017)
- **Fragmented leisure** ("time confetti") makes people feel more time-poor than they are. (Source: [[Whillans - Time Confetti and the Broken Promise of Leisure]])
- "Time poverty" is a distinct, measurable construct, not just busyness.
