# Vibram FiveFingers

The original modern minimalist shoe, introduced in 2005 by Vibram (the high-performance rubber-sole maker). Distinctive **individual toe pockets** wrap each toe separately for a maximally natural, tactile feel. (Source: [[Research - Barefoot Shoes Benefits and Best Brands]])

- **Positioning:** ultra-minimalist with maximum ground feel; best suited to **experienced** barefoot users, not beginners.
- **Use cases:** running, gym/CrossFit, water/trail; the toe-glove form is polarizing in looks but unmatched for proprioception.
- **Note:** Vibram is also the dominant *outsole supplier* to many other footwear brands; "FiveFingers" specifically is its own barefoot shoe line.

For an easier entry point, see [[Lems Shoes]] or [[Xero Shoes]]; follow the [[Barefoot Shoe Transition Protocol]] before extended use.
