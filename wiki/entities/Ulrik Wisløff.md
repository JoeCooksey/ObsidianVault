# Ulrik Wisløff

Professor at NTNU and head of the **Cardiac Exercise Research Group (CERG)**, the lab that has done the largest body of clinical and longevity research on **[[Norwegian 4x4 Interval Protocol]]**-style high-intensity training.

## Significance
- Led the 5-year [[Generation 100 Study — HIIT and Mortality 2020]] (1,567 older adults), linking 4x4-style HIIT to larger VO2 max gains and a mortality-reduction signal vs moderate continuous training.
- CERG also produced the widely-used **"Fitness Age" / VO2 max estimator** based on NTNU population data.
- Extended 4x4 evidence into coronary artery disease, heart failure, metabolic syndrome, and obesity populations.

## Relation to the Protocol
Distinct from but complementary to [[Jan Helgerud]] and Jan Hoff (who defined/popularized the 4x4 format): Wisløff/CERG supplied much of the *clinical and longevity* evidence base.
