# Merrell

Mainstream outdoor brand whose **Glove** line is a top-rated minimalist option, valued for trail protection plus barefoot feel. (Source: [[OutdoorGearLab 2026 - Best Barefoot Shoes]])

- **Best overall (men's, editor pick):** Trail Glove 7 — more protective for trail use, beginner-friendly, versatile (~$130). Trade-off: less minimal feel; pebbles can lodge in the tread.
- **Best for minimalists (women's pick):** Vapor Glove 7 — exceptional ground feedback, excellent wet-surface traction, very light (~$110).
- **Positioning:** strong bridge between conventional outdoor shoes and true barefoot; easy retail availability.

A good trail-oriented alternative to [[Vivobarefoot]]'s Primus/Tracker lines.
