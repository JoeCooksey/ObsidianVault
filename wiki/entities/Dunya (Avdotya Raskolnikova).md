# Dunya (Avdotya Raskolnikova)

[[Rodion Raskolnikov|Raskolnikov's]] intelligent, strong-willed, and morally upright sister (Avdotya Romanovna Raskolnikova, called Dunya) in *[[Crime and Punishment - Fyodor Dostoevsky|Crime and Punishment]]*.

Dunya nearly sacrifices herself in a loveless marriage to the pompous, controlling **Luzhin** to rescue her brother and mother from poverty — a self-sacrifice that mirrors Raskolnikov's crime and helps trigger it. She had earlier worked as a governess for [[Arkady Svidrigailov|Svidrigailov]], who became obsessed with her. In the novel's climax he lures her and tries to force himself on her; she draws a revolver and, when it misfires, refuses him outright — her moral courage is what finally breaks him and precedes his suicide. She ultimately finds a worthy partner in [[Rodion Raskolnikov|Rodya's]] loyal friend [[Dmitri Razumikhin|Razumikhin]].

*Source: [[Crime and Punishment - Fyodor Dostoevsky]].*
