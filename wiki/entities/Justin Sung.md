# Justin Sung

Medical doctor turned **learning coach and educator** who teaches accelerated learning, with a focus on **higher order thinking and learning** (see [[Higher Order Thinking]]). Runs a YouTube channel and a weekly newsletter built around the same material, and coaches professionals one-on-one on the skill.

## Significance to this topic

- Argues the durable, AI-resistant skill for knowledge workers is **higher order thinking** — integrating contextual, multifactorial problems — not memorizing or speeding up existing workflows with AI.
- Rejects the standard Bloom's-taxonomy "ladder": claims lower → higher order is **not a progression**; they are different habits, and HOT only develops through deliberate practice of HOT itself.
- Practical method centers on **applying knowledge from day zero**, **mental (not physical) organization** via revisable mind maps, and **thinking on paper** to escape analysis paralysis.
- Notably critiques elaborate note/organization systems (color-coding, tags, flashcards, perfect templates) as **"productive procrastination"** — a direct caution for second-brain users.

## Why Joe cares

His framing supplies the *target* for the vault's "how to learn" cluster — what learning should aim at in the AI era — and pairs with [[Learning Agility (Meta-Skill)]]. Source video: *How To Learn So Fast That AI Can Never Replace You* (YouTube, 2026, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Xc_ExgwLs8).
