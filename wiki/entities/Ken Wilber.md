# Ken Wilber

## Overview
Ken Wilber (b. 1949) is an American philosopher and writer, the originator of **Integral Theory** and its **[[AQAL Model (Integral Theory)|AQAL ("All Quadrants, All Levels") model]]** — an ambitious attempt to synthesize insights from science, psychology, philosophy, and the world's contemplative traditions into a single framework for understanding reality, consciousness, and human development. He founded the Integral Institute and has written many books, including *Sex, Ecology, Spirituality*, *A Brief History of Everything*, and *Integral Psychology*.

## Key Contributions / Role
- **The [[AQAL Model (Integral Theory)|AQAL model]]** — the "map of all knowledge" that anchors **[[Craig Perry]]**'s video: [[Four Quadrants (AQAL)|four quadrants]] (interior/exterior × individual/collective), developmental **levels** (see [[Nine Levels of Thinking]]), **[[Lines of Development|lines]]** of development, plus states and types (not covered in the video).
- Insisted the framework is **a work in progress, not final** — meant to be built upon, which Perry highlights approvingly.
- Popularized the integration of developmental-stage models (e.g., Spiral Dynamics) with quadrant-based perspective-taking.

## Connections
- His levels/lines overlap with **Spiral Dynamics** and constructive-developmental psychology (Kegan, Cook-Greuter).
- Source of the structural backbone Perry pairs with [[David Deutsch]]'s [[Good Explanations (Hard-to-Vary)|good explanations]].
