# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator. Former Director of AI at Tesla (led Autopilot's neural network stack), co-founder of OpenAI, and now independent educator/researcher. Widely considered one of the most effective AI educators in the world.

## "Neural Networks: Zero to Hero" Series

The most respected free deep learning curriculum in existence. Builds everything from scratch in pure Python — no frameworks, just math and code. Sequence:

1. **micrograd** — autograd engine from scratch; backpropagation made explicit
2. **makemore** — character-level language model (bigram → MLP → RNN → transformer)
3. **nanoGPT** — GPT from scratch; the payoff of the whole series

Available at `karpathy.ai/zero-to-hero.html` (videos) and `github.com/karpathy/nn-zero-to-hero` (code).

## Key Teaching Philosophy
- No "magic black boxes" — if you can't build it from scratch, you don't understand it
- Pause. Type every line. Run it. Break it. That's the learning.
- Code first, theory second — theory makes sense once you've seen it work

## Why He Matters for Joe
- Karpathy's "micrograd" exercise produces exactly the understanding needed to read Ranjram's ML-CEMS paper intelligently
- The nanoGPT exercise connects directly to MAT 343 (linear algebra underpins every transformer layer)
- His teaching style matches the EE mental model: start with a working circuit, then derive the equations
