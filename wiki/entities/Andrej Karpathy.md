# Andrej Karpathy

AI researcher and educator. Co-founder of OpenAI, former Senior Director of AI at Tesla (led Autopilot vision), and former Stanford instructor (CS231n).

## Significance to this topic

- **Coined "vibe coding"** in a February 2025 post on X: a new kind of coding "where you fully give in to the vibes, embrace exponentials, and forget that the code even exists." (Source: [[Vibe Coding — Wikipedia]])
- Described the workflow as talking to Cursor Composer (running Claude Sonnet) via voice (SuperWhisper), accepting suggestions without reading diffs, and pasting errors back in until things work.
- Earlier (2023) claimed **"the hottest new programming language is English"** — the seed idea behind vibe coding: natural language as the primary interface to computers. (Source: [[Vibe Coding — Wikipedia]])
- Also originated the **autoresearch** loop pattern this vault's research skill is based on.

## Why Joe cares

Karpathy is one of the clearest teachers in AI. His framing of vibe coding sets the vocabulary for the whole [[Programming Skills AI Cannot Replace]] / [[AI-Assisted Programming Learning Roadmap]] discussion. He is listed S-tier on [[YouTube Channels for STEM Learning]].
