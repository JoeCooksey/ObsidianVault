# Moore4Power

**Moore4Power** ("More than Moore for Disruptive Innovations in Power Electronics") is an EU flagship R&D project launched **May 2026**, led by **[[Infineon Technologies]]** (Source: [[Moore4Power Launch — Semiconductor Today]]).

## Facts

| | |
|---|---|
| Lead | Infineon Technologies AG (Munich) |
| Budget | **€91 million** |
| Partners | **62** (large corporates, SMEs, research institutes) |
| Countries | **15** European nations |
| Duration | **3 years** |
| Funding | EU Horizon Europe — **Chips Joint Undertaking** (Innovation Action), co-funded by participating nations |
| Named partners | ALSTOM, ABB Finland, Fraunhofer ENAS, INNOVATION DISCO, INGETEAM |

## Technical thesis

Combine **Si + SiC + GaN** materials with embedded **sensing, control, and communication** into tightly integrated systems — each material used for what it does best. This is [[Heterogeneous Integration (Power Electronics)|heterogeneous integration]] as the post-[[Moore's Law and Dennard Scaling|Dennard]] path to gains ("More than Moore").

## Target applications & goals

- **Wind energy** — improved turbine power conversion / energy harvesting
- **E-mobility** — up to **99% efficiency**, near-loss-free **bidirectional** charging
- **Railway** — cut propulsion losses by **≥30%**
- **Digital Product Passport** embedded in modules for lifecycle / circular-economy transparency

## Significance

A concrete, well-funded institutional bet that the future of power electronics is **system-level integration of multiple materials**, not single-material scaling. Surfaced in [[wiki/newsletters/2026-05-29-ee-daily|EE Daily — May 29, 2026]] as the day's lead story.
