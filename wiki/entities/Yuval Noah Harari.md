# Yuval Noah Harari

**Born**: February 24, 1976, Kiryat Ata, Israel
**Known for**: *[[Sapiens - Yuval Noah Harari|Sapiens: A Brief History of Humankind]]* (2011/2014), *Homo Deus* (2016), *21 Lessons for the 21st Century* (2018), and *Nexus* (2024)

---

## Background

Harari is a historian and professor at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, with a doctorate in history from Oxford. Originally a specialist in medieval and military history, he became a global public intellectual with *Sapiens*, which reframes all of human history around our unique capacity for large-scale cooperation through [[Shared Fictions|shared fictions]].

His later work turns from the past to the future — the prospects and dangers of biotechnology, AI, and data ([[The End of Homo Sapiens]]) — making him a prominent (and sometimes controversial) commentator on technology and society. He practices Vipassana meditation, which he credits with shaping his focus.

---

## Core Ideas

- **[[Shared Fictions]] / [[Imagined Orders]]** — money, religion, nations, and law are collective inventions that enable mass cooperation
- **[[Money as Trust]]** — money is the most universal system of mutual trust ever devised
- **The four revolutions** — [[Cognitive Revolution|Cognitive]], [[Agricultural Revolution|Agricultural]], [[Unification of Humankind|Unification]], [[Scientific Revolution|Scientific]]
- **Power vs. happiness** — humanity's power compounds while wisdom and contentment lag ([[Are We Happier|Are We Happier?]])
