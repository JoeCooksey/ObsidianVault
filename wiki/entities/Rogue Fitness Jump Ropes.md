# Rogue Fitness Jump Ropes

Rogue's jump-rope line spans the full price range and supplies the **best-overall** pick in most 2026 roundups.

## Key models
- **SR-2 3.0 Speed Rope ($46.50)** — *best overall.* Knurled aircraft-grade aluminum handles, fast smooth rotation, sold in two handle sizes. Indoor-only; thin cables can warp. (Source: [[BarBend - Best CrossFit Jump Ropes (2026)]])
- **SR-1 Bearing Speed Rope** — super-light for double-unders, coated kink-resistant cable, glass-filled nylon handles, 7 colors / multiple lengths.
- **Thai-Style 3.0 ($16)** — heavy (13.6 oz) PVC rope for upper-body strength/endurance; cheapest way into a weighted rope. (Source: [[Yahoo Health - Best Jump Ropes 2026 (Trainer-Tested)]])
- **Licorice Rope ($8.50)** — best for kids/outdoor; thick PVC, lengths from 7'; no rotation system. (Source: [[BarBend - Best Jump Ropes for Beginners (2026)]])

## Take
Rogue is the safe default for a quality speed rope (SR-2) and also covers the cheap-heavy (Thai) and kids/outdoor (Licorice) niches — one brand across budget tiers.
