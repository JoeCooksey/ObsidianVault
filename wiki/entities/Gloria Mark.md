# Gloria Mark

Chancellor's Professor of Informatics at **UC Irvine**; the leading empirical researcher on **interruption, multitasking, and attention** in real work. Author of *Attention Span* (2023).

## Why she matters here

She is the authority behind the **[[Interruption Resumption Cost]]** finding — the widely cited "it takes ~23 minutes to get back to a task after an interruption." Her shadowing studies of real office workers established that work is highly **fragmented** and that resuming an interrupted task carries a real cognitive cost.

## Signature findings

- "The Cost of Interrupted Work: More Speed and Stress" (Mark, Gudith & Klocke, 2008): after an interruption it takes an average of **23 min 15 s** to return to the original task; interrupted people work *faster* but with more stress, frustration, and effort. (Source: [[Gloria Mark - The Cost of Interrupted Work]])
- Real work is shredded into short segments — average focus on a screen before switching has fallen to ~47 seconds in her later tracking.
- ~82% of interrupted work is resumed the same day, but only after several intervening tasks.
