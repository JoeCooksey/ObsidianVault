# Mel Robbins

**Born**: October 6, 1968, Kansas City, Missouri
**Known for**: *The 5 Second Rule* (2017) and *[[The Let Them Theory - Mel Robbins|The Let Them Theory]]* (2024); *The Mel Robbins Podcast*; one of the most-booked motivational speakers in the world

---

## Background

A former criminal defense attorney, Robbins reinvented herself as a speaker and author after a period of personal and financial difficulty. Her 2011 TEDx talk "How to Stop Screwing Yourself Over" went viral and led to *The 5 Second Rule* — a simple behavioral tool (count 5-4-3-2-1 and act) for overcoming hesitation.

Her 2024 book *The Let Them Theory*, co-written with her daughter Sawyer Robbins, became a runaway bestseller built around the two-word tool **[[Let Them]]** (release control of others) plus its essential second half **[[Let Me]]** (own your response).

---

## Core Themes

- **Action over feeling** — small mechanical interventions to beat hesitation and anxiety (the 5 Second Rule)
- **Releasing control** — most stress comes from trying to manage other people; the cure is the Let Them / Let Me pairing
- **Self-responsibility** — reclaiming agency over your own response to anything
