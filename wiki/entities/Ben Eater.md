# Ben Eater

Educator and YouTuber famous for building complete digital systems on breadboards from discrete logic chips, filmed at a pace where a viewer can build along. His [[Ben Eater — Build an 8-bit Computer|8-bit computer series]] is the single most-cited "breadboard capstone" project on the internet and effectively created the breadboard-computer genre.

## Signature builds
- **8-bit computer** — clock, registers, ALU, RAM, control logic, output, all in 74-series TTL on breadboards.
- **Breadboard computer playing Snake** on a small display.
- **VGA / "world's worst video card"** generated from logic chips.
- **6502-based computer** kit — a real retro CPU wired up by hand.

## Why he matters for Joe
He is the bridge between [[Digital Logic and Boolean Algebra|Boolean algebra/logic gates]] (theory) and **computer architecture you can touch**. Working through his series turns abstract CPU concepts into placed wires — a high-signal portfolio piece for an [[Engineering|EE student]].
