# Kirkland Signature Stir-Fry Vegetable Blend

Costco's house-brand frozen stir-fry mix, 5.5 lb bag. **Conventional (not organic)** — Costco sells a separate *organic* Normandy blend and *organic* mixed-vegetable line, but this stir-fry blend is conventional. No preservatives; an "excellent source of Vitamin C."

## Ingredients vs pesticide ranking
Mapped against the [[EWG Shopper's Guide to Pesticides in Produce (2025)]] (Dirty Dozen = buy organic; Clean Fifteen = conventional is fine):

| Ingredient | 2025 EWG status | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Onions | **Clean Fifteen (#5)** | Conventional fine |
| Sweet peas (snap peas) | **Clean Fifteen (#6, frozen)** | Conventional fine |
| Carrots | **Clean Fifteen (#13)** | Conventional fine |
| Mushrooms | **Clean Fifteen (#14)** | Conventional fine |
| Broccoli | Not listed (consistently low residue) | Conventional fine |
| Water chestnuts | Not tested (peeled/canned-type) | Conventional fine |
| Green beans | Not in 2025 top 12; **historically flagged** | Borderline |
| Red bell peppers | Not in 2025 top 12; **historically flagged** | Borderline |

**Bottom line: ~6 of 8 ingredients are on or near the Clean Fifteen.** Only green beans and bell peppers have any pesticide-priority history, and neither made the 2025 Dirty Dozen. This blend is a **poor candidate** for paying an organic premium.
