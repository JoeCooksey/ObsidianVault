# D.E. Shaw

## Profile

**D. E. Shaw & Co.** is a quantitative investment firm founded **1988** by **David E. Shaw**, a former Columbia computer-science professor. Nicknamed the **"King of Quants,"** it pioneered computational, technology-driven trading and is famous as the firm where **Jeff Bezos worked** before founding Amazon.

## Strategy

Like Renaissance, D.E. Shaw hunts **statistical anomalies and inefficiencies** across markets using heavy computation and quantitative models — see [[Statistical Arbitrage]] and [[Quantitative Trading]]. It now runs both systematic (quant) and discretionary strategies as a multi-strategy platform.

## Track Record

- **~22% annualized returns from 1988 inception through 2000.** (confidence: medium)
- **2024: topped all hedge funds with $11.1B net investor gains** — its **Oculus fund +36.1%**, diversified **Composite +18%**, beating Millennium ($9.4B) and Citadel ($9.0B). (Source: [[2024 Hedge Fund Returns Reporting]]) (confidence: medium — press-reported)

## Is It Actually Successful?

Yes — one of the most consistent and largest quant platforms, and the **2024 leader by dollar profits**. But like all the platform quants its **net returns (~15–18%) sit well below Medallion's ~39%**, and access is institutional. It validates that quant works at scale — while underscoring that Medallion remains the unreachable outlier.
