# NotebookLM

Google's **source-grounded AI research/study assistant** (Google Labs). You upload sources (PDFs, docs, slides, pasted text, even a book's chapters) and it answers, summarizes, and generates study materials **strictly from those sources, with inline citations** — it does not roam the open web.

## Why It Stands Apart for Reading

- **Grounded, not generative-from-thin-air.** Answers cite the exact passage, so it can't drift away from *your* book. This is the key difference from a general chatbot.
- **Study-material generation:** flashcards, quizzes, study guides, glossaries.
- **Audio Overviews:** podcast-style Brief / Critique / Debate of your material.
- **Learning Guide:** tutor mode that asks probing questions instead of dumping answers.

## How Joe Uses It

The concrete engine for the "AI second" pass on each chapter — see [[NotebookLM Study Workflow]]. Already referenced in [[Self-Study Textbook List + NotebookLM Prompt]] for technical textbooks.

> [!note] Use it *after* the handwritten summary, never before — see [[Cognitive Offloading (Learning Risk)]].
