# Old School RuneScape

**Old School RuneScape (OSRS)** is a point-and-click MMORPG by Jagex, a 2013 restoration of the 2007 build of RuneScape. It is built around 23 grind-based skills, quests, slayer, and group/solo PvM (bossing and raids). The design centres on long, low-input "grinds," which is why "what to do while playing" splits into two distinct questions: what is the best **in-game** activity right now, and what to do **alongside** the game while a low-attention grind runs.

## Core loops
- **Skilling** — train 23 skills (combat + gathering + production + support) toward level 99 each / max cape.
- **PvM** — slayer tasks, solo bosses, group bosses, and raids (Chambers of Xeric, Theatre of Blood, Tombs of Amascut).
- **Money-making** — fund gear/consumables via skilling, PvM drops, flipping on the Grand Exchange, and passive runs (herb/birdhouse/Miscellania).
- **Account progression** — quests, achievement diaries, combat achievements, and gear unlocks that gate higher content.

## The "AFK spectrum"
OSRS activities range from fully active (raids) to near-idle (10–30 min between clicks). The game has a ~20-minute logout timer, so the most AFK methods are designed to keep the character active without input up to that limit. (Source: [[OSRS Wiki — Idle (AFK Methods)]])
