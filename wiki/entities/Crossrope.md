# Crossrope

Premium **weighted jump rope system** built around interchangeable ropes that click into one set of handles. The standard "best weighted rope" pick across 2026 roundups, and the most expensive.

## What makes it different
- **Modular ropes:** swap ¼-lb (speed/endurance) and ½-lb (strength) ropes — and up to 1-lb/2-lb in fuller kits — into the same handles, so you never outgrow it.
- **Lifetime warranty** on handles; 4.8★ average rating.
- **AMP smart handles ($99):** Bluetooth, jump tracking, 6-month rechargeable battery, 3,000+ guided workouts via app.

## Costs / caveats
- App **premium membership** is extra: ~$79.99/yr or $11.99/mo.
- Handles feel heavy; it's a system/app purchase, not a featherweight speed rope.

The value case: vs a $15 rope it's expensive, but vs a gym membership the AMP+app system is positioned as a full guided-training platform. (Source: [[BarBend - Best CrossFit Jump Ropes (2026)]], [[Yahoo Health - Best Jump Ropes 2026 (Trainer-Tested)]])
