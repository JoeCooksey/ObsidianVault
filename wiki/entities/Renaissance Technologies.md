# Renaissance Technologies

## Profile

**Renaissance Technologies ("RenTech")** is a quantitative hedge fund in East Setauket, NY, founded by **[[Jim Simons]]** in 1978 (as Monemetrics; renamed 1982). It runs the legendary **Medallion Fund** and is the benchmark by which all quant funds are measured. AUM ~$130B (2021). Current CEO: **Peter Fitzhugh Brown**.

## The Medallion Fund

- **~66% gross / ~39% net** annualized returns since 1988; ~71.8% gross 1994–mid-2014. (confidence: high)
- **Fee structure: 5% management + 44% performance** — extreme, and justified only by the returns.
- **+98.2% in 2008** (S&P −38.5%); **+76% in 2020**; worst year 2001–2013 was +21% net.
- **Closed to outside investors since 1993**; employees and families only; capped ~$10B to preserve the edge.

(Source: [[Renaissance Technologies — Wikipedia]])

## Strategy & Moat

Mines **statistical correlations and repeating anomalies** in petabyte-scale data with pattern-recognition / ML; many small, short-horizon, market-neutral trades. The moat is **secrecy + talent density** (mathematicians, physicists, ex-cryptanalysts — almost no traditional finance hires) + the discipline to keep the fund small. See [[Statistical Arbitrage]], [[Quantitative Trading]].

## Public Funds vs. Medallion

The investor-accessible **RIEF** (2005) trails Medallion by ~17–19% — direct evidence that the edge does not scale to large, liquid, long-hold strategies. Outsiders cannot buy the real magic.

## Controversy

**2021 IRS settlement up to $7B** over a basket-options structure that recharacterized short-term gains as long-term capital gains — one of the largest tax settlements in US history.
