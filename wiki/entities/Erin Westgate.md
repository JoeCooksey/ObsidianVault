# Erin Westgate

Social psychologist, University of Florida (search coverage also lists Florida State — UF per her own 2020 paper byline). The leading theorist of boredom.

- Co-author (with Timothy Wilson) of the **[[MAC Model of Boredom]]** — "Boring Thoughts and Bored Minds," *Psychological Review* 2018
- Author of [[Westgate 2020 - Why Boredom Is Interesting]] (*Current Directions in Psychological Science*) — boredom as a pain-like signal of attention/meaning deficits
- Co-author of the famous "Just Think" study (Wilson et al. 2014, *Science*) where participants preferred electric shocks to sitting alone with their thoughts

Core position: boredom is neither good nor bad — "Boredom is a signal: it is what we do with that signal that counts."
