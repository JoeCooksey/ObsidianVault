# Grant Sanderson

**Role**: Mathematician, educator, and creator of the YouTube channel **3Blue1Brown** — the most-recommended channel for mathematical intuition and a top recommendation for machine-learning fundamentals.

## Significance

- 3Blue1Brown is ranked the uncontested #1 channel for building visual mathematical understanding, appearing on essentially every "best math/STEM channel" list surveyed in 2026 (Source: cross-confirmed across multiple independent lists — **high confidence**).
- Former Khan Academy contributor. Built **Manim**, an open-source Python animation engine, to produce his visual proofs; Manim is now widely used by other educators.
- His *Neural Networks* series is frequently cited as the single best free introduction to how machine learning works, bridging math education and AI (complements [[Andrej Karpathy]]'s practitioner-level "Zero to Hero" series).

## Flagship Series

- **Essence of Calculus** (~11 videos) — visual derivation of core calculus concepts
- **Essence of Linear Algebra** (~16 videos) — transformations, eigenvectors, determinants as geometry
- **Neural Networks** (core series) — gradient descent and backpropagation from first principles
- **Differential Equations** — ODE intuition relevant to circuits and physics

## For Joe

Essence of Linear Algebra directly supports MAT 267 and EEE 350 (Signals); the Neural Networks series is the gateway to the Phase 4 AI track. Highest-leverage single channel for the EE + math path. See [[YouTube Channels for STEM Learning]].
