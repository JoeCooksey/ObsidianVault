# Sönke Ahrens

German writer and education researcher. Author of **_How to Take Smart Notes_** (2017), the book that brought [[Niklas Luhmann]]'s **Zettelkasten** method to a mainstream English-speaking audience and turned it into a step-by-step workflow.

## Significance

- Systematized the slip-box into the now-standard pipeline: **fleeting → literature → permanent → linked → project** notes → [[How to Take Smart Notes - Sonke Ahrens]].
- Popularized the principle that **writing notes is the act of thinking**, not a chore that follows it — making note systems the engine of learning and writing.
- His framework is the conceptual backbone of modern personal-knowledge-management tools (Obsidian, Roam, Logseq) and of this vault's own structure.
