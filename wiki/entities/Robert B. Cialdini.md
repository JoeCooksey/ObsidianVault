# Robert B. Cialdini

**Born**: April 27, 1945, Milwaukee, Wisconsin
**Known for**: *[[Influence - Robert Cialdini|Influence: The Psychology of Persuasion]]* (1984) and *Pre-Suasion* (2016); the foundational research on the principles of persuasion

---

## Background

Cialdini is Regents' Professor Emeritus of Psychology and Marketing at Arizona State University. To understand how compliance actually works in the real world, he spent years going **undercover** — training as a car salesman, fundraiser, and telemarketer — to observe the tactics "compliance professionals" use. That field research became *Influence*, one of the most cited works in social psychology and a staple of marketing and negotiation.

He later founded the consultancy Influence at Work and is widely regarded as the leading scientific authority on persuasion and ethical influence.

---

## Core Contributions

- The **six (now seven) principles of influence**: [[Reciprocity]], [[Commitment and Consistency]], [[Social Proof]], [[Liking]], [[Authority]], [[Scarcity]], and [[Unity]] (added 2021)
- **Pre-suasion** — the idea that *what you do before* a message (framing attention) shapes its persuasiveness as much as the message itself
- A focus on **ethical influence** and defending against manipulation, not just wielding it
