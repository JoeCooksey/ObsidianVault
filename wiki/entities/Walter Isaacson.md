# Walter Isaacson

**Walter Isaacson** (b. 1952) — American author, journalist, and professor; the most prominent biographical writer of tech and scientific innovators of his generation. Former chairman and CEO of CNN and former managing editor of *Time* magazine. Currently University Professor of History at Tulane. His biography of Steve Jobs (2011) — written at Jobs's personal request — is the definitive account of Silicon Valley's defining figure and established Isaacson as the go-to chronicler of transformative innovators.

## Recurring Thesis
Across all biographies, Isaacson returns to a consistent argument: the greatest innovators are distinguished not by raw intelligence but by an **intense curiosity that bridges disciplines** — Leonardo crossed art and anatomy, Franklin crossed science and civic life, Jobs crossed technology and liberal arts, Musk crosses physics and manufacturing economics. Isaacson frames this as a learnable disposition, not a genetic gift.
