# Hypertrophy Academy

Fitness YouTube channel focused on **hypertrophy** (muscle growth) training, nutrition, recovery, and supplementation, aimed at healthy high-performing young adults (the creator cites a 15–30 demographic). Content blends cited science with the creator's coaching experience and personal protocols.

The creator is a coach who works with young high performers and competes in **powerlifting** (mentions a competition in Sydney). He shares personal health details candidly — a [[Sleep Apnea and Nose Breathing|deviated septum and treated sleep apnea]], an excellent blood-work profile, and detailed supplement dosing.
