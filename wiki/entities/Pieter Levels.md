# Pieter Levels

Dutch solo founder ("@levelsio"), the archetype of the one-person internet business. Runs a portfolio generating **~$3M/year ARR with zero employees** (~$138K/month reported Nov 2025). (Source: [[Pieter Levels — How He Builds (Case Study)]])

## Products

- **Nomad List** — community/data site for remote workers (grew to ~$3M ARR)
- **RemoteOK** — remote job board
- **PhotoAI** — AI headshot/photo generator (his largest single revenue source, ~70% of income)

## Method

- **Deliberately simple stack**: vanilla PHP, jQuery, SQLite — no React, no microservices, no team. Contradicts mainstream engineering advice. (Source: [[Pieter Levels — How He Builds (Case Study)]])
- Leans into **vibe coding** — uses AI to iterate "at light speed" rather than writing every line; built a working flight simulator by vibing with the model. (Source: [[Pieter Levels — How He Builds (Case Study)]])
- **Ships before ready**: PhotoAI v1 had terrible output and people paid anyway; he improved from real usage, not hypotheticals. See [[Profitable Micro-SaaS Playbook]].
- **12 startups in 12 months** challenge (2014) — volume of shots over perfection of one.
- **Audience as distribution moat**: builds in public on X, shares live revenue numbers. See [[Building in Public]].

## Why Joe cares

Levels is the proof case that [[Permissionless Leverage]] (code + media) lets one person reach $1M+/yr. His "ship ugly, charge immediately, simple tech" doctrine is the practical counterweight to over-engineering. Relevant to [[Online Income Methods Tier List]].
