# Stephen Covey

**Stephen Richards Covey** (1932–2012) — American educator, author, and businessman best known for *The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People* (1989), which has sold over 40 million copies and been translated into 40+ languages. A professor and vice-chairman at the Jon M. Huntsman School of Business at Utah State University. Founder of the Covey Leadership Center (later merged with Franklin Quest to form FranklinCovey). His core thesis: lasting effectiveness is built on the Character Ethic — principles such as integrity, humility, and courage — not the Personality Ethic of image and technique that dominates modern self-help.

## Core Framework: The 7 Habits
Organized around the **[[Maturity Continuum]]** (Dependence → Independence → Interdependence):

- **Private Victory (Habits 1–3):** Be Proactive ([[Circle of Concern vs Circle of Influence]]), Begin with the End in Mind, Put First Things First ([[Time Management Matrix]])
- **Public Victory (Habits 4–6):** Think Win-Win ([[Win-Win Paradigm]] + [[Abundance Mentality]]), Seek First to Understand ([[Empathic Listening]]), Synergize
- **Renewal (Habit 7):** Sharpen the Saw

The [[P/PC Balance]] and [[Emotional Bank Account]] are cross-cutting concepts that run through all seven habits.
