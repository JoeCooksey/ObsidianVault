# Cal Newport

Georgetown University computer science professor and author of multiple productivity books. Coined the terms **deep work** and **shallow work**; his framework defines the gold standard for high-value focused work in the knowledge economy.

## Books
- *Deep Work: Rules for Focused Success in a Distracted World* (2016) — defines deep vs. shallow work; 4 scheduling philosophies; core framework
- *So Good They Can't Ignore You* (2012) — career capital theory; deliberate practice for building rare skills
- *Digital Minimalism* (2019) — philosophy of intentional technology use
- *A World Without Email* (2021) — critique of hyperactive hive mind workflow

## Core Ideas
- **Deep Work**: cognitively demanding, distraction-free, value-creating professional work
- **Shallow Work**: logistical, easily replicable, can be done while distracted
- **4-hour ceiling**: even expert performers rarely sustain >4 hours of true deep work daily (aligns with Ericsson's deliberate practice research)
- **Deep work audit**: track 15–30 min blocks for 1 week, classify deep/shallow/neither, set ambitious shift target
