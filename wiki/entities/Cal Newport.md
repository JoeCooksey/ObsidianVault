# Cal Newport

Georgetown computer-science professor and author. Best known for ***So Good They Can't Ignore You*** (2012) and **[[Deep Work — Cal Newport|*Deep Work*]]** (2016).

## Core ideas

- **"Don't follow your passion."** Passion is an *output* of mastery, not a prerequisite. People grow passionate as they get good at something. (Source: [[Career Capital]])
- **Career capital.** Build rare and valuable skills first; those skills are the currency you trade for autonomy, meaning, and money. The mechanism for building them is [[Deliberate Practice]].
- **Craftsman mindset > passion mindset.** Focus on the value you produce, not what the job offers you.
- **Deep work.** The ability to focus without distraction on cognitively demanding tasks is rare, valuable, and trainable — see [[Deep Work — Cal Newport]].

His work is a central pillar of the skills/career dimension of [[Research - Best Way to Invest in Yourself]].
