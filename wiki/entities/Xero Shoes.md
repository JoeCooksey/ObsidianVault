# Xero Shoes

Value-leader barefoot brand offering genuine barefoot performance at roughly half the price of premium rivals. Thin, flexible soles with a close-to-barefoot feel and roomy toe box. (Source: [[Research - Barefoot Shoes Benefits and Best Brands]])

- **Best for running (editor pick):** HFS II — ~5.5 mm stack, roomy toe box, lug tread for grip, lightweight breathable upper (~$130). A top pick for people transitioning into barefoot running.
- **Other lines:** Prio (all-rounder), Mesa Trail (trail), Z-Trail / Genesis (sandals), casual styles.
- **Positioning:** best price-to-performance; common entry brand alongside [[Lems Shoes]].
- **Trade-offs:** ground feel slightly less refined than premium brands; styling more athletic than dressy.

Most-compared rival: [[Vivobarefoot]] (Xero wins on price, Vivobarefoot on ground feel).
