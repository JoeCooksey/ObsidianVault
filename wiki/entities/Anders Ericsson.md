# Anders Ericsson

Swedish-American psychologist (1947–2020) at Florida State University. The world's foremost expert on expert performance. His research on **deliberate practice** fundamentally changed how we understand skill acquisition and expertise.

## Key Research
- Studied violin students at Berlin Academy of Music (1993 study with Krampe & Tesch-Römer) — distinguished "best," "good," and future music teachers by accumulated deliberate practice hours
- Showed top performers across chess, music, medicine, sports converge on the same practice pattern: short intense sessions (60–90 min) with immediate feedback
- Deliberate practice ceiling: ~**4 hours per day** of truly concentrated skill-building effort

## Core Concept
**Deliberate practice**: focused, structured training on specific weaknesses; designed by teacher/coach; includes immediate feedback and repeated refinement. Distinct from *experience* (just doing something) or *naive practice* (unthinking repetition).

## Influence on Newport
Cal Newport used Ericsson's deliberate practice model as the neurological foundation for his [[Deep Work]] framework — both require the same distraction-free focused state; both hit the same 4-hour daily ceiling.
