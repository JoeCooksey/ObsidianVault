# J. Kenji López-Alt

Chef, food writer, and food scientist; the leading popularizer of **science-based home cooking**. Former chief culinary advisor at Serious Eats and author of *The Food Lab* (2015), which applies test-kitchen experimentation to everyday techniques. MIT-trained background; known for rigorous "why does this work" recipe development.

## Relevance here
- **Scrambled eggs:** developed the **[[Scrambled Egg Science|starch-slurry hack]]** (cornstarch/potato/tapioca) that lets you make creamy soft-scrambled eggs over higher heat in minutes, and a **cream-stream method** (borrowed from egg-drop soup) for silky ribbons. Argues salting eggs *before* cooking softens their texture. (Source: [The Kitchn](https://www.thekitchn.com/kenji-lopez-alt-scrambled-eggs-review-23143709))
- General authority cited throughout the vault's food-science pages on the [[Maillard Reaction]], browning, and protein coagulation.

> [!note] One of the more authoritative culinary voices because his claims come from controlled kitchen testing rather than tradition.
