# Scott Young

Writer and self-directed-learning expert; author of **_Ultralearning_** (2019). Known for aggressive self-education projects that test learning science in practice.

## Notable projects

- **The MIT Challenge** — completed the curriculum of MIT's 4-year computer science degree (33 courses) in ~12 months, self-studied, using the university's free online materials and final exams.
- **The Year Without English** — learned four languages (Spanish, Portuguese, Mandarin, Korean) in a year by living in each country and refusing to speak English (an extreme application of *directness*).

## Ultralearning: 9 principles

A framework for intense, self-directed learning that operationalizes the cognitive-science findings:

1. **Metalearning** — draw a map first → [[Metalearning (Drawing the Map)]]
2. **Focus** — cultivate the ability to concentrate
3. **Directness** — learn by doing the actual thing you want to be good at
4. **Drill** — attack your weakest sub-skill
5. **Retrieval** — test to learn → [[Active Recall (Retrieval Practice)]]
6. **Feedback** — seek fast, informative feedback
7. **Retention** — fight the forgetting curve → [[Spaced Repetition]]
8. **Intuition** — dig deep to understand, don't memorize hollowly → [[The Feynman Technique]]
9. **Experimentation** — go beyond the standard path once you have the basics

## Why he matters for learning

Young is the **synthesizer/practitioner**: his 9 principles package the academic findings (retrieval, spacing, [[Deliberate Practice]], [[Desirable Difficulties]]) into an actionable project framework, demonstrated on himself at extreme scale.
