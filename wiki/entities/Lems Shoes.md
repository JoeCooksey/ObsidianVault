# Lems Shoes

The go-to "gateway" barefoot brand for beginners. Designs are slightly more forgiving than ultra-minimal shoes, making the transition less jarring. (Source: [[Research - Barefoot Shoes Benefits and Best Brands]])

- **Best casual (editor pick):** Primal Zen — ~11 mm stack (roughly double the [[Xero Shoes]] HFS II's 5.5 mm), versatile styling, flexible, beginner-appropriate (~$120).
- **Best women's boot:** Chelsea Waterproof — stylish, waterproof, winter-appropriate (~$175).
- **Positioning:** easiest on-ramp into barefoot footwear; wide toe box with a touch more underfoot stack/cushion than true minimal shoes.
- **Trade-offs:** more stack = less ground feel than [[Vivobarefoot]] or [[Vibram FiveFingers]]; a deliberate beginner compromise.

Pairs naturally with the [[Barefoot Shoe Transition Protocol]] — a good first shoe before stepping down to thinner soles.
