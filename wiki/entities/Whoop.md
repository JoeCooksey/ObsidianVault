# Whoop

A **wrist-worn** fitness and recovery tracker that monitors heart rate, sleep, strain, and recovery, surfacing a daily "recovery"/readiness score. Positioned as a direct competitor to the [[Oura Ring]].

In [[The Sleepmaxxing Protocol — Hypertrophy Academy]], the creator evaluated Whoop but chose Oura instead, reasoning that a wrist-worn strap would "piss me off too much." Like Oura, it carries the risk of fostering score-checking anxiety if used compulsively — see [[Wearable Health Metric Validity]].
