# Niklas Luhmann

German sociologist (1927–1998) and the originator of the modern **Zettelkasten** ("slip-box") note system. One of the most prolific social theorists of the 20th century.

## Significance

- Built a slip-box of roughly **90,000 index cards** over his career, each an atomic idea linked to others by a hand-numbered addressing scheme.
- Credited his extraordinary output — **~70 books and ~400 scholarly articles** — to the slip-box, which he treated as a thinking partner ("I never force myself to do anything I don't feel like... I always do what comes easily").
- His method was rediscovered, proceduralized, and popularized by [[Sönke Ahrens]] in [[How to Take Smart Notes - Sonke Ahrens]].
- [[Ryan Holiday]] and [[Robert Greene]]'s analog notecard system is the same idea in physical form → [[The Notecard System - Ryan Holiday]].
