# Craig Perry

## Overview
Craig Perry is a self-education writer and YouTuber who publishes long-form essays on **thinking, learning, reading, and self-development** — primarily on **Substack** (where he reports 1M+ reads over ~10–11 months) and mirrored to YouTube and Spotify. He writes as a self-described non-expert sharing what he's learned, framing his own past as an "anxious, insecure kid" with high neuroticism who was transformed by studying these frameworks. He explicitly disavows quick-fix / TikTok content in favor of dense, demanding explainers.

## Key Contributions / Role
- Creator of **[[How To Increase Your Intelligence — Craig Perry]]** (~42 min), which popularizes **[[Ken Wilber]]**'s [[AQAL Model (Integral Theory)|Integral Theory]] and **[[David Deutsch]]**'s [[Good Explanations (Hard-to-Vary)|good-explanations]] idea as a practical "map of knowledge."
- Frames intelligence as the quality of one's [[Good Explanations (Hard-to-Vary)|explanations]] and promotes a daily [[Explanation-Building Writing Practice|writing practice]] for raising awareness.
- Has a related video "How to Think on Paper" and publishes free reading/learning guides ("profound self-education guide," "guide to profound reading") plus a free outcome-based 30-day learning plan; at time of recording was building a **writing course**.

## Connections
- Intellectual sources he draws on: [[Ken Wilber]], [[David Deutsch]], [[Carl Jung]], [[Albert Einstein]] (quoted), [[Alan Watts]], [[Terence McKenna]].
