# Jan Helgerud

Norwegian exercise physiologist at NTNU (Norwegian University of Science and Technology); with **Jan Hoff**, co-developer of the **[[Norwegian 4x4 Interval Protocol]]** and co-founder of Myworkout. Helgerud and Hoff have together published 200+ peer-reviewed articles on aerobic interval training and VO2 max.

## Significance
- Established and validated the 4 × 4 min @ ~90–95% HRmax format as a superior stimulus for raising VO2 max versus moderate continuous training.
- Helgerud's running studies showed 4x4 intervals produce significantly larger VO2 max gains than MICT or lactate-threshold training in moderately active people.

## Related Work
- Closely associated with [[Ulrik Wisløff]]'s Cardiac Exercise Research Group (CERG) at NTNU, which extended 4x4 research into clinical and longevity populations ([[Generation 100 Study — HIIT and Mortality 2020]]).
