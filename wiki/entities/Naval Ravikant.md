# Naval Ravikant

Entrepreneur and angel investor; co-founder of AngelList. Best known publicly for a body of aphoristic writing and talks on wealth creation, leverage, and judgment, collected by Eric Jorgenson in *The Almanack of Naval Ravikant* (2020).

## Why he's in this vault

His framework is the spine of [[Compounding Daily Projects Tier List]]:

- **Four kinds of leverage** (labor, capital, code, media) — code and media are **permissionless**. (Source: [[Permissionless Leverage]])
- **Specific knowledge** — built by following genuine curiosity, not credentials; "escape competition through authenticity."
- **Productize yourself** — *"You're never going to get rich renting out your time"*; *"earn with your mind, not your time"*; build assets that earn while you sleep.
- **Long-term games with long-term people** — *"all returns in life — wealth, relationships, knowledge — come from compound interest."*

His investing conclusions align with what's already in Joe's vault: own equity/assets, let compound interest run, avoid renting time. See [[Index Fund Investing]], [[Financial Literacy Roadmap]].
