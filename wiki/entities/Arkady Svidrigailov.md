# Arkady Svidrigailov

The dark, enigmatic antagonist of *[[Crime and Punishment - Fyodor Dostoevsky|Crime and Punishment]]* (Arkady Ivanovich Svidrigailov). A wealthy, sensual former employer of [[Dunya (Avdotya Raskolnikova)|Dunya]] whom he had lusted after, he is rumored to have caused several deaths and feels no remorse for any of them.

Svidrigailov is [[Rodion Raskolnikov|Raskolnikov's]] double and shadow — the man Raskolnikov would become if [[The Extraordinary Man Theory|the theory]] were actually true. He has genuinely placed himself beyond good and evil, and the result is not Napoleonic greatness but boredom, emptiness, and despair. After overhearing Raskolnikov's confession to [[Sonya Marmeladov|Sonya]], he tries to coerce Dunya; when she refuses him even at gunpoint, he releases her, performs a few final acts of charity, and shoots himself on a bleak, rainy night. His suicide is one of the novel's two responses to guilt — self-destruction — set against Raskolnikov's confession and possible renewal.

*Source: [[Crime and Punishment - Fyodor Dostoevsky]].*
