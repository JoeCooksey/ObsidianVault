# Andy Weir

American hard-science-fiction author and former software engineer; the defining writer of "engineering-joy" SF, where the plot *is* the protagonist solving constrained technical problems with real science.

## Why He Matters Here
Weir's books are the single best fiction on-ramp for an EE student: every chapter models the feel of constraint-driven problem solving and first-principles reasoning — the engineering mindset that the [[EE Student Skill Stack (Ranked)]] is built to develop.

## Key Works
- **The Martian (2011)** — Astronaut-engineer Mark Watney survives Mars by chaining mechanics, chemistry, botany, and orbital math; solutions track real NASA research. Adapted into the 2015 film.
- **Project Hail Mary (2021)** — First-contact puzzle built on physics, chemistry, and improvised instrumentation; standout audiobook.
- **Artemis (2017)** — Heist on a lunar colony; lighter, but still economics-and-engineering grounded.
