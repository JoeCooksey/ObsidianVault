# Carl Jung

## Overview
Carl Gustav Jung (1875–1961) was a Swiss psychiatrist and the founder of **analytical psychology**. Originally a close collaborator of Freud, he broke away to develop his own school, contributing enduring concepts: the **collective unconscious**, **archetypes**, the **shadow**, **individuation**, and the **psychological types** (introversion/extraversion) that later influenced personality models like the MBTI and the Big Five.

## Key Contributions / Role
- Quoted in **[[Craig Perry]]**'s video as "the OG psychologist": *"The world will ask you who you are, and if you do not know, the world will tell you."* — used to make the point that if you can't author your own [[Good Explanations (Hard-to-Vary)|explanation]] of yourself, others will impose theirs.
- His ideas on self-knowledge and individuation underpin the video's theme that taking ownership of your self-narrative is the route out of suffering.
