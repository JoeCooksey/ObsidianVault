# Dacher Keltner

American psychologist, professor at **UC Berkeley**, and founding director of the **Greater Good Science Center**. He is the leading researcher on the emotion of **awe** and a central figure in the science of positive emotion, compassion, and wellbeing.

## Key Facts

- **Role**: Professor of Psychology, UC Berkeley; founding director, Greater Good Science Center
- **Specialty**: emotion science — awe, compassion, power, the biology of positive emotion
- **Public science**: science consultant on Pixar's *Inside Out*; host/teacher of the "Science of Happiness" course and podcast

## Core Contribution

Keltner's research reframed **awe** from a rare mountaintop experience into an **everyday, frequent, and health-relevant emotion**. His daily-diary work found people feel awe two to three times per week, most often from "moral beauty" (others' kindness, courage, skill) and ordinary nature, music, and design — the *extraordinary in the ordinary*. His lab documented awe's "small self" effect and links to humility, generosity, lower inflammation, sharper reasoning, and expanded time perception. → [[Awe (Everyday Wonder)]]

His thesis, stated plainly: **happiness comes substantially down to finding awe** — making it directly relevant to "how to fall in love with your life."
