# Ice Cream for Bears

St. Louis–based "farm-to-cone" ice cream maker founded by **Timothy Berg** (a former NYC tennis trainer who developed the concept while doing an MBA at Washington University in St. Louis) (Source: [[Feast Magazine — Ice Cream for Bears]]). The name has a double meaning: it's sweetened with **honey**, and every ingredient is something **"a bear would naturally consume"** — an ancestral, whole-food framing of dessert.

## The signature 3-ingredient base
Their entire philosophy reduces to a clean-label custard:
- **Grass-fed / grass-grazed organic milk** (from regenerative farms)
- **Raw, unfiltered honey** (the only sweetener — no refined sugar)
- **Organic free-range egg yolks** (the natural emulsifier — no gums or stabilizers)

(Source: [[Ice Cream for Bears (official site)]]; Source: [[Feast Magazine — Ice Cream for Bears]])

Berg on honey: *"It's a product that we've eaten in that exact form for at least 8,000 years."* Add-ins are kept free of processed sugar; chocolate flavors use **Honeymoon Chocolates** (a local maker who also connected him to his honey producer).

## Flavors (17+)
Honey Honey, Queen & Bean (vanilla), Bearista (coffee + cacao nib), Mint to Bee, Chocturnal (triple chocolate), ButterBear (butterscotch caramel), BlueBeary Stained Paws (wild Maine blueberries), Strawbeary, Beary Cherry Chip, Basic Bear (pumpkin spice). Sold in pints at local grocers and by the scoop at Honeymoon Chocolates' Clayton location.

## To replicate it at home
Their base is a classic **French-style honey custard** — see [[Honey-Sweetened Custard Ice Cream]] for the method and ratios.
