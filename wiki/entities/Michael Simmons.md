# Michael Simmons

Entrepreneur and writer; founder of Empact; coined the **5-Hour Rule** and the **Modern Polymath** framework. Writes at *Accelerated Intelligence* on Medium.

## Profile

- **Role**: Entrepreneur, author, founder of Empact
- **Known for**: The 5-Hour Rule (1 hr/day deliberate learning); defining the modern polymath as "competent in at least three diverse domains integrated into a top 1% skill set"
- **Key works**: "The 5-Hour Rule" (Medium, viral); "Modern Polymath" (Medium, Accelerated Intelligence)

## Core Frameworks

**Modern Polymath Definition**: Someone who becomes competent in at least three diverse domains and integrates them into a **top 1-percent skill set**. Not about being the world's best at one thing — about combining three "top 25%" competencies that nobody else holds together.

**The 5-Hour Rule**: Dedicating at least 5 hours per week (1 hr/day × 5 days) to deliberate learning. Observed as a consistent pattern across Bill Gates, Warren Buffett, Elon Musk, and Oprah Winfrey.

**Research citation**: Analysis of 26 million scientific papers found that the most impactful work came from teams combining "atypical" disciplinary backgrounds — empirical support for the combinatorial advantage of polymath thinking.

## Connection to Joe's Work

Simmons's 1%+1%+1% model (three top-25% competencies combining into a unique 1% package) is the practical career framework for a young polymath. His 5-Hour Rule is directly actionable as a daily learning habit.
