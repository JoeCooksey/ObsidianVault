# Mortimer Adler

American philosopher and educator (1902–2001). Best known for **_How to Read a Book_** (1940, rev. 1972, with Charles Van Doren) and the essay **_How to Mark a Book_** (1940).

## Significance

- Defined the **four levels of reading** — Elementary, Inspectional, Analytical, Syntopical — already used in [[Reading Retention Methods]].
- His essay [[How to Mark a Book - Mortimer Adler]] is the foundational case for **active reading through marginalia**: "marking up a book is not an act of mutilation but of love."
- Supplied the standard **marking toolkit** (underline, margin line, star, numbered points, page cross-references, marginal notes) that begins every book-note workflow.
- Chaired the editorial board of the *Encyclopædia Britannica* and led the *Great Books of the Western World* project.
