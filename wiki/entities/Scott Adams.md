# Scott Adams

Creator of the *Dilbert* comic strip (1989–present); entrepreneur; author of *How to Fail at Almost Everything and Still Win Big* (2013). Originated the **Talent Stack** concept.

## Profile

- **Role**: Cartoonist, author, entrepreneur
- **Known for**: *Dilbert* (syndicates in 65+ countries, ~$75M net worth); the Talent Stack framework
- **Key work**: *How to Fail at Almost Everything and Still Win Big* (2013) — argues that skill combinations beat world-class mastery in a single domain

## The Talent Stack Insight

Adams describes his own Dilbert success as the product of a **talent stack**, not genius:
- Average drawing ability (top 25%, not world-class)
- Average joke-writing ability (top 25%)
- Business knowledge
- Strong work ethic
- Risk tolerance
- Humor sense

"The magic is that few people can draw well AND write jokes." The combination of two ordinary-to-good skills created a niche occupied by almost no one else.

## Core Principle

Capitalism rewards things that are both *rare* and *valuable*. You make yourself rare by combining two or more "pretty goods" until no one else holds your specific mix. Top 25% in any skill is achievable with focused practice. Top 1% in a combination of three such skills is uniquely yours.

## Connection to Joe's Work

The Talent Stack model provides the career strategy logic for a polymath approach: EE depth + ML knowledge + strong communication = a combination almost no one else holds in the power electronics space.
