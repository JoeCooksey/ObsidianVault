# Oura Ring

A smart **ring** that tracks sleep, heart rate, body temperature, and recovery, producing daily metrics like "readiness." Worn on the finger rather than the wrist, which makes it less obtrusive than wrist wearables for people sensitive to things touching them.

In [[The Sleepmaxxing Protocol — Hypertrophy Academy]], the creator chose Oura over [[Whoop]] specifically because he disliked the idea of a wrist wearable, and recommends checking it only when something feels off — not obsessively reading the daily score, a habit that can create anxiety (see [[Wearable Health Metric Validity]]). Example cited: the ring flagged only 15 minutes of "downtime" during a powerlifting competition, prompting him to rest more.
