# Alan Watts

## Overview
Alan Watts (1915–1973) was a British writer and speaker best known for **popularizing Zen Buddhism, Taoism, and Hindu philosophy** for Western audiences. Through books like *The Wisdom of Insecurity* and *The Book: On the Taboo Against Knowing Who You Are* — and a large body of recorded lectures still widely circulated — he explored the idea that the felt separation between the self and the universe is an illusion.

## Key Contributions / Role
- Cited in **[[Craig Perry]]**'s video as one of the thinkers who "pointed at" the highest, **unitive** level of awareness (level 9 in the [[Nine Levels of Thinking]]) — the egoless state where the boundary between observer and world dissolves.
