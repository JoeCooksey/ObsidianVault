# RPM Fitness

Maker of high-performance speed ropes favored by CrossFit Games athletes.

## Key model
- **RPM Comp 4 ($70)** — *best for pure speed.* Dual-axis rotation eliminates cable torque (stays smooth at high RPM), aggressive full-handle knurling, ships with a travel case. One of the most popular double-under ropes and a CrossFit Games staple. Cons: small 5.5" handles, tiny adjustment screws, not beginner-friendly. (Source: [[BarBend - Best CrossFit Jump Ropes (2026)]])
- The **Session4** and **Comp 4** are repeatedly cited as among the most-recommended ropes for serious double-under training.

## Take
RPM is for athletes chasing speed and competition performance — overkill for beginners, excellent for advanced double-unders.
