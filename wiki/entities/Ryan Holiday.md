# Ryan Holiday

**Born**: June 16, 1987, Sacramento, California
**Known for**: Popularizing modern [[Stoicism]] through *The Obstacle Is the Way* (2014), *[[Ego Is the Enemy - Ryan Holiday|Ego Is the Enemy]]* (2016), and *Stillness Is the Key* (2019); the "Daily Stoic" brand

---

## Background

Holiday dropped out of college at 19 to apprentice under author/strategist Robert Greene ([[The Laws of Human Nature - Robert Greene|The Laws of Human Nature]]), then became director of marketing at American Apparel in his early 20s. His first book, *Trust Me, I'm Lying* (2012), exposed media manipulation tactics he had used as a marketer.

He has since become the best-known popularizer of practical Stoicism, translating Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, and Epictetus into actionable guidance for ambition, adversity, and self-management. His books are favorites among athletes, executives, and military leaders.

---

## Core Themes

- **Ego as the enemy** — humility, self-awareness, and devotion to the work over recognition ([[The Canvas Strategy]], [[Alive Time vs Dead Time]], [[The Effort Is Enough]])
- **Obstacles as fuel** — adversity is raw material for growth (amor fati)
- **Stillness** — clarity and tranquility ([[Euthymia]]) as the foundation of good judgment
