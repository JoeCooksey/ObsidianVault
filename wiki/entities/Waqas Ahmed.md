# Waqas Ahmed

Author of *The Polymath: Unlocking the Power of Human Versatility* (2019), the most comprehensive modern academic treatment of polymathic development.

## Profile

- **Role**: Author, entrepreneur, advisor
- **Known for**: Coining the contemporary intellectual framework for polymathy; arguing against hyper-specialization as a societal pathology
- **Key work**: *The Polymath* (2019, Wiley) — argues that every human has polymathic potential and that modern educational/professional structures suppress it

## Core Claims

- Hyper-specialization is a historical aberration, not a natural human state
- The six core mind skills ([[Individuality]], [[Curiosity]], [[Intelligence]], [[Versatility]], [[Creativity]], [[Unity]]) underpin all polymathic development
- Portfolio careers and sequential careers are structurally superior to single-track careers in volatile economies
- Automation will eliminate specialist jobs faster than polymathic roles

## Connection to Joe's Work

Ahmed's six-mind-skills framework is a useful audit checklist for anyone pursuing a polymath path. His career model taxonomy (portfolio vs. sequential vs. polymathic professions) is directly applicable to career planning.
