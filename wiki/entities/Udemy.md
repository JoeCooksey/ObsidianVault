# Udemy

Open-marketplace online course platform (NASDAQ: UDMY). Anyone can publish a course, which is the source of both its breadth and its variance in quality.

## Scale (2026-07)

- **250,000+** courses in the full marketplace
- **28,000+** courses curated into the [[Udemy Personal Plan]]
- 9,000+ instructors in the curated collection
- Claims 17,000+ companies as customers of its business tier

## Three ways to buy

| Route | Price | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Individual course | $19.99–$199.99 | **Lifetime**, 30-day refund |
| [[Udemy Personal Plan]] | $35/mo list | While subscribed, **no refunds** |
| Udemy Business | enterprise | org-managed |

## Why the marketplace model matters for an EE

Because instructors self-publish, depth is driven by who happened to show up. That produces a lopsided catalog: **3,651 Python courses versus 16 LTspice courses**. Udemy is excellent where the hobbyist-and-bootcamp crowd is large (Python, embedded, PCB) and thin where the audience is small and academic (SPICE, device physics, WBG semiconductors) (Source: [[Udemy Catalog Audit — EE Topics, Premium Badge Method (July 2026)]]).

**Not accredited.** Its certificates carry no academic credit and are not a credential for engineering (Source: [[MOOC Certificate Credential Value for Engineering Hiring]]).
