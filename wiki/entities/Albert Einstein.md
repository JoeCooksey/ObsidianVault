# Albert Einstein

## Overview
Albert Einstein (1879–1955) was a German-born theoretical physicist, author of the **special and general theories of relativity** and a 1921 Nobel laureate (for the photoelectric effect). One of the most influential scientists in history, his name is also attached — sometimes apocryphally — to many popular aphorisms about thinking and creativity.

## Key Contributions / Role
- Quoted in **[[Craig Perry]]**'s video for the line: *"We cannot solve our problems with the same thinking we used when we created them."* Perry uses it to support the [[Nine Levels of Thinking|levels-of-thinking]] claim that you must rise to a higher level of awareness to solve a problem than the one that produced it.
