# Terence McKenna

## Overview
Terence McKenna (1946–2000) was an American ethnobotanist, author, and lecturer known for his explorations of **psychedelics, consciousness, and altered states**. A charismatic speaker, he wrote *Food of the Gods* and *The Archaic Revival* and developed idiosyncratic ideas about consciousness and the boundary-dissolving nature of certain experiences.

## Key Contributions / Role
- Cited in **[[Craig Perry]]**'s video alongside [[Alan Watts]] as someone who **described** the highest, **unitive** level of awareness (level 9 in the [[Nine Levels of Thinking]]) — the egoless state in which the sense of being a separate self dissolves.
