# Infineon Technologies

**Infineon Technologies AG** (Munich, Germany) is a leading European power-semiconductor manufacturer and one of the largest suppliers of Si, SiC, and GaN power devices and modules.

## Relevance to this vault

- **Leads [[Moore4Power]]** — the €91M EU flagship [[Heterogeneous Integration (Power Electronics)|heterogeneous integration]] project (62 partners, 15 countries) launched May 2026 (Source: [[Moore4Power Launch — Semiconductor Today]]).
- Major player across all three power materials tracked here: [[Silicon Carbide Power Electronics|SiC]], [[Gallium Nitride Power Electronics|GaN]], and Si — positioning it to commercialize co-packaged multi-material modules.
