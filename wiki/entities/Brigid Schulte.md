# Brigid Schulte

Journalist and author of *Overwhelmed: Work, Love, and Play When No One Has the Time* (2014). She **coined the term "[[Time Confetti]]"** to describe leisure shredded into tiny, scattered, low-quality fragments — the idea later studied and popularized by [[Ashley Whillans]].
