### US-Iran Interim Peace Deal — Strait of Hormuz to Reopen

Key terms: a 60-day ceasefire extension, reopening the Strait of Hormuz to shipping, lifting the US naval blockade of Iranian ports, and end of related hostilities in Lebanon. Formal signing is scheduled for June 19 in Switzerland. A potential $300B investment fund for Iran is under negotiation as part of follow-on talks.

**Who is affected:** Global oil markets (Strait carries ~20% of world oil), Iran's economically isolated population, Hezbollah-connected parties in Lebanon, and US allies including Israel (which has said it will not withdraw from Lebanon under the deal).

## Science & Health

### GLP-1 Drug Users Significantly Less Physically Active — First Large Wearable Study (ENDO 2026)

**What happened:** Research presented at ENDO 2026 (Endocrine Society annual meeting, Chicago) found that people taking GLP-1 receptor agonist medications — Ozempic (semaglutide), Wegovy, Mounjaro (tirzepatide), and Zepbound — became significantly less physically active after starting treatment. The study, the first to use large-scale wearable fitness tracker data, documented decreased daily step count and reduced moderate-to-vigorous physical activity (MVPA) duration. Effect was largest in males and people with musculoskeletal pain.

**Who is affected:** An estimated tens of millions of GLP-1 users globally; healthcare providers prescribing these medications; insurers and employers covering GLP-1 costs (some of whom are now reducing coverage); Eli Lilly and Novo Nordisk shareholders.

**What's contested:** Whether the activity reduction is directly drug-caused (appetite suppression reducing energy available for exercise) or explained by confounders (patients losing weight may feel less pain, then less motivated to move). The study design (observational, not randomized) limits causal conclusions.

**What's still unknown:** Long-term effects on lean muscle mass and metabolic health; whether structured exercise prescriptions alongside GLP-1 treatment reverse the activity decline; optimal combination protocols.

**Coverage spread:** Healthline, ScienceDaily, NPR — Center to Center-Left outlets were primary reporters. Not prominently covered in right-leaning outlets in today's results. BLINDSPOT: Conservative media has covered GLP-1 costs and employer coverage skepticism more, but today's specific research finding was underrepresented there.
