## Science & Health

**Eli Lilly Retatrutide Phase 3: 28.3% Weight Loss in 80 Weeks — Most Powerful Obesity Drug Demonstrated**

**What happened:** Eli Lilly announced Phase 3 trial results for retatrutide on May 21, 2026: patients on the highest dose lost a mean of 28.3% of body weight (70.3 lbs) over 80 weeks, compared with 2.2% for placebo. Roughly 45% of the 2,500 patients achieved ≥30% weight loss. Retatrutide is a triple-acting GIP/GLP-1/glucagon receptor agonist, making it mechanistically distinct from Ozempic and Wegovy (GLP-1 only) and Zepbound/Mounjaro (GIP+GLP-1).

**Who is affected:** An estimated 800 million people globally have chronic kidney disease (a comorbidity targeted by GLP-1 class drugs); obesity affects ~1 billion adults worldwide; Novo Nordisk faces new competitive pressure from a potentially class-leading drug.

**What's contested:** Safety profile — 20.9% of patients on the highest dose experienced dysesthesia (abnormal skin sensation), though this improved from earlier trials. Long-term cardiovascular and kidney outcome data have not been published.

**What's still unknown:** FDA filing timeline for retatrutide; pricing and payer coverage; whether the triple-agonism mechanism produces additional off-target effects not yet apparent in the 80-week window.

**Coverage spread:** NPR, CNBC, Healthline, BioPharma Dive all covered the data factually. No significant political framing divergence — coverage was unusually consistent across outlets. No BLINDSPOT.
