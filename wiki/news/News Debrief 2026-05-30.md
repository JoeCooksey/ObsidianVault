**What happened:** U.S. and Iranian negotiators reached a tentative 60-day memorandum of understanding (MOU) to extend their ceasefire and begin negotiations on Iran's nuclear program, according to Axios and PBS NewsHour reporting from May 28. **Who is affected:** Global shipping through the Strait of Hormuz (20% of world oil supply transits the strait); U.S. and Iranian military personnel; regional countries (Gulf states, Israel, Iraq); global energy markets.

**What's contested:** Whether a 60-day MOU meaningfully constrains Iran's nuclear program. Critics question how Iran's commitment not to "pursue a nuclear weapon" would be verified, and whether the highly enriched uranium (HEU) disposal mechanism would be enforceable. The MOU framework stops short of a full treaty.

**What's still unknown:** Trump's final decision and timeline; the specific verification mechanism for HEU disposal; whether Congress would need to ratify any agreement; Iran's domestic political reaction.

**Coverage spread:** PBS and Fortune cover the deal's terms factually. Axios provided the detailed scoop on contents. Left-leaning outlets emphasize diplomatic progress and economic relief. Right-leaning outlets question verification mechanisms and Iranian compliance. Center outlets (AP, Reuters) note Trump has not signed.
