## US

**Story: Supreme Court Voting Rights Act Ruling Triggers Redistricting Across Multiple States**

**What happened:** The Supreme Court's 6-3 ruling in *Louisiana v. Callais* effectively nullified Section 2 of the Voting Rights Act, removing federal requirements for states to draw electoral maps that give racial minority voters the opportunity to elect their preferred candidates.

**Who is affected:** Black, Hispanic, and other racial minority voters in states such as Alabama, Louisiana, and Tennessee; the Congressional Black Caucus estimates up to 19 Black lawmakers could lose their seats as states revert to or redraw single-majority-minority districts.

**What's contested:** Whether the ruling constitutes a narrowing of the VRA or a substantive elimination of its protections. Advocacy organizations (Campaign Legal Center, Common Cause) characterize it as eviscerating the law; the majority opinion's reasoning has not been widely summarized in right-leaning media.

**What's still unknown:** How many states will redraw maps and on what timeline; whether the John Lewis Voting Rights Act can advance in the Senate; the full downstream effect on state legislative and local office races.

**Coverage spread:** NPR, Stateline, Time, US News, Common Cause, Campaign Legal Center covering in detail. WSJ and Fox News coverage not surfaced in cross-coverage search. **BLINDSPOT: Right-leaning major outlets (WSJ, Fox, NY Post) absent from prominent coverage.**
