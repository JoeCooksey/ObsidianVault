# Research: Function Health Test Prep and Priority Biomarkers

## Overview
Joe has a Function Health draw in **~6 days** (asked 2026-06-09) and wants the most important biomarkers to watch + how to prep. This synthesis is **personalized against his last panel (2025-12-29: 120 markers, 93 in-range, 13 out-of-range)** — so it focuses on the markers that were actually flagged for him, and the prep steps that matter most given those flags.

## Bottom Line
**Prep is unusually important for Joe specifically** because his last panel flagged **liver enzymes (ALT/AST)** and several **hormone-adjacent** markers — exactly the markers that intense training and a non-fasted/afternoon draw corrupt. Do a clean draw (fasted, morning, **48–72 h rest from hard training**, no alcohol, biotin held) so he can tell a real signal from a workout artifact. His one unchangeable finding — **high Lp(a)** — is the reason to keep every other cardiovascular lever tight.

## Joe's Out-of-Range Markers Last Time (what to watch)

### 🫀 Cardiovascular — the main story
- **Lp(a) = 152 nmol/L** (ref <75) — **~2× high. Genetic, lifestyle-resistant, measured once is enough.** This is the headline: it permanently elevates CVD risk, so ApoB/LDL/inflammation must stay tight for life. Won't change on a re-draw — confirm it, then stop re-testing it. (Source: [[Most Important Biomarkers (Priority Shortlist)]])
- **Atherogenic lipid pattern:** LDL particle number 1296 (ref <1138), LDL-small 210 (<142), LDL-medium 291 (<215), LDL peak size 218.1 Å (smaller = worse), HDL-large 4876 (low), total chol 173 (<170), LDL-C 100 (<100). Borderline-high cluster of small dense LDL + low large HDL. **This is the actionable lever** — the diet/fiber/omega-3 work moves it.
- **Omega-3 Total 6.3%** (optimal >8%) with Omega-6 39.3% — a low omega-3 index is both a CVD marker and a fixable input feeding the lipid picture.

### 🫁 Liver — prep-sensitive
- **ALT = 69 U/L** (ref 8–46) and **AST = 39** (ref 12–32) — mildly elevated. In a lean 19-yo athlete the **#1 benign cause is intense exercise before the draw** — muscle leaks ALT/AST (Source: [[Exercise Effects on Blood Test Results]]). **The test of whether this is real: rest 48–72 h from lifting, then re-draw.** If still high when rested → look at fatty liver, alcohol, or supplements (his ~25-supplement stack is a candidate). Don't conclude "liver problem" from an unrested draw.

### 🦋 Thyroid / hormones — minor
- **Free T4 = 1.5 ng/dL** (ref 0.8–1.4) — slightly high, isolated; recheck with TSH/Free T3 in context (biotin can falsely raise it — **hold biotin 72 h**).
- LH 1.6 (low-normal), FSH 3.1, Prolactin 6.3, E2 25 — all in/near range; worth a clean morning-fasted-rested read to trust them.
- **Leptin 1.0 ng/mL (low)** — expected for a very lean athlete, not a concern.
- **Biological Age 19.1 vs 18.9** — essentially at chronological age. Fine.

## Key Findings (prep)
1. **Fast 8–12 h** (water only; 10–12 h since he has a full lipid/particle panel). (Source: [[Function Health Prep Guidance]])
2. **No intense exercise 48–72 h before** — the single most important step *for him* given the ALT/AST flags and his training. (Source: [[Exercise Effects on Blood Test Results]])
3. **No alcohol 48–72 h** — protects triglycerides + liver enzymes (GGT/ALT/AST).
4. **Hold biotin 72 h** — it's in multis/B-complex and falsely shifts thyroid + hormone markers (relevant to his Free T4 flag).
5. **Morning draw, 7–10 AM, rested, fasted** — testosterone, LH, cortisol are diurnal and exercise-sensitive.
6. **Hydrate well** the day before and morning of; **keep prescription meds**; **skip seafood 48 h** before (he has a heavy-metals panel).
7. **Priority markers to read first:** ApoB + LDL particle number, Lp(a) (confirm), hs-CRP, HbA1c + fasting insulin, ALT/AST (rested), Tg:HDL ratio, Test/Free-T/SHBG/LH, Vitamin D, ferritin, omega-3 index. (Source: [[Most Important Biomarkers (Priority Shortlist)]])

## Contradictions
- **Function's own guidance (8 h fast, no alcohol/exercise mention) vs best practice.** Function is minimalist; the broader clinical literature is firmer on **no alcohol and no intense exercise 48–72 h**. For Joe's flagged markers, follow the stricter version — Function's silence is not "it doesn't matter."
- **Black coffee:** some sources allow it while fasting; safest is water-only to avoid nudging glucose/cortisol.
