# Research: Joe's Supplement Stack Audit

## Overview

Joe runs a ~25-supplement stack across four timing blocks (fasted, after breakfast, with coffee, before bed). The stack is well-built overall — the bedtime sleep stack and the after-breakfast fat-soluble block are correctly placed. There is **one clear timing error** (Bacopa taken fasted), a couple of minor optimizations, one notable gap (zinc), and several low-priority items that could be trimmed. The biggest meta-point: the foundational 5–6 supplements do ~90% of the work; the rest are 5% gains on top.

---

## The One Real Problem: Bacopa Taken Fasted

**Move Bacopa Monnieri from the fasted block to the after-breakfast block.** (confidence: **high**)

- Bacopa is **fat-soluble** and requires a lipid carrier to absorb. Taken fasted, absorption drops and GI side effects (nausea, cramping, loose stool) spike — these are its most common side effects. (Source: Bacopa bioavailability review 2024, PMC12158153; Examine.com)
- This already matches the existing [[Bacopa Monnieri]] page: "Take with food (fat-soluble; absorption improved with dietary fat)."
- **Fix:** take it after breakfast alongside the omega-3 / D3 / turmeric block, where the dietary fat is.

## Ranking (Evidence × Value for a 19-y-o Male)

**S — Keep, highest evidence:**
- **Creatine** — physical + cognitive-under-load benefit; cheap, safe (Source: Univ. of Utah Health 2025)
- **Omega-3 fish oil** — cardiovascular + brain membrane; correctly with food
- **Vitamin D3 (+K2)** — most common deficiency; correctly with food
- **Magnesium glycinate** — sleep + broad role; correctly at night
- **Caffeine + L-theanine** (your coffee + theanine) — best acute study tool

**A — Solid, keep:**
- **L-theanine**, **Glycine** (sleep + GlyNAC), **Apigenin** (sleep), **Vitamin K2**, **Electrolytes**, **Vitamin C**, **Alpha-GPC** (good 2025 RCT in healthy young men; Source: PMC11644786), **L-tyrosine** (acute stress/focus)

**B — Decent / situational:**
- **NAC** (+ glycine = GlyNAC glutathione support), **TMG** (homocysteine/methylation), **Taurine**, **Rhodiola** (deadline-stress adaptogen), **Bacopa** (semester-long retention only), **Turmeric** (needs black pepper + fat), **Boron**, **Inulin fiber** (prebiotic), **Aged garlic extract** (modest CVD benefit)

**C — Lowest priority / optional to drop:**
- **Astaxanthin** (fine antioxidant, low marginal value), **Lion's Mane** (thin human evidence — Tier 4 in [[Supplement Priority Stack for Young Males]])

## Timing Verdict by Block

| Block | Verdict |
|-------|---------|
| **Fasted** (NAC, glycine, rhodiola, taurine, TMG, electrolytes, creatine, vit C) | ✅ Good — **except remove Bacopa** (see above). Creatine is marginally better with carbs but fasted is fine. |
| **After breakfast** (turmeric, D3+K2, omega-3, astaxanthin, garlic, boron) | ✅ Correct. All fat-soluble → need the meal's fat. **Add black pepper/piperine** with turmeric (up to ~20× curcumin absorption). |
| **With coffee** (lion's mane, inulin, L-theanine, alpha-GPC, L-tyrosine) | ✅ Mostly good. **L-tyrosine and L-theanine compete with protein for absorption** — fine with *black* coffee, but if you add milk/protein, move tyrosine to the fasted block. Alpha-GPC daytime = correct (avoid evening). |
| **Before bed** (mag glycinate, glycine, NAC, apigenin, L-theanine) | ✅ Excellent. Mag + glycine + apigenin + theanine hit four independent sleep mechanisms with no tolerance risk. (Source: sleep-stack reviews 2025) |

## Add / Remove

**Consider adding:**
- **Zinc** (10–15 mg) — the main gap; supports testosterone, immunity, recovery. Take **with food, away from the morning** (competes with magnesium/copper; don't co-dose with your night magnesium). Get bloodwork first — don't mega-dose. (confidence: medium; Source: Univ. of Utah Health 2025)

**Consider removing / demoting (to cut clutter, not because harmful):**
- **Lion's Mane** — insufficient human evidence in healthy young adults
- **Astaxanthin** — low marginal value once omega-3 + D3 are in place
- **Aged garlic** — CVD benefit is modest at 19 with no risk factors

**Redundancy check (all fine, no action needed):**
- L-theanine 2×/day (coffee + bed), glycine 2×/day, NAC 2×/day — all intentional and well-tolerated. Glycine+NAC at both ends = GlyNAC-style glutathione support.

## Key Findings

- Bacopa fasted is the only outright timing error — fat-soluble, causes nausea and poor uptake. (Source: PMC12158153)
- The fat-soluble after-breakfast block (D3/K2, omega-3, turmeric, astaxanthin) is placed correctly; up to 32% better D absorption with dietary fat. (Source: body-science/cooper reviews 2025)
- Alpha-GPC has fresh 2025 RCT support for acute cognition in healthy young men; daytime/pre-study dosing is right, evening dosing can disrupt sleep. (Source: PMC11644786)
- L-tyrosine/L-theanine absorption is reduced 30–40% by protein in the same window — keep them with black coffee, not a protein shake. (Source: Life Extension; Mind Lab Pro)
- GlyNAC (glycine + NAC) is a legitimate glutathione strategy, but its strongest RCT evidence is in *older* adults with low baseline glutathione — modest expected upside for a healthy 19-y-o. (Source: Frontiers in Aging 2022)
- Zinc is the clearest missing piece for a young male. (Source: Univ. of Utah Health 2025)

## Contradictions

- Marketing sources frame Bacopa as flexible-timing; clinical/bioavailability sources are clear it must be taken with dietary fat. The clinical view wins. (Source: PMC12158153)

## Open Questions

- Joe's actual blood levels (vitamin D 25-OH, ferritin, zinc) — targeted correction beats blind dosing. A panel would confirm whether D dose is right and whether zinc is needed.
- Total methyl-donor load (TMG + glycine + diet): generally self-balancing via BHMT feedback, but unverified for this specific combination.
- Whether 25 supplements is worth the cost/adherence vs. trimming to the S/A tier — not researched, depends on Joe's budget and goals.
