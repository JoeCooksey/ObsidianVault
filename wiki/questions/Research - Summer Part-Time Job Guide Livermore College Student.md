# Summer Part-Time Job Guide — Livermore / Tri-Valley College Student

Full guide for a college student living in Livermore, CA seeking summer part-time work. Covers where to search, best local employers, job types by pay, resume strategy, timeline, and application tactics.

---

## 8 Key Findings

1. **The Livermore market is large but competitive** — 111,000+ active job postings as of May 2026 (Indeed). California minimum wage is now $16.50/hr, so even baseline retail/service jobs pay better than most states. Teen/young adult unemployment near 21% in CA — early applications (April–May) dramatically improve odds vs. waiting until June.

2. **The Tri-Valley Career Center is the #1 underused local resource** — Free, located at 5860 Owens Dr, Pleasanton (5 min from Livermore). Offers free job board, one-on-one career coaching until you land a job, workshops, resume review, and employer connections. Run by EastBay Works. Walk in or call 925-416-5100. Most students don't know it exists.

3. **LARPD is the best local entry-level employer** — Livermore Area Recreation and Park District (larpd.org) hires Park Aides at **$23.42/hr** — higher than most retail. Summer positions: Recreation Leader I/II ($16.54–$19.20/hr), Park Aide ($23.42/hr), Youth Services ($22.08–$26.84/hr). Only accepts online applications. Apply NOW — positions open spring for summer start.

4. **Handshake is the best platform for college students** — Partners with 1,400+ universities including ASU, 750,000+ employers, personalized recommendations by major. Free with .edu email. Use ASU login at handshake.com. Separate from general-public boards (Indeed/ZipRecruiter) because employers on Handshake are specifically seeking students.

5. **Four distinct job categories to consider** — (1) Local in-person (retail, food, recreation), (2) Skilled local (tutoring, camp counselor, research aide), (3) Remote/flexible (online tutoring, virtual assistant, social media), (4) Government/institution (LARPD, City of Livermore, LVJUSD). Each has different pay, competition level, and resume value.

6. **Resume with no experience is fully viable** — Replace work history with: class projects, extracurriculars, volunteer work, any freelance/side work, and relevant skills. Education section is your #1 asset — list major, GPA (if ≥3.0), relevant coursework, and any honors. One-page only. Tailor keywords to each specific job description.

7. **Best highest-paying no-experience options** — Tutoring ($25–50/hr, flexible, highest hourly), LARPD Park Aide ($23.42/hr, best local employer), warehouse work at Amazon/UPS ($18–22/hr, physically demanding, fast hiring), retail at Marshalls (~$28/hr avg Livermore), restaurant/cafe (tips push $18–25/hr effective).

8. **Remote options are real and accessible** — Online tutoring (Wyzant, Tutor.com, Varsity Tutors) requires only subject mastery. Virtual assistant work ($20+/hr) for small businesses. Social media management ($300–800/month per client). Data entry/transcription ($15–20/hr). These require no commute and flexible hours.

## Where to Search — Tier List

### S-Tier Platforms
- **Handshake** (handshake.com) — student-specific; use ASU login; best for internship-adjacent jobs and on-campus-style roles
- **Tri-Valley Career Center Job Board** (jobs.trivalleycareercenter.org) — local employers actively posting; free resume support
- **Indeed** (indeed.com) — largest aggregator; filter by Livermore, CA and "part-time" + "no experience"; set email alerts
- **Walk-in / in-person applications** — for retail, food service, and local businesses; show up with a printed resume; many managers hire on the spot or within 1–2 days

### A-Tier Platforms
- **LinkedIn** (linkedin.com/jobs) — filter "Entry Level" + "Part-time" + "Livermore, CA"; also signal you're open to work with a headline update
- **ZipRecruiter** (ziprecruiter.com) — strong for local and remote entry-level; allows one-click apply
- **SimplyHired** (simplyhired.com) — good aggregator, often surfaces niche local jobs
- **Glassdoor** (glassdoor.com) — 7,182 part-time Livermore jobs as of March 2026; also shows salary ranges and reviews

### B-Tier Platforms
- **College Recruiter** (collegerecruiter.com) — millions of postings; specializes in students and recent grads
- **CollegeGrad** (collegegrad.com) — entry-level and internship focus since 1995
- **ZipRecruiter California College page** (ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/College-Students-Summer/--in-California)
- **CoolWorks** (coolworks.com) — for outdoor/seasonal positions nationwide; camps, resorts, parks
- **FlexJobs** (flexjobs.com) — vetted remote jobs; $14.95/month fee; worth it for zero-scam remote search

### C-Tier (Specific Use Cases)
- **EDJOIN** (edjoin.org) — school district jobs; LVJUSD positions here
- **GovernmentJobs** (governmentjobs.com/careers/livermoreca) — City of Livermore openings
- **Jooble** (jooble.org) — international aggregator, ok for overflow search
- **Upwork / Fiverr** — freelance gig work; better for skilled remote work (writing, design, coding)

## Resume Guide for First Job

### Structure (no prior work experience)
```
[Name | Phone | Email | City, CA | LinkedIn]

EDUCATION
Arizona State University — BS Electrical Engineering (Expected 2029)
GPA: X.X | Relevant coursework: [list 3–5 relevant courses]

SKILLS
Technical: Python, LTSpice, Git, MATLAB, Microsoft Office
Soft: Customer service, problem-solving, team collaboration

PROJECTS / ACTIVITIES
[Any class project, personal project, GitHub repo, club]
[Frame as bullet with result: "Built X that achieved Y"]

VOLUNTEER / ACTIVITIES
[Any volunteer, sports, church, community involvement]
[Even 1 item here shows character and commitment]
```

### Key Principles
- **One page, always** — no exceptions for college students
- **Tailor keywords** — read the job posting, echo their language exactly
- **Results, not duties** — "Reduced X by Y%" > "Responsible for X"
- **GPA if ≥ 3.0** — include it; if below, omit
- **Include your city** — Livermore, CA signals you're local (reduces employer concern about commute)
- **Use free tools** — Canva resume templates (free), Google Docs, or Zety/Novoresume for formatting

### Resume Review Resources
- Tri-Valley Career Center (free, in-person): 5860 Owens Dr, Pleasanton
- ASU Career Services (free, online via MyASU)
- Resume.io / Novoresume (online feedback tools)

## Interview Tips

- **Research the company first** — 5 minutes on their website; know what they sell / do
- **Dress one level above the role** — retail job = clean casual to business casual; not a suit
- **Arrive 10 min early** — showing up late disqualifies you instantly
- **Prepare 3 answers** using STAR format (Situation → Task → Action → Result) for questions like "Tell me about a time you solved a problem" — use school or volunteer examples if no job history
- **End with a question** — "What does a successful first month look like here?" shows initiative
- **Follow up within 24h** — simple thank-you email; most candidates don't; you immediately stand out
