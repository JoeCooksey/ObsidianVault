# Research: Video Game Tier List to Increase IQ

**Question:** What is the video game tier list to increase IQ?

**Answer (one line):** The full ranked S–F tier list already lives in **[[Video Games Cognitive Benefits]]** — this page is the *IQ-specific* lens on it, updated with 2022–2025 evidence. Short version: **no game reliably raises a true IQ score, but strategically deep games (chess, MOBAs, RTS, complex puzzles) train the reasoning skills IQ tests measure, while reflex and gacha/idle games do little or net harm.**

## Overview
"Increase IQ" is a stricter claim than "good for your brain." IQ measures general fluid reasoning (*g*), and the science says that is **hard to move with training**. The honest tier list ranks games by how much their core demand overlaps with fluid reasoning and how good the evidence is — not by a literal promise of IQ points.

## Key Findings
1. **Gaming as a whole tracks real IQ gains in kids — about +2.5 points.** The Karolinska/ABCD study (5,000+ children, 2 years, controlled for genetics + SES) found heavy gamers gained ~2.5 IQ points more than average; TV and social media showed no effect. But it lumped all genres together. (Source: [[Klingberg-Sauce 2022 — Gaming and Children IQ]], high confidence)
2. **Genre matters: MOBAs and strategy games align with the IQ construct.** League of Legends skill correlates with lab-measured fluid intelligence, and MOBA skill peaks at ~age 25 — the same curve as raw IQ — while twitch FPS games peak in the teens like reaction time. (Source: [[York 2017 — MOBA Skill and Fluid Intelligence]], medium confidence)
3. **In a head-to-head training study, action beat strategy for durable gains.** A 2025 30-week EEG study on college students (mean age 19.8) found League of Legends produced *greater and longer-lasting* spatial-attention and working-memory gains than a turn-based strategy card game. (Source: [[Brain Sciences 2025 — Action vs Strategy Game EEG Study]], medium confidence)
4. **Dedicated "brain-training" apps mostly DON'T raise general intelligence.** Meta-analyses show near transfer (you get better at the trained task) but null far transfer to fluid intelligence once active control groups are used. Lumosity settled FTC false-advertising claims. (Source: [[Cognitive Training — A Field in Search of a Phenomenon]], high confidence)
5. **Entertainment games can out-train brain-trainers.** Portal 2 beat Lumosity on problem-solving and spatial skill — deep, varied engagement transfers better than repetitive drills. (Source: [[Video Games Cognitive Benefits]])
6. **Variety beats any single game.** Benefits plateau once a game becomes automatic; rotating strategy + puzzle + action exercises different systems.
7. **The floor: gacha and idle/clicker games are net-negative** — they impair inhibitory control and reinforce probability blindness rather than training reasoning.

## The IQ Tier List (condensed)
Full table with platforms and evidence: **[[Video Games Cognitive Benefits]]**.

- **S — strongest reasoning + evidence:** Chess, StarCraft II (RTS cognitive flexibility), Portal 2 (beat Lumosity), Factorio (systems thinking), The Talos Principle.
- **A — high benefit:** Civilization, Baba Is You, The Witness, Into the Breach, Kerbal Space Program; **MOBAs (League of Legends / Dota 2)** — promoted on 2017+2025 evidence tracking fluid intelligence and durable spatial-WM gains.
- **B — solid:** Action FPS (CS2/Valorant — perception + attention), XCOM 2, Tetris, Mini Metro, Hearthstone, Sudoku, Monument Valley; brain-trainers (BrainHQ best-validated, but near-transfer only).
- **C–D — mostly entertainment / minimal:** narrative RPGs, Minecraft, Candy Crush, endless runners.
- **F — neutral to harmful:** gacha games, idle/clicker, slot-machine mobile games.

## Contradictions
- **Strategy vs action for transfer.** Older framing (and [[York 2017 — MOBA Skill and Fluid Intelligence]]) emphasizes deliberate strategy/reasoning; [[Brain Sciences 2025 — Action vs Strategy Game EEG Study]] found the fast action MOBA produced *larger, longer-lasting* spatial gains than a turn-based strategy game. Resolution: action games strongly train attention/spatial/working-memory; strategy/puzzle games train deliberate reasoning/planning. They hit different facets of *g* — so the real recommendation is to play across types, and MOBAs (which blend real-time action with strategy) score well on both.
- **"Brain training raises IQ" vs meta-analytic null.** Marketing and a few narrow 2025 gamified-training studies claim far transfer; the weight of meta-analytic evidence ([[Cognitive Training — A Field in Search of a Phenomenon]]) says no general-IQ transfer. The skeptics are more credible (better controls, larger pooled samples).

## Open Questions
1. Do the kid IQ gains in [[Klingberg-Sauce 2022 — Gaming and Children IQ]] hold for adults, whose fluid intelligence is less plastic?
2. What's the minimum effective "dose" (hours/week) for measurable gains, and where does opportunity cost overtake benefit?
3. Does the 2025 action-game advantage replicate, or is it an artifact of one title (LoL) vs one strategy game?
4. Do MOBA fluid-intelligence correlations reflect training, selection (smarter people play them), or both?
