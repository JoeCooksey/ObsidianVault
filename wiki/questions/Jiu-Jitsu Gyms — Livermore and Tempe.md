# Jiu-Jitsu Gyms — Livermore and Tempe

Shortlist of top Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu (BJJ) academies in Joe's two home bases: **Livermore, CA** (home) and **Tempe, AZ** (ASU). BJJ surfaced as a top **for-fun, non-instrumental hobby** pick — social, physical, competitive, and deep — the kind of play missing from an otherwise optimization-heavy routine. Ties into [[Martial Arts for Young Men]].

**General playbook:** Almost every gym offers a **free trial week** — take it at each before committing. Pick by how the room *feels* and whether people roll with a beginner safely, not by the website. Typical pricing: **$150–$250/mo** unlimited, **$20–$40** drop-in (Bay Area runs at or above the high end).
