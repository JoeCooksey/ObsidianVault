# Research: Long-Term Compounding Daily Projects

## Overview

Joe asked for **options of long-term projects he can work on a little every day that compound and be worth something in the future**. The answer decomposes into three questions: *what* compounds (leverage type), *how* it compounds (compound time + deliberate practice), and *how to keep doing it* (streak design). The concrete menu, ranked for Joe's WBG-power-electronics → LLNL/Sandia trajectory, is in [[Compounding Daily Projects Tier List]].

## Key Findings

1. **Not all daily work compounds — leverage type decides.** Labor and capital are *permissioned*; **code and media are permissionless** and replicate at zero marginal cost. The highest-compounding daily projects are therefore a code repo and a body of writing/media. (Source: [[The Almanack of Naval Ravikant]] → [[Permissionless Leverage]])
2. **The best daily project is skill practice + public artifact + career evidence at once.** Stack the three and one hour pays three times. A build-in-public GitHub log does all three. (Source: [[Building in Public]])
3. **Linear daily input → compounding output.** 15 min/day = ~312 focused hours over 5 years — more deliberate practice than most professionals get after their degree. (Source: [[Compound Time — Michael Simmons]])
4. **One merged PR > five solo clone projects.** Real open-source contribution proves you can read an unfamiliar codebase, take feedback, and ship — the day-one job skills. Quality over quantity; solve a real problem; every repo needs a README. (Source: [[Building in Public]])
5. **Investing is the one *literal* compounder.** Automated index-fund DCA needs ~zero daily effort and runs while he sleeps: $200/mo from 19 → ~$1.87M at 65. (Source: [[Index Fund Investing]])
6. **Writing is permissionless media leverage with triple payoff** — clarifies thinking, builds reputation, and is career capital that makes specialization legible to employers. (Source: [[Building in Public]], [[Permissionless Leverage]])
7. **Streaks die from too-high daily bars, not low ambition.** A 5–15 min floor, flexible output by energy, identity-over-perfection framing, and killed start-up friction keep a multi-year project alive. (Source: [[Sustainable Daily Practice (Streak Design)]])
8. **Recommended stack: two compounders + the automated one** — GitHub engineering log + a learn-in-public blog fed by this vault + automated index-fund DCA. Covers all three leverage types in ~30–45 min/day, each surviving a 5-minute floor on bad days.

## Deep-Work variant (follow-up)

Joe pushed back: the S/A-tier above are deliberately *low* daily floor (one commit, automated DCA) and don't require full focus. The companion answer ranks projects on the opposite axis — **difficulty is the point** — in [[Deep-Work Compounding Projects Tier List]], spined on Newport's [[Deep Work]] (*High-Quality Work = Time × Intensity of Focus*; the new economy rewards mastering hard things fast + producing at an elite level; concentration is itself a trainable, compounding skill).

- **Deep S-tier for Joe**: (1) build a real **power converter end-to-end** (design→SPICE→PCB→firmware→closed-loop control→bench) — the single highest-fit deep project; (2) build a **RISC-V CPU on an FPGA** from scratch; (3) **reproduce a paper / apply to ASU FURI** undergrad research.
- **Deep A-tier**: build something hard from scratch (own SPICE sim, neural net à la Karpathy, FFT lib), work a hard textbook (Erickson power electronics) every problem, or competitive practice to a ranked result (Codeforces/Kaggle).
- **Reconciliation**: deep projects break the 5-minute-floor model — sustain them with a protected 60–120 min daily distraction-free block (rhythmic philosophy) instead. The repo/blog from the first list are the *container*; deep projects are what *fills* it. Do the deep work, then make it visible.

## Contradictions

- **"Passive income" vs reality.** Get-rich-quick "passive" plays (dropshipping, crypto bots) market themselves as compounding but are negative-EV with no career capital — D-tier in [[Online Income Methods Tier List]]. The genuinely compounding "passive" asset is automated index investing, whose passivity comes *after* a one-time setup, not from a business model.
- **Quantity vs quality of projects.** Some sources push high-volume project counts / streak numbers; 2026 hiring sources say three meaningful projects and one real merged PR beat twelve clones. Resolved in favor of quality — the hiring-manager view is closer to the decision Joe actually faces.
