# Research - Habits That Raise and Lower hs-CRP

> Attribution: findings below are drawn from articles retrieved from **PubMed**; each carries its DOI link.

## Overview

**[[hs-CRP (High-Sensitivity C-Reactive Protein)|hs-CRP]]** is a modifiable marker of chronic low-grade inflammation and a Tier-1 cardiometabolic biomarker. It responds to everyday habits within **weeks to months**. The single biggest lever is **body fat** — most habits move hs-CRP partly by changing adiposity — but **exercise and diet quality also lower it independently of weight**. Below, habits are split into those that **lower** and those that **raise** hs-CRP, ranked roughly by strength of evidence and effect size.

## ⬇️ Habits that LOWER hs-CRP

| Habit | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Regular exercise** | 83-trial meta: CRP ES 0.26; works with fat loss (ES 0.38) *and* without it (ES 0.19) ([[Fedewa 2016 — Exercise Training and CRP Meta-Analysis|Fedewa 2016]]) | **high** |
| **Losing excess body fat / visceral fat** | Fat loss is the largest single mediator of CRP change (Fedewa 2016) — but short-term dieting alone underperforms (see contradictions) | **high** |
| **Mediterranean / anti-inflammatory diet pattern** | 22-RCT meta: Mediterranean lowered CRP ~1.0 mg/L, best of all patterns ([[Koelman 2022 — Dietary Patterns and Inflammation RCT Meta-Analysis|Koelman 2022]]); low [[Dietary Inflammatory Index|DII]] tracks lower CRP ([[Bastos 2024 — Dietary Inflammatory Index and Systemic Inflammation|Bastos 2024]]) | **high** |
| **Omega-3 (fatty fish / fish oil)** | Umbrella of 32 metas: CRP ES −0.40 ([[Kavyani 2022 — Omega-3 and Inflammatory Biomarkers Umbrella Meta|Kavyani 2022]]) | **high** |
| **Fiber-rich, whole-food eating** | Low-DII foods (fiber, fruit, veg, whole grains, polyphenols) lower inflammation ([[Dietary Inflammatory Index]]; [[Fiber Gut Microbiome and SCFA Research]]) | **medium–high** |
| **Coffee (esp. filtered/ground)** | 11-study meta (n=66,691): linear inverse coffee↔CRP, −0.14 at ~4.5 cups/day ([[Choi and Je 2024 — Coffee Consumption and CRP Meta-Analysis|Choi & Je 2024]]) | **medium** |
| **Adequate, consistent sleep (~7–8 h)** | The healthy middle of the U-curve; poor and short/long sleep both raise CRP ([[Irwin 2016 — Sleep Disturbance and Inflammation Meta-Analysis|Irwin 2016]]) | **high** |
| **Quitting smoking** | Markers normalize toward baseline over ~5 yrs after cessation ([[Bakhru 2005 — Smoking Cessation and Inflammatory Markers (NHANES III)|Bakhru 2005]]) | **high** |
| **Stress reduction (meditation)** | 45-RCT meta vs active controls: meditation lowered CRP, cortisol, TNF-α ([[Pascoe 2017 — Mindfulness Meditation and Stress Markers Meta-Analysis|Pascoe 2017]]) | **medium** |
| **Breaking up sitting / staying active** | Reduces the inflammatory cost of sedentary time ([[Breaking Up Sedentary Time]]) | **medium** |

## ⬆️ Habits that RAISE hs-CRP

| Habit | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Excess body fat (esp. visceral)** | The dominant driver; adipose tissue secretes IL-6 → liver CRP. Losing it is the biggest lever (Fedewa 2016) | **high** |
| **Smoking** | Dose-dependent rise in CRP with smoking intensity ([[Bakhru 2005 — Smoking Cessation and Inflammatory Markers (NHANES III)|Bakhru 2005]]) | **high** |
| **Poor sleep / short or very long sleep** | Sleep disturbance ↑CRP (ES 0.12); U-shaped by duration ([[Irwin 2016 — Sleep Disturbance and Inflammation Meta-Analysis|Irwin 2016]]) | **high** |
| **Pro-inflammatory diet (high-DII, ultra-processed, refined carbs, processed/red meat, trans fats)** | High DII tracks higher CRP ([[Bastos 2024 — Dietary Inflammatory Index and Systemic Inflammation|Bastos 2024]]; [[Dietary Inflammatory Index]]) | **medium–high** |
| **Heavy / daily alcohol** | Dose-response ↑hs-CRP; daily OR 1.38, excessive OR 1.57, worse in flushers (OR 2.34) ([[Xu 2016 — Alcohol Use and hs-CRP (Guangzhou Biobank)|Xu 2016]]) | **medium** |
| **Chronic psychological stress** | The inverse of the meditation finding — sustained stress elevates inflammatory markers ([[Pascoe 2017 — Mindfulness Meditation and Stress Markers Meta-Analysis|Pascoe 2017]]) | **medium** |
| **Sedentary lifestyle** | Independent cardiometabolic/inflammatory risk beyond a single workout ([[Breaking Up Sedentary Time]]) | **medium** |
| **Added sugar / sugar-sweetened beverages** | Associated with pro-inflammatory plasma proteins and T2D risk; direct CRP link is mixed (see open questions) | **low–medium** |

## Key Findings

- **Adiposity is the master variable.** Most habits move hs-CRP partly through body fat; exercise and diet pattern add independent effects on top (Source: [[Fedewa 2016 — Exercise Training and CRP Meta-Analysis]]).
- **Pattern beats single foods.** The Mediterranean/low-DII *pattern* has the strongest evidence; isolating ultra-processed food didn't add signal beyond overall pattern (Sources: [[Koelman 2022 — Dietary Patterns and Inflammation RCT Meta-Analysis]], [[Bastos 2024 — Dietary Inflammatory Index and Systemic Inflammation]]).
- **Sleep is U-shaped.** Both too little and too much, plus poor quality, raise CRP — but one bad night doesn't (Source: [[Irwin 2016 — Sleep Disturbance and Inflammation Meta-Analysis]]).
- **Coffee is anti-inflammatory, not pro-** — contrary to common intuition, more coffee tracks *lower* CRP (Source: [[Choi and Je 2024 — Coffee Consumption and CRP Meta-Analysis]]).
- **Smoking's inflammation is reversible** but slowly — ~5 years to normalize (Source: [[Bakhru 2005 — Smoking Cessation and Inflammatory Markers (NHANES III)]]).

## Key Concepts
- [[hs-CRP (High-Sensitivity C-Reactive Protein)]]: the biomarker, its ranges, and why it's modifiable.
- [[Dietary Inflammatory Index]]: scoring a whole diet from anti- to pro-inflammatory.

## Contradictions
- **Weight-loss dieting vs. CRP.** Fedewa 2016 ties fat loss to CRP reduction, but the BMJ network meta of 14 named diets found **no diet significantly improved CRP at 6 months** despite real weight/BP gains, and benefits faded by 12 months except for the Mediterranean diet ([[Ge 2020 BMJ — Named Diets and Cardiovascular Risk Factors|Ge 2020]]). Reconciliation: *sustained* fat loss and *dietary pattern quality* matter more than short-term calorie cutting.
- **Alcohol shape.** Xu 2016 shows heavy/daily drinking raises hs-CRP, but some studies report a J-shape where *light* drinking is flat or slightly lower. The safe read: heavy and daily drinking clearly raise it.

## Open Questions
- Does **added sugar / SSB** raise hs-CRP directly? One large cohort (Ramne 2020, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/nu12103129)) found SSB/added sugar associate with pro-inflammatory proteins and T2D risk but **not** with CRP specifically — the direct CRP link is unsettled.
- What is the **minimum effective dose** of exercise and of sitting-breaks for CRP? ([[Breaking Up Sedentary Time]] flags this gap.)
- How much of each habit's effect is **independent of body fat** vs. mediated by it? Only partially quantified.
- Few long-term RCTs use **hs-CRP with hard cardiovascular endpoints** as the primary outcome; most data are shorter trials or cross-sectional cohorts.
