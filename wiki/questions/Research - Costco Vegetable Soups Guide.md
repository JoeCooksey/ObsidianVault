# Research: Costco Vegetable Soups Guide

**Query**: How to make various vegetable soups using only Costco vegetables and spices.

## Key Findings

### 1. Costco's Fresh Vegetable Core for Soup Is Exceptional Value
Carrots (6 lb ~$3.50), potatoes (10 lb ~$5), and onions (<$1/lb) form the mirepoix base for virtually every vegetable soup. Costco bulk pricing on these three items alone can fund 10+ pots of soup at <$2/serving. Bell peppers (6-count), celery, garlic (bulk), fresh tomatoes (Roma/Campari), and Chef's Blend mushrooms round out the fresh roster.

### 2. Kirkland Frozen Stir-Fry Blend Is a Complete Quick-Soup Ingredient
The 5.5-lb Kirkland Stir-Fry Special Blend (broccoli, snap peas, green beans, carrots, yellow carrots, mushrooms, red bell peppers, water chestnuts, onions) contains 9 vegetables pre-cut and frozen at peak ripeness. Drop it directly into hot broth for a 15-minute complete vegetable soup. Nutritional value is equal to or slightly better than fresh (frozen at peak).

### 3. Kirkland Organic Chicken Stock (6-pack ~$8.99) Is the Broth Backbone
At ~$1.50/carton, this is among the best-value quality broths available. Contains organic chicken + carrots, celery, onion. No standalone Kirkland vegetable broth confirmed — for vegan soups, substitute water + Kirkland No-Salt Seasoning, or buy third-party vegetable broth in-store.

### 4. Two Spice Purchases Cover 80% of All Soup Seasoning
- **Kirkland Organic No-Salt Seasoning** (21 organic spices, $9.99/14.5 oz) replaces 8+ individual spice bottles and works as a finishing seasoning on every soup.
- **Kirkland Ground Turmeric** (2×12 oz, ~$19.99) covers curry-style, lentil, and anti-inflammatory applications.
- Supporting: granulated garlic, paprika, and standard pantry spices (thyme, bay leaf, cumin, oregano) available in warehouse.

### 5. Seven Distinct Soups Are Buildable From This Pantry
See [[Costco Vegetable Soup Recipes]] for full instructions:
1. **Classic Vegetable Soup** — 40 min, onions/carrots/potatoes/frozen blend/broth
2. **Roasted Tomato Basil** — 50 min, fresh tomatoes roasted at 450°F then blended
3. **French Onion Soup** — 75 min (45 min caramelizing), 5–6 onions + broth
4. **Minestrone** — 45 min, mirepoix + diced tomatoes + beans + pasta
5. **Curried Lentil** — 40 min, turmeric/curry powder + red lentils + coconut milk
6. **Quick Frozen-Veg** — 15 min, broth + frozen blend + garlic
7. **Mushroom Garlic** — 35 min, Chef's Blend mushrooms seared then simmered

### 6. Technique Matters More Than Ingredients
The three most impactful techniques that separate flat from excellent:
- **Bloom spices in oil** before adding liquid (1–2 min over medium heat)
- **Sear mushrooms without stirring** — moisture must evaporate before browning
- **Caramelize onions low and slow** (40+ min) for French onion — rushing destroys the dish

### 7. Roasting Is the Most Effective Flavor Amplifier
Roasting fresh tomatoes at 450°F before blending concentrates sugars, removes excess water, and adds caramelized depth that stovetop tomato soup cannot replicate. The same principle applies to any vegetable-forward soup — roasting root vegetables before simmering produces noticeably better results.

### 8. Soups Scale and Freeze Exceptionally Well
Every soup listed produces 6–8 servings from one pot. Freeze in quart-size containers for up to 3 months (all soups except pasta-containing minestrone freeze well — freeze pasta separately). Actual cost per serving with Costco ingredients: $0.75–$1.50 for a full bowl.
