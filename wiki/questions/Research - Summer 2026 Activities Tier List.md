## Key Findings

### Finding 1 — Cold Email Quality Is the FURI Variable
The single biggest factor in getting undergraduate research is whether the professor knows you before application. Cold emails that reference a specific paper and ask a specific intellectual question get 3–5× higher response rates. Generic "I want to join your lab" emails are almost never answered. The campaign must start now (May) to be operational by October FURI deadline.

### Finding 2 — The Execution Gap Is Joe's Real Constraint
Across multiple prior research sessions, the vault has consistently identified execution (not knowledge) as Joe's bottleneck. The summer tier list is designed around high-output artifacts (working PCB, opened Roth IRA, FURI application submitted) rather than passive consumption (courses watched, books read). Research without artifact = comfort-seeking.

### Finding 3 — Roth IRA Is the Highest ROI-Per-Hour Action
2026 contribution limit confirmed at $7,500. Fidelity FZROX (0.00% expense ratio) is the recommended fund. The 2-hour setup cost vs. $195,000+ additional compounding tail (vs. opening at 25) makes this the highest ROI/hr action in the tier list by a large margin. Opened with any earned income including part-time jobs.

### Finding 4 — Summer = Strength Phase (Per Existing Plan)
Per [[Joe's Custom Seasonal Training Plan]]: Summer is the confirmed strength phase. Upper/Lower 4-5 days/week, 3-5 rep range, 85-90% 1RM, maintenance calories, 2 Zone 2 cycling days. This is already defined; the Summer Strength Program page simply operationalizes it with exact exercises, sets, and progressive overload rules.

### Finding 5 — MIT 18.06 Fits Perfectly in 87 Days
35 lectures × 45 min average = 26 hrs total lecture time + problem sets. At 3 lectures/week, done in 12 weeks. Summer 2026 = exactly the right window before Fall MAT 343. The 3Blue1Brown Essence of Linear Algebra series (3 hrs) is the required pre-course visual foundation.

### Finding 6 — Hardware Artifact Is the FURI Differentiator
No freshman EE applicant who has not done research will have: a working synchronous buck converter PCB, efficiency curve, Bode plot, and step-response measurement. The [[Buck Converter Build Guide]] (already in vault) provides the full 12-week schedule. This artifact single-handedly lifts the FURI application from "potential" to "demonstrated capability."

### Finding 7 — Roblox Game Dev Is B-Tier for Joe's Context
Roblox Studio went fully agentic April 2026. Beginners can ship a working game in 4-6 weeks. Realistic summer earnings: $50-300 total. Worth doing as one project among many, but ROI vs. FURI/18.06/buck converter is much lower for Joe's specific EE/research career track. Clicker Idle Empire remains the recommended first game (lowest complexity, fastest to ship, teaches full monetization pipeline).

### Finding 8 — DoD SMART Opens August 1
Critical deadline: DoD SMART (full tuition + $30-46k/year stipend) opens August 1. Application requires personal statement, 3 LoRs, GPA. Summer is the perfect time to draft the personal statement before the application opens. Do not miss this window — the next opening is August 1 and closes in December.

### Finding 9 — The Execution First Principle
Multiple sources confirm: depth over breadth for summer activities. A single long-term project with a concrete artifact (working hardware, opened account, submitted application) is far stronger than a scattered list of courses completed. Joe's recommended stack is 4-5 activities max, all tied to artifacts.
