# Research - Health Hacks Leveled 1-100 by Cost & Barrier to Entry

## Overview

A gamified ladder of 100 health interventions ordered by **cost + barrier to entry**, not by impact. Level 1 is free, instant, and zero-skill; Level 100 is six-figure, gatekept, and frontier. Barrier here combines four things: **money, effort/access, knowledge required, and medical/risk gatekeeping**.

> [!important] The single most important finding: **impact and cost are nearly inverted.** The cheapest tiers (Levels 1–30) deliver the overwhelming majority of real-world healthspan gains. ~90% of measurable results come from interventions costing under ~$2,000/year (Source: [[Bryan Johnson Immortals DIY — Empirical Health]]). Climb the ladder for diminishing returns, better data, and edge optimization — not for the bulk of the benefit.

---

## Key Findings

- **Exercise alone reduces cardiovascular mortality 30–40%; statins reduce heart-attack risk 25–35%** — both sit low on the cost ladder (Source: [[Bryan Johnson Immortals DIY — Empirical Health]]). (high)
- **Tiny lifestyle improvements compound.** ~5 extra minutes of sleep/day associated with ~1 extra year of life; minimal combined upgrades to sleep + activity + nutrition measurably extend both lifespan and healthspan (Source: [[Minimum Lifestyle for Lifespan — Lancet eClinicalMedicine 2025]]). (high)
- **The "sweet spot" is ~$2,000/year:** lifestyle (free) + comprehensive bloodwork ($190–399/yr) + core supplements ($660–1,080/yr) + a wearable (~$300–500 once) captures ~90% of the value of a $1M/yr program (Source: [[Bryan Johnson Immortals DIY — Empirical Health]]). (high)
- **Longevity-clinic tier costs $8k–$150k/year** out of pocket and is largely not insurance-covered; marginal benefit over the DIY sweet spot is small and mostly diagnostic (Source: [[Longevity Clinic Costs — Fountain Life]]). (high)
- **Cost per healthy year of life** for the cheapest public-health-grade interventions (behavioral, preventive) is under $100/QALY; frontier longevity therapies are effectively unmeasured on this scale. (medium)

## The Ladder

### Tier I — Free & Instant (Levels 1–10)
*Cost: $0. Barrier: just doing it.*

1. **Hydrate first thing** — a glass of water on waking.
2. **Morning sunlight, 5–10 min** — anchors circadian rhythm (Source: [[Minimum Lifestyle for Lifespan — Lancet eClinicalMedicine 2025]]).
3. **Walk daily** — accumulate steps; biggest free lever on all-cause mortality.
4. **Consistent sleep/wake time** — same schedule 7 days/week.
5. **Nasal breathing + box breathing** — downregulate stress on demand.
6. **Break up sitting** — stand/move every 30 min.
7. **Stop eating ~3h before bed** — entry-level time-restricted eating.
8. **Eat one extra serving of vegetables/fiber** from whole food.
9. **Dim lights / screens-down at night** — protect melatonin.
10. **Social connection** — call a friend; loneliness is a mortality risk factor.

### Tier II — Trivial Cost (Levels 11–20)
*Cost: under ~$50 one-time or pennies/day.*

11. **Cut sugary drinks** — swap soda/juice for water.
12. **Reduce alcohol** — fewer drinks, more alcohol-free days.
13. **Reusable water bottle** — makes hydration a default.
14. **Bodyweight strength** — squats, push-ups, planks at home.
15. **Resistance bands** (~$15–30).
16. **Blackout the bedroom** — sleep mask, earplugs, cheap curtains.
17. **Step counter** — phone app or ~$20 pedometer.
18. **Cook at home** — default to whole foods.
19. **Hit a protein target each meal** (Source: [[Protein Per Meal Target]]).
20. **Cold-shower finish** — free hormetic stressor.

### Tier III — Low Monthly Cost (Levels 21–30)
*Cost: ~$10–60/month. Barrier: buying and remembering to take things.*

21. **Creatine monohydrate** (~$0.10/day) — best evidence-per-dollar supplement (Source: [[Supplement Tier List Complete]]).
22. **Vitamin D3 (+K2)** — DO-HEALTH trial slowed aging markers at 2000 IU/day (Source: [[Foundational Health Supplements]]).
23. **Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)** — cardiovascular + cognitive support.
24. **Magnesium glycinate** — sleep, muscle, nervous system.
25. **Electrolytes** — especially if active or low-carb.
26. **Psyllium / fiber supplement** — fills the near-universal fiber gap.
27. **Basic multivitamin** — cheap insurance against deficiency.
28. **Protein powder** (whey/plant) — hits protein target affordably.
29. **Caffeine timing** — cut off ~8h before bed.
30. **Free HRV / sleep app** — first quantification with hardware you own.

### Tier IV — Gym & Equipment Commitment (Levels 31–40)
*Cost: ~$30–150/month or modest one-time gear. Barrier: time, consistency, technique.*

31. **Gym membership + structured strength program** — 3×/week progressive overload.
32. **Zone 2 cardio program** + chest-strap HR monitor (Source: [[Biohacking Tier List]]).
33. **Adjustable dumbbells / home rack basics.**
34. **Standing desk or under-desk treadmill.**
35. **HEPA air purifier** for the bedroom — air quality as a passive lever.
36. **Mattress / pillow upgrade** — sleep surface quality.
37. **Sauna access** via gym/spa — heat exposure lowers CV risk and all-cause mortality.
38. **Mobility / foam-rolling routine.**
39. **Food scale + meal prep** — measurement enables nutrition control.
40. **Annual physical + basic bloodwork** through a primary-care doctor.

### Tier V — Self-Quantification (Levels 41–50)
*Cost: ~$200–1,500/year. Barrier: money + learning to act on data.*

41. **Fitness watch** (Garmin / Apple Watch, ~$300–500).
42. **Oura Ring** (~$349 + $70/yr) — sleep/HRV/readiness.
43. **Whoop** subscription — recovery/strain.
44. **Smart scale** — trend body composition.
45. **Home blood-pressure cuff** — track the #1 silent killer.
46. **DEXA scan** (~$40–150) — gold-standard body composition.
47. **VO2max lab test** (~$150–200) — fitness's strongest mortality predictor.
48. **Comprehensive private blood panel** (~$200–400/yr) — e.g. Function Health, 100+ biomarkers (Source: [[Bryan Johnson Immortals DIY — Empirical Health]]).
49. **Recurring at-home blood-test subscription.**
50. **Continuous Glucose Monitor** trial — Levels (~$125/yr membership + sensors), $40–90/sensor.

### Tier VI — Premium Home Setup & Advanced Tests (Levels 51–60)
*Cost: ~$2,000–15,000 one-time, or several hundred/test. Barrier: capital + space.*

51. **Home infrared sauna** (~$2,000–5,000).
52. **Cold plunge tub** (~$5,000–15,000; premium $14k+).
53. **Red-light therapy panel** (photobiomodulation).
54. **Full home-gym build-out** — rack, barbell, plates.
55. **Whole-home air/water filtration.**
56. **Epigenetic / biological-age test** (~$300–500/yr).
57. **Microbiome testing** (Source: [[Research - Gut Optimization Masterclass]]).
58. **Advanced nutrient / micronutrient panels.**
59. **Personal trainer** (ongoing).
60. **Registered dietitian / nutrition coach.**

### Tier VII — Medical-Adjacent Optimization (Levels 61–70)
*Cost: $500–5,000/year. Barrier: requires a prescriber and medical oversight.*

61. **Preventive cardiology workup** — ApoB, Lp(a), advanced lipids.
62. **Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) scan** — direct plaque measurement.
63. **Full-body MRI screening** — Prenuvo (~$2,500).
64. **GLP-1 medications** (semaglutide / tirzepatide) for metabolic health/weight.
65. **Hormone panel + optimization consult.**
66. **TRT / hormone replacement** — medically supervised.
67. **Thyroid optimization.**
68. **Sleep study + CPAP** if apnea is present.
69. **Functional-medicine physician** membership.
70. **Concierge / direct-primary-care** membership.

### Tier VIII — Longevity-Clinic Tier (Levels 71–80)
*Cost: $8,000–50,000/year. Barrier: wealth + clinic access (Source: [[Longevity Clinic Costs — Fountain Life]]).*

71. **Longevity-clinic membership** — Fountain Life / Human Longevity (executive screening ~$8k).
72. **Executive health screening** — MRI + DEXA + VO2max + full bloods bundled.
73. **Peptide therapy** (e.g. BPC-157).
74. **NAD+ infusions / IV therapy.**
75. **Rapamycin** — off-label longevity protocol.
76. **Hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT).**
77. **Multi-cancer early detection** — Galleri-type blood test.
78. **Whole-genome sequencing + interpretation.**
79. **Quarterly full diagnostic panels.**
80. **Compounded personalized supplements.**

### Tier IX — Cutting-Edge Interventions (Levels 81–90)
*Cost: $5,000–100,000/year. Barrier: experimental, thin evidence, real risk.*

81. **Cryotherapy chamber** (home or membership).
82. **Stem-cell therapy** (~$5,000–25,000).
83. **Exosome therapy.**
84. **Therapeutic plasma exchange** (plasmapheresis).
85. **Senolytics protocols** — clearing senescent cells.
86. **Gene therapy** — e.g. follistatin (~$25,000).
87. **Full Blueprint stack at home** — Bryan Johnson's complete daily protocol.
88. **Dedicated longevity-physician team.**
89. **Residential longevity program** abroad (~$15k–50k for 1–2 weeks).
90. **Advanced mitochondrial / NAD IV protocols.**

### Tier X — Frontier / Ultra-High Barrier (Levels 91–100)
*Cost: $100,000–$1,000,000+/year. Barrier: only the ultra-wealthy; mostly unproven.*

91. **Annual advanced imaging suite** — MRI + CT + ultrasound + full bloods.
92. **Bryan Johnson "Immortals" program** (~$1M/yr, 30-person medical team) (Source: [[Bryan Johnson Immortals DIY — Empirical Health]]).
93. **Private longevity-research partnership.**
94. **24/7 personal medical + performance team.**
95. **Experimental clinical-trial access** for longevity drugs.
96. **Custom multi-vector gene therapy.**
97. **Whole-blood / young-plasma replacement protocols.**
98. **Cryopreservation membership** (e.g. Alcor) — the ultimate moonshot.
99. **Self-funded n=1 biotech lab.**
100. **Bespoke longevity concierge running continuous personal experiments** — the frontier, $1M+/yr, no proven payoff.

## How to Climb (practical guidance)

- **Max out Tiers I–III before spending real money.** They are free-to-cheap and hold most of the gains.
- **Tier IV–V is the highest-leverage paid zone:** strength training, Zone 2, a wearable, and one good annual blood panel. This is the ~$2k/yr sweet spot.
- **Tier VI+ buys data and marginal optimization,** not foundational health. Climb only after lower tiers are habitual.
- **Tier VIII+ is mostly experimental** — treat claims as low confidence and weigh real risk.

## Contradictions

- **Bryan Johnson's $2M/yr vs. the basics.** Johnson spends ~$2M/yr yet states the day-to-day protocol driving most of his results is "very low cost," and a DIY approach reportedly beat him on 27/50 biomarkers at ~1/3000th the cost (Source: [[Bryan Johnson Immortals DIY — Empirical Health]]). The expensive tiers are mostly research/measurement, not intervention. **Verdict: the cheap end wins on cost-effectiveness, decisively.**
- **Cold plunge timing.** Cold exposure aids recovery and resilience but **post-resistance-training cold can blunt strength/hypertrophy gains** — so its placement matters more than its level. (medium)

## Open Questions

> [!gap] Exact dollar-per-healthspan-year for Tiers VII–X is essentially unmeasured — longevity therapies lack the QALY data that public-health interventions have. Treat all frontier claims as low confidence.

- How much marginal healthspan does the longevity-clinic tier actually add over the DIY $2k/yr sweet spot in a head-to-head? No RCT exists.
- Which Tier VIII–IX interventions (rapamycin, senolytics, peptides) will survive rigorous human trials? Unknown.
- Personalization: at what point does CGM/epigenetic data change behavior enough to justify its cost for a healthy person? Unclear.
