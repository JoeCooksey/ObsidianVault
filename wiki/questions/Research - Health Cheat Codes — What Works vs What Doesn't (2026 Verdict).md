# Research: Health Cheat Codes — What Works vs What Doesn't (2026 Verdict)

## Overview

A blunt, evidence-graded split of popular "health cheat codes": the ones with real, replicated science behind them versus the ones that are mostly hype, marketing, or placebo. The honest meta-finding of the whole field: **the highest-ROI cheat codes are free and unglamorous** (sleep, light, movement, food, people). The expensive gadgets and longevity pills are where the evidence gets thin. This page is the verdict layer on top of the broader [[Biohacking Tier List]].

> [!tip] If you only do five: fix your **sleep/wake time**, get **morning sunlight**, **lift + Zone 2 cardio**, eat **mostly whole foods with enough protein and fiber**, and protect **social connection**. These beat every supplement and device combined.

## ✅ Cheat Codes That Actually Work (S–A tier)

| Cheat code                                            | Why it works                                                                                       | Evidence               | Cost     |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- | -------- |
| **Consistent wake time, 7 days/week**                 | Strongest circadian anchor; stabilizes cortisol/melatonin rhythm                                   | High (decades of RCTs) | Free     |
| **7–9 h sleep**                                       | Master variable; nothing compensates for chronic deprivation                                       | High                   | Free     |
| **Morning sunlight, 10–30 min**                       | Resets master clock, improves mood/alertness; ~20–30% serotonin-turnover bump in trials            | High                   | Free     |
| **Resistance training 2–4×/week**                     | Muscle = metabolic reserve; strongest longevity signal alongside VO2 max                           | High                   | Low      |
| **Zone 2 cardio + VO2 max intervals**                 | VO2 max is the single strongest predictor of all-cause mortality                                   | High                   | Free–Low |
| **Daily walking (7–10k steps)**                       | Linear dose-response with all-cause mortality                                                      | High                   | Free     |
| **Whole-food diet + adequate protein (1.6–2.2 g/kg)** | Cutting ultra-processed food beats any supplement on mortality                                     | High                   | Low      |
| **30+ plants/week + fermented foods**                 | Microbiome diversity; Stanford RCT: fermented foods beat fiber alone for diversity                 | High                   | Low      |
| **Sauna 4–7×/week**                                   | 40% lower all-cause mortality, 63% lower sudden cardiac death (dose-responsive)                    | High (large cohort)    | Med      |
| **Meditation / breathwork**                           | 8-week structural brain changes (MBSR); immediate HRV effect from cyclic sighing                   | High / Med             | Free     |
| **Social connection**                                 | After smoking cessation, the strongest longevity predictor known                                   | High                   | Free     |
| **Caffeine + L-theanine**                             | Best-validated acute cognitive stack; theanine smooths the jitter                                  | High                   | Low      |
| **Creatine monohydrate (5 g/day)**                    | Strength/power proven; modest cognitive benefit (SMD ~0.31), bigger for vegetarians/sleep-deprived | High                   | Low      |
| **Omega-3 (EPA/DHA)**                                 | Lowers systemic inflammation; mood/cardiometabolic support                                         | Med–High               | Low      |
| **Magnesium glycinate (300–400 mg)**                  | Sleep onset/depth, nervous-system calm; corrects a common shortfall                                | Med                    | Low      |

Detail on cold exposure (a B-tier "works, but oversold" case): see [[Cold-Water Immersion Meta-Analysis (PLOS One 2025)]] — real for **transient stress relief, mood, and fewer sick days**, but acutely *pro*-inflammatory and it **blunts muscle growth if done right after lifting**.

## ❌ Cheat Codes That Are Mostly Hype (C–D tier)

| Popular "cheat code" | The claim | Reality | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **NMN / NR / NAD+ pills** | Reverse aging, more energy/strength | Raises blood NAD+ but 2025 meta-analysis of 10 RCTs found **no benefit** for muscle, strength, or function; mouse results haven't replicated | Biomarker-only; **skip for longevity** |
| **Microdosing psychedelics** | Mood, creativity, focus | Largest placebo-controlled trial (n=191) and multiple double-blinds: **no benefit beyond placebo** | Placebo |
| **Grounding / earthing mats** | "Earth electrons" reduce inflammation/disease | No established mechanism; no high-quality evidence; benefits attributable to being outdoors | Unproven hype |
| **CGMs for healthy non-diabetics** | Optimize diet via glucose spikes | Little evidence of benefit; **inaccurate in non-diabetics**; risk of "glucorexia" anxiety | Not worth it (unless diabetic/prediabetic) |
| **Whole-body cryotherapy chambers** | Fat loss, superior recovery | No fat-loss evidence; similar-or-worse than a cheap ice bath; $50–100/session | Overpriced |
| **High-dose melatonin (5–10 mg)** | Stronger sleep | Pharmacological overshoot; 0.3–0.5 mg works better as a chronobiotic | Misused |
| **Detox cleanses / juice fasts / colonics** | "Flush toxins" | Liver and kidneys do this; no scientific basis; colonics disrupt microbiome | Pseudoscience |
| **"Brain training" apps (Lumosity)** | Smarter, sharper | No transfer to real-world cognition in RCTs (FTC fined Lumosity) | No transfer |
| **Lettuce water for sleep** | TikTok sedative | Would need a trash-bag's worth of lettuce for any effect | Viral nonsense |
| **Young-blood / plasma transfusions** | Anti-aging | No proven human benefit; FDA warned against it; Ambrosia shut down | Discredited |
| **GH / "longevity" hormone injections (healthy adults)** | Live to 120 | Mayo Clinic: substantial risks, little benefit in healthy adults | Risky, unjustified |

> [!warning] The biggest tell of a fake cheat code: it costs a lot, has a slick device or pill, and the human RCT evidence either doesn't exist or shows placebo-level effects. ~98% of TikTok diet/nutrition videos in one DCU analysis were inaccurate or unsupported.

## Key Findings

1. **Free beats paid.** Every credible review lands here: sleep, sunlight, exercise, whole foods, and social connection are the highest-leverage interventions and cost nothing. (Source: multiple 2025–2026 reviews)
2. **"Raises a biomarker" ≠ "improves health."** NAD+ pills are the cleanest example — NAD+ goes up, outcomes don't. (Source: [[NMN-NR NAD+ Clinical Evidence (2025 Reviews)]])
3. **Cold plunges are real but oversold and mistimed.** Benefits are transient (stress relief ~12h, fewer sick days); the marketed "anti-inflammatory" effect is backwards acutely, and post-lift ice baths blunt hypertrophy. (Source: [[Cold-Water Immersion Meta-Analysis (PLOS One 2025)]])
4. **Heat therapy is underrated vs cold.** Frequent sauna shows a 40% all-cause mortality association — a larger, more consistent signal than cold immersion. (Source: [[Laukkanen Sauna Cohort Studies (KIHD)]])
5. **Placebo is a confound, not a cheat code.** Microdosing's effects vanish under blinding — belief and ritual are doing the work. (Source: Szigeti et al.; Nature 2022)
6. **Tracking only helps if it changes behavior.** Wearables (Oura/WHOOP) are useful feedback loops; CGMs for healthy people mostly aren't, and can fuel anxiety. (Source: The Conversation; JHSPH 2026)

## Contradictions

- **Cold exposure**: enthusiast sources (Ice Barrel, Huberman-adjacent) frame it as broadly anti-inflammatory and transformative; the **PLOS One 2025 meta-analysis** shows acute *pro*-inflammation and only transient wellbeing gains. The meta-analysis is more credible (pooled RCTs > single-site advocacy).
- **CGMs for healthy people**: vendor/wellness sources (Levels, VCU optimistic framing) vs **academic consensus** (JHSPH, The Conversation) that routine use is unsupported and devices are inaccurate in non-diabetics. Academic consensus wins.
- **NAD+ precursors**: supplement-industry pages emphasize "NAD+ rises + insulin-sensitivity signal"; independent **2025 RCT meta-analysis** finds no functional benefit. Independent meta-analysis wins.

---

## Open Questions

- Does **chronic, repeated** cold exposure produce lasting benefits? Current evidence is almost all single-exposure. [needs long-term RCTs]
- Do NAD+ precursors help any **specific population** (prediabetic, very old, NAD-depleted) even if not the general public? The insulin-sensitivity signal hints yes but isn't replicated.
- **Rapamycin / metformin** off-label longevity: promising animal data, human endpoints still pending — parked in the "emerging, not yet cheat code" zone (see [[Biohacking Tier List]] §9).
- Optimal **sauna protocol for non-Finnish populations** and whether infrared matches traditional Finnish heat for the mortality benefit.
