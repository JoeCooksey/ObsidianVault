## Market Context

The semiconductor sector is digesting Broadcom's Q3 AI chip guidance miss ($16B vs. $17.2B analyst estimate), which triggered a broad chip selloff affecting AMD, Intel, and Micron despite no negative company-specific news at those firms. Geopolitically, Iran's threatened closure of the Strait of Hormuz (through which ~20% of world oil supply transits) is the primary macro risk — an actual closure would spike energy costs and tighten industrial supply chains. Alphabet's record $84.75B equity raise signals continued mega-cap confidence in AI infrastructure spending, providing a counterweight to the semiconductor sector's near-term headwinds.
