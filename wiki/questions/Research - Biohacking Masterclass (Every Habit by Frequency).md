# Research: Biohacking Masterclass (Every Habit by Frequency)

## Overview

This is the **master checklist** — every evidence-supported biohacking habit, organized by *what it optimizes*, with the **frequency, dose, and evidence grade** for each. It pulls the whole health cluster of the vault into one place and adds 2026 quantitative backing (step-count dose-response, Attia's four-pillar framework, the cyclic-sighing RCT, social-connection mortality data).

**The one rule that beats everything:** the highest-ROI habits are nearly free and foundational — **sleep, movement, whole food, sunlight, social connection.** No supplement, device, or exotic protocol compensates for missing these. Stack one keystone habit per category onto an existing routine ([[Four Laws of Behavior Change]]); don't try to do all of them at once.

**Evidence key:** 🟢 strong (RCTs / large cohorts / replicated) · 🟡 moderate (good single studies / mechanism + association) · 🔵 emerging or low (mechanistic, small, or hyped).

## 1. 😴 SLEEP — the master variable

> Fix sleep before anything else. 6h vs 8h causes 24% more next-day calorie intake and cognition equal to being legally drunk. See [[Sleep Optimization Supplements]], [[Sleep Hygiene]].

| Habit | Frequency | Dose / detail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fixed wake time** (same time 7 days/wk, incl. weekends) | Daily | The single most important sleep habit; kills "social jet lag" | 🟢 |
| Target 7–9h sleep opportunity | Nightly | Spend ~8.5h in bed to get ~7.5h asleep | 🟢 |
| **Morning sunlight** within 30–60 min of waking | Daily | 5–10 min bright day / 20+ min overcast; anchors circadian clock | 🟢 |
| Dim / warm light 2–3h before bed | Nightly | Lowers melatonin suppression; shifts melatonin onset earlier | 🟢 |
| Cool bedroom (~65–68°F / 18–20°C) | Nightly | Core temp must drop to fall asleep | 🟢 |
| Dark + quiet room (blackout, earplugs/white noise) | Nightly | — | 🟢 |
| **Caffeine cutoff** ~8–10h before bed (≈2 PM) | Daily | 4+ caffeinated drinks/day cut total sleep 11–229 min | 🟢 [[Caffeine Timing Protocol]] |
| Stop eating ~3h before bed | Nightly | Improves sleep quality + glucose | 🟡 |
| No alcohol within 3h of bed | As applicable | Alcohol fragments REM | 🟢 |
| Consistent wind-down routine | Nightly | — | 🟡 |
| Magnesium glycinate PM (optional) | Nightly | 200–400 mg | 🟡 [[Sleep Optimization Supplements]] |
| Track sleep with a wearable | Continuous | Trend RHR/HRV/total sleep, NOT sleep-stage accuracy | 🟡 [[Wearable Health Metric Validity]] |

## 2. 🏋️ EXERCISE & MOVEMENT — the most robust longevity lever

> VO2 max is the strongest single predictor of all-cause mortality (top vs bottom fitness quintile ≈ 5× lower death). The full protocol is the **four pillars**: Zone 2, VO2 max, strength, stability ([[Attia Four-Pillar Exercise Framework]]).

| Habit | Frequency | Dose / detail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Zone 2 cardio** (conversational pace) | 3–4×/wk | 150–240 min/wk total, 45–60 min sessions | 🟢 [[Polarized Training]] |
| **VO2 max intervals** | 1×/wk | e.g. [[Norwegian 4x4 Interval Protocol]] (4×4 min @ 85–95% HRmax) — ~2× the VO2max gain of steady cardio | 🟢 [[VO2 Max Interval Training]] |
| **Resistance training** (major muscle groups, progressive overload) | 2–3×/wk | ≥2×/wk cuts all-cause mortality ~15%; builds the muscle/bone you can't make up later | 🟢 |
| **Stability / mobility** (balance, controlled movement) | 10–20 min/day | Attia's most-skipped pillar; the foundation that prevents falls + injury | 🟡 |
| **Daily steps** | Daily | **7,000–9,000/day** is where mortality risk plateaus (47% lower vs 2,000); <60yo aim 8–10k | 🟢 [[Daily Steps and Mortality Meta-Analysis (Lancet 2025)]] |
| Break up sitting (move every ~30–60 min) | Hourly | Micro-walks blunt glucose; counters sitting harm | 🟡 |
| Rucking / weighted walking (optional) | 1–3×/wk | Strength + cardio in one accessible session | 🟡 |
| Grip-strength work (carries) | 2–3×/wk | Every 5 kg grip decline = 17% higher mortality (Lancet) | 🟡 |
| Post-meal walk (10–15 min) | After meals | Lowers post-prandial glucose spike | 🟡 |

**Weekly target (Attia full version):** ~3–4h Zone 2 + 3–4h strength + 30 min VO2 max + daily stability ≈ 9–11 h/wk. A strong *minimum effective dose*: 150 min Zone 2 + 2 lifts + 1 VO2 session + 8k steps/day.

## 3. 🥗 DIET & NUTRITION — quality beats any supplement

> Eliminating ultra-processed food outperforms any supplement ever studied. The most replicated pattern is **Mediterranean / whole-food, plant-forward** ([[Mediterranean Diet]], [[Food Health Tier List — Overall]], [[Research - Whole Foods Diet vs Supplement-Only Diet]]).

| Habit | Frequency | Dose / detail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Whole-food, minimally-processed base** (cut UPFs + sugary drinks) | Every meal | Single biggest diet lever; SSBs = +7% mortality per daily serving | 🟢 |
| **Protein target** | Daily | 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day for a young training male; ~30–40 g/meal for MPS | 🟢 [[Protein Per Meal Target]] |
| **Fiber** | Daily | 25–35 g/day from diverse plants | 🟢 [[Fiber Supplement Tier List]] |
| **30+ different plants / week** | Weekly | Maximizes microbiome diversity > any probiotic pill | 🟡 [[Gut Health Foods Tier List]] |
| Unsaturated fats over saturated; zero trans fat | Every meal | Swapping sat→unsat fat = 15–27% lower mortality; EVOO 4+ tbsp/wk | 🟢 |
| Fish (esp. small wild: salmon/sardines) for omega-3 | 2–3×/wk | Joe's omega-3 index was low (6.3% vs >8%) — relevant | 🟢 [[Animal Product Sourcing (Grass-Fed, Pasture-Raised, Wild-Caught)]] |
| Limit red/processed meat | Weekly cap | Processed = +20% mortality per daily serving | 🟢 |
| Fermented foods (kefir, kimchi, yogurt) | Daily | Microbiome support | 🟡 [[Milk Kefir Making — Step-by-Step Guide]] |
| **Time-restricted eating** (8–10h window) | Daily (optional) | Metabolic benefit mostly via calorie control + circadian alignment | 🟡 |
| "Hara hachi bu" — eat to ~80% full | Every meal | Mimics mild calorie restriction; weight stability is a top longevity factor | 🟡 |
| Hydration (~half body-weight lbs in oz) | Daily | Cognition, energy | 🟡 |
| Coffee / green tea (polyphenols) | Daily | 3–5 cups coffee → lower T2D/CVD risk; matcha/green tea S-tier | 🟢 [[Caffeinated Drinks Health Tier List]] |
| Cook to preserve nutrients + limit heat toxins | Every cook | Steam/poach > grill/char; marinate meat; "golden not brown" | 🟢 [[Healthiest Cooking Methods — Master Guide]] |

## 5. 🌡️ TEMPERATURE (HEAT & COLD) — hormesis

> Strongest "longevity-style" hormesis evidence is **sauna**. Cold is better supported for mood/resilience/recovery than for lifespan ([[Health Protocol Tier List]]).

| Habit | Frequency | Dose / detail | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sauna** | 4–7×/wk | ~20 min @ ~175–195°F; 4–7×/wk ≈ 40–50% lower CV + all-cause mortality (Finnish cohorts) | 🟢 |
| Huberman heat target | Weekly | ≥57 min total heat/week | 🟡 |
| **Cold exposure** (cold shower / plunge) | 2–5×/wk | 2–5 min @ 50–59°F; ~11 min total/wk; ↑norepinephrine ~200–300%, mood, resilience | 🟡 |
| Contrast therapy (hot → cold) | After sauna | Additive; end on cold for alertness, heat for recovery | 🔵 |

## The Minimum Effective "Everything" Day (for Joe)

1. Wake same time → 10 min sunlight + hydrate
2. Delay caffeine ~90 min
3. Train (lift or Zone 2 or 4×4 per weekly plan) + hit 8k steps
4. Protein-forward whole-food meals, 30+ plants/wk, fish 2–3×/wk
5. 5-min cyclic sighing or 10-min NSDR
6. Sauna 4×/wk (+ cold finish), social connection, 20 min hard reading
7. Caffeine off by ~2 PM, dim lights at night, cool dark room, fixed bedtime

Everything else (red light, peptides, CGM, exotic supplements) is optimization on top of this — worth far less than nailing the seven above.

## Key Findings

- **Frequency matters more than intensity for the foundational habits** — daily sunlight, daily movement, consistent wake time beat any occasional "hack." (Source: [[Daily Steps and Mortality Meta-Analysis (Lancet 2025)]], sleep cohorts)
- **Exercise is four distinct habits, not one** — Zone 2 (3–4×/wk), VO2 max (1×/wk), strength (2–3×/wk), stability (daily). Most people do one and skip the rest. (Source: [[Attia Four-Pillar Exercise Framework]])
- **7,000–9,000 steps/day is the mortality plateau** — chasing 10k+ adds little; getting from 2k→7k is where ~47% of the risk reduction is. (Source: [[Daily Steps and Mortality Meta-Analysis (Lancet 2025)]])
- **Sauna 4–7×/wk has the strongest longevity-association data of any temperature hack** (~40–50% lower CV/all-cause mortality in Finnish cohorts). Cold is for mood/resilience, not proven for lifespan.
- **5 minutes of cyclic sighing/day beat mindfulness meditation** for cortisol + mood in a head-to-head RCT — the highest ROI/min stress habit. (Source: [[Cyclic Sighing Breathwork RCT (Cell Reports 2023)]])
- **Social connection is a top-tier, under-tracked longevity factor** — strong ties ≈ 26% lower premature death, on par with quitting smoking or losing obesity. (Source: [[Social Connection and Mortality (Holt-Lunstad)]])
- **Supplements are gap tools, not the program** — the foundational 5 (D3, omega-3, Mg, creatine, zinc-if-low) cover a young male; everything exotic waits.

## Contradictions

- **"15 cigarettes" loneliness claim** — the famous Holt-Lunstad equivalence is debated; a 2021 UK Biobank analysis found smoking a *stronger* mortality predictor than isolation. The direction (connection protects) is solid; the exact magnitude is oversold. (Source: [[Social Connection and Mortality (Holt-Lunstad)]])
- **Cold exposure & longevity** — Huberman gives precise protocols (11 min cold/wk); Attia is skeptical that cold extends lifespan. Both agree it helps mood/resilience/recovery. File cold as a wellbeing habit, not a proven longevity one.
- **Time-restricted eating** — benefit is real but mostly mediated by reduced calories + circadian alignment, not magic; recent trials show modest weight effects.

## Open Questions

- Optimal *order* to add habits for adherence (this guide implies sleep → movement → diet → the rest).
- Long-term cold-exposure lifespan data (currently only mechanism + short-term mood).
- How much of the "30 plants/week" target is causal vs a marker of an overall good diet.
