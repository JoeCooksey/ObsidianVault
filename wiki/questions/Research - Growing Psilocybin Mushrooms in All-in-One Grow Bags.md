# Research: Growing Psilocybin Mushrooms in All-in-One Grow Bags

## Overview

An **all-in-one (AIO) grow bag** combines grain spawn and bulk substrate in one sealed, pre-sterilized bag, so a complete grow runs from a single sterile injection to harvest **without ever opening the bag** until fruiting. It is the highest-success, lowest-effort method for beginners. The cultivation technique is **identical to gourmet mushroom growing** — only the culture injected differs. Expect roughly **3–7 weeks** from inoculation to first harvest.

> [!warning] Legal note — read first
> Psilocybin is **federally Schedule I** and illegal to cultivate in most U.S. states. Only **Colorado** currently permits personal home cultivation (adults 21+, private 12×12 ft plot); **Oregon** allows only supervised use, not home-grow. Decriminalization in 20+ cities is *not* legalization. **Verify your own jurisdiction before doing anything** → [[Psilocybin Legal Status (US)]]. This guide is educational.

## The full guide (start to finish)

### Phase 0 — What's in the bag

A standard AIO bag holds, bottom to top: a **grain layer** (the nutrient-rich start), a **bulk substrate layer** (CVG — coir/vermiculite/gypsum — or a manure blend, for water and fruiting), a **self-healing injection port**, and a **0.2-micron filter patch** for contamination-free breathing → [[All-in-One Mushroom Grow Bag]].

### Phase 1 — Inoculate (Day 0)

Work in **still air** (fans/AC off, doors closed). Sanitize the surface, glove up, spray gloves with 70% isopropyl, wipe the injection port and syringe with alcohol, and **flame-sterilize the needle** (cool ~10 s). Inject **5–7 cc of spore solution or ~10 cc of liquid culture into the grain layer**. The port reseals itself → [[Sterile Inoculation Technique]].

> Liquid culture colonizes faster and more reliably than a spore syringe.

### Phase 2 — Colonize (Days 3–45)

Store at a stable **72–76°F (22–24°C)** in the **dark**, bag sealed. White mycelium appears in **3–10 days** and the grain layer approaches full colonization in **25–45 days**. Don't open it — the filter patch handles gas exchange. Inspect daily for off-colors/smells → [[Mycelium Colonization Phase]].

### Phase 3 — Break and shake (≈50–75% colonized)

When the grain is **50–75% white**, let the bag inflate via the filter for 5 minutes, then **break the colonized grain into marble-sized pieces from the outside and knead** to distribute mycelium evenly. This **dramatically speeds** the remaining colonization. Re-flatten and return to 72–76°F → [[Break and Shake (Spawn Distribution)]]. **Never shake a bag showing any contamination.**

### Phase 4 — Full colonization (≈1–2 weeks later)

The bag finishes as a **solid white block, top to bottom**. A short consolidation rest after 100% helps. Now it's ready to fruit.

### Phase 5 — Fruit (Days ~30–50+)

Open by cutting a few inches above the substrate and fold the top into a **hood**. Hit the four triggers → [[Mushroom Fruiting Conditions]]:

| Variable | Target |
|---|---|
| Fresh air exchange | Several/day (fold + paper-clip for slow passive FAE) |
| Humidity | ~90–95% (light-mist hood walls **once daily**, don't soak the cake) |
| Light | **12–16 h/day** indirect (5000–6500K) |
| Temperature | **60–75°F** |

Pins appear within days and grow over ~3–7 days.

### Phase 6 — Harvest

Pick when the **veil under the cap just begins to tear** (peak maturity, before spore drop). Twist gently at the base or cut with a sanitized blade. Remove **every** mushroom, including aborts.

### Phase 7 — Second flush (optional)

After harvesting fully, re-mist the hood and close it to rebuild humidity; a second flush usually appears in **1–2 weeks**, typically **≤50%** of the first. AIO bags usually hold enough internal water — a full **dunk** (4–6 h soak in 65–70°F non-chlorinated water, max 12 h, drain 30–60 min) is only needed if the block has visibly dried → [[Multi-Flush Rehydration (Dunking)]].

### Phase 8 — Dry and store

Dry to **"cracker dry"** (~5% moisture, snaps cleanly): **fan-dry first, then finish with food-safe silica gel**, keeping temp **below 95–115°F**. Store **whole**, vacuum-sealed, frozen, and dark → **80–95% potency for 1–2 years**. The four enemies are moisture, heat, oxygen, light → [[Drying and Storing Psilocybin Mushrooms]].

## Key Findings

- **One injection, one opening.** The whole advantage of AIO bags is that the contamination-prone phases (inoculation, colonization) happen sealed; you only open the bag once, to fruit (Source: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]]).
- **Colonization is warm (72–76°F); fruiting is cooler (60–75°F) and needs light + air.** Same block, two different environments (Sources: [[Midwest Grow Kits — AIO Bag Step-by-Step Guide]], [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]]).
- **Break and shake at 50–75%** is the single biggest speed lever, but only on a clean bag (Source: [[North Spore — ShroomTek All-in-One Bag Method]]).
- **Still-air sterile technique at inoculation prevents most failures** — most contamination traces to that step or to over-wet substrate (Source: [[Fungi Academy GroCycle — Mushroom Contamination Guide]]).
- **Green = dead.** *Trichoderma* (green mold) is unsalvageable; isolate and dispose without opening indoors. Cobweb (grey wispy) caught early can be treated with 3% H₂O₂ (Source: [[Fungi Academy GroCycle — Mushroom Contamination Guide]]).
- **Cracker-dry + vacuum + freeze + dark = 1–2 year shelf life at 80–95% potency** (Source: [[Healing Maps — Cracker Dry: Drying and Storing Mushrooms]]).
- **Typical yield**: ~2–3 oz dried from a 5 lb bag on the first flush, plus a smaller second (Source: [[Zombie Myco — Revitalization (Rehydration) TEK]]).

## Key Concepts

- [[All-in-One Mushroom Grow Bag]]: sealed bag combining spawn + substrate + port + filter
- [[Sterile Inoculation Technique]]: still-air, alcohol-and-flame injection of spores/LC

- [[Break and Shake (Spawn Distribution)]]: speed lever at 50–75%

## Contradictions

- **Fruiting temperature band.** Vendor guides differ: some say 60–75°F generally, the Midwest AIO guide keeps the *same* 72–76°F used for colonization, and North Spore cites 60–80°F. Resolution: these are overlapping species-and-vendor ranges; **the high 60s–low 70s°F satisfies most**, and exact optimum is genetics-dependent.
- **Dunking necessity.** Dedicated dunking TEKs detail full submersion, but the same vendor notes AIO bags usually don't need it. Resolution: **mist-only for AIO unless the block has clearly dried out** — dunking adds a contamination vector (Source: [[Zombie Myco — Revitalization (Rehydration) TEK]]).
