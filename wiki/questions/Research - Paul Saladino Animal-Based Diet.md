# Research: Paul Saladino Animal-Based Diet

## Overview

[[Paul Saladino]] ("Carnivore MD") argues grass-fed ruminant meat is the single best human food and that most vegetables are net-harmful; after strict carnivore damaged his own hormones and sleep, he pivoted (2022) to the "animal-based diet" — meat, organs, fruit, honey, raw dairy. The research verdict: meat's nutrient density is real, the grass-fed upgrade is modest, the anti-vegetable case is mostly inverted relative to the evidence, and the diet's reported benefits are explainable by what it shares with any whole-food cleanup rather than by removing plants.

## Why He Says Grass-Fed Meat Is the Best Food (→ [[Saladino Grass-Fed Meat Argument]])

1. **Bioavailability**: heme iron, zinc, B12, creatine, complete protein — absorbed better than plant forms, with no antinutrient interference *(true, uncontroversial)*
2. **Grass-fed micronutrient edge**: more vitamin E, beta-carotene, CoQ10, omega-3, CLA *(real but tiny in absolute terms — his own vitamin C delta is ~4 mg/lb)*
3. **Contaminant avoidance** — his real centerpiece: glyphosate/atrazine/zearalenone/dioxins enter via feedlot grain *(plausible mechanism, unquantified exposure)*
4. **Evolutionary fit**: ~2M years of ruminant hunting *(half-true — foragers also ate substantial plants)*
5. **No plant defense chemicals** *(the premise the evidence rejects — see vegetables section)*

## How to Follow It (→ [[Animal-Based Diet (Saladino)]])

≥1 lb grass-fed ruminant meat per 100 lbs bodyweight · 0.5 oz liver daily + other organs · carbs from fruit/honey scaled to activity (×0.7–1.7 g per lb goal weight; he eats ~300 g/day) · dairy and pasture-raised eggs · three toxicity tiers: eat (meat/organs/fruit/honey/dairy/avocado/squash) → conditional (white rice, sweet potato, fermented veg, seafood, coffee) → avoid (seed oils, grains, legumes, nuts, crucifers, nightshades, leafy greens, UPF).

## Reasons TO Follow It

- **Whole-food core**: eliminates ultra-processed food, added sugar junk, refined seed-oil snack foods — the single most agreed-on dietary win
- **High protein + satiety**: 1 g/lb protein supports body composition; organ meats genuinely fix common micronutrient gaps (B12, retinol, iron, zinc, choline)
- **Not keto**: the fruit/honey carbs avoid the low-carb hormonal hole Saladino himself fell into
- **Elimination value**: for specific GI/autoimmune sufferers, a structured elimination that removes FODMAPs/gluten/legumes can relieve symptoms (then ideally reintroduce)
- **Self-correcting author**: he changed his diet when his labs went bad — better epistemics than most diet influencers

## Reasons NOT To Follow It

- **Zero fiber** → starves butyrate-producing gut bacteria; fiber is the best-attested diet-quality marker ([[Carnivore and Animal-Based Diet Risk Reviews (PMC)]]; vault: [[Gut Microbiome Fundamentals]], [[Fiber Supplement Tier List]])
- **Saturated fat/LDL**: intake far exceeds WHO limits; KETO-CTA showed coronary plaque progression within 1 year in high-LDL keto adults ([[Saladino Diet Critiques (Gene Food + Hone Health)]]). **Joe-specific: with Lp(a) at 152 nmol/L, a deliberately LDL-raising diet is the wrong direction** ([[Most Important Biomarkers (Priority Shortlist)]])
- **Raw dairy is a genuine hazard post-2024**: H5N1 found in commercially sold raw milk; CDC says pasteurized only ([[Raw Milk Safety and H5N1 (CDC FDA)]])
- **Opposes the largest evidence base in nutrition**: vegetable/whole-grain intake associates with lower all-cause mortality across ~800k-participant meta-analyses; no long-term outcome data exist for animal-based
- **Conflict of interest**: Heart & Soil sells the organ supplements the diet requires
- **Cost and social friction**: grass-fed everything + raw dairy sourcing is expensive and restrictive

## His Views on Vegetables (→ [[Paul-Saladino-Oats-Vegetables-Claims]], [[Antinutrients-Plant-Defense-Compounds]])

Frame: plants can't run, so they defend chemically ("50,000+ defensive strategies"); leaves/stems/roots/seeds carry the most toxins; **fruit is exempt because plants "want" it eaten** (seed dispersal). Named villains: oxalates, lectins, phytates, tannins, goitrogens, phytoestrogens, isothiocyanates, polyphenols. Prior vault verdict stands: **2 of 9 claims hold** (mycotoxin + glyphosate contamination of conventional oats — fixable by buying organic); the rest are overstated (meal-specific, dose-dependent, destroyed by cooking) or inverted (polyphenols and isothiocyanates are protective in human data). Post-pivot softening: fermented vegetables, squash, cucumber, white rice, sweet potato now "conditional," and his own food-list page concedes "no exact formula — individual tolerance varies."

## Contradictions

- [[Saladino Animal-Based Diet Official Guide]] claims raw dairy has "superior nutritional value" vs [[Raw Milk Safety and H5N1 (CDC FDA)]]: nutrition delta is minimal, pathogen delta is large. The CDC/FDA position is better evidenced.
- Saladino: high LDL is benign in the metabolically healthy vs KETO-CTA: plaque progressed faster than in diabetic cohorts. The trial data side against him.
- His "vegetables are toxic" frame vs his own food list allowing squash, cucumber, fermented veg, carrots — the rhetoric is stricter than the actual protocol.

## Open Questions

- No long-term RCT of animal-based (or carnivore) diets exists in either direction — all hard-outcome claims are extrapolation
- Whether "lean mass hyper-responders" (high LDL + high HDL/low TG) genuinely differ in plaque risk — KETO-CTA suggests no, but n=100, 1 year
- Actual measured contaminant-residue difference (glyphosate/atrazine) between grain-fed and grass-fed retail beef — Saladino's centerpiece claim, still unquantified
