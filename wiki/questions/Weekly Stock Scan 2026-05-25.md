**1. AAPL CEO Succession (Highest Priority)**

Markets have historically been volatile around Apple leadership transitions. Watch for: investor sentiment shifts, institutional repositioning, any further commentary from Cook or Ternus on strategic direction.

**2. AMD Cluster Insider Selling + Daiwa Downgrade**
CEO Lisa Su and another executive sold a combined ~$96M in AMD shares within one day of each other, immediately followed by a Daiwa downgrade (even though Daiwa raised their PT to $500, the rating cut to Hold is significant). Meanwhile, Evercore set a $579 street-high PT. The divergence between bullish sell-side PTs and insider behavior is a tension worth watching. Q2 guidance and data center demand signals will be key.

**3. LLY — Verzenio Safety Concern**
Eli Lilly's Verzenio (abemaciclib) is generating negative press related to a reported liver failure case. While Lilly remains a consensus Buy with strong GLP-1/Zepbound pipeline momentum, any FDA communication, label change, or additional adverse event reports could move the stock. Monitor closely if additional cases emerge.
