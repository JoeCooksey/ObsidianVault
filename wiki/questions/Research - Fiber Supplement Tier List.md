# Research - Fiber Supplement Tier List

## Overview
Autoresearch run (Joe asked for a fiber supplement tier list). The vault had fiber only mentioned in passing inside [[Supplement Tier List Complete]] and [[Gut Health Foods Tier List]] — no dedicated ranking. This run created the standalone **[[Fiber Supplement Tier List]]** plus the explanatory **[[Functional Fiber Framework (McRorie)]]**. **Bottom line: psyllium husk is the S-tier default for almost everyone; pick anything else only for a specific reason.**

## Key Findings
- **Psyllium wins on weight of evidence.** Only common fiber that is gel-forming **and** minimally fermented → normalizes stool both directions, FDA cholesterol claim, glycemic + IBS-pain benefits, low gas. Topped the constipation meta-analysis (RR 1.82, +~3 BMs/week). (Source: [[Fiber for Chronic Constipation Meta-Analysis (2022)]])
- **The mechanism, not the "soluble/insoluble" label, is what matters.** Benefit = **viscosity (gel) × resistance to fermentation.** Fermentable fibers feed bacteria but lose bulk and cause gas. (Source: [[Functional Fiber Framework (McRorie)]])
- **Dosing rule: >10 g/day for ≥4 weeks** is where constipation benefits appear; lower/shorter doesn't work.
- **Targeted picks:** oat β-glucan (3–4 g) for LDL ~5–7% ↓ (A-tier); methylcellulose / calcium polycarbophil for gas-sensitive guts (non-fermentable, A-tier); PHGG for IBS that flares on psyllium (low-gas prebiotic, B-tier).
- **Inulin is overrated as a supplement.** Non-viscous, fully fermentable → bloating, no cholesterol benefit, and Stanford 2022 found 30 g/day caused inflammation + liver-enzyme spikes in some. C-tier. (Source: [[Inulin High-Dose Inflammation Finding (Stanford 2022)]])
- **Safety:** glucomannan must never be taken dry (choking/obstruction); separate all fiber from meds by ~2 h; ramp slowly.

## Key Concepts
- [[Functional Fiber Framework (McRorie)]]: viscous + non-fermented = best functional fiber.
- [[Food Matrix Effect]]: whole-food fiber still beats isolated supplements; supplements are the backstop.

## Tier Summary
- **S:** Psyllium husk
- **A:** Oat β-glucan · Methylcellulose · Calcium polycarbophil
- **B:** PHGG · Glucomannan · Acacia/gum arabic
- **C:** Inulin/FOS · Wheat dextrin · Resistant starch
- **D/F:** Isolated wheat bran · Polydextrose

## Contradictions
- Marketing frames inulin/wheat dextrin ("prebiotic", "clear & tasteless") as premium, but clinical-outcome evidence ranks them below psyllium and behind methylcellulose for regularity. Resolution: prebiotic ≠ functional benefit; gas-prone fermentable fibers are convenience/microbiome plays, not first-line.

## Open Questions
- No direct head-to-head RCT of PHGG vs psyllium was found — B vs S ranking rests on tolerability and indication, not comparison.
- Resistant starch evidence is promising (butyrate, glycemia) but heterogeneous; not fully tiered here.
- The Stanford inulin-inflammation signal needs replication before treating it as more than a high-dose caution.
