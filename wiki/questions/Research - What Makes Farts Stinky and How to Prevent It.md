# Research - What Makes Farts Stinky and How to Prevent It

## Overview
Fart **odor** and fart **volume** are two separate phenomena with separate causes and separate fixes. The bulk of gas (N₂, O₂, CO₂, H₂, CH₄) is odorless; the smell comes from trace **sulfur gases (<1%)**, dominated by **hydrogen sulfide (H₂S)**. Those sulfur gases are made by gut bacteria from **dietary sulfur**. So you reduce smell by cutting sulfur intake and/or binding H₂S — not by reducing gas volume.

## Key Findings
- **Odor ≠ volume.** Bulk gases are odorless; a loud fart can be smell-free. (Source: [[Chemistry World - The Chemistry of Farts]])
- **Three odorants**, ~50 ppm each: **hydrogen sulfide** (rotten egg), **methanethiol** (rotten cabbage), **dimethyl sulfide** (garlic). (Source: [[Suarez 1998 - Identification of Gases Responsible for Odour of Human Flatus]])
- **H₂S is dominant** (1.06 µmol/L) and its concentration **correlates with malodor, p ≤ 0.001**. Confidence: **high**.
- **Source of the smell:** gut **[[Sulfate-Reducing Bacteria (Desulfovibrio)|sulfate-reducing bacteria]]** convert H₂ + sulfate → H₂S; bacterial breakdown of sulfur amino acids (methionine/cysteine from protein) adds methanethiol/H₂S.
- **Diet drives it:** high-sulfur foods — cruciferous veg, garlic/onion, red meat, eggs, dairy, legumes — feed H₂S production. (Source: [[Cary Gastroenterology - Why Do My Farts Smell So Bad]])
- **Best odor fix — bismuth subsalicylate:** 524 mg 4×/day cut fecal H₂S **>95%** by binding it as insoluble bismuth sulfide. Episodic use. Confidence: **high**. (Source: [[Suarez 1998 - Bismuth Subsalicylate Decreases Hydrogen Sulfide]])
- **Oral activated charcoal does NOT work** (saturates in transit), but **external charcoal garments do** (briefs ~100%). (Source: [[Suarez 1999 - Failure of Activated Charcoal to Reduce Gas]])
- **Simethicone shows no benefit** for common flatulence; **alpha-galactosidase (Beano)** reduces *volume* from fermentable carbs. (Source: [[AAFP 2009 - Effective Management of Flatulence]])

## Contradictions
- **Activated charcoal "works" vs "doesn't."** Resolved: charcoal *adsorbs* sulfur gas (so external garments work and in-vitro it removes odor), but **swallowed** charcoal saturates before the colon and fails. Both Suarez papers and AAFP agree. (Sources: [[Suarez 1998 - Identification of Gases Responsible for Odour of Human Flatus]], [[Suarez 1999 - Failure of Activated Charcoal to Reduce Gas]], [[AAFP 2009 - Effective Management of Flatulence]])

## Open Questions
- Can the **sulfate-reducing vs methanogen** balance be shifted long-term by diet or probiotics to permanently lower H₂S? Not established.
- Quantitative dose-response for **specific foods** → measured H₂S in everyday eaters (most data is from bean+lactulose challenge studies).
- Efficacy of **bismuth subgallate (Devrom)** specifically vs subsalicylate for flatus (the one RCT found was in duodenal-switch surgery patients, mixed results).
- Long-term safety ceiling for **episodic bismuth** use.

## Bottom Line
Stinky farts = trace **hydrogen sulfide** made by sulfate-reducing gut bacteria from the **sulfur in your food**. Prevent it by eating less sulfur (smaller meat servings; go easy on cruciferous/allium/eggs at one sitting), eating slower with fewer carbonated drinks, and — for events — taking **bismuth subsalicylate**, which slashes H₂S by >95%. Skip oral charcoal and Gas-X for odor; they don't work. See a doctor if foul gas comes with pain, bleeding, fever, or weight loss.
