# Research - Ways to Make Money Online (Tier List)

## Overview
Surveyed the 2026 landscape of online income (≈30 distinct methods across freelancing, content, products, gig/microtask, and "passive" plays) and ranked them by **career-capital fit for Joe** — a 19-year-old ASU EE student, already a Python programmer, strong in math/EE, building toward an LLNL/Sandia internship (Oct 2026), time-constrained, and value/compounding-minded. The full ranked artifact is **[[Online Income Methods Tier List]]**.

## Key Findings
- **The ranking flips the internet's usual order.** For Joe, the methods that double as résumé + GitHub portfolio toward the EE track rise to the top; the "scalable passive income" darlings sink because they burn his scarcest resource — deep-work hours — on marketing skills that don't compound into engineering. (Source: synthesis; [[Free Time Tier List]])
- **Top 3 (S-tier):** STEM/math tutoring ($25–60/hr), AI-training/data work for coders ($40–100+/hr), freelance Python automation ($15–40/hr rising). Each pays *and* builds career capital. (Sources: [[Online Tutoring Rates 2026 — Brighterly-Glassdoor]], [[AI Training Platforms for Coders 2026 — aitrainer.work]], [[Side Hustles for College Students 2026 — Upwork]])
- **AI training is the standout *new* fit:** highest async $/hr for his exact skillset, and the work itself is AI literacy worth a 56% EE wage premium. (Source: [[AI Training Data Work (Online Income)]])
- **"Passive income" is mostly active income with the failures edited out.** Realistic: ~$500–2,000 month one, $3k–8k/month within a year *for people who go deep on a real skill* — not hands-off. (Source: [[Why Most Make-Money-Online Advice Fails 2026 — NSF Tech]])
- **D-tier avoids align with Joe's own prior research:** dropshipping, crypto "AI trading bots"/HYIP, day trading, and MLM funnels are negative-EV or scams — consistent with [[Index Fund Investing]] and [[Research - Stock Buy-Sell Factors and Quant Finance]].

## Contradictions
- **Pay ranges diverge by source.** Freelance Python beginners are quoted $10–25/hr (Upwork beginner) but $30 median overall; tutoring spans $25–80/hr. Resolution: beginner *entry* rates are low; STEM/coding niche + reviews move you to the top of each band within months. Trust the *skill-gated* upper ranges for Joe, not the floor.
- **"Passive income" framing.** Listicles (whop, Hostinger, due.com) sell courses/products/affiliate as passive; the reality-check source ([[Why Most Make-Money-Online Advice Fails 2026 — NSF Tech]]) and consumer-protection regulators (DFPI, ASIC) call most of it active-or-fraudulent. The skeptical source is more credible (cites mechanics + regulator action).

## Open Questions
- Which specific AI-training platform currently has the best *coding-queue availability* and pay for a US-based STEM student? (Volume fluctuates monthly — verify with a current review before committing hours.)
- Does ASU / LLNL have any policy on outside freelance work that could matter once Joe holds an internship or clearance-adjacent role? (Worth checking before scaling freelance income in 2027+.)
- Not deeply costed this pass (page budget): voice-over, podcasting, UGC economics, and digital-product launch funnels — all landed C-tier on fit, so deprioritized.
