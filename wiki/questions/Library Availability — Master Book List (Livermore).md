# Library Availability — Master Book List (Livermore)

Availability snapshot of every title from the [[Research - Book Recommendations Master List]] checked against the **Livermore Public Library** catalog (livermorelibrary.bibliocommons.com), filtered to physical Books. Checked **2026-05-31**. Status is a point-in-time reading — checkouts and holds change daily.

**Tally:** ~31 available now · ~19 checked out (holdable) · 11 not in catalog (request via LINK+).

> [!tip] How to act on this
> - **Available** → grab on the shelf (call numbers cluster around 158/332/155 for nonfiction; FIC for fiction).
> - **Checked out** → place a hold online; you're queued automatically.
> - **Not in catalog** → use **LINK+**, Livermore's interlibrary loan consortium, which pulls from dozens of partner libraries. Most of these are common titles and arrive within days.
