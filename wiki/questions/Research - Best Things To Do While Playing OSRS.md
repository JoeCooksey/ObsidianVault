# Research - Best Things To Do While Playing OSRS

## Overview
"Best things to do while playing OSRS" splits into three real questions: **make GP** (AFK or active), **progress the account** (what's worth doing next), and **play in the background** (what to grind while doing something else). The right answer depends on how much attention you have and where your account is. The single biggest lever is **matching the activity's input demand to your available attention** — pick by idle-window and risk, not by raw GP/hr.

## Key Findings
- **AFK income, best picks:** Sacred Eels (300k–440k, 87 Fishing), Brutal Black Dragons (300k–500k, 77 Slayer), Amethyst Mining (200k–400k, 92 Mining). The most *hands-off* income is **Kingdom of Miscellania** (~50k–100k/day for one click) and **herb/birdhouse runs**. (Source: [[OSRS Money Making Guide — Best AFK Methods 2025]])
- **Active income ceiling is ~10–50× AFK:** bossing tops the list — Theatre of Blood, Nex 12M–16M (group), Vorkath up to 4.5M, Phantom Muspah 1.5M–5M (solo). High RC (blood/soul/lava runes) gives 1.2M–2M/hr; GE flipping scales with cash stack. (Source: [[Best GP Per Hour OSRS 2025]])
- **Most-AFK methods by idle window:** Infected Roots (WC, 30 min), Sailing (~30 min), Nightmare Zone (~20 min), Crashed Stars (~63 min total), Gemstone/Ammonite Crabs (~10 min). These define the best **second-screen** grinds. (Source: [[OSRS Wiki — Idle (AFK Methods)]])
- **Highest-leverage progression goals:** Barrows Gloves → Dragon Defender → Fire Cape → base-90s combat → Slayer → grandmaster quests → 83 Construction. Each unlocks more content than it costs. (Source: [[Theoatrix — 20 Mid-Game Goals OSRS]])
- **The deciding variable is attention, not GP/hr:** the best activity for a maxed player at the keyboard (raids) is the *worst* choice for someone working on a second monitor (where Infected Roots / NMZ win despite lower GP).
