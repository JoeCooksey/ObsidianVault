## Market Context

US inflation reached 4.1% — its highest level in three years — driven by a spike in gasoline prices attributable in part to the US-Iran military conflict disrupting oil shipping through the Strait of Hormuz. The Fed is expected to maintain its current rate posture given the supply-side inflation driver, but sustained elevated energy prices could feed into broader CPI. Semiconductor stocks experienced a sharp sell-off on June 5 (Broadcom cautious AI outlook + HBM supply concerns) but partially recovered on June 11 on Oracle's reaffirmed AI infrastructure expansion — the pattern illustrates how single-demand-signal events are now moving the entire chip complex. Geopolitical risk premium in oil/energy remains elevated for the near term given the Iran escalation.
