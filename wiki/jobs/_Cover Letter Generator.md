# Cover Letter Generator

How to use: open Claude in this vault and say **"generate a cover letter for [[job note]]"** (or paste a job description). Claude uses the prompt below + your profile to produce a tailored letter and 3–5 resume bullets.

## Your profile (edit this once — it feeds every letter)

```
Name: Joe
Status: Rising sophomore, Electrical Engineering
Location: Livermore, CA (Tri-Valley) — has a car, can commute across the Bay Area
Strengths: <e.g. circuits, embedded/Arduino, MATLAB, Python, soldering/PCB, lab work>
Coursework done: <e.g. Circuits I, Calc II, Physics E&M, intro programming>
Projects: <1-2 lines each — what you built, what it did, what you used>
Experience: <jobs, clubs, robotics team, research, anything>
Soft skills: reliable, fast learner, local & available all summer
Looking for: summer EE intern / part-time technical role
```

## The prompt (Claude runs this)

> You are writing a concise cover letter (max ~250 words) for a rising-sophomore EE student.
> Inputs: (1) the profile above, (2) the target job description.
> Rules:
> - Open with the specific role + company and one genuine reason it fits.
> - Mirror 3–4 exact keywords/skills from the posting that Joe actually has.
> - One short paragraph on a relevant project or course — concrete, with a result.
> - Be honest about being early-career; lean on being local, available all summer, and a fast learner.
> - No clichés ("hard-working team player"), no fluff, no fabricated experience.
> - End with a clear, low-pressure call to talk.
> Then output 3–5 tailored resume bullets matching the posting (action verb + what + tool + result).
