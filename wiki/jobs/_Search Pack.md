# Job Search Pack — EE / Tri-Valley

Run these 2–3x/week. Add any good hit to the tracker (copy `_templates/Job.md` into `wiki/jobs/`).

## Indeed
Paste into the search box (location: Livermore, CA, radius 35 mi). Indeed boolean uses quotes + AND/OR.
- `("electrical engineering" OR "EE") (intern OR internship)`
- `(electronics OR hardware OR "test technician") (intern OR "part time" OR entry)`
- `(PCB OR soldering OR "electrical assembly" OR "lab technician")`
- `(solar OR "renewable energy") (installer OR technician OR intern)`  ← big in CA
- `(engineering OR technical) "student" -senior -lead`

## LinkedIn Jobs
- Filter: Internship + Entry level, "On-site/Hybrid", posted in last week.
- Searches: `electrical engineering intern`, `hardware engineering intern`, `electronics technician`, `test engineer intern`.
- Turn ON "Easy Apply OFF" mentally — company-site applications convert better.

## Handshake (do this first — built for students)
- Filter to: Internship, your school, on-site within 50 mi of 94550.
- Many Bay Area hardware firms post here *only*.
